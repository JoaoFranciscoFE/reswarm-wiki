    Paper Planter¶

    Paper Planter
    "A biodegradable pot that grows in about 1hr. Harvest to collect pollen, items, and Nectar boosts!"

    The Paper Planter is a single-use planter added in the 2021-12-26 update. Alone, it grows in 1 hour and stores 50,000 pollen. It can be bought in the Pro Shop for 25,000 Honey and 1 Sunflower Seed. A player can store up to 100 Paper Planters at a time, and up to 3 planters can be planted at once.

    Growth, pollen and nectar¶

    • Grows in 1 hour of playtime.
    • Glitter used in its field speeds it up: x1.5 for one, rising in halving steps up to x2 at 6 stacks. The stacks stay on the planter until it is harvested.
    • Holds 50,000 pollen at full growth. A harvest gives capacity × growth × pollen bonus. It has no pollen bonus.
    • Nectar multipliers: x0.75 Satisfying, x0.75 Comforting, x0.75 Invigorating, x0.75 Motivating, x0.75 Refreshing. On harvest it gives one Nectar type from its field, lasting grow time × growth × multiplier × 3 seconds.
    • Up to 3 bees can sip from it at once.

    Puffshrooms¶

    When it reaches 95% growth, it has a 10% chance to smoke and spawn 3 Puffshrooms (one at the planter and two nearby) at level 1.

    They are always Common.

    Drops¶

    A full harvest gives 10 item tokens. Harvesting early gives fewer, in proportion to growth, but always at least 1. Tokens are filled in this order: guaranteed items (full harvests only), then field rules, then random picks from the pool below.

    Field rules¶

    • Full harvest in the Pineapple Patch: +1 Enzymes. In the Sunflower Field: +1 Oil.
    • In the Coconut Field the pool also gets Coconuts (1 / 1 / 3, weight 30).

    Trivia¶

    • This is the cheapest and fastest-growing planter in the game.
    • It is one of the many planters to not be sold in Dapper Bear's Shop, the others being the Red Clay Planter, Blue Clay Planter, Festive Planter, Ticket Planter, Sticker Planter, Petal Planter, Hydroponic Planter and Heat-Treated Planter.
    • This is the cheapest item in the Pro Shop in terms of honey, only costing 25,000 honey.
    • This is the only item in the Pro Shop that is cheaper than an item in the Noob Shop, being the Helmet.
    • This, the Ticket Planter and Sticker Planter the are the only one-time use planters that are renewable.
    • This is the only planter in the game that stores more pollen than the honey it costs.
    • It is one of the four planters that are one-time use. The remaining three are the Sticker Planter, Ticket Planter and the Festive Planter.
    • It is also the only one-time use planter that can be bought from a store.
    • This is also the only planter that reduces nectar gained, multiplying all nectar by 0.75x. All other planters either boost nectar gain (although some of them only boost certain types) or do not change it at all.
