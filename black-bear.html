    Black Bear is a quest giver located at the very beginning of the game. He is one of the eight permanent Quest Bears that can be accessed in the game, with the others being Brown Bear, Mother Bear, Panda Bear, Science Bear, Dapper Bear, Polar Bear, and Spirit Bear. After the player has completed all of Black Bear's main questlines, the player can obtain repetitive quests every hour, similar to Brown Bear.

    He is usually the first quest bear to be accessed, along with Mother Bear, Brown Bear, and Traveling Bears (only during special events). He provides quests to players to help them progress in the game and to provide extra goals to reach for. This particular bear focuses on pollen collecting. He is located near the Ticket Tent and the Red Cannon, but he is also near the Sunflower Field and the Hives. Black Bear was a quest giver for the Egg Hunt 2019 and Egg Hunt 2020 events and was one of the NPCs to give an Ornament quest for Beesmas 2019. He also had a quest during Beesmas (2020 and beyond) for the Honey Wreath.

    Black Bear's quests require collecting pollen for him. In return, he will give the player various rewards, including Honey, Royal Jelly, a Silver Egg, a Gold Egg, a Diamond Egg, a Star Jelly, and eventually gives a Mythic Egg. He also gives many side rewards in the later quests.

    Through all of his quests, he rewards 2,352,532,300 Honey. When doing the quests for the Diamond Egg, he will give1 Royal Jelly every 4 quests for a total of 5 royal jellies, and a Diamond Egg. When doing quests for the Star Jelly, he'll reward 5 Tickets and aRoyal Jelly for every quest completed except the quest "Solo On The Stump,” for which Black Bear gives 10 Tickets instead of the usual 5 Tickets.

    Trivia¶

    • Black Bear proudly calls himself a "Honey Connoisseur" in his dialogue. This is what the "Black Bear's Title?" hint (behind the Instant Converter where Tunnel Bear is) refers to, leading to the Code "Connoisseur".
    • He is the third bear to have a list of endless repeating quests.
    • Black Bear uses the Cartoony Animation Package.
    • He is the only quest giver to have normal quests and repeatable quests.
    • The repeatable quests used to be given one after another, with no cooldown.
    • As of now, the repeatable quests have a cooldown of one hour.
    • After the 2020-04-06 Update, where Black Bear got his Mythic Egg quests, if the player already had a repeatable quest, it was unable to be turned in until they had completed all of Black Bear's Mythic Egg quests.
    • Black Bear is one of the twelve Cub Buddy skins.
    • He is self-aware that he is in a Roblox game, along with Panda Bear, Mother Bear, Bee Bear, Science Bear, and Spirit Bear, as he says, “But this is ROBLOX! Anything goes!” He also says "the 'luck' stat in the game," mentions waiting for an update to happen, and mentions that he's a 'virtual bear.'
    • He was the first bear to give a Diamond Egg, with Panda Bear being the second, Brown Bear being the third, and Dapper Bear being the fourth.
    • He was the second bear to give a Mythic Egg, with Brown Bear being the first and Dapper Bear being the third.
    • He, Mother Bear, and Brown Bear were "nice" in 2018, as stated by Bee Bear during the Beesmas 2018 Event.
    • In "Pretty Pumpkins" during dialogue, originally it was only "Test.". This was a mistake, and the actual dialogue was added sometime later.
    • He, Panda Bear, and Spirit Bear are the only bears that mention the history of the mountain.
    • He mentions a mysterious bee once "destroyed an entire field with powerful gusts of wind" in the "Black Bear, Why?" dialogue. This was later revealed that said bee was Wild Windy Bee.
    • He is acrophobic, or afraid of heights, as said in the quest "High Altitude".
    • He is also arachnophobic, or afraid of spiders, as said in the quest "Cobweb Sweeper".
    • In the Egg Hunt 2019 Event, Black Bear had instructed to collect 200 pollen from the Sunflower Field, but when the player got the quest, it told them to collect 200 pollen from the Dandelion Field instead.
    • In the dialogue for "Colorful Craving", he stated that 250 Moon Charms were needed to generate a Moon Amulet, but only 100 are needed. This was fixed in an unknown update.
    • The "Rojo-A-Go-Go" quest is the quest that requires the least amount of pollen collected from one field that being 8 pollen from the Cactus Field.
    • Black Bear references external many multiple times throughout his quests.
    • The "Bamboo Boogie 2: Bamboo Boogaloo" quest's name is reminiscent of the film title "Breakin’ 2: Electric Boogaloo."
    • The quest "Strawberry Field Forever" is a reference to The Beatles' "Strawberry Fields Forever" song.
    • The quest "Pumpkins, Please!" may be referencing the popular game "Papers, Please".
    • The quest "All-Whitey Then" could be a reference to actor Jim Carrey and one of his catchphrases "Alrighty then!"
    • Black Bear has the most amount of quests in the game, with a total of 75 quests (excluding repeatable & event quests).
    • The quest “Sweet and Sour” may be a reference to what Gummy Bear says when a player approaches him in Lair.
    • Excluding event quests, Black Bear and Brown Bear are the only quest givers that only require pollen to be collected.
    • In "Weed Wacker 2," Black Bear explains to the player that he isn't convincing the player to do his "dirty work."
    • In “Azure Adventure” Black Bear explains to the player that would pick Ninja Bee to lead the Blue HQ, as opposed to Bucko Bee.
