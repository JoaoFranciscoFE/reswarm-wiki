    Pineapple¶

    Pineapple

    "Increases your bond with a bee by 25."

    A Pineapple is a type of treat that can be fed to a bee to raise its bond by 25. However, when fed to a Hasty, Brave, Honey, Exhausted, Shocked, Fire, Demon, Lion, or Fuzzy Bee, the amount of bond they gain is doubled. These bees also have a small chance of becoming gifted when fed Pineapples. Mythic bees have a 1/24,000 chance to be gifted; Legendary Bees, a 1/12,000 chance; Epic bees, a 1/10,000 chance; and Rare bees, a 1/8,000 chance. Feeding a radioactive bee a pineapple has a 0.004% (1 in 25,000) to mutate a bee.

    Ways to Obtain¶

    • 5 Pineapples can be found behind the pineapple near the Treat Dispenser.
    • 1 Pineapple is given to the player every time they use the Treat Dispenser.
    • As a reward from the Ant Challenge.
    • As a reward from Memory Matches (Every type except Night Memory Match and the Winter Memory Match).
    • As a stack reward for adding certain Stickers to the Sticker Stack:
    • Shocked Hive Slot Sticker rewards 50 Pineapples.
    • Rhubarb Sticker rewards 50 Pineapples.

    Drops¶

    • Farming in the Pineapple Patch and in the Hub Field.
    • Harvesting leaves in Pineapple Patch may also yield Pineapples.
    • As a drop from mantises, scorpions, spiders, werewolves, Commando Chick, Stick Nymphs, Stick Bug, King Beetle, and Tunnel Bear.
    • As a drop from a Festive Gift or Festive Mark.
    • As a gift from a cub buddy.
    • As a reward for completely growing any kind of sprout except for Moon, Gummy, and Debug sprouts.
    • Planting a sprout in the Pineapple Patch yields 7x more Pineapples than other fields.
    • Harvesting sparkles (Higher chances in the Pineapple Patch).
    • As a drop from Puffshrooms.
    • As a drop from planters, which include:
    • Ticket Planters
    • Festive Planters
    • Plastic Planters
    • Candy Planters
    • Blue Clay Planters
    • Red Clay Planters
    • Pesticide Planters
    • Planters Of Plenty
    • Digging into the Beesmas Feast sometimes grants Pineapples.

    Quests¶

    • Onett gives pineapples on the following quest:
    • Star Journey 1 gives 25 Pineapples.
    • Spirit Bear gives Pineapples on the following quests:
    • Witness Grandeur gives 100 Pineapples.
    • Nature's Lessons gives 250 Pineapples.
    • Rhubarb That Could Have Been gives 250 Pineapples.
    • Do You Bee-Lleve in Magic? gives 300 Pineapples.
    • Beans Becoming gives 500 Pineapples.
    • Echoing Call gives 500 Pineapples upon receiving the quest.

    Crafting Uses¶

    Total required for all single-purchase items: 305 Pineapples
