    Dapper Bear is a quest giver, and one of the eight permanent quest Bears that can be accessed in the game, the others being Black Bear, Brown Bear, Mother Bear, Panda Bear, Science Bear, Polar Bear and Spirit Bear. Dapper Bear is located inside his shop next to Stump Field.

    Inside Dapper Bear's shop, he sells many different planters ranging from750K-100T Honey (alongside crafting materials). He also sells many different types of Beequips for Honey and Tickets, with some being locked behind a certain amount of Dapper Bear Quests completed.

    After speaking with him, Dapper Bear rewards the player 3 Ticket Planters and a Beequip Case (if the player does not already have one).

    Requirements¶

    You need the following Accessories to enter Dapper Bear's shop, those being the Hiking Boots and the Propeller Hat. Players can also use gear that is better than the ones previously listed.

    Quests¶

    Dapper Bear currently has 16 Quests. His 16 main quests focus on collecting nectar, collecting tokens from planters, and popping Puffshrooms; with the exception of the 16th quest (added after the 2026-01-19 Update) which focuses mainly on stickers instead of the aforementioned. The quest for obtaining the Blue Drive in order to repair Robo Bear does still focus on Puffshrooms, planters, and nectar, however.

    His 2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th, 10th, 12th, 14th and 16th Quest rewards an additional Beequip Case slot. If you received Beequip Case slots from Bee Bear previously, you will instead be rewarded one Ticket Planters until you catch up to the amount of additional slots a new player would have.

    Main Quests¶

    Trivia¶

    • Dapper Bear is shorter than every other bear in the game. This is referenced in his dialogue and is a key detail in the Tacky Planters chain that rewards the Dapper From Above Sticker.
    • Dapper Bear, Gummy Bear, Tunnel Bear, Noob Bear, Pro Bear, Top Bear, and Shadow Bear are the only bears to reside indoors.
    • Dapper Bear and Gummy Bear are the only quest bears to reside indoors.
      • Dapper Bear is the only permanent quest bear to reside indoors.
    • This bear, Mother Bear, Gummy Bear, Robo Bear, and Bee Bear are the only bears to own a shop.
    • This bear, Bee Bear, and Mother Bear are the only quest bears to own a shop.
    • This bear, Mother Bear, and Robo Bear are the only permanent bears to own a shop.
    • Like Mother Bear, Dapper Bear uses the Stylish Animation Pack.
    • Like Mother Bear and Gummy Bee, Dapper Bear's face emits light. This is most noticeable if the player looks at Dapper Bear at night.
    • He is the only permanent quest giver whose first quest was a seasonal one.
    • Dapper Bear may have a possible Russian background. When the player completes his Samovar quest (the Samovar is also of Russian origins), Dapper Bear mentions that he made room for some beequips and the Samovar on his travel back to Moscow.
    • In Beesmas Winter 2024, one of the requirements for his samovar quest was “Collect 25 tokens from planters in the Pineapple Patch” However, it was broken and no tokens collected in Pineapple Patch would count towards the quest. This was later fixed by setting the requirement to finished for all players.
    • Dapper Bear, Sun Bear, and Spirit Bear are the only bears to wear 3D accessories.
    • Of the three, Dapper Bear is the only one to wear accessories that the player can't use.
    • Upon the soft launch of Beesmas 2025, Dapper Bear's shop was broken to some players.
    • This resulted in a broken UI when the shop was opened, and for the beequips to appear to be 5 Red Extracts per beequip.
    • Dapper Bear, Gifted Bucko Bee, and Gifted Riley Bee are the only NPCs that own shops that require quests to be completed to unlock certain items in their shop.
    • This also makes Dapper Bear the only bear to own a shop with items requiring the player to complete quests.
