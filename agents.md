# Re://:Swarm Wiki

> The community wiki for Re://:Swarm (Re Swarm, ReSwarm), a reimagined take on classic Bee Swarm Simulator on Roblox. Bees, items, fields, quests, codes and update logs.

Re://:Swarm (also written Re Swarm, ReSwarm or Reswarm) is a Roblox game made by ReplicatedEvents and released on August 26, 2026. It is a reimagined version of an older Bee Swarm Simulator, rebuilt with new assets. This wiki (Re://:Swarm Wiki, Reswarm Wiki) documents its bees, items, beequips, fields, mobs, quests, codes and update logs.
