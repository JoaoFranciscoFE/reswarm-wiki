**Frosty Bee** is a Blue [Epic bee](bees-epic.md).

Frosty Bee's favorite treat is [Blueberries](blueberry.md).

Frosty Bee likes the [Blue Flower Field](blue-flower-field.md), and the [Mountain Top Field](mountain-top-field.md). It dislikes the [Mushroom Field](mushroom-field.md) and the [Pepper Patch](pepper-patch.md).

## Stats

* Collects 10 [Pollen](pollen.md) in 4 seconds.
* Makes 80 [Honey](honey.md) in 4 seconds.
* +25% [Energy](energy.md), -20% [Movespeed](stats.md#Speed).
* 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](gifted-bee.md): x1.25 [Blue Bomb Pollen](system-page.md#Blue_Bomb_Pollen).

### Abilities

* **[Blue Boost](ability-tokens.md#Boost)** Grants x1.2 pollen from blue [Flowers](flowers.md) for 15s. Stacks up to 10 times.
* **[Blue Bomb+](ability-tokens.md#Bomb)** Collects 10 pollen from 29 surrounding blue flowers (+10% pollen per Level). Combo with other bombs to increase power.

## Trivia

* Frosty Bee, [Commander Bee](commander-bee.md), and [Buoyant Bee](buoyant-bee.md) are the only [bees](bees.md) that wear hats.
* Frosty Bee is likely a reference to Frosty the Snowman.
* The red counterpart of Frosty Bee is [Rage Bee](rage-bee.md).
* A Frosty Bee Jelly could have been obtained from one of the bundles/packs in Bee Bear's Catalog.
* This bee's name is the placeholder's name for any bee in a hive slot that hasn't loaded yet.
* The bee may like [Mountain Top Field](mountain-top-field.md) because it is cold on high mountains.
