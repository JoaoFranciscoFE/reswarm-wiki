# Quality of life

![Quality of life](img/Sprinklers.png){ .update-log-icon }

**Released:** September 1, 2026

Auto sprinklers, auto gifted basic eggs, quest chaining and balance changes.

## What's new

* Clicking the Rebirth icon opens the Rebirth menu.
* Auto Supreme Sprinklers: places [sprinklers](sprinklers.md) in a pattern that covers the whole field.
* Auto until gifted Basic: uses Basic Eggs until you get a [gifted](gifted-bee.md) one (off by default).
* Clicking a completed quest turns it in and gives you the next one.
* Bees far away from you move faster, scaling with distance.
* [Digital Bee](digital-bee.md) is always in your first round if it is in your hive.

## Changes

* Glitched Drive price: 5 of each [drive](drives.md) + 100 Cogs > 900 Cogs.
* Colored drive caps 5 > 15.
* Solo and same-field Coconut combo: each bounce gives +5 instead of +1.
* [Polar Bear](polar-bear.md) quests only need 1 of each mob.

## Fixes

* Fixed bees sometimes disappearing.
