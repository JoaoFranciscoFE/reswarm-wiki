# Swirled Wax

Swirled Wax

*"A spiraled hunk of beeswax. Used to redetermine the stats of a Bee's Beequip."*

**Swirled Wax** is an inventory item and a type of [wax](waxes.md) that can be used on a [beequip](beequip.md) to reroll its stats. Beequips with higher potential have a greater chance of having better stats when rerolled. Swirled wax has [Upgrade Value](beequip.md#Stats,_Waxes,_Chances) of 0.

When used on a beequip, it will change its stats. If the Beequip has waxes used on it, it will alter the enhancements as well.

When a swirled wax is applied to a beequip, a message pops up saying:

The Wax altered the Beequip's stats

Sometimes, the stats won't show any changes when you use a swirled wax. Most of the time, the bug can be fixed by rejoining the game.

## Ways to Obtain

* Crafting it via the [Blender](blender.md) for:
  * 3 [Hard Waxes](hard-wax.md)
  * 9 [Soft Waxes](soft-wax.md)
  * 6 [Purple Potions](purple-potion.md)
  * 3,333 [Royal Jellies](royal-jelly.md)
* As a stack reward for adding certain [stickers](sticker.md) to the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md):
  * Auryn rewards 1 swirled wax.
  * Pearl Girl rewards 1 swirled wax.
* As a reward from the [Winter Memory Match](memory-match.md#Winter_Memory_Match).
* Opening Crafty Gift Box (11th) gives 5 Swirled Waxes alongside other rewards.
* Purchasing "Frozen Forest Pack" and "Snow Queen Bundle" bundles in [Bee Bear's Catalog](bee-bear-s-catalog.md) gives 5 Swirled Waxes amongst other rewards.
* Purchasing "Purple-Turple Mega Pack" bundle in [Bee Bear's Catalog](bee-bear-s-catalog.md) gives 10 Swirled Waxes and other rewards.
* Giving a [Present](present.md) to [Honey Bee](honey-bee-npc.md) rewards 3 Swirled Waxes alongside other items.
* Purchasing "Merry Mythic Mondo-Haul" bundle in Shop rewards 25 Swirled Waxes with other rewards

### Drops

* As a reward from Gifted Riley Bee's [Honeyday Candles](honeyday-candles.md). (1.62%)
* As a drop from a level 13 [Snowbear](snowbear.md).
* As a rare drop from an Epic Puffshroom. An uncommon drop from a Legendary Puffshroom, and a common drop from a Mythic Puffshroom.
* As a very rare drop from [Diamond Aphids.](aphid.md)
* As a drop from certain [planters](planter.md).
  * Planting a [Plastic Planter](plastic-planter.md) in the [Coconut Field](coconut-field.md) guarantees one every month.
  * Planting a [Candy Planter](candy-planter.md) in the [Cactus Field](cactus-field.md) guarantees one every month.
  * Planting a [Blue Clay Planter](blue-clay-planter.md) in the [Spider Field](spider-field.md) guarantees one every month.
  * Planting a [Red Clay Planter](red-clay-planter.md) in any field has a rare chance of dropping one. However, planting it in any blue field has a higher chance of dropping one swirled wax, and planting it in the [Sunflower Field](sunflower-field.md) guarantees one every month also planting it in the [Pine Tree Forest](pine-tree-forest.md) guarantees one every week.
  * Planting a [Tacky Planter](tacky-planter.md) in the [Pumpkin Patch](pumpkin-patch.md) guarantees one every month.
  * Planting a [Pesticide Planter](pesticide-planter.md) in the [Strawberry Field](strawberry-field.md) guarantees one every month.
  * Planting a [Heat-Treated Planter](heat-treated-planter.md) in the [Blue Flower Field](blue-flower-field.md) guarantees one every month.
  * Planting a [Hydroponic Planter](hydroponic-planter.md) in the [Mushroom Field](mushroom-field.md) guarantees one every month.
  * Planting a [Petal Planter](petal-planter.md) in the [Strawberry Field](strawberry-field.md) guarantees one every month.
  * [The Planter Of Plenty](the-planter-of-plenty.md) has a high chance of dropping one swirled wax in any field.
  * [The Planter Of Plenty](the-planter-of-plenty.md) may drop 5 swirled waxes with one every month.

## Trivia

* This is the first wax to be added to the game. It was added in Beesmas 2020 while the rest were added in Beesmas 2021.
* This wax, [Soft Wax](soft-wax.md) and [Debug Wax](debug-wax.md) are the only waxes guaranteed to change a Beequip's stats.
* Before the 2021-12-26 update, this item had no favor when donated to the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md).
* Swirled wax used to have a limit of 5, but the limit was removed in the [2021-12-26 update](updates.md#2021-12-26).
* Before the 2021-12-26 update, swirled wax did not have a wick in its icon design.
* This is the only wax that was not included in the Waxy Bundle.
* Swirled wax's raw ingredients cost:
  * 90 [Honeysuckles](honeysuckle.md)
  * 900 [Sunflower Seeds](sunflower-seed.md)
  * 4,050 [Pineapples](pineapple.md)
  * 3,600 [Strawberries](strawberry.md)
  * 3,600 [Blueberries](blueberry.md)
  * 99 [Bitterberries](bitterberry.md)
  * 18 [Neonberries](neonberry.md)
  * 4,602 [Royal Jellies](royal-jelly.md) or 4.60B [Honey](honey.md)
