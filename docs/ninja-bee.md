**Ninja Bee** is a Blue [Legendary bee](bees-legendary.md).

Ninja Bee's favorite treat is [Sunflower Seeds](sunflower-seed.md).

Ninja Bee likes the [Blue Flower Field](blue-flower-field.md) and [Bamboo Field](bamboo-field.md). It dislikes the [Mushroom Field](mushroom-field.md).

## Stats

* Collects 10 [Pollen](pollen.md) in 2 seconds.
* Makes 80 [Honey](honey.md) in 2 seconds.
* +50% [Movespeed](stats.md#Speed), Gather Speed, and Convert Speed, +3 [Attack](stats.md#Attack), +10% [Critical Chance](critical-hits.md)
* 🌟[Gifted Hive Bonus](gifted-bee.md): +5% [Bee Movespeed](stats.md#Speed).

### Abilities

* **[[Haste]](ability-tokens.md#Haste)** Grants +10% [Player Movespeed](system-page.md#Movespeed) for 20 seconds. Stacks up to 10 times.
* **[[Blue Bomb+]](ability-tokens.md#Bomb)** Collects 10 pollen from 29 surrounding Blue [Flowers](flowers.md) (+10% pollen per Level). Combo with other bombs to increase power.

## Trivia

* In the early versions of the game, Ninja Bee had an ability called "[Haste+](buffs-debuffs.md#From_Areas)," which doubled the player's Movespeed for 20 seconds. This ability has now been stripped from the bee and is currently only obtainable through the [Star Hall's](star-hall.md) [Royal Jelly Dispenser](royal-jelly-dispenser.md) and [codes](codes.md).
  * Ninja Bee, [Hasty Bee](hasty-bee.md), [Shocked Bee](shocked-bee.md), and [Photon Bee](photon-bee.md) are the only bees that grant haste without any certain [Beequips](beequip.md).
* Ninja Bee is the only colored bee to grant haste, with the exception of those with the [Toy Drum](toy-drum.md) Beequip.
* This bee, [Shy Bee](shy-bee.md), [Fuzzy Bee](fuzzy-bee.md), and [Windy Bee](windy-bee.md) are currently the only bees that have a solitary colored skin.
* Ninja Bee is the fastest bee in the game, tied with Photon Bee.
  * In Ninja Bee's description, it is supposedly the "swiftest bee". However, Photon Bee is just as fast.
* Before the [2018-11-25 Update](updates.md#2018-11-25), Ninja Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus was +20% Player Movespeed instead of +5% Bee Movespeed.
* Ninja Bee, [Cobalt Bee](cobalt-bee.md), and [Vicious Bee](vicious-bee.md) are the only blue bees that do not like [Blueberries](blueberry.md).
  * It is the only non-[Event](bees-event.md) blue bee to not like blueberries.
  * This and [Precise Bee](precise-bee.md) are also the only non-Colorless bee to like [Sunflower Seeds](sunflower-seed.md).
* This bee, Photon Bee, Cobalt Bee, [Crimson Bee](crimson-bee.md), [Tadpole Bee](tadpole-bee.md), and Windy Bee are the only bees that leave a trail.
* Ninja Bee is the blue counterpart of Shy Bee.
* This bee is the placeholder image for any bee in a hive slot that hasn't loaded yet.
* Ninja Bee's favorite treat was originally [Pineapples](pineapple.md).
* Ninja Bee is stated by [Black Bear](black-bear.md) to be his choice of leader for the [Blue HQ](blue-hq.md).
