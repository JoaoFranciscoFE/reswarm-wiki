# Nectar

**Nectar** is a set of [buffs](buffs-debuffs.md) that were added in the [2021-12-26 update](updates.md#2021-12-26). It is granted from [planters](planter.md), either through harvesting them, having [bees](bees.md) sip from planters, using [Dapper Bear](dapper-bear.md)'s [Samovar](samovar.md) during Beesmas, or using a nectar vial item. Some [quests](quests.md) require a certain amount and type of nectar to be collected. All sources of getting Nectar can contribute to those quests (except collecting Nectar from the Nectar Pot).

There are five types of Nectar: Invigorating, Satisfying, Motivating, Comforting and Refreshing.

Nectar buffs can last for up to 24 hours and the boosts granted scale with the amount of time remaining. 12 hours of Nectar can be converted to a [Vial](nectar-vial.md) using the [Nectar Condenser](nectar-condenser.md). If the player has more than 12 hours of nectar, the remaining nectar will not be condensed. Nectar can also be stored inside the [Nectar Pot](nectar-pot.md) located in the 30 Bee Zone for 5 [Tickets](ticket.md). All of the Nectar types can be stored, but in order to deposit Nectar, any stored Nectar must be withdrawn first. If the Nectar is not stored, it will continue to deplete even if the player is not in the game.

## Scaling

The following formula can be used to calculate how much of a buff a player receives from a given nectar with a given amount of time.

* Let *t* be the number of seconds left on the buff.
* Let *min* and *max* be the minimum and maximum possible value of the given buff.
* Then the amount of the buff the player receives is equal to \(min+(max-min)\times {({\frac {t}{86400}})}^{0.7}\), rounded to the nearest 0.001.

For example, let's calculate the amount of Blue Pollen a player gets from 18 hours of Comforting Nectar.

* 18 hours is 64800 seconds, so \(t=64800\).
* Comforting Nectar gives between x1.1 and x1.5 Blue Pollen, so \(min=1.1\) and \(max=1.5\).
* \(min+(max-min)\times {({\frac {t}{86400}})}^{0.7}=1.1+(1.5-1.1)\times {({\frac {64800}{86400}})}^{0.7}=1.42704150727\), which is rounded to 1.427.
* This means the player will get x1.427 Blue Pollen.

## Trivia

* Every nectar icon (except Refreshing) has a letter on it that represents the first initial of the type of nectar.
  * Refreshing has no letter, although it is presumed that the icon has the gaps of the letter 'R'.
  * Comforting has the letter 'C' resembling a crescent moon shape.
  * Satisfying has the letter 'S' resembling the lightning bolt in its decal.
  * Motivating has the letter 'M' in the lightbulb resembling the filament.
  * Invigorating has the lower part of 'I' or a small capital 'I' at the bottom of the fire.
* [Shy Bee](shy-bee.md) has the passive [Nectar Lover](passive-abilities.md#Nectar_Lover), where it is twice as likely to sip nectar and gather twice as much nectar. It also contributes twice as much to the planter's growth.
* Nectar cannot be collected from the [Ant Field](ant-field.md) although Onett announced that Ant Field contains Invigorating Nectar.
  * Likewise, nectar cannot be collected from the [Hub Field](hub-field.md), though a glitch allowed planters to be placed there.
    * As such, harvesting a planter in this field grants Comforting Nectar, though abusing this glitch may result in having your account flagged.
