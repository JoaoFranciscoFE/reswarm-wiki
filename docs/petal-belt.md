## Stats

* +250,000 [Capacity](capacity.md).
* +50% Capacity.
* +100 [Convert Amount](system-page.md#Convert_Amount).
* +101% [Loot Luck](loot-luck.md).
* +30% [Buzz Bomb Pollen](system-page.md#Buzz_Bomb_Pollen).
* +75% [Honey From Tokens](system-page.md#Honey_From_Tokens).
* +1 [Colorless Bee Attack](system-page.md#Colorless_Attack).
* [+Passive: Petal Storm](passive-abilities.md#Petal_Storm).
  * Every 30th [boost](ability-tokens.md#Boost) token collected fires 30 Petal Shurikens around you. Every [bee](bees.md) hit will instantly convert 10,000 (+6% of their [Convert Amount](system-page.md#Convert_Amount), +4% per [Field Boost](field-boost.md) stack) [pollen](pollen.md) into honey. It has a 30-second cooldown before another usage.

## Trivia

* If the ingredients to craft this item were to be crafted, a total of 15,000,000,000 [Honey](honey.md), 1 [Spirit Petal](spirit-petal.md), 61,875 [Strawberries](strawberry.md), 61,875 [Blueberries](blueberry.md), 77,500 [Pineapples](pineapple.md), 125 [Magic Beans](magic-bean.md) and 6,625 [Royal Jellies](royal-jelly.md) are needed.
* This is the 9th most expensive [item](items.md) in terms of honey (excluding [hive slots](hive-slot.md)).
* This item emits a yellow particle effect in the center of the belt, similarly to the [Petal Wand](petal-wand.md).
* This is one of the three belt accessories that were not obtainable from [Sun Bear](sun-bear.md).
* This is one of many accessories to be worn by an NPC, following the [Mondo Belt Bag](mondo-belt-bag.md) and the [Beekeeper's Boots](beekeeper-s-boots.md) (both worn by [Sun Bear](sun-bear.md) and [Onett](onett.md)), and the [Porcelain Port-O-Hive](porcelain-port-o-hive.md), [Riley](riley-guard.md) and [Bucko guards](bucko-guard.md) and also the [Beekeeper's mask](beekeeper-s-mask.md) worn by Onett.
* The Petal Belt worn by [Spirit Bear](spirit-bear.md) is much bigger than the one worn by a player.
* The Petal Shurikens can pop [bubbles](passive-abilities.md#Gathering_Bubbles), [fuzz bombs](ability-tokens.md#Fuzz_Bombs), and also spawn a petal when they hit [blooms](bloom.md).
* This is the only belt on display which doesn't have a hitbox.
* This is one of two belts to have a [passive](passive-abilities.md), the other being the [Coconut Belt](coconut-belt.md).
* Although the Petal Belt's passive in-game states that a Petal Shuriken converts 10,000 pollen +7% of the bee's convert amount, Onett has stated in the change log during the [2024-05-23 update](updates.md#2024-05-23) that it was changed to its current state.
