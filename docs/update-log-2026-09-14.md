# Puffshroom changes

**Released:** September 14, 2026

## Changes

* [Sticker](sticker-planter.md) and [Ticket](ticket-planter.md) Planters now spawn 3 Puffshrooms.
* [Puffshrooms](puffshroom.md) get a level 30 lower than the damage dealt, raising the chance of rare or better.
* Puffshrooms are more likely to appear in the [Coconut Field](coconut-field.md) and [Pepper Patch](pepper-patch.md).
