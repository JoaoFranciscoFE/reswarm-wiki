# First Edition Bee

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 189px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The decal for the First Edition Flag.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

A **first edition bee** is a designation given to certain [Event bees](bees-event.md) bought or earned during their first release. This designation is represented by a white and yellow "1st" flag appearing on top of the bee, and the flag emits a sparkling effect. The flag is purely cosmetic and does not change or affect gameplay at all.

This designation is currently only available for [Bear Bee](bear-bee.md), [Tabby Bee](tabby-bee.md), [Gummy Bee](gummy-bee.md), [Puppy Bee](puppy-bee.md), [Festive Bee](festive-bee.md), [Digital Bee](digital-bee.md), and [Windy Bee](windy-bee.md).

**If the player removes a first edition bee, the bee will retain its first edition status when added back to the [hive](hive.md).**

## First Edition Bees

* Bear Bee: Bought during the first month after the game's launch (all First Edition Bear Bees got an additional bonus of being automatically [gifted](gifted-bee.md)).
* Tabby Bee and Puppy Bee: Purchased during the their respective release sales (half price).
* Gummy Bee: Obtained after completing all of [Gummy Bear's](gummy-bear.md) [quests](quests.md) during the Gummy Invasion Event.
* Festive Bee: Obtained after completing all of [Bee Bear's](bee-bear.md) quests during the Beesmas 2018 Event or obtaining it from the Festive Bee Pack for 1,600 Robux in the [Robux Shop](robux-shop.md).
* Windy Bee: Obtained as a special prize from the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md) or purchased in the Robux Shop for 800 Robux before October 27, 2019.
* Digital Bee: Obtained from [Robo Bear's Shop](robo-bear-s-shop.md) behind [Robo Bear](robo-bear.md) or by purchasing the Digital Bee Pack for 2,500 Robux in the Robux Shop during Beesmas 2022.

## Trivia

* [Onett](onett-developer.md) has stated that the reason a First Edition [Vicious Bee](vicious-bee.md) does not exist is because there is no room for a 1st Flag on its back with the spikes.
* The earliest 1st Edition Bee was [Bear Bee](bear-bee.md), coming in at the game's release.
* Even though First Edition Bear Bees are automatically gifted, the gifted status can still be removed if a player adds it to the hive again.
