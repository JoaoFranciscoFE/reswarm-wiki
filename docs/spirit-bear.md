**Spirit Bear** is a [quest giver](quest-givers.md) and is one of the eight permanent quest bears that can be accessed in the game, the others being [Black Bear](black-bear.md), [Brown Bear](brown-bear.md), [Mother Bear](mother-bear.md), [Panda Bear](panda-bear.md), [Science Bear](science-bear.md), [Dapper Bear](dapper-bear.md), and [Polar Bear](polar-bear.md). She holds a [Petal Wand](petal-wand.md) and wears a [Petal Belt](petal-belt.md). The player must have 35 [bees](bees.md) or more to get access Spirit Bear. She is located between the [Coconut Field](coconut-field.md) and the [Pepper Patch](pepper-patch.md), and in front of the [Extreme Memory Match](memory-match.md#Extreme_Memory_Match). She has a total of 30 non-repeatable quests she can give to the player.

The main rewards from her quests are [Spirit Petals](spirit-petal.md), which are granted every ten quests. In total, the player can obtain three, one to donate to the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md) (to get the [Windy Bee](windy-bee.md) or a [Star Treat](star-treat.md) if the player already obtained a [Windy Bee](windy-bee.md) through buying it in an expired robux pack), another for the [Petal Wand](petal-wand.md), and another for the [Petal Belt](petal-belt.md).

## Trivia

* She is one of the two female bears in the game, the other being [Mother Bear](mother-bear.md).
* You can get to her without 35 bees by using the [Red Cannon](red-cannon.md), then using the [Parachute](parachute.md) or [Glider](glider.md) to glide over, then make sure you have high ping so you are able to talk to her before you get kicked out of the 35 bee zone/disconnected.
* Her idle animation comes from the [Elder Animation Package](https://www.roblox.com/bundles/48/Elder-Animation-Package).
* She quite often refers to her sister, another bear who was very close to her.
* She, [Stick Bug](stick-bug.md), the [Cub Buddies](cub-buddy.md), Bee Bear and [Shadow Bear](shadow-bear.md) are the only NPCs that dance.
* She is the second NPC to hold a tool, the first being [Onett](onett.md), which makes her the only bear holding a tool.
* She is the third NPC to wear a belt, the other two being [Sun Bear](sun-bear.md) and [Onett](onett.md).
  * Spirit Bear, Sun Bear, and Dapper Bear are the only Bears to wear accessories, Spirit Bear and Sun Bear being the only bears to wear in-game accessories.
* Spirit Bear is based off of [Kermode Bears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kermode_bear), which are sometimes referred to as spirit bears.
  * Kermode bears are a rare subspecies of the black bear.
* She mentions the [Re://:Swarm Wiki](index.md) in the quest "Spirit's Starter".
* Both she and [Science Bear](science-bear.md) have [items](items.md) that are only obtainable from their quests, which are [Spirit Petals](spirit-petal.md) and [Translators](translator.md) respectively.
* The [Code](codes.md) "BeesBuzz123" is her account's password on her own website, proven from her dialogue in "QR Quest".
* She, [Panda Bear](panda-bear.md), [Black Bear](black-bear.md), [Gummy Bear](gummy-bear.md) and [Science Bear](science-bear.md) are the only bears that mention/have a code.
* It is possible to glitch into Spirit Bear and get a quest from her. The player has to jump on the [Windy Bee](windy-bee.md) statue on top of the [35 Bee Gate](windy-bee-gate.md) and glide or jump to her, or, if the player has enough ping, climb up the big rock left of the gate and nearest to the [Coconut Field](coconut-field.md) and talk to her.
* She has the most words per quest in her dialogue, at around 2,000 per quest, even surpassing Science Bear with the most words per quest dialogue.
  * She has around 21 thousand words in her whole dialogue.
* Spirit Bear, [Robo Bear](robo-bear.md), and [Dapper Bear](dapper-bear.md) are the only permanent quest bears to not have a [Bear Morph](ability-tokens.md#Bear_Morph).
* All of Spirit Bear's quests require collecting an increasing amount of white pollen. (With the exception of her Beesmas quests.)
* Spirit Bear is the second bear to require the completion of another bear's quest, the first being [Bee Bear](bee-bear.md) and [Sun Bear](sun-bear.md) being third.
* Spirit Bear can summon a multitude of server-wide events.
  * She is the only quest giver that can summon a [Wild Windy Bee](wild-windy-bee.md), and a Snowstorm.
  * She, along with [Honey Bee](honey-bee-npc.md), are the only quest givers that can summon a [Honeystorm](honeystorm.md).
  * She, along with [Bee Bear](bee-bear.md), are the only quest givers that can summon Nighttime alongside .
  * According to Bee Bear, [Bubble Bee Man](bubble-bee-man.md) can also summon honeystorms.
* She is self-aware that she is in a game, as she mentions "game balance'' in ''Sway Away."
  * This could be because of multiple reasons, such as her saying "Ol' Stumpy", her mentioning the wiki you are viewing this on, or her age.
* She is the only permanent quest giver that requires the player to play [Memory Matches](memory-match.md).
* She is the only permanent quest giver that requires the player to use the [Wealth Clock](wealth-clock.md) for her quests.
* Spirit Bear refers to the Stump Snail as ''Ol' Stumpy''. This may be a reference to the youtuber [ThnxCya](https://www.youtube.com/user/ThnxCya) as he references it as ''Ol' Stumpy'' too.
* Sources confirm that she's a 1983's Memory Match tournament champion, and that explains why she gives quests that require players to play [Memory Match](memory-match.md).
* Some of her quests give items upon receiving the quest. These items include: [Jelly Beans](jelly-beans.md), [Star Jelly](royal-jelly.md#Star_Jelly), [Glitter](glitter.md), [Magic Beans](magic-bean.md), [Coconuts](coconut.md), [Pineapples](pineapple.md), [Sunflower Seeds](sunflower-seed.md) and [Gumdrops](gumdrops.md).
* Her favorite holiday is Galentine's Day.
* Her sleep talking during the quest "Do You Bee-lieve In Magic?" is probably a reference to the image of [Basic Bee](basic-bee.md) winking that can be found at the end of the White Tunnel, hinting to the code "Wink".
* In the 2021 Beesmas Quest, when she tells the player where to get [Satisfying Nectar](nectar.md), she mentions the wrong fields; The [Dandelion Field](dandelion-field.md), the [Bamboo Field](bamboo-field.md), and the [Pine Tree Forest](pine-tree-forest.md) is where the player can get [Comforting Nectar](nectar.md), not [Satisfying Nectar](nectar.md).
* In her first quest, Spirit Bear reveals that she planted the Sunflowers in the [Sunflower Field](sunflower-field.md).
  * The Sunflowers were requested by her sister, who loves to snack on [Sunflower Seeds](treats.md#Sunflower_Seed).
* The dialogue of her quest "Witness Grandeur" reveals she has known [Brown Bear](brown-bear.md) since he was a cub.
* In the quest “Beauty Duty“, she mentions that her fur was originally a shade of white, specifically one close to a cream color.
* Spirit Bear mentions in her quest “The Ways Of The Winds / The Wind And Its Ways” that her mother was always in a state of stress, and that she would pull on her fur as a nervous tick.
* Spirit Bear mentions in her quest “Rhubarb That Could Have Been” that she initially wanted to plant Rhubarb to bake pies. She let [Stick Bug](stick-bug.md) decide and the rhubarb field was replaced by the [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md).
* Through the donation quests in her storyline, your favor in the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md) will increase by 486,753 (might need a recalculation).
