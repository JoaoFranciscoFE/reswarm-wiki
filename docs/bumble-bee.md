**Bumble Bee** is a Blue [Rare bee](bees-rare.md).

Bumble Bee's favorite treat is [Blueberries](blueberry.md).

Bumble Bee likes the [Blue Flower Field](blue-flower-field.md) and the [Stump Field](stump-field.md). It dislikes the [Mushroom Field](mushroom-field.md).

## Stats

* Collects 18 [pollen](pollen.md) in 4 seconds
* Makes 80 [honey](honey.md) in 4 seconds.
* +100% [energy](energy.md), +8 [gather amount](stats.md#Gather_Amount), -25% [movespeed](stats.md#Speed).
* 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](gifted-bee.md): x1.1 [Capacity](capacity.md).

### Abilities

* **[Blue Bomb](ability-tokens.md#Bomb)** Collects 10 pollen from 13 surrounding blue [Flowers](flowers.md) (+10% pollen per lvl). Combine with other bombs to increase power.

## Trivia

* Bumble Bee has the same skin color as the [Bucko Bee](bucko-bee.md).
* Bumble Bee is the blue counterpart of the [Rascal Bee](rascal-bee.md).
* Defeated [Rhino Beetles](rhino-beetle.md) will sometimes drop [Bumble Bee Eggs](egg.md#Bumble_Bee_Egg).
* A [special jelly](royal-jelly.md#Royal_Jelly_Variants) that changes a bee into a Bumble Bee could have been obtained from [Bubble Bee Man](bubble-bee-man.md) and the [code](codes.md), 'BeeDay2019' and 'Cubly' (expired).
* Bumble Bee, [Carpenter Bee](carpenter-bee.md), and [Honey Bee](honey-bee.md) are named after their real-life counterparts.
* Bumble Bee, Bucko Bee, and [Buoyant Bee](buoyant-bee.md) are the only blue bees to like at least three fields.
* Bumble Bee is the slowest rare bee.
* Bumble bees gifted stat used to be +20% blue bomb pollen and has since been changed.
