A **Toy Drum** is a [level](bond.md) 7 Beesmas [beequip](beequip.md). It has an equip limit of two. It has no color requirement. It can only be equipped to [Brave](brave-bee.md), [Demo](demo-bee.md), [Bucko](bucko-bee.md), or [Riley](riley-bee.md) [Bees](bees.md).
