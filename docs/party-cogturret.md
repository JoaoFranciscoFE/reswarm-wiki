*Not to be confused with [Cogturrets](cogturret.md), the normal version of these.*

The **Party Cogturret** is one of the mobs exclusive to the Robo Party. It behaves similarly to a normal [Cogturret](cogturret.md) but spawns on a random field instead of following the player on the field they are standing on. They first appear on Cake Rank 5 and continue to spawn in the following ranks.

## Strategies

* Party Cogturrets aim for where the player is, not where the player will be. Because of this, moving to the side after they fire a cog will often result in Party Cogturrets missing their attack.
* Party Cogturrets will always shoot straight at where they are facing so you can avoid their attacks simply by not being in front of them.
* Stand near the Party Cogturrets, this will allow your bees to lock on to the Party Cogturrets and quickly deal damage.
* [Vicious Bee](vicious-bee.md) and [Windy Bee](windy-bee.md) provide excellent crowd control, especially since Party Cogturrets often come in pairs or packs. [Digital Bee](digital-bee.md) can also stun many Party Cogturrets at once and allow them to take extra damage via [Mind Hack](ability-tokens.md#Mind_Hack).

## Trivia

* The Party Cogturret is the only Party Robot not wearing a Party Hat.
* This, [Party Mechsquitos](party-mechsquito.md), and [Party Mega Mechsquitos](party-mega-mechsquito.md) have the same health per level as their normal counterparts.
