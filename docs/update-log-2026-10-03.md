# Bee levels, sticker inventory and fixes

**Released:** October 3, 2026

A display change for bee levels and a few fixes.

## What's new

* Added a way to stop [stickers](sticker.md) from going into your inventory (look in the Index).

## Changes

* Bee levels on bees are now drawn with a surface GUI instead of one decal per level.

## Fixes

* Fixed a softlock between the [Tutorial](tutorial.md) and other NPCs.
* Fixed inventory syncing with hotbar auto-use.
