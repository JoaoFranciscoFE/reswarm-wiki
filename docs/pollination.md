# Pollination

![Pollination](img/Pollination.png){ align=right width=150 }





**Pollination** is a [mechanic](mechanics.md) that causes [flowers](flowers.md) to grow to higher tiers, increasing their total [pollen](pollen.md) and multiplier. Flowers that are lower tier than other flowers in the [field](fields.md) have a higher chance of being pollinated. When flowers are pollinated, they will burst flower particles.

Flowers only stay pollinated for 1 minute, but this can be stacked up to 5 times for 5 minutes. Every minute that an already pollinated flower is not pollinated, it will go down a tier, until it has reverted to its original state.

Pollination is the only method to achieve large and star flowers, as the highest natural tier is triple.

## Ways To Pollinate Flowers

* [Fuzzy Bee's](fuzzy-bee.md) [Fuzz Bombs](ability-tokens.md#Fuzz_Bombs) can pollinate flowers upon popping it.
* Fuzzy Bee's [Fuzzy Coat](passive-abilities.md#Fuzzy_Coat) passive has a chance to pollinate up to 5 surrounding flowers while collecting pollen. This increases to 9 when gifted.
* Fuzzy Bee's [Pollen Haze](ability-tokens.md#Pollen_Haze) pollinates up to 30 flowers every second. [Bomb](ability-tokens.md#Bomb) tokens produced in a field with pollen haze are automatically collected and pollinate flowers.
* [Diamond Mask's](diamond-mask.md) [Diamond Drain](passive-abilities.md#Diamond_Drain) pollinates 10 blue flowers when activated. The number of flowers pollinated increases by 3 for every blue bee the player has.
* If [Guiding Star](star-amulet.md#Supreme_Star_Amulet) is active, the star will pollinate 15 (+1 per [gifted bee](gifted-bee.md) type) flowers every 15 seconds.
* A bee with the [Poinsettia](poinsettia.md) [Beequip](beequip.md) equipped has an 11% to 20% chance to pollinate flowers while gathering.
* A bee with the [Lei](lei.md) Beequip equipped has a 20% to 50% chance to pollinate flowers while gathering.
* A bee with the [Rose Headband](rose-headband.md) has a 10% to 20% chance to pollinate flowers while gathering.

## Trivia

* This is the only way to directly change flowers in a field.
* Fuzzy Bee is the only bee in the game to pollinate flowers without a Beequip.
