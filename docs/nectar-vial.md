# Nectar Vial

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">Nectar Vials inside a player's inventory.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

**Nectar Vials** are a type of inventory [item](items.md) that can be used to increase a player's current [Nectar](nectar.md) by 4 hours when used. Using any Nectar Vial grants 4 hours of their respective nectar type. You can hold a maximum of 10 Nectar Vials of each type at a time. However, this limit can be bypassed by obtaining them through [quests](quests.md) or [Robux Packs](robux-shop.md).

## Ways to Obtain

* Using the [Nectar Condenser](nectar-condenser.md) to convert 12 hours of nectar into a Nectar Vial which stores 4 hours of nectar.
* As a reward for adding certain [stickers](sticker.md) to the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md).
  * Adding the Satisfying, Refreshing, Motivating, Invigorating, and Comforting Nectar Icon rewards the player with their respective nectar vial.
  * Adding the [Offline Voucher](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) rewards 1 [Nectar Shower Vial](nectar-shower-vial.md).
* As a reward from certain [quests](quests.md).
  * Completing several [Dapper Bear](dapper-bear.md) quests reward the player with at least one of every nectar vial.
    * [Satisfying Vials](satisfying-vial.md):
      * Means of Mutation (5) rewards 1 [Satisfying Vial](satisfying-vial.md) and other items.
      * The Trick To Tickets (11) rewards 1 [Satisfying Vial](satisfying-vial.md) and other items.
      * More Than Myth (15) rewards 1 [Satisfying Vial](satisfying-vial.md) and other items.
    * [Comforting Vials](comforting-vial.md):
      * Creative Comforts (6) rewards 1 [Comforting Vial](comforting-vial.md) and other items.
      * The Long Haul (10) rewards 1 [Comforting Vial](comforting-vial.md) and other items.
      * Ongoing Maintenance (13) rewards 1 [Comforting Vial](comforting-vial.md) and other items.
      * Barter For The Blue Drive rewards 1 [Comforting Vial](comforting-vial.md) and other items.
    * [Refreshing Vials](refreshing-vial.md):
      * Keeping It Chromatic (7) rewards 1 [Refreshing Vial](refreshing-vial.md) and other items.
      * Solitarily Steadfast (14) rewards 1 [Refreshing Vial](refreshing-vial.md) and other items.
      * Dapper Bear's Samovar rewards 1 [Refreshing Vial](refreshing-vial.md) and other items.
    * [Motivating Vials](motivating-vial.md):
      * Wandering Ambition (8) rewards 1 [Motivating Vial](motivating-vial.md) and other items.
    * [Invigorating Vials](invigorating-vial.md):
      * Unintended Intimidation (9) rewards 1 [Invigorating Vial](invigorating-vial.md) and other items.
      * Invigorating View (12) rewards 1 [Invigorating Vial](invigorating-vial.md) and other items.
    * [Nectar Shower Vials](nectar-shower-vial.md):
      * Sticker Style (16) rewards 1 [Nectar Shower Vial](nectar-shower-vial.md) and other items.

## Variants

There is a total of six variants of Nectar Vials. Five of which; immediately grant 4 hours of a type of Nectar buff to the player. The other grants 4 hours of every Nectar buff to all players in the server. The type of Nectar that the player is granted depends on the type of Nectar Vial that they choose to use, with the exception of the [Nectar Shower Vial](nectar-shower-vial.md).

## Trivia

* While most of the Nectar Vials have a letter on them which represents the first letter of their name (C for Comforting, I for Invigorating, M for Motivating, S for Satisfying), the [Refreshing Vial](refreshing-vial.md) doesn't have an R on it to represent the first letter of its name. The [Nectar Shower Vial](nectar-shower-vial.md) has no icon at all, as it just seems to be a mix of all the previous Nectars.
* There is only a limited amount of Nectar Vials that can be obtained by the player without the use of the [Nectar Condenser](nectar-condenser.md).
  * For [Comforting Vials](comforting-vial.md), the total amount that can be obtained is 26.
  * For [Invigorating Vials](invigorating-vial.md), the total amount that can be obtained is 22.
  * For [Motivating Vials](motivating-vial.md), the total amount that can be obtained is 13.
  * For [Refreshing Vials](refreshing-vial.md), the total amount that can be obtained is 14.
  * For [Satisfying Vials](satisfying-vial.md), the total amount that can be obtained is 19.
  * For [Nectar Shower Vials](nectar-shower-vial.md), the total amount that can be obtained is 34.
* The [Nectar Shower Vial](nectar-shower-vial.md) is the only vial that is not obtainable from the [Nectar Condenser](nectar-condenser.md).
* Using Nectar Vials does count toward collecting nectar in [quests](quests.md).
  * However, they do not contribute to the [Top Nectar Collector Leaderboards](daily-top-nectar-collectors.md).
