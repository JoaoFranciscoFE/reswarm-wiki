**Gummy Bear** is an NPC that resides in [his lair](gummy-bear-s-lair.md), killing players who go near him without any gear sold in the shop. Either the [Gummy Mask](gummy-mask.md), [Gummy Boots](gummy-boots.md), or [Gummyballer](gummyballer.md) needs to have been purchased to talk to him, though they aren't required to be worn. The player must have the [Goo Hotshot Badge](badges.md#Goo_Badge) or above to enter [Gummy Bear's Lair](gummy-bear-s-lair.md), and have 20 [bees](bees.md) to access the [20 Bee Zone](ant-gate.md) as the [Gummy Bee Egg Claim](gummy-bee-egg-claim.md) is at the entrance of it.

The player can either talk to him, which gives a short paragraph of dialogue about the Gummy Army, claim his Beesmas quest or give him a present (during Beesmas), or teleport the player back to their [Hive](hive.md). If a player who does not have any of the Gummy gear would like to see Gummy Bear without losing their pollen, they should empty out their bag before going into the lair. If the player is already inside the lair, the player can use a [Micro-Converter](micro-converter.md) to empty their bag, or a [Whirligig](whirligig.md) to teleport them back to their hive. The whirligig **must** be used **before** the dialogue begins, otherwise the player will still die as it ends, regardless of the fact they are no longer in the lair.

His first appearance on the mountain was during the Gummy Invasion on May 26, 2018. He gave 15 [quests](quests.md) that rewarded the player a [Gummy Bee Egg](egg.md#Gummy_Bee_Egg) at the end of the questline. During this event, he used the Gummy Cannon to shoot [Gumdrops](gumdrops.md) at [fields](fields.md) and cover flowers with [goo](goo.md). He targeted every field except the [Mountain Top Field](mountain-top-field.md) (and the [Stump Field](stump-field.md), [Coconut Field](coconut-field.md), and [Pepper Patch](pepper-patch.md), as they weren't in the game at that time). Gummy Bear left on June 24, 2018, and returned as an NPC in the Nov. 25, 2018 update in his current location, accessible through the Gummy Bee model that used to be on the [Ticket Tent](ticket-tent.md).

Starting from Beesmas 2020 and continuing throughout the following Beesmas events, he gave out a Beesmas quest called "Gummy Bear's Goo Years Beacon".

## Lair Dialogue

If the player walks close enough to Gummy Bear in his lair, and that player does not own the [Gummy Mask](gummy-mask.md), [Gummy Boots](gummy-boots.md) or [Gummyballer](gummyballer.md), the following dialogue will appear in chat, and in the player's notifications:

"Gummy Bear: "What's sweeter than honey?"  

"Glorious gumdrops... they're just the start!"  

"A sweet and sour, ooey-gooey universe..."  

"Can't you see it too?" (slight pause)  

"What's that Gummy Bee?"  

"They're not seeing clearly?"  

"YOU'RE ALWAYS RIGHT!"  

"GOO-dbye, beekeeper... HAH!"

After the last line, Gummy Bear will use the point emote and kill the player, taking them back to spawn.

Even if the player uses a [Whirligig](whirligig.md) during the dialogue to exit the lair, the dialogue will continue regardless, and they will still be killed when Gummy Bear's dialogue ends.

If the player owns the [Gummy Mask](gummy-mask.md), [Gummy Boots](gummy-boots.md) or [Gummyballer](gummyballer.md), they can talk to Gummy Bear, but he does not give a quest.

## Trivia

* Gummy Bear was originally the traveling bear for the Gummy Invasion event, spanning from May 26 to June 24, 2018 (30 days, or 4 weeks and 2 days). He gave 15 [quests](quests.md) with a final reward of a [Gummy Bee Egg](egg.md#Gummy_Bee_Egg). He came back in the 2018-11-25 Update.
  * Onett added 1 more week to the Gummy Bear's timer when he first arrived because many players requested an extension.
* Gummy Bear uses the [Levitation Animation Pack](https://www.roblox.com/catalog/619547874/Levitation-Animation-Pack).
  * Gummy Bear is one of two bears (the other being [Shadow Bear](shadow-bear.md)) to float, rather than stand on the ground.
* Like all of the permanent bears (except [Spirit Bear](spirit-bear.md) and [Dapper Bear](dapper-bear.md)), he also has his own Bear Morph. Unlike them, the player doesn't need a [Bear Bee](bear-bee.md) to get it, but rather it can be activated by Gummy Mask's [Gummy Morph Passive Ability](passive-abilities.md#Gummy_Morph).
  * Gummy Bear is the only traveling bear to have a morph of his own.
* Gummy Bear brought the following during his visit to the mountain:
  * A [Gummy Bee](gummy-bee.md), which could have been seen flying around him.
    * Gummy Bear was the first bear to introduce his own bee, with [Bee Bear](bee-bear.md) being the second, introducing [Festive Bee](festive-bee.md), and [Robo Bear](robo-bear.md) being the third, introducing [Digital Bee](digital-bee.md).
  * A Gummy Cannon, which he used to fire [Gumdrops](gumdrops.md) at [fields](fields.md) and the only cannon the player is unable to use for transportation.
  * The [Gumdrop Shop](gumdrop-shop.md) where the player could buy gumdrops with [Tickets](ticket.md) (which was moved to its current position near [Panda Bear](panda-bear.md)).
* In total, the players could have received up to 31 tickets from his quests.
* Gummy Bear is translucent, like the candy he is named after. (His colors of pink and teal, on the other hand, don't resemble any of the colors of [classic gummy bear candy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gummy_Bear).)
  * Gummy Bear is the first and currently only bear to have colors unnatural to their real-life counterpart.
* Gummy Bear gave a [code](codes.md) in the dialogue of one of his last quests "Peace Through Goo-nity", which was the "Tapioca" code.
* Spirit Bear has stated that Gummy Bear was made out of gumdrops. In addition, according to his dialogue, he and his Gummy Bee are made of the same [goo](goo.md).
* Gummy Bear, [Tunnel Bear](tunnel-bear.md) and [Onett](onett.md) were "Naughty" during the Beesmas Event, as stated by Bee Bear.
* Gummy Bear was featured in the "Dance Party" advert.
* Gummy Bear appears in the [Goo Badge](badges.md#Goo_Badge) for its icon.
* He was voted as the strongest bear in Onett’s StrawPoll voting.[1]
* He is also the first and only quest-giver that can kill the player.
  * He is the second bear out of three bears that can kill the player, the first being [Tunnel Bear](tunnel-bear.md) and the third being [Snowbear](snowbear.md).
* Gummy Bear, [Sun Bear](sun-bear.md) and Bee Bear have all had a second visit to the mountain, but Gummy Bear is the only one to stay permanently (for now).
* Gummy Bear was the first bear to require the usage of transportation devices (cannons and the [Slingshot](slingshot.md)) to complete his quests.
* Gummy Bear is one of four NPCs to have moved locations, with the other three being [Honey Bee](honey-bee-npc.md), [Bubble Bee Man](bubble-bee-man.md), and Sun Bear.
