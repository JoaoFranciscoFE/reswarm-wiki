# Bags

**Bags** are accessories that can be bought in the various [shops](shops.md) around the map. The main purpose of bags is to store [pollen](pollen.md) gathered by the player until it is full, to which the player will need to go to their [hive](hive.md) and convert it into [honey](honey.md), use a Coconut, use an [Instant Converter](instant-converter.md), use a [Micro-Converter](micro-converter.md), use a [Honeysuckle](honeysuckle.md) (only converts some) or admire [Black Bear's](black-bear.md) [Honey Wreath](honey-wreath.md) (only during Beesmas). When the player's bag is full, the following message is displayed:
Pollen container full. Go to your hive to make honey. They may use an [Instant Converter](instant-converter.md) to instantly get all their honey (costs 1 ticket) or use a micro-converter. Certain bags are intended to give the player additional boosts other than capacity, such as [Instant Conversion](system-page.md#Instant_Conversion), [Convert Rate](system-page.md#Convert_Rate), [Bee Attack](bee-attack.md) and even [Passives](passive-abilities.md) (currently only from the [Coconut Canister](coconut-canister.md)). There are other accessories (such as belt bags) that increase capacity. The Pouch bag is given automatically to new players. There are currently 12 bags in the game.
