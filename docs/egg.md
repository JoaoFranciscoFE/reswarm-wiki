# Egg

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">Eggs available inside the <a href="robux-shop.html">Robux Shop</a>.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

**Eggs** are a type of inventory item where the player drags into their [hive](hive.md) to hatch a [bee](bees.md), can be donated to the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md) or are used to roll the [Sticker Printer](sticker-printer.md). They are also the items that help players start playing in the [game](re-swarm.md). Eggs come in many different types, some offering chances at different rarities of bees, and some hatching a specific bee. Currently, a total of 6 (or 11 if counting the [gifted](gifted-bee.md) variants) different types of eggs exist in the game, not including Plastic Eggs, specific bee eggs, the Flight of the Bumble Egg, or the Swarming Egg of the Hive.

Ever since the [2019-09-28 update](updates.md#2019-09-28), players do not need a vacant [hive slot](hive-slot.md) to hatch an egg. Now, players can hatch an egg on top of an existing bee to replace it. The level of the bee remains the same, acting like a [Royal Jelly](royal-jelly.md).

Please also note that due to regulations in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands, Belgium, and Australia, any eggs in the [Robux Shop](robux-shop.md) won't be available to purchase for British, Dutch, Belgian, and Australian consumers. This restriction applies to most of the eggs in the shop.

## General Egg Types

### Basic Egg

### Basic Egg

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:Error mw:File"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Basic Egg is the most common type of egg found in the game. It will almost always hatch into a [Basic Bee](basic-bee.md) (87% chance), but in rare cases, it can hatch into a [Rare Bee](bees-rare.md) (10% chance), an [Epic Bee](bees-epic.md) (2.5% chance), a [Legendary Bee](bees-legendary.md) (0.5% chance), or into a [Gifted Bee](gifted-bee.md) (0.4% chance), with a 0.348% (1 out of 287.35632184) chance of getting a Gifted Basic Bee from the egg. [Mythic bees](bees-mythic.md) and [Event bees](bees-event.md) cannot be hatched from this egg. This egg cannot be used on a Basic Bee.

Ways to obtain a Basic Egg:

* First-time players receive a free Basic Egg in their inventory.
* Purchasing from the [Basic Egg Shop](basic-egg-shop.md) located near the [Sunflower Field](sunflower-field.md) for a base price of 1,000 [Honey](honey.md) and capping at 10,000,000 [Honey](honey.md). Every time the player purchases a Basic Egg, the price of the next Basic Egg scales up.
* Adding the Round Basic Bee [Sticker](sticker.md) to the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md) rewards 1 Basic Egg.

### Silver Egg

### Silver Egg

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:Error mw:File"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Silver Egg is a type of egg that will most likely hatch into a Rare bee (64.9% chance), but it has a small chance to hatch into an Epic bee (30% chance), a Legendary bee (5% chance), or a Mythic bee (0.1% chance). A Basic Bee or an Event bee cannot be hatched from this egg.

Ways to obtain a Silver Egg:

* Purchasing from [Ticket Tent](ticket-tent.md) for 50 [Tickets](ticket.md) each.
* Purchasing from the [Robux Shop](robux-shop.md) for 100 Robux.
* Finishing [Black Bear's](black-bear.md) and [Panda Bear's](panda-bear.md) Silver Egg [questline](quests.md).
* As a reward from [Brown Bear's](brown-bear.md) 25th quest.
* Completing the 25th [Sticker-Seeker quest](sticker-seeker-quest-machine.md) rewards you 1 Silver Egg.
* An extremely low chance of dropping from a [Mantis](mantis.md) (in increments of 1 or 5).
* A low chance of dropping from the [King Beetle](king-beetle.md) and [Tunnel Bear](tunnel-bear.md).
* A low chance to drop from [Rare Sprouts](sprout.md).
* An extremely rare chance from [leaves](leaves.md) when collecting [pollen](pollen.md) in the [Clover Field](clover-field.md).
* As a drop from a [Festive Planter](festive-planter.md).
* A rare chance to drop from [stockings](stockings.md).
* A rare reward from the [Winter Memory Match](memory-match.md#Winter_Memory_Match).

### Gifted Silver Egg

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:Error mw:File"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

A Gifted Silver Egg shares the same probabilities as a Silver Egg, but the egg will always hatch the bee into its gifted variant.

Ways to obtain a Gifted Silver Egg:

* Defeating [Tunnel Bear](tunnel-bear.md) for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg including a Gifted Silver Egg, but with lower chances of obtaining upon subsequent defeats.
* As a reward for getting Top 25 in the [Daily Top Legendary Bee Pollen Collectors](daily-top-legendary-bee-pollen-collectors.md) by the end of a day.
* Completing [Black Bear's](black-bear.md) "Getting Stumped" quest.
* Completing the 100th quest of the [Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine](sticker-seeker-quest-machine.md) rewards you 1 Gifted Silver Egg.
* An exceptionally rare chance of dropping from [werewolves](werewolf.md).
* A rare chance to obtain when defeating the [Stump Snail](stump-snail.md).
* An exceptionally rare chance of dropping from a [Scorpion](scorpion.md).
* Drop from a Sticker Sprout

### Gold Egg

### Gold Egg

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:Error mw:File"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Gold Egg is a type of egg most likely to hatch into an Epic bee (79% chance), but it has a chance to hatch into a Legendary bee (20% chance) or a Mythic bee (1% chance). A Basic Bee, Rare bee, or an Event bee cannot hatch from this egg.

5 Gold Eggs are required in order to craft the [Honey Mask](honey-mask.md).

Ways to obtain a Gold Egg:

* Purchasing from the [Ticket Tent](ticket-tent.md) for 100 [Tickets](ticket.md) each.
* Purchasing from the [Robux Shop](robux-shop.md) for 200 Robux each.
* A rare reward from the [Robo Bear Challenge](robo-bear-challenge.md).
* A rare reward from the [Extreme Memory Match](memory-match.md#Extreme_Memory_Match).
* In token form in the [Werewolf's Cave](werewolf-s-cave.md), which is guarded by two [cave monsters](cave-monster.md).
* Completing [Black Bear's](black-bear.md) and [Panda Bear's](panda-bear.md) Gold Egg [questline](quests.md).
* As a reward from [Brown Bear's](brown-bear.md) 50th, 340th and 390th quest.
* Completing the [Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine](sticker-seeker-quest-machine.md)'s 50th quest rewards 1 Gold Egg.
* Capturing [Commando Chick](commando-chick.md) 10 times.
* A low chance of dropping upon defeating the [King Beetle](king-beetle.md).
* An extremely low chance of dropping upon defeating a [Scorpion](scorpion.md).
* A rare chance to drop from [Epic sprouts](sprout.md).
* An extremely rare chance of dropping from [leaves](leaves.md) when collecting pollen in the [Clover Field](clover-field.md).
* A rare drop from a [Ticket Planter](ticket-planter.md), [Candy Planter](candy-planter.md), [Red Clay Planter](red-clay-planter.md), [Festive Planter](festive-planter.md), [Pesticide Planter](pesticide-planter.md) and [Petal Planter](petal-planter.md).
* A rare drop from the [Stockings](stockings.md).
* A low chance to drop from a [Festive Nymph](festive-nymph.md).
* As a rare reward from the [Winter Memory Match](memory-match.md#Winter_Memory_Match).

### Diamond Egg

### Diamond Egg

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:Error mw:File"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Diamond Egg is a type of egg that will most likely hatch into a Legendary bee (95% chance), but it has a rare chance to hatch into a Mythic bee (5% chance). A Basic Bee, Rare bee, Epic bee, or an Event bee cannot hatch from this egg.

5 Diamond Eggs are required in order to craft the [Diamond Mask](diamond-mask.md).

Ways to obtain a Diamond Egg:

* A Diamond Egg token is located past the [Bear Gate](bear-gate.md), under the platform where [Onett](onett.md) stands on. The player can use a [glider](glider.md) or a [parachute](parachute.md) and glide towards the token in front of [Shadow Bear](shadow-bear.md).

* Purchasing from the [Ticket Tent](ticket-tent.md) for 400 [Tickets](ticket.md) each.
* A rare reward from [Robo Bear Challenge](robo-bear-challenge.md).
* A rare reward from the [Extreme Memory Match](memory-match.md#Extreme_Memory_Match).
* Completing [Black Bear's](black-bear.md) Diamond Egg questline.
* Completing [Panda Bear's](panda-bear.md) fifth [star treat](star-treat.md)/[ant](ants.md) quest, "Colossal Combat".
* Completing [Dapper Bear's](dapper-bear.md) tenth quest, "The Long Haul".
* Completing [Brown Bear's](brown-bear.md) 75th and 525th quest.
* Completing [Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine](sticker-seeker-quest-machine.md) 75th quest.
* A rare chance of dropping upon defeating the [Stump Snail](stump-snail.md), [Diamond Aphids](aphid.md#Diamond_Aphid), [King Beetle](king-beetle.md), [Tunnel Bear](tunnel-bear.md), and [Coconut Crab](coconut-crab.md).
* A very rare chance of dropping upon defeating [Spiders](spider.md), [Mantises](mantis.md), and [Werewolves](werewolf.md).
* A rare chance to drop from [Legendary sprouts](sprout.md).
* A guaranteed drop from [Supreme sprouts](sprout.md).
* An extremely rare chance of dropping from [leaves](leaves.md) while collecting pollen from the [Clover Field](clover-field.md).
* A very rare reward from catching a [meteor](mythic-meteor-shower.md).
* A rare drop from a [Festive Planter](festive-planter.md).
* A rare reward from [Winter Memory Match](memory-match.md#Winter_Memory_Match).

### Mythic Egg

### Mythic Egg

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:Error mw:File"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Mythic Egg is a type of egg that will always hatch into a Mythic bee. The player cannot obtain any other type of bee from this egg besides a Mythic bee. (The player can obtain a Gifted Mythic bee from this egg, although it is very rare.)

Ways to obtain a Mythic Egg:

* As a reward for getting Top 25 in the [Daily Top Memory Matchers](daily-top-memory-matchers.md) by the end of a day.
* Purchasing from the [Robux Shop](robux-shop.md) for 1,200 Robux each (limited to 3 eggs per player).
* Purchasing from the [Ticket Tent](ticket-tent.md) for 2,500 [Tickets](ticket.md) each.
* As a very rare reward from [Robo Bear Challenge](robo-bear-challenge.md).
* An extremely rare chance of getting from [Extreme Memory Match](memory-match.md#Extreme_Memory_Match).
* Completing [Black Bear's](black-bear.md) 75th quest, "Myth in The Making".
* Completing [Dapper Bear's](dapper-bear.md) 15th quest, "More Than Myth".
* As a reward from [Brown Bear's](brown-bear.md) 100th, 200th, and 400th quests.
* Completing the [Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine](sticker-seeker-quest-machine.md) 300th quest awards one Mythic Egg.
* Capturing [Commando Chick](commando-chick.md) for the 50th time guarantees a Mythic Egg.
* An extremely rare chance of dropping upon defeating the [Stump Snail](stump-snail.md), [Mondo Chick](mondo-chick.md), and [Coconut Crab](coconut-crab.md). Mondo Chick has a higher chance to drop when defeated faster.
* An extremely rare chance of dropping when catching a [meteor](mythic-meteor-shower.md).
* A very rare drop from a [Festive Planter](festive-planter.md).
* Purchasing it for 500 [Snowflakes](snowflake.md) and 100 [Gingerbread Bears](gingerbread-bear.md) in [Bee Bear's Catalog](bee-bear-s-catalog.md).
* Completing [Gummy Bear’s](gummy-bear.md) Goo Year’s Beacon quest during Beesmas 2025 rewards you 1 Mythic Egg and other items.

### Star Egg

### Star Egg

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:Error mw:File"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Star Egg is a type of egg that hatches into any type of [gifted bee](gifted-bee.md) that is not already in the player's hive, aside from gifted Event bees. If the player already has all the different types of gifted bees in their hive besides gifted event bees, the egg will hatch into a random type of gifted bee instead. The egg will never hatch a gifted mythic bee until all other gifted non-event bees are in the hive.

Ways to obtain a Star Egg:

* Defeating [Tunnel Bear](tunnel-bear.md) for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg, including a star egg, but with lower chances of obtaining upon subsequent defeats.
* Purchasing from the [Robux Shop](robux-shop.md) for 800 Robux each (limited to 5 eggs per player).
* An exceptionally rare chance of dropping from a [Mythic Meteor Shower](mythic-meteor-shower.md).
* Completing [Mother Bear's](mother-bear.md) first ten [quests](quests.md) ("Seven To Seven" quest).
* Completing [Panda Bear's](panda-bear.md) first ten star treat/ant quests ("Royal Rumble" quest).
* An exceptionally rare chance to drop upon defeating the [King Beetle](king-beetle.md).
* An exceptionally rare chance of dropping from a [Festive Planter](festive-planter.md).
* As an exceptionally rare drop from the [Party Mechsquito](party-mechsquito.md).
* As an exceptionally rare drop from the Sticker Sprout.

## Trivia

* The [Digital Bee](digital-bee.md), [Vicious Bee](vicious-bee.md), [Windy Bee](windy-bee.md), [Bear Bee](bear-bee.md) and [Gummy Bee](gummy-bee.md) Eggs are the only types of [Event bee](bees-event.md) eggs that could or can be obtained without spending Robux or [Tickets](ticket.md). (A Bear Bee Egg can be obtained by redeeming a [Bear Bee Voucher](sticker.md).)
* Star Eggs and Basic Eggs are the only two eggs that can hatch into a [Basic Bee](basic-bee.md)(Gifted variant if Star Egg)
* Plastic Eggs, the Flight of the Bumble Egg (Egg Hunt 2019), and the Swarming Egg of the Hive (Egg Hunt 2020) are the only types of eggs that do not hatch into a [bee](bees.md).
* Any kind of specific bee egg and event bee egg will have the same icon as a Basic Egg in the inventory.
* The Bear Bee, Festive Bee, [Puppy Bee](puppy-bee.md), Windy Bee, and Digital Bee Eggs are the only type of Event bee eggs that can or could be obtained directly by spending robux. However, as of now, Bear Bee is the only event bee that can be obtained directly by Robux
* Basic Eggs are the only type of eggs that can be obtained directly with honey.
* The star that appears on the Star Egg is slightly larger than the stars on the other types of gifted eggs.
* The Mythic Egg is the only type of egg with the name of the bee rarity that it hatches into.
  * The Mythic Egg is also the only egg that has a 100% of hatching into a certain bee rarity (except for Event bee eggs and specific bee eggs). However, before mythic bees were added, Diamond Eggs used to have a 100% chance of hatching into a Legendary bee.
* The Diamond and Basic Eggs are the only non-specific or event eggs that can hatch into a bee of their name (the [Diamond](diamond-bee.md) and Basic Bees, respectively), though you can get other bees from these eggs.
* The Mythic Egg is also the only egg with a letter on it.
* The Choose-a-Mythic Egg is the only egg that allows the player to choose which bee hatches.
* The [Fuzzy Bee](fuzzy-bee.md), [Buoyant Bee](buoyant-bee.md), and the [Precise Bee](precise-bee.md) Eggs are the only (non-event) specific bee eggs that could have been bought from the Robux Shop.
* The Gifted Silver Egg and the Star Egg are the only Gifted eggs that can be obtained by completing the main questlines from NPCs (Black Bear's "Getting Stumped", Mother Bear's "Seven To Seven", and Panda Bear's "Royal Rumble").
* The player cannot hatch a specific bee egg on that specific bee. For example, a Bumble Bee Egg cannot be hatched on a Bumble Bee.
  * Even though Basic Eggs don't guarantee a Basic Bee, they still can't be used on Basic Bees.
* The Basic Egg and Choose-A-Mythic Egg are the only eggs without gifted variants of themselves, excluding specific bee eggs.
  * Similarly, the Star Egg is the only gifted egg without a regular variant.
* The Bubble Bee Egg is the only non-Event specific bee egg that can be obtained from a quest (B.B.M's Naughty List).
* The Tadpole Bee Egg is the only egg that can be obtained by giving an NPC present (obtained specifically from Bubble Bee Man).
