**Tadpole Bee** is a Blue [Mythic bee](bees-mythic.md).

Tadpole Bee's favorite treat is [Blueberries](blueberry.md).

Tadpole Bee likes the [Pine Tree Forest](pine-tree-forest.md) and [Stump Field](stump-field.md). It dislikes the [Cactus Field](cactus-field.md).

## Stats

* Collects 10 [Pollen](pollen.md) in 6 seconds.
* Makes 120 [Honey](honey.md) in 4 seconds.
* +40 [Convert Amount](system-page.md#Convert_Amount), -20% [Movespeed](stats.md#Speed), -50% Gather Speed, and [Energy](energy.md).
* 🌟[Gifted Hive Bonus](gifted-bee.md): +25% [Bubble](bubble.md) Duration

### Abilities

* **[[Blue Boost]](ability-tokens.md#Blue_Boost)** Grants x1.2 pollen from Blue [Flowers](flowers.md) for 15 seconds. Stacks up to 10 times.
* **[[Summon Frog]](ability-tokens.md#Summon_Frog)** Summons a [frog](frog.md) that lasts for 20s (+2s per Level). Frogs can make [bubbles](bubble.md) after every few hops, or after collecting a token with their tongue. They also attack nearby enemies with attack equal to 5x this bee's attack.
  * If Gifted, this has a 10% (+2% per Level) chance to summon a Gifted Frog instead, which has increased tongue range, Token gathering and attack speed, 50% more attack, has a chance to summon [golden bubbles](bubble.md#Golden_Bubbles), and can hit [balloons](balloon.md) with its tongue to convert pollen to Honey Tokens.
* **[[🌟Gifted Ability: Baby Love]](ability-tokens.md#Baby_Love)** Grants x2 pollen and +50% [Loot Luck](loot-luck.md) for 30 seconds. Loot Luck increases your chance of obtaining prizes from defeating enemies.
* **[[Passive:](passive-abilities.md#Gathering_Bubbles) [Gathering Bubbles+]](bubble.md#Gathering_Bubbles)** 65% chance (85% if Gifted) to spawn a Bubble when gathering. If any player touches it, it pops, collecting 4 Red/6 White/8 Blue pollen (+15% per Gifted blue bee type, +5% per bee level) from 29 nearby flowers and causing them to replenish.

## Trivia

* The Tadpole Bee, [Lion Bee](lion-bee.md), [Tabby Bee](tabby-bee.md), [Bear Bee](bear-bee.md), and [Puppy Bee](puppy-bee.md) are the only [bees](bees.md) to represent realistic animals.
  * The Tadpole Bee is the only Mythic bee that represents a realistic animal.
* Tadpole Bee and [Baby Bee](baby-bee.md) are the only bees with the Baby Love ability.
  * Tadpole Bee and [Baby Bee](baby-bee.md) are also the only bees that appear smaller than the normal bee size.
* Tadpole Bee and [Windy Bee](windy-bee.md) are the only bees with 2 gifted bonuses, excluding other Event Bees who enhance their ability.
* Tadpole Bee, [Vector Bee](vector-bee.md), [Fuzzy Bee](fuzzy-bee.md), [Spicy Bee](spicy-bee.md), [Buoyant Bee](buoyant-bee.md) and [Digital Bee](digital-bee.md) are the only bees with gifted exclusive abilities.
* This bee and [Spicy Bee](spicy-bee.md) are the only bees who can summon other entities.
  * This is the only bee that can summon a [Mob](mobs.md).
* Tadpole Bee and [Bubble Bee](bubble-bee.md) are the only bees with the Gathering Bubbles passive ability.
* Tadpole Bee, [Fuzzy Bee](fuzzy-bee.md), [Spicy Bee](spicy-bee.md), [Buoyant Bee](buoyant-bee.md), and [Digital Bee](digital-bee.md) all have four different [Abilities](ability-tokens.md), the most for any bee.
* Tadpole Bee used to be the only bee in the game that had a decimal base attack (0.5).
* Tadpole Bee is the only Mythic bee that has its Gifted-exclusive ability token shared with another bee.
* Along with [Vector Bee](vector-bee.md) and [Spicy Bee](spicy-bee.md), Tadpole Bee was one of the first [Mythic Bees](bees-mythic.md) to be introduced to the game.
* A [Tadpole Bee Egg](egg.md#Specific_Bee_Eggs) could be obtained by giving [Bubble Bee Man](bubble-bee-man.md) a [Present](present.md) during the Beesmas 2020 event.
  * Tadpole Bee is the second mythic bee to have an egg of its own, the first being [Fuzzy Bee](fuzzy-bee.md).
* Tadpole Bee is one of the 8 other bees with a unique trail.
* Tadpole Bee is the blue counterpart of [Spicy Bee](spicy-bee.md).
* Tadpole Bee and [Fuzzy Bee](fuzzy-bee.md) are the only Mythics, when gifted, to not have any glowing parts.
