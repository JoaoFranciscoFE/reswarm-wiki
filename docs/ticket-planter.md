A **Ticket Planter** is a single use [planter](planter.md) added in the 2021-12-26 update. Alone, it grows in about 2 hours and stores 50,000 [pollen](pollen.md). When harvested, it grants bonus Tickets. A player can store up to 100 Ticket Planters at a time, and up to 3 planters can be planted at once.

## Growth, pollen and nectar

* Grows in 2 hours of playtime.
* [Glitter](glitter.md) used in its field speeds it up: x1.5 for one, rising in halving steps up to x2 at 6 stacks. The stacks stay on the planter until it is harvested.
* Holds 50,000 [pollen](pollen.md) at full growth. A harvest gives capacity × growth × pollen bonus. It has no pollen bonus.
* [Nectar](nectar.md) multipliers: x2 Satisfying, x2 Comforting, x2 Invigorating, x2 Motivating, x2 Refreshing. On harvest it gives one Nectar type from its field, lasting grow time × growth × multiplier × 3 seconds.
* Up to 3 bees can sip from it at once.

## Puffshrooms

When it reaches 95% growth, it always smoke and spawn 3 [Puffshrooms](puffshroom.md) (one at the planter and two nearby) at levels 1 to 2.

They are always Common.

## Drops

A full harvest gives 25 item tokens. Harvesting early gives fewer, in proportion to growth, but always at least 1. Tokens are filled in this order: guaranteed items (full harvests only), then field rules, then random picks from the pool below.

On every full harvest:

## Ways to obtain

* [Dapper Bear](dapper-bear.md) gives 3 Ticket Planters if the player speaks to him for the first time.
* As a reward from certain [quests](quests.md):
  * If the player completes Dapper Bear's quests and they already got Beequip slots from [Bee Bear](bee-bear.md) in previous years, they get a Ticket Planter for each Beequip slot.
  * Bee Bear's "Searching For Snowflakes" quest rewards 1 Ticket Planter and other items.
* As a rare drop from [Sticker Sprouts](sprout.md#Variants).
* As a Stack Reward for adding certain stickers to the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md):
  * [Dapper From Above Sticker](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) rewards 1 Ticket Planter.
  * [Ticket Voucher](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) rewards 1 Ticket Planter
* Buying the Tempting Ticket Pack from [Bee Bear's Catalog](bee-bear-s-catalog.md) for 55,555 [Snowflakes](snowflake.md) gives 5 Ticket Planters and other items.
* As a drop from Dapper Bear's [Samovar](samovar.md) after every 5th use (except for the 25th, which drops a [turpentine](turpentine.md) instead).

## Trivia

* This is one of the two planters that is guaranteed to spawn a [Puffshroom](puffshroom.md). The other is the [Sticker Planter](sticker-planter.md).
* This, the [Sticker Planter](sticker-planter.md) and the [Paper Planter](paper-planter.md) share the same base model.
  * They also share the same outline for their decals.
* It is one of two planters that mentions an in-game item in its name, the other planter is the [Sticker Planter](sticker-planter.md).
* It gives the second-best nectar multiplier in the game, 2x, behind the [Festive Planter](festive-planter.md) with 3x.
* It is one of 4 single-use planters, the others being the [Paper Planter](paper-planter.md), the [Festive Planter](festive-planter.md) and the [Sticker Planter](sticker-planter.md).
* This, the [Sticker Planter](sticker-planter.md) and the [Paper Planter](paper-planter.md) are the only one-use planters in the game that can be obtained infinitely.
