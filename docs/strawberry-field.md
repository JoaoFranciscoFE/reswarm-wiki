The **Strawberry Field** is a field located past the [Basic Bee Gate](basic-bee-gate.md), to the left of the [Spider Field](spider-field.md), and beside the [Vicious Bee Egg Claim](vicious-bee-egg-claim.md). This field is protected by 2 [ladybugs](ladybug.md); one of which is level 2 and the other level 3.

This field can spawn [honey](honey.md), [Strawberry](strawberry.md), [Red Boost](ability-tokens.md#Boost), [Red Extract](red-extract.md), [Bitterberry](bitterberry.md), [Ticket](ticket.md) and [Royal Jelly](royal-jelly.md) tokens while collecting [pollen](pollen.md).

[Bees](bees.md) that like the Strawberry Field are [Fire Bee](fire-bee.md), [Riley Bee](riley-bee.md), and [Shy Bee](shy-bee.md). Bees that dislike the Strawberry Field are [Cool Bee](cool-bee.md), [Bubble Bee](bubble-bee.md), [Bucko Bee](bucko-bee.md), and [Windy Bee](windy-bee.md).

This field grants Refreshing [Nectar](nectar.md) when a [planter](planter.md) is placed here.

It is a 22x26 field, containing 572 [flowers](flowers.md). The flowers are red and white, in sizes of single, double, and triple.

## Obtaining Boosts

* Using the [Red Field Booster](red-field-booster.md) may give a Strawberry [Field Boost](field-boost.md). When it does, it will give +100% Strawberry Field pollen for 15 minutes.

  

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 75px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The Strawberry Field boost icon.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

* Using [glitter](glitter.md) in the Strawberry Field will add 100% Strawberry Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if the boost isn't already maxed out.
* Using [field dice](field-dice.md), [smooth dice](smooth-dice.md), or [loaded dice](loaded-dice.md) may add 100% - 300% Strawberry Field pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out and also what type of dice is used.
* Strawberry [Field Winds](field-wind.md) can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md) or [codes](codes.md). They have a base of +20% instant Strawberry Field conversion, and give +5% instant Strawberry Field conversion and +15% Strawberry Field pollen for each stack. Strawberry Field Winds can stack up to a total of 15 times.
* The field can be boosted by the [King Beetle Amulet](king-beetle-amulet.md). If it has a Strawberry Field Boost, it can boost the field by +1% up to a max of +100% pollen.

### The methods below are no longer possible.

* Giving a [present](present.md) to certain NPCs:
  * [Gifted Riley Bee](gifted-riley-bee.md) gave Strawberry Field Boost x3 and other boosts and [items](items.md).
  * [Onett](onett.md) gave Strawberry Field Boost x1 and other boosts and items.
* Purchasing a Strawberry Field [Market Boost](buffs-debuffs.md#From_Areas) from the [Boost Market](boost-market.md) will give x1.5 Strawberry [Field Capacity](field-capacity.md), x1.25 Strawberry Field Pollen, x1.25 [Red Bomb Pollen](system-page.md#Red_Bomb_Pollen), and x1.25 [Convert Rate At Hive](system-page.md#Hive_Convert_Rate).
* Redeeming certain expired codes:
  * Friday (Gave Strawberry Field Boost x5 + other stuff).
  * SugarRush (Gave Strawberry Field Boost x3 + other stuff).
  * FruitMachine (Gave Strawberry Field Boost x1 + other stuff).
  * Berlin (Gave Strawberry Field Boost x4 + other stuff).
  * 1mFavorites (Gave Strawberry Field Boost x1 + other stuff).
  * WikiHonor (Gave Strawberry Field Boost x3 + other stuff).
  * JollyJelly (Gave Strawberry Field Boost x3 and Strawberry Field Winds x3 + other stuff).
  * FestiveFinale (Gave Strawberry Field Boost x3 and Strawberry Field Winds x2 + other stuff)
  * WeekExtension (Gives Strawberry Field Boost x3 + Strawberry Field Winds x10 + other stuff)
  * 1MLikes (Gives Strawberry Field Boost x1 + other stuff).

## Trivia

* This is the third smallest field in the game, outranked by the [Stump Field](stump-field.md) and [Pumpkin Patch](pumpkin-patch.md).
* There is a strawberry token behind the biggest strawberry in the top left of the field.
* This is one of six fields that [fireflies](fireflies.md) can travel to during [nighttime](day-night-cycle.md), with the others being [Bamboo Field](bamboo-field.md), [Cactus Field](cactus-field.md), [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md), [Rose Field](rose-field.md), and Spider Field.
* [Mother Bear](mother-bear.md) states that finding strawberry tokens are much more common in this field than in any other field.
* According to [Spirit Bear](spirit-bear.md), the Strawberry in the field were planted from seeds imported from Sweden. She also states that [sprouts](sprout.md) in this field spawn the most strawberry tokens, 8 times more than normal sprouts.
* If you run towards the strawberry on the corner fast enough, you may glitch inside of the strawberry. If this happens you will have to reset your character, rejoin the game, or use a [Whirligig](whirligig.md).
