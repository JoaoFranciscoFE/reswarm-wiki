# Painter Stickers

The **Painter stickers** are a set of [stickers](sticker.md) added in the [Painter Bee event](painter-bee-event.md).

## Stickers

| Sticker | How to get it | Stack buff | Stack reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| Red Paint Splatter | Collect a Color Splat token with Red Paint (1 in 1,000) | +1% Red Pollen | 1 Red Extract |
| Blue Paint Splatter | Collect a Color Splat token with Blue Paint (1 in 1,000) | +1% Blue Pollen | 1 Blue Extract |
| White Paint Splatter | Collect a Color Splat token with White Paint (1 in 1,000) | +1% White Pollen | 1 Glitter |
| Painter Bee Painting | Found by Painter Bee while gathering (1 in 7,500, or 1 in 6,000 when Gifted) | +0.25% Bee Ability Rate | 3 Neonberry |
| Gifted Painter Bee | Summoning Painter's Haze (1 in 5,000) | +1% Bee Gather Pollen | 5 Royal Jelly |
| Painter's Doodle | Stack all 3 Paint Splatters | +1% Pollen | 3 Glitter |
| Supreme Puffshroom | Dropped by [Supreme Puffshrooms](supreme-puffshroom.md) (1 in 100) | +1% Bee Ability Rate | 5 Neonberry |

The code `splat!` also gives one random Paint Splatter (see [Codes](codes.md)).

## Vouchers

| Voucher | Stack buff | Stack reward |
|---|---|---|
| Painter Bee Voucher | +1% Bee Gather Pollen | 5 Red Extract, 5 Blue Extract, 5 Tropical Drink |
| 1st Edition Painter Bee Voucher | +2% Bee Gather Pollen | 5 Red Extract, 5 Blue Extract, 5 Tropical Drink |

## Painter Bee's Artistic Hive Skin

*"A hive splashed in paint, signed by Painter Bee."*

Awarded after you stick every Painter Bee sticker to the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md). Its easel shows the hive owner's Roblox headshot.

* Stack buff: +1% Honey At Hive
* Stack reward: 10 Tickets
