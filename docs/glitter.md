# Glitter

Glitter

*"Boosts the field you're standing in, granting +100% pollen for 15 minutes."*

COOLDOWN

15 minutes 5 seconds

**Glitter** is a craftable inventory item that was added in the [2018-11-25 update](updates.md#2018-11-25). It grants a +100% [pollen](pollen.md) boost in the field the player is standing in for 15 minutes upon activation. If used in a field with a [planter](planter.md), the planter gains a 25% growth rate boost until it is harvested. **Glitter** has a 15-minute and 5 second cooldown between uses. This is to prevent the user from stacking the item's buff for one field.

It can be used in all fields except the [Ant Field](ant-field.md). Attempting to do so will result in the following message:
You can't boost a challenge field

## Ways to Obtain

* Crafting via [Blender](blender.md) for:
  * 25 [Moon Charms](moon-charm.md)
  * 1 [Magic Bean](magic-bean.md)
* Playing any type of [Memory Match](memory-match.md) (Increments of 1 or 5).
* As a reward from donating to the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md).
* From a [Festive Gift](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Gift) or [Festive Mark](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Mark).
* As a stack reward for adding certain [Stickers](sticker.md) to the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md):
  * Sideways Spirit Bear rewards 1 glitter.
  * Shining Halo rewards 1 glitter.
  * Glowing Smile rewards 1 glitter.
  * Every Star Sign sticker rewards 1 glitter.

### Drops

* As a drop from certain mobs:
  * [Tunnel Bear](tunnel-bear.md)
  * [Coconut Crab](coconut-crab.md)
  * [Stump Snail](stump-snail.md)
  * [Scorpion](scorpion.md) (Rare)
  * [Mantis](mantis.md) (Rare)
  * [Diamond Aphid](aphid.md#Diamond_Aphid) (Rare)
  * [King Beetle](king-beetle.md) (Uncommon)
  * [Wild Windy Bee](wild-windy-bee.md) (Rare)
* Farming in the [Dandelion Field](dandelion-field.md), [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md), and [Spider Field](spider-field.md) can rarely drop glitter.
  * Harvesting leaves in these fields may also yield glitter.
* Can be generated by the [fireflies](fireflies.md) during [nighttime](day-night-cycle.md).
* As a rare gift from a [cub buddy](cub-buddy.md).
* As a rare drop from [Mythic Meteors](mythic-meteor-shower.md).
* All [sprouts](sprout.md) (excluding the Debug Sprout) can generate a glitter token (possibilities differ depending on the type of sprout)
  * Rare drops from Moon [Sprouts](sprout.md) will usually yield glitter.
* As a rare drop from harvesting [Sparkles](sparkles.md).
* As a rare drop from [Puffshrooms](puffshroom.md).
* As a drop from certain [Planters](planter.md):
  * [Candy Planter](candy-planter.md)
  * [Tacky Planter](tacky-planter.md)
  * [Festive Planter](festive-planter.md)
  * [Pesticide Planter](pesticide-planter.md)
  * [Petal Planter](petal-planter.md) (Drops bonus glitter)
    * Planting the [Petal Planter](petal-planter.md) in any white field may yield a bonus 10 **Glitter** (1 week cooldown).

### Quests

* [Black Bear](black-bear.md) gives glitter on the following quests:
  * Pretty Pumpkins gives 1 glitter and other items.
  * All-Whitey Then gives 1 glitter and other items.
  * Black Bear can occasionally give glitter after completing a repeatable quest.
* [Science Bear](science-bear.md) gives glitter on the following quests:
  * Smart, Not Hard gives 1 glitter and other items.
  * Meditating On Phenomenon gives 1 glitter and other items.
  * Hive Minded Bias gives 3 glitter and other items.
  * Epistemological Endeavor gives 4 glitter and other items. Before its nerf, the quest gave 5 glitter.
* [Dapper Bear](dapper-bear.md) gives glitter on the following quests:
  * Unintended Intimidation gives 2 glitter and other items.
  * The Trick To Tickets gives 1 glitter and other items.
  * Ongoing Maintenance gives 5 glitter and other items.
* All of [Spirit Bear's](spirit-bear.md) quests except for her 10th and 17th reward glitter.
* [Polar Bear](polar-bear.md) has a small chance of rewarding glitter after one of his quests.

### Token Locations

* In the [Diamond Room](obstacle-courses.md#Diamond_Room), in front of the [Diamond Mask](diamond-mask.md).
* Behind the [Gummy Boots](gummy-boots.md)' honeycomb in [Gummy Bear's Lair](gummy-bear-s-lair.md).

### Beesmas

* As a drop from [Dapper Bear's](dapper-bear.md) [Samovar](samovar.md). (Appeared at the 4th use and every 5 uses.)
* As a drop from [Onett's Lid Art](onett-s-lid-art.md).
* Completing [Spirit Bear](spirit-bear.md)’s Galentine Shrine quest rewards 10 glitter and other items.

## Trivia

* This is one of four consumable items to grant a [field boost](field-boost.md), the others being [Field Dice](field-dice.md), [Smooth Dice](smooth-dice.md), and [Loaded Dice](loaded-dice.md)
  * It also is the one of the four consumables that shows glitter particles on the field when the said field is boosted.
  * This is the only consumable to grant a Field Boost of the player's choice.
* Spirit Bear mentions in one of her quest dialogues, Spirit's Starter, that glitter was once so rare that 1 pouch of it costed 5 million honey.
* Glitter's raw ingredients cost:
  * 375 [Strawberries](strawberry.md)
  * 375 [Blueberries](blueberry.md)
  * 500 [Pineapples](pineapple.md)
  * 25 [Royal Jellies](royal-jelly.md) or 25M [Honey](honey.md)
  * 1 [Magic Bean](magic-bean.md) or 20 [Tickets](ticket.md)
