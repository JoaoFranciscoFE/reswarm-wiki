# Moon Charm

![Moon Charm](img/Moon_Charm.png){ align=right width=150 }

Moon Charm

*"Increases your bond with a bee by 250."*

<figure class="thumb mw-halign-right" style="width: 220px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The two types of moon charm tokens: On the left, there is a token that is not glowing, dropped by mobs or mythic meteors. On the right, there is a glowing variant that is dropped during nighttime from fireflies, sparkles, and moon sprouts.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

A **Moon Charm**, when fed to a [bee](bees.md), raises its [bond](bond.md) by 250. Moon Charms are also used in generating [Moon Amulets](moon-amulet.md) and they are used in crafting of [glitter](glitter.md) and the [Helmet](helmet.md). If fed to a [radioactive](radiation.md) bee, it has a 1/2,500 (0.04%) chance to give that bee a [mutation](mutation.md).

Moon charm tokens last for 10 seconds before disappearing.

## Ways to Obtain

* Crafting via the [Blender](blender.md) for:
  * 5 [Pineapples](pineapple.md)
  * 5 [Gumdrops](gumdrops.md)
  * 1 [Royal Jelly](royal-jelly.md)
* As a reward from [Memory Matches](memory-match.md) (Every type except Extreme Memory Match and the Winter Memory Match).
  * A potential with an increment of 5 in the Memory Match.
  * A potential pair with an increment of 10 in the Mega Memory Match.
  * Two pairs with increments of 25 in the Night Memory Match.
* As a reward from the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md).
* As a Stack Reward for adding certain [stickers](sticker.md) to the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md):
  * [Waxing Crescent Moon Sticker](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) rewards 10 moon charms.
  * [Petal Wand Sticker](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) rewards 10 moon charms.

### Drops

* As a drop from certain [mobs](mobs.md):
  * [King Beetle](king-beetle.md) and [Tunnel Bear](tunnel-bear.md) always drop moon charms upon defeat.
  * A common outcome from chasing all 11 [Fireflies](fireflies.md) (Increments of 1, 5, and 10.)
  * As a rare drop from [Mantis](mantis.md) and [Stump Snail](stump-snail.md).
* As a common drop from [Mythic Meteors](mythic-meteor-shower.md).
* A common drop from harvesting [sparkles](sparkles.md).
* A guaranteed drop from a [Moon Sprout](sprout.md).
* Harvesting [planters](planter.md) in the [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md).

### Quests

* Black Bear rewards moon charms on the following quest:
  * Rare Red Clover gives 25 moon charms.
  * Red Delicacy gives 50 moon charms.
* Spirit Bear rewards moon charms on the following quests:
  * The First Offering gives 5 moon charms.
  * Rules of The Road gives 10 moon charms.
  * Nature's Lessons gives 20 moon charms.
  * The Gifts Of Life gives 25 moon charms.
  * Out-Questing Questions 25 moon charms.
  * Forcefully Friendly gives 25 moon charms.
  * Memories of Memories give 30 moon charms.
  * Beans Becoming gives 30 moon charms.
  * The Ways Of The Winds / The Wind And Its Ways gives 50 moon charms.
  * Witness Grandeur gives 50 moon charms.
  * Glory Of Goo gives 50 moon charms.
  * Beeternity gives 100 moon charms.
  * The Sky Over The Stump gives 100 moon charms.
  * Spirit Spree gives 150 moon charms.
  * Dreams Of Being A Bee gives 200 moon charms.
  * Space Oblivion Gives 250 moon charms.
  * Echoing Call gives 250 moon charms.

## Trivia

* This item is one of the 8 things that can have a glowing token, the others being [Festive Gift](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Gift), [Festive Blessing Tokens](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Blessing), [Beesmas Cheer Tokens](ability-tokens.md#Beesmas_Cheer), [Map Corruption](ability-tokens.md#Map_Corruption), as well as [Glitter](glitter.md), [Night Bells](night-bell.md), [Star Jellies](royal-jelly.md#Star_Jelly), and [Star Treats](star-treat.md) tokens (as exceptionally rare drops from [fireflies](fireflies.md)).

* Moon Charm's raw ingredients cost:
  * 15 [Strawberries](strawberry.md)
  * 15 [Blueberries](blueberry.md)
  * 20 [Pineapples](pineapple.md)
  * 1 [Royal Jelly](royal-jelly.md) or 1M [Honey](honey.md)
