# Field Dice

Field Dice

*"Boosts a random field, granting +100% pollen for 15 minutes."*

COOLDOWN

1 second

**Field Dice** is an inventory item added in the [2019-04-05 update](updates.md#2019-04-05). When used, It boosts a random [field](fields.md) the player has access to, granting a +100% [pollen](pollen.md) [Field Boost](field-boost.md) for that field for 15 minutes. If the player uses another field dice, or any other type of dice while a Field Boost from a dice is still active, the new field boost will override the old one, preventing the Field Boost from stacking up.

As of the 2021-12-26 Update, there are currently 3 types of dice in the game, those being Field Dice, [Smooth Dice](smooth-dice.md) and [Loaded Dice](loaded-dice.md).

## Ways of Obtaining

* Crafting via [Blender](blender.md) for:
  * 1 [Soft Wax](soft-wax.md).
  * 1 [Whirligig](whirligig.md).
  * 1 [Red Extract](red-extract.md).
  * 1 [Blue Extract](blue-extract.md).

### Drops

* As a drop from [mobs](mobs.md), such as [aphids](aphid.md), [ladybugs](ladybug.md), [scorpions](scorpion.md), [spiders](spider.md), [werewolves](werewolf.md), [Wild Windy Bee](wild-windy-bee.md), and [Mondo Chick](mondo-chick.md).
* A rare drop from [Festive Gift](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Gift) (if [Festive Bee](festive-bee.md) is [gifted](gifted-bee.md)).
* A rare drop from [Festive Mark](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Mark).
* As a reward by donating to the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md).
* As a reward from the [Robo Bear Challenge](robo-bear-challenge.md).
* As a rare drop from a [Cub Buddy](cub-buddy.md) gift.
* As a drop from specific [planters](planter.md).
  * The [Tacky Planters](tacky-planter.md) and [Petal Planters](petal-planter.md) drop field dice as a bonus drop.
* As a drop from [Mythic Meteor Shower](mythic-meteor-shower.md).

* Matching a pair of field dice in [Memory Match](memory-match.md).
* Adding the Wishbone [sticker](sticker.md) to the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md) rewards 5 field dice

## Trivia

* If another dice is rolled while a dice is already being rolled, this notification would pop up:

You are already rolling a dice

* Field Dice's raw ingredients cost:
  * 1 [Whirligig](whirligig.md)
  * 5 [Honeysuckles](honeysuckle.md)
  * 50 [Sunflower Seeds](sunflower-seed.md)
  * 50 [Pineapples](pineapple.md)
  * 50 [Blueberries](blueberry.md)
  * 50 [Strawberries](strawberry.md)
  * 50 [Royal Jellies](royal-jelly.md) or 50,000,000 [Honey](honey.md)
* The field icons shown on the dice are the [Pine Tree Forest](pine-tree-forest.md), the [Strawberry Field](strawberry-field.md), and the [Blue Flower Field](blue-flower-field.md).
* This item is similar to [Glitter](glitter.md), because they both give a 100% pollen boost on a field. However, it can only be used every 15 minutes, and the player can choose which field to boost.
* Field Dice is actually a plural name. The singular version of this item would be Field Die.
* It is possible to get a [Mountain Top Field](mountain-top-field.md) Boost and [Coconut Field](coconut-field.md) Boost from field dice.
  * Unlike this, [Field Boosters](field-booster.md) can't give a boost to said fields.
* Donating 3 field dice to the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md) is almost guaranteed to summon [Wild Windy Bee](wild-windy-bee.md).
* There used to be a limit of 25 field dice, but it was removed in the 2021-12-26 update.
* The Field Dice used to be able to stack field boost allowing player to get up to 300% field boosts from it, it was later reworked in 2021-12-26 update, where dice system was reworked and with the introduction of new dice types.
* Rolling the same field three times in a row grants the [Triple Exclamation Sticker](sticker.md), spawning it in the field rolled.
  * The chance of this happening is 1/289 (≈0.34%). This is because the first field can be any field and the others must be the same.
  * In addition, rolling the same four or more times in a row will continue to grant the sticker.
  * The message for this is "Oh! That Field Dice had something on it."
* Normally, rolling the same field twice with field dice would just refresh the boost, but if you roll a field, leave, and then roll until you get the field again, it would stack (doesn't go beyond the limit though).
