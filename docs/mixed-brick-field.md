# Mixed Brick Field

Mixed Brick Field

FIELD COLOR

Assorted

BEE PREREQUISITES

10 bees

FIELD DIMENSIONS

19 × 19 flowers

FLOWER COUNT

361

BEE PREFERENCES

LIKE

None

DISLIKE

None

The **Mixed Brick Field** is one of the four [fields](fields.md) located in the [Retro Swarm Challenge](retro-swarm-challenge.md).

This is a 19x19 field, containing 361 [flowers](flowers.md). The flowers are white, blue, and red, in sizes of single, double, and triple.

This field can produce [brick](brick.md) tokens while collecting [pollen](pollen.md).

There are no bees with any preference towards this field.

Since [planters](planter.md) cannot be used in the Retro Swarm Challenge, there is no confirmed nectar type for this field.

## Obtaining Boosts

* Using [Glitter](glitter.md) in the Mixed Brick Field will grant a 100% Mixed Brick Field pollen boost for 15 minutes.

## Trivia

* This field and three other fields present in the Retro Swarm Challenge are the only fields without an associated field stamp.
  * All of these fields also borrow the [Sunflower Field](sunflower-field.md)'s boost icon.
