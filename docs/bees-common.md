## Trivia

* The Common rarity and the [Event](bees-event.md) rarity are the only rarities of bees that can't be obtained by using a normal [Royal Jelly](royal-jelly.md) or [Star Jelly](royal-jelly.md#Star_Jelly).
* Excluding [gifted](gifted-bee.md) forms, this and [Rare bees](bees-rare.md) are the only rarity of bees that has a bee that can't spawn [ability tokens](ability-tokens.md). The rare bee that can't spawn ability tokens is [Brave Bee](brave-bee.md).
* The [Hive Slot](hive-slot.md) color for a **Common Bee** is brown, which may represent bronze.
* The Common rarity is the rarity with the least amount of bee types, having only 1 bee type.
* This rarity has only three ways of getting a bee of this rarity which are [Basic Eggs](egg.md#Basic_Egg), [Star Eggs](egg.md#Star_Egg), and the [Basic Bee Jelly](royal-jelly.md#Specific_Bee_Jelly_Variants).
