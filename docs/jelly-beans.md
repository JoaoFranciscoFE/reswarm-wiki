# Jelly Beans

This piece of content contains information obtained through datamining.

Due to the nature of the information, details may be inaccurate or outdated.

Datamined information: The buffs of every jelly bean type. While this information is accessible in-game, Spoiled Jelly Beans have extra information that can't be verified due to its rarity. — December 19th, 2024

Jelly Beans

*"Scatters various buff-granting beans on nearby flowers. Works best when shared."*

COOLDOWN

30 seconds

CAP

100

**Jelly Beans** are an inventory item added in the [Egg Hunt 2019 update](updates.md#2019-04-17). When used, it spreads jelly beans of varying colors in the direction the player is facing, with each color representing a different [buff](buffs-debuffs.md). They will turn into jelly bean tokens when they land on [flowers](flowers.md), making them collectable. Each buff lasts for a minute. Each jelly bean item spawns 11-13 jelly bean tokens.

If multiple players are around, a different player picking up the tokens will give both the owner and the collector the token's buffs, as well as the Jelly Bean Sharing Bonus to the owner. Therefore, it is best to share jelly beans.

The player can only hold a maximum of 100 at a time in their inventory. The player will need to use at least one in order to get any more unless they are obtained from [quests](quests.md), purchased from any [Robux pack](robux-shop.md), or from the [Colorful Present](ornament-presents.md) (now unobtainable).

***Note:** Jelly Beans can be activated anywhere, though it is recommended to be in a field because if the beans do not land on a field, no tokens will be spawned.*

## Ways To Obtain

* As a reward from the [Retro Swarm Challenge](retro-swarm-challenge.md)
* As a reward from the [Ant Challenge](ant-challenge.md).
* A common reward from [Robo Bear Challenge](robo-bear-challenge.md).
* As a reward from [Memory Match](memory-match.md).
* As a Stack Reward for adding the following [stickers](sticker.md) to the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md):
  * [Standing Bean Bug Sticker](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) grants 5 Jelly Beans.
  * [Baseball Swing Sticker](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) grants 5 Jelly Beans.
  * [Colorful Buttons Sticker](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) grants 5 Jelly Beans.

### Drops

* As a bonus drop from certain [planters](planter.md):
  * [Candy Planter](candy-planter.md)
  * [Petal Planter](petal-planter.md)
  * [The Planter Of Plenty](the-planter-of-plenty.md)
* Donating certain items to the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md).
* Rarely through a [Cub Buddy](cub-buddy.md) gift in increments of 1 or 5.

### Quests

* [Black Bear](black-bear.md) gives jelly beans on the following quests:
  * Can't Without Ants rewards 3 jelly beans and other items after completion.
  * Low Tier Treck rewards 3 jelly beans and other items after completion.
  * Mountain Mix rewards 3 jelly beans and other items after completion.
  * You Blue It rewards 5 jelly beans and other items after completion.
  * Getting Stumped rewards 10 jelly beans and other items after completion.
  * Red Delicacy rewards 3 jelly beans and other items after completion.
* [Brown Bear](brown-bear.md) rewards jelly beans after every third quest completed.
* [Spirit Bear](spirit-bear.md) rewards jelly beans on the following quests:
  * QR Quest rewards 5 jelly beans and other items after completion.
  * Pleasant Pastimes gives the player 3 jelly beans once the quest is started.
  * Out-Questing Questions rewards 10 jelly beans and other items after completion.
  * Do You Bee-lieve In Magic? rewards 10 jelly beans and other items after completion.
  * Witness Grandeur rewards 10 jelly beans and other items after completion.
  * Glory Of Goo rewards 5 jelly beans and other items after completion.
  * Rhubarb That Could Have Been rewards 10 jelly beans and other items after completion.
  * Space Oblivion rewards 10 jelly beans and other items after completion.
  * Dancing With Stick Bug rewards 10 jelly beans and other items after completion.
  * Bear Without Despair rewards 10 jelly beans and other items after completion.
  * Spirit Spree rewards 10 jelly beans and other items after completion.

### Tokens

* Going into the [Blue Maze](mazes.md#Blue_Maze) next to the [Ticket Tent](ticket-tent.md). Taking the path to the right will lead to a jelly bean token.
* Going into the [Coconut Cave](coconut-cave.md) on top of the [Coconut Dispenser](coconut-dispenser.md) there will be a hole, Going through the hole has jelly beans and others.

## Trivia

* [Bean Bugs](bean-bug.md) are passive mobs that spawn in random fields. They scatter jelly bean tokens around their current field. They are the second and only other way of getting jelly bean tokens.
* When picking up a jelly bean item token (the consumable), it said "You found a Jelly Beans!" instead of "You found Jelly Beans!" or "You found a Jelly Bean!". This was an error, fixed at an unknown date.
* The Merigold Jelly Bean is misspelled throughout the game. The correct spelling should be 'Marigold'.
* The hearts in the Jelly Bean Sharing Bonus buff are similar to the heart of the [Baby Love ability token](ability-tokens.md#Baby_Love), but they are not blushing.
* Rarer jelly beans emit sparkles when being thrown into a field, and the token will also emit a sparkle effect.
* Prior to the [2019-09-28 update](updates.md#2019-09-28), Jelly Beans did not have a maximum capacity and players could obtain them in an unlimited amount.
* If the player donates any number of Jelly Beans for the first time to the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md), it gives back 7 Jelly Beans.
  * After donating, it says "- ( Number ) Jellybeanss". It should be "Jelly Beans" with one S.
  * When claiming the tokens, it says "+ ( Number ) Jelly Beans ( from >>☻︎ ). This is a clue from the Ready Player Two Event.
* If the player attempts to throw Jelly Beans in the [Ant Field](ant-field.md) from outside, the beans will be thrown, but no tokens will spawn even if they hit flowers in the Ant Field.
* Like [Field Winds](field-wind.md), further Jelly Bean stacks do not add up on each other. Instead, they gain their own stacks of percentage.
* The Spoiled Jelly Bean is the only jelly bean to grant a debuff.
