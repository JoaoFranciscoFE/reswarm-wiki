The tables below give the probability of a stat of Single Mitten falling within a more precise range, and being upgraded within a more precise range by a wax point. Note that only stats that have a range of base/upgrade stats are shown.

Due to the size of the tables affecting readability, it has been moved to a separate subpage.

*Results are rounded to the 3rd-5th decimal digits. Note that the precise percentages may be off due to floating point errors.*
