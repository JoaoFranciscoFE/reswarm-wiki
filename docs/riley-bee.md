**Riley Bee** is a Red [Epic bee](bees-epic.md).

Riley Bee's favorite [treat](treats.md) is [Strawberries](strawberry.md).

Riley Bee likes the [Mushroom Field](mushroom-field.md), [Strawberry Field](strawberry-field.md), and [Rose Field](rose-field.md). It dislikes the [Bamboo Field](bamboo-field.md) and the [Pine Tree Forest](pine-tree-forest.md).

## Stats

* Collects 10 [Pollen](pollen.md) in 2 seconds.
* Makes 140 [Honey](honey.md) in 4 seconds.
* +10% [Movespeed](stats.md#Speed), +25% [Energy](energy.md), +4 [Attack](stats.md#Attack), +50% gather speed, +60 [Convert Amount](system-page.md#Convert_Amount).
* 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](gifted-bee.md): +20% Red Field Capacity.

### Abilities

* **[Red Boost](ability-tokens.md#Boost)** Grants x1.2 pollen from red [Flowers](flowers.md) for 15 seconds. Stacks up to 10 times.

## Trivia

* Based on its description, this [bee](bees.md) is the rival and counterpart of [Bucko Bee](bucko-bee.md).
  * Both of them are the leaders of their respective colors.
* Riley Bee, [Rascal Bee](rascal-bee.md), and [Demon Bee](demon-bee.md) all have the same skin colors.
* A giant model of a Riley Bee is on the front of the [Red HQ](red-hq.md).
* [Gifted Riley Bee](gifted-riley-bee.md) is a quest giver represents the gifted version of it in the game.
* The [Riley Guard](riley-guard.md) is based on this bee.
* Riley Bee and [Festive Bee](festive-bee.md) are the only red bees to like three fields.
* Riley Bee, [Bucko Bee](bucko-bee.md), [Crimson Bee](crimson-bee.md) and [Cobalt Bee](cobalt-bee.md) are the only bees whose description has another bee's name in it.
  * Riley Bee is also the only red bee to dislike two fields.
