> *Not to be confused with the [Free Ant Pass Dispenser](free-ant-pass-dispenser.md), which gives ant passes for free with a cooldown.*

The **Ant Pass Dispenser** is a dispenser that is located behind the [Ant Gate](ant-gate.md) and to the right of the [Free Ant Pass Dispenser](free-ant-pass-dispenser.md). It gives an [Ant Pass](ant-pass.md) for 10 [Tickets](ticket.md), which can be used to access the [Ant Challenge](ant-challenge.md). The price does not increase and there is no cooldown on using the dispenser, unlike the Free Ant Pass Dispenser. Players cannot use the dispenser if they already have 10 or more ant passes in their inventory.

## Trivia

* This, the Free Ant Pass Dispenser, the [Robo Pass Dispenser](robo-pass-dispenser.md), and the [Free Robo Pass Dispenser](free-robo-pass-dispenser.md) are the only dispensers that don't have any visible faucets, and instead have a small compartment, like a vending machine that gives out tickets.
* This, the [Royal Jelly Dispenser](royal-jelly-dispenser.md), and the Robo Pass Dispenser are the only dispensers that require tickets to use.
