**Robo Bear** is an NPC released in Beesmas 2022 update. It is located in the 15 Bee Zone, found next to the Red HQ and at the other end of the [Rose Field](rose-field.md). The main feature of Robo Bear is initiating the [Robo Bear Challenge](robo-bear-challenge.md). Before gaining access to Robo Bear, the player will be first given a quest to assemble the missing parts of Robo Bear being Red Drive, White Drive, Blue Drive, and Glitched Drive. The drives can be obtained in the following quests:

* [Science Bear](science-bear.md): Repairing the White Drive
* [Dapper Bear](dapper-bear.md): Bartering for the Blue Drive
* [Panda Bear](panda-bear.md): Ready For the Red Drive?
* [Science Bear](science-bear.md): Corrupting the Glitched Drive

When all requirements are met, the player can then access talking to Robo Bear and initiate the Robo Bear Challenge at the cost of 1 Robo Pass, obtainable through the dispensers in the 30 bee zone.

During Beesmas 2022, players were able to complete the quest by purchasing a white drive in [Bee Bear's Catalog](bee-bear-s-catalog.md), completing Science Bear's Beesmas 2022 quest for a red drive, completing Dapper Bear's Beesmas 2022 quest for a blue drive, and completing [Bee Bear's](bee-bear.md) “Decorating For Drives” quest for a glitched drive.

## Trivia

* Robo Bear is the second NPC in the game that can initiate a challenge, the first being [Stick Bug](stick-bug.md).
* Robo Bear uses the [Robot Animation Pack](https://www.roblox.com/bundles/82/Robot-Animation-Pack).
* Robo Bear is one of the few quest givers who own a shop, the others being [Mother Bear](mother-bear.md), [Dapper Bear](dapper-bear.md), [Gummy Bear](gummy-bear.md), and [Bee Bear](bee-bear.md).
* Robo Bear's original face decal was made on 2019-02-24, eventually releasing on 2022-12-14 for Beesmas.
* Robo Bear is the only bear to publicly have its design changed from before release.
* Robo Bear's Quest, "Repairing Robo Bear" can be instantly completed once redeemed, that is if you already have the [Drives](drives.md).
