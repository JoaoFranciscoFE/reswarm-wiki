# Onett (Developer)

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 220px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">Onett's Roblox avatar.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

*This page is for the developer of the game. For the quest-giving NPC, see [Onett](onett.md). For other contributors to the game, see Bee Swarm Simulator#Contributors.*

**Piercen Harbut**, better known as **[Onett](https://www.roblox.com/users/1912490/profile)** on Roblox, is the owner and programmer of Bee Swarm Simulator. Bee Swarm Simulator is his first and main game on Roblox. He owns the [Bee Swarm Simulator Club](bee-swarm-simulator-club.md) group and [Onett's Testing Group](onett-s-testing-group.md).

Onett runs alternate accounts to handle messages about errors experienced while playing Bee Swarm Simulator. [BeeSwarmBugReport](https://www.roblox.com/users/556387844/profile) handles bug reports and [BeeSwarmSupport](https://www.roblox.com/users/556383852/profile) handles the purchase and saves file reports. Onett also has a secret alternate account named [ltpb2020](https://www.roblox.com/users/1953773238/profile/), which he mainly used to upload decals for the Ready Player Two event. Another account was found by the name [HiddenBeePotential](https://www.roblox.com/users/5098329817/profile) but seems to only be used for testing as of now.

[Onett's Twitter account](https://twitter.com/OnettDev/) can be found by searching @OnettDev.

Onett also has an [Instagram account for the game](https://www.instagram.com/beeswarmsimulator/), a [personal Instagram account](https://www.instagram.com/piercenharbut/), a [Threads account](https://www.threads.com/@piercenharbut), a [YouTube account](https://www.youtube.com/@PiercenHarbut), a [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/OnettBSS) account, a [Reddit account](https://www.reddit.com/u/BeeSwarmDev/s/iutuKGHL2H), and a Discord account (onett1).

Onett is the owner of Bear Bee [LLC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limited_liability_company), which is presumably his avenue for legal work surrounding the game. Bee Swarm Simulator is a registered [tradmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trademark) under the company.[1]

Onett lives in the CST timezone, as confirmed on Discord.

## Trivia

* Onett had a cat named Sam[2] in real life, which was the inspiration for [Tabby Bee](tabby-bee.md). It passed away later in 2018.[3]
* Onett's favorite bees are [Basic Bee](basic-bee.md),[4] [Rascal Bee](rascal-bee.md),[5] [Tabby Bee](tabby-bee.md),[5] and [Brave Bee](brave-bee.md).[6]
* Onett's favorite ability is Triangulate, since he thinks it's satisfying.
* Onett's username is a reference to a city in the Nintendo game [EarthBound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EarthBound).[7]
* Onett owns a special [tool](tools.md) called the Honey Hammer.
* Onett's favorite [mob](mobs.md) is the [Rhino Beetle](rhino-beetle.md).[8]
* Onett's favorite in-game music is [bpatrol](music.md).[9]
* Occasionally, Onett summons mini events. They have various aliases which replace the username, such as "[Alias] has summoned (a) [mini event]".
* Onett's birthday is on 1989-12-24, and he is currently 36 years old.
* Onett was interviewed by Thundermaker300 in November 2018 as part of the Roblox Wiki's Developer Connections project. See [here](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/User_blog:RBLXDeveloperInterviews/Onett:_Bee_Swarm_Simulator) to read the interview.
