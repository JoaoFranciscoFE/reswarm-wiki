# Blueberry

Blueberry

*"Increases your bond with a bee by 25."*

A **Blueberry** is a type of [treat](treats.md) that can be fed to a [bee](bees.md) to raise its bond by 25. However, when fed to a [Bumble](bumble-bee.md), [Cool](cool-bee.md), [Bubble](bubble-bee.md), [Frosty](frosty-bee.md), [Bucko](bucko-bee.md), [Diamond](diamond-bee.md), [Music](music-bee.md), [Tadpole](tadpole-bee.md), or [Buoyant](buoyant-bee.md) Bee, the amount of [bond](bond.md) they gain is doubled. These bees also have a small chance of becoming gifted when fed blueberries, though this only happens with a 0.0125% (1 in 8,000) chance for [Rare bees](bees-rare.md), 0.01% (1 in 10,000) chance for [Epic bees](bees-epic.md), 0.0083% (1 in 12,000) chance for [Common](bees-common.md) and [Legendary bees](bees-legendary.md), and 0.00417% (1 in 24,000) chance for [Mythic bees](bees-mythic.md) per blueberry. Feeding a radioactive bee a blueberry has a 0.004% (1 in 25,000) to [mutate](mutation.md) a bee.

## Ways to Obtain

* From using the [Blueberry Dispenser](blueberry-dispenser.md) inside the [Blue HQ](blue-hq.md). The player will get one blueberry per blue bee in their hive per use.
* As a reward from the [Ant Challenge](ant-challenge.md).
* Redeeming certain valid [codes](codes.md):
  * 1MLikes (Gives 1 blueberry + other stuff).
  * 15MMembers (Gives 15 blueberries + other stuff).
* As a reward from [memory matches](memory-match.md).

### Drops

* Farming in the [Blue Flower Field](blue-flower-field.md), [Bamboo Field](bamboo-field.md), [Cactus Field](cactus-field.md), and the [Pine Tree Forest](pine-tree-forest.md).
  * Harvesting [leaves](leaves.md) in these fields may also yield blueberries.
* As a drop from [Rhino Beetles](rhino-beetle.md), [Mantises](mantis.md), and [King Beetle](king-beetle.md).
* As a drop from a [Festive Gift](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Gift) or [Festive Mark](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Mark).
* As a gift from your [Cub Buddy](cub-buddy.md).
* As a reward for completely growing any [sprout](sprout.md) except for Moon, Gummy, and Debug sprouts.
  * Planting a sprout in the Blue Flower Field, Bamboo Field, Pine Tree Forest, or Stump Field yields 6x more blueberries than other fields.
* Harvesting [sparkles](sparkles.md) (Higher chances in blue fields).
* As a drop from [Puffshrooms](puffshroom.md).
* As a drop from planters in blue fields.
  * Non-blue fields that contain a significant portion of blue flowers can drop Blueberries (Clover Field, Cactus Field, Mountain Top Field).
  * When in blue fields, [Paper Planter](paper-planter.md) drops multiple Blueberry tokens in increments of 1, 3, 5, and 10.

### Quests

* Gifted Bucko Bee may reward the player blueberries as a special reward.
* Spirit Bear gives blueberries on the following quest:
  * Out-Questing Questions gives 1 blueberry.
