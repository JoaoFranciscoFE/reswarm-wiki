# Gingerbread Bear

Gingerbread Bear

*"A cookie shaped like a bear! Gives 250 bond to the fed bee and has a 1% chance to turn that bee Gifted."*

*This article is about the item. For the similarly named Gingerbread Cub skin, see [Cub Buddy](cub-buddy.md)*.

*Not to be confused with an [Aged Gingerbread Bear](aged-gingerbread-bear.md).*

A **Gingerbread Bear** is a unique type of [treat](treats.md) that was added in the [2020-12-25 update](updates.md#2020-12-25). It is obtainable by checking [Mother Bear's](mother-bear.md) [Gingerbread House](gingerbread-house.md), defeating certain [mobs](mobs.md) and bosses, opening [Gift Boxes](gift-boxes.md), completing [Bee Bear's](bee-bear.md) quests, giving [Presents](present.md) to certain [NPCs](quest-givers.md) around the map, completing NPCs Beesmas quests, using [Onett's Lid Art](onett-s-lid-art.md), and buying certain [Robux Packs](robux-shop.md).

## Overview

Gingerbread bears have 2 functions. The player could save them up and spend it on [Bee Bear's Catalog](bee-bear-s-catalog.md) during the Beesmas 2020, 2021, 2022, 2024 and 2025 events, or feed them to [bees](bees.md). In addition to granting [bond](bond.md), it has a 1% chance to make them [gifted](gifted-bee.md). They can also be saved up until the next Beesmas event, where they turn into [Aged Gingerbread Bears](aged-gingerbread-bear.md).

The formula of turning a bee gifted: \((1-0.99^{x})\times 100\%\) (𝑥 is the number of the **Gingerbread Bears** fed to a bee)

## Ways to Obtain

* As a reward from Polar Bear's Beesmas 2025 quest.
* Using [Mother Bear's](mother-bear.md) [Gingerbread House](gingerbread-house.md) (1 for every 2 hours of playtime).
* As a prize from the [Winter Memory Match](memory-match.md#Winter_Memory_Match).
* Finding it on the obby in the [Hive Hub](hive-hub.md).
* Finding it behind the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md) Machine.
* Finding it in the [Beesmas Tree](beesmas-tree.md) near the [20 bee zone](ant-gate.md).

### Drops

* As a drop from a [Festive Sprout](sprout.md).
* Chance from getting combos with [Puppy Bee's](puppy-bee.md) [Beequip](beequip.md) ability, [Reindeer Fetch](ability-tokens.md#Reindeer_Fetch) (limit 1 per day).
* As a rare drop from a [Festive Mark](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Mark).
* As a drop from a [Festive Planter](festive-planter.md).
* As a drop from [Onett's Lid Art](onett-s-lid-art.md) (Can be used every 8 hours).
* As a common drop from [Aphids](aphid.md) (except for diamond aphid), [Stump Snail](stump-snail.md), [Coconut Crab](coconut-crab.md), [King Beetle](king-beetle.md), and [Tunnel Bear](tunnel-bear.md).
* As a rare drop from [Scorpions](scorpion.md), [Ladybugs](ladybug.md), [Werewolves](werewolf.md), and [Festive Nymphs](festive-nymph.md).

## Trivia

* **Gingerbread Bears**, along with [Snowflakes](snowflake.md), are the only currencies that can be used in Bee Bear's Catalog.
  * However, unlike [Snowflakes](snowflake.md), **Gingerbread Bears** are not removed from the player's inventory when Beesmas ends and are still obtainable and usable.
* This, along with the [Star Treat](star-treat.md) and [Aged Gingerbread Bears](aged-gingerbread-bear.md), are the only items in the game that can turn [Event bees](bees-event.md) gifted.
* This and the [Festive Bean](festive-bean.md) are the only event items that are infinitely renewable outside of an event.
* Feeding 100 **Gingerbread Bears** to a bee does not guarantee to turn it gifted. The chance of it becoming gifted is around 63%.
