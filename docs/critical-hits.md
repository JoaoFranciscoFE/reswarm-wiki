Their frequency is increased with higher [Critical Chance](system-page.md#Critical_Chance), and their strength is increased by greater [Critical Power](system-page.md#Critical_Power). ## Critical Chance

**Critical Chance** is the chance of a hit being critical hit. The player can see how much their Critical Chance is by looking for the Critical Chance stat in the System Page. Critical Chance can be improved by a variety of ways such as [ability tokens](ability-tokens.md), guards, and inventory items.

### [Bees](bees.md)

#### Tokens

* [Focus](ability-tokens.md#Focus) gives +3% Critical Chance per token and caps at +30% Critical Chance from 10 tokens.
* [Precise Mark](ability-tokens.md#Target_Practice) from the [Target Practice](ability-tokens.md#Target_Practice) ability gives +7% Critical Chance per token and caps at +21% Critical Chance from three Marks.
* The [Tabby Blessing](buffs-debuffs.md#From_Ability_Tokens) buff, granted from collecting [Tabby Love](ability-tokens.md#Tabby_Love) while having maximum Tabby Love stacks, grants +1% Critical Chance.

#### [Gifted Hive Bonuses](gifted-bee.md#List_of_Hive_Bonuses)

* Gifted [Commander Bee](commander-bee.md) gives +3% Critical Chance.

#### [Beequips](beequip.md)

* Individual Bee:
  * A [Bottle Cap](bottle-cap.md) gives from +1% Critical Chance to +2% Critical Chance. Using [waxes](waxes.md) can increase the amount up to +12% Critical Chance.
  * A [Charm Bracelet](charm-bracelet.md) gives from +1% Critical Chance to +14% Critical Chance.
  * A [Bang Snap](bang-snap.md) gives from +1% Critical Chance to +3% Critical Chance. Using waxes can increase the amount up to +23% Critical Chance.
  * [Pink Shades](pink-shades.md) can give from +22% Critical Chance to +26% Critical Chance. Using waxes can increase the amount up to +50% Critical Chance.
  * A [Beesmas Top](beesmas-top.md) gives from +5% Critical Chance to +10% Critical Chance. Using waxes can increase the amount up to +20% Critical Chance.
  * A [Beesmas Tree Hat](beesmas-tree-hat.md) gives from +2% Critical Chance to +5% Critical Chance. Using waxes can increase the amount up to +10% Critical Chance.
  * A [Snow Tiara](snow-tiara.md) gives +1% Critical Chance.
  * A [Toy Drum](toy-drum.md) gives +1% Critical Chance.

### Guards

* The [Looker Guard](looker-guard.md) gives +1% Critical Chance.
* The [Elite Red Guard](elite-red-guard.md) gives +5% Critical Chance.
* The [Riley Guard](riley-guard.md) gives +8% Critical Chance.
* The [Crimson Guard](crimson-guard.md) gives +10% Critical Chance.

### [Amulets](amulet.md)

* [Star Amulets](star-amulet.md):
  * The Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Diamond Star Amulet can give from +1% Critical Chance up to +5% Critical Chance.
  * The Supreme Star Amulet can give from +1% Critical Chance up to +7% Critical Chance.
* [Ant Amulets](ant-amulet.md):
  * The Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, and Supreme Ant Amulet can give up to +3% Critical Chance.

### [Buffs](buffs-debuffs.md)

* Using a [Tropical Drink](tropical-drink.md) gives +5% Critical Chance.
* Using a [Super Smoothie](super-smoothie.md) gives +6% Critical Chance. The buff can also be given from certain codes.
* Collecting [green jelly beans](jelly-beans.md) gives +3% Critical Chance per jelly bean and caps and +5% Critical Chance from 3 jelly beans.
* Collecting green petals from blooms gives 1% Critical Chance

### [Robo Bear Challenge](robo-bear-challenge.md)

There are several upgrades that can be bought with [Cogs](cog.md) during the Robo Bear Challenge which can upgrade Critical Chance.

* Rare Upgrades:
  * Commit: This upgrade starts at +5% Critical Chance and maxes at 2 upgrades with +10% Critical Chance.
  * Virus: This upgrade starts at +1% Critical Chance and maxes at 3 upgrades with +3% Critical Chance.
* Epic Upgrades:
  * RGB: This upgrade gives 1% Critical Chance.
* Legendary Upgrades:
  * Pseudo-RNG: This upgrade gives 3% Critical Chance.

#### Debuffs

There are certain upgrades that can be bought with cogs during the Robo Bear Challenge which can downgrade Critical Chance.

* Rare Upgrades:
  * SSD: Blue: This upgrade starts at -3% Critical Chance and maxes at 3 upgrades with -6% Critical Chance.
* Epic Upgrades:
  * FOV: This upgrade gives -2% Critical Chance.
  * Normalize: This upgrade gives x0 Critical Chance.

## Critical Power

**Critical Power** is how strong a critical hit can be. The player can see how much their Critical Power is by looking for the Critical Power stat in the System Page. Critical Power can be improved by a variety of ways such as ability tokens, guards, and beequips.

  * [Pink Shades](pink-shades.md) gives from +29% Critical Power to +49% Critical Power.

There are several upgrades that can be bought with [Cogs](cog.md) during the Robo Bear Challenge which can upgrade Critical Power.

## Super-Crit

When performing a critical hit, there is a chance to perform a **Super-Crit** (purple version of a critical hit) which doubles the pollen collection while also instantly converting the pollen collected, while for the attack damage is increased by roughly 33% of the player's Super-Crit Power. The frequency for a Super-Crit is increased with higher Super-Crit Chance, and their strength is increased by greater Super-Crit Power.

### Super-Crit Chance

**Super-Crit Chance** is how often a critical hit will be a Super-Crit. The player can see how much their Super-Crit Chance is by looking for the Super-Crit Chance stat in the System Page. Super-Crit Chance can be improved by several ways.

#### Bees

##### Tokens

* The [Precision](buffs-debuffs.md#Precision) buff granted from the [Target Practice](ability-tokens.md#Target_Practice) ability gives +2% per token and caps at +20% from ten tokens.
* The [Precise Mark](buffs-debuffs.md#Precise_Mark) buff from the Target Practice ability gives +7% Super-Crit Chance per token and caps at +21% Super-Crit Chance from three tokens.
* The [Tabby Blessing+](buffs-debuffs.md#From_Ability_Tokens) buff, granted from collecting [Tabby Love](ability-tokens.md#Tabby_Love) from a [Gifted](gifted-bee.md) [Tabby Bee](tabby-bee.md) while having maximum Tabby Love stacks, grants +1% Super-Crit Chance.

##### Gifted Hive Bonuses

* Gifted [Precise Bee](precise-bee.md) gives +3% Super-Crit Chance.

##### Beequips

* Individual Bee:
  * [Pink Shades](pink-shades.md) can give from +10% Super-Crit Chance to +15% Super-Crit Chance. Using waxes can increase it to +40% Super-Crit Chance.
* Hive Bonus:
  * [Pink Shades](pink-shades.md) can give +1% Super-Crit Chance as a hive bonus.

### Buffs

* Using a [Super Smoothie](super-smoothie.md) gives +1% Super-Crit Chance. The buff can also be given from certain codes.
* Collecting Violet Petals from blooms give 5% Super-Crit Chance.

### Robo Bear Challenge

* Legendary Upgrades:
  * Bruteforce: This upgrade gives +6% Super-Crit Chance.

### Super-Crit Power

**Super-Crit Power** is how strong a Super-Crit will be. The player can see how much their Super-Crit Power is by looking for the Super-Crit Power stat in the System Page. Super-Crit Power can be improved by several ways.

#### Bees

##### Beequips

  * [Pink Shades](pink-shades.md) from +2% Super-Crit Power to +30% Super-Crit Power from waxing.
  * A [Demon Talisman](demon-talisman.md) can give up to 10% Super-Crit Power as a hive bonus from waxing.

### Amulets

* [Cog Amulets](cog-amulet.md):
  * The Diamond Cog Amulet can give from +10% Super-Crit Power up to +12% Super-Crit Power.
  * The Supreme Cog Amulet can give from +15% Super-Crit Power up to +25% Super-Crit Power.

### Buffs

#### Stickers
