The **Kazoo** is a [level](bond.md) 9 [Beequip](beequip.md). It has an equip limit of one. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to [Brave](brave-bee.md), [Bumble](bumble-bee.md), [Bubble](bubble-bee.md), [Riley](riley-bee.md), [Shocked](shocked-bee.md) and [Buoyant](buoyant-bee.md) [bees](bees.md).

## Trivia

* The Kazoo was released in the [2022-04-01 update](updates.md).
* This is one of the first non-Beesmas Beequips to be added into the game.
* This is one of three instrumental Beequips.
  * The other two are [Toy Drum](toy-drum.md) and [Toy Horn](toy-horn.md).
* This is one of eight Beequips to give a negative stat.
  * The other seven are [Whistle](whistle.md), [Beesmas Top](beesmas-top.md), [Toy Horn](toy-horn.md), [Toy Drum](toy-drum.md), [Demon Talisman](demon-talisman.md), [Pink Eraser](pink-eraser.md), and [Lump Of Coal](lump-of-coal.md).
