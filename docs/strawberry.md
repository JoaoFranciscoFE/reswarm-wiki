# Strawberry

Strawberry

*"Increases your bond with a bee by 25."*

A **Strawberry** is a type of [treat](treats.md) that can be fed to a [bee](bees.md) to raise its bond by 25. However, when fed to a [Rascal](rascal-bee.md), [Rad](rad-bee.md), [Rage](rage-bee.md), [Riley](riley-bee.md), [Baby](baby-bee.md), [Stubborn](stubborn-bee.md), [Shy](shy-bee.md), or [Spicy](spicy-bee.md) Bee, the amount of [bond](bond.md) they gain is doubled. These bees also have a small chance of becoming gifted when fed strawberries, though this only happens with a 0.0125% (1 in 8,000) chance for [Rare bees](bees-rare.md), 0.01% (1 in 10,000) chance for [Epic bees](bees-epic.md), 0.0083% (1 in 12,000) chance for [Common](bees-common.md) and [Legendary bees](bees-legendary.md), and 0.00417% (1 in 24,000) chance for [Mythic bees](bees-mythic.md) per strawberry. Feeding a radioactive bee a strawberry has a 0.004% (1 in 25,000) to [mutate](mutation.md) a bee.

## Ways to Obtain

* There is a strawberry token hidden behind the largest strawberry in the corner of [Strawberry Field](strawberry-field.md).
* From using the [Strawberry Dispenser](strawberry-dispenser.md) near the [Red HQ](red-hq.md). The player will get one strawberry per red bee in their hive per use.
* As a reward from the [Ant Challenge](ant-challenge.md).
* Redeeming certain valid [codes](codes.md):
  * 15MMembers (Gives 15 strawberries + other stuff).
* As a reward from [memory matches](memory-match.md).
* As a stack reward for adding certain [stickers](sticker.md) to the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md):
  * [Pink Cupcake Sticker](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) rewards 50 strawberries.
  * [Pale Heart Sticker](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) rewards 50 strawberries.
* Digging in to the [Beesmas Feast](beesmas-feast.md).

### Drops

* Farming in the [Mushroom Field](mushroom-field.md), [Strawberry Field](strawberry-field.md), [Rose Field](rose-field.md), [Cactus Field](cactus-field.md), and the [Pepper Patch](pepper-patch.md).
  * Harvesting [Leaves](leaves.md) in these fields may also yield strawberries.
* As a drop from [Ladybugs](ladybug.md), [Scorpions](scorpion.md), and [King Beetle](king-beetle.md).
* As a drop from a [Festive Gift](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Gift) or [Festive Mark](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Mark).
* As a gift from a [cub buddy](cub-buddy.md).
* As a reward for completely growing any kind of [Sprout](sprout.md) except for Moon, Gummy, and Debug sprouts.
  * Planting a sprout in the [Mushroom Field](mushroom-field.md), [Rose Field](rose-field.md), and [Pepper Patch](pepper-patch.md) yields 4x more strawberries than other fields. Planting a sprout in the [Strawberry Field](strawberry-field.md) yields 8x more strawberries than other fields.
* Harvesting [Sparkles](sparkles.md) (higher chances in red fields).
* As a drop from [Puffshrooms](puffshroom.md) in red fields or fields with high amounts of red flowers ([Clover Field](clover-field.md), [Cactus Field](cactus-field.md), and [Mountain Top Field](mountain-top-field.md)).
* As a drop from planters (besides [Tacky Planter](tacky-planter.md), [Blue Clay Planter](blue-clay-planter.md) and [The Planter Of Plenty](the-planter-of-plenty.md)), most likely in red fields.
  * The [Red Clay Planter](red-clay-planter.md) always drops strawberries upon harvest at full growth.
