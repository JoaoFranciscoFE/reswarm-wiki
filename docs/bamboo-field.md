# Bamboo Field

39 × 18 flowers

FLOWER COUNT

LIKE

DISLIKE

The **Bamboo Field** is a field located past the [Basic Bee Gate](basic-bee-gate.md), to the right of the [Spider Field](spider-field.md), and 2 fields away from the [Strawberry Field](strawberry-field.md). It is protected by two [Rhino Beetles](rhino-beetle.md), one being level 2 and the other level 3.

This is an 18×39 field, containing 702 [flowers](flowers.md). The flowers are blue and white, in sizes of single, double, and triple.

This field grants Comforting [Nectar](nectar.md) when a [planter](planter.md) is planted here.

This field can spawn [honey](honey.md), [blueberry](blueberry.md), [ticket](ticket.md), [blue boost](ability-tokens.md#Boost), [magic bean](magic-bean.md), and [blue extract](blue-extract.md) tokens when collecting [pollen](pollen.md).

[Bees](bees.md) that like the Bamboo Field are [Cool Bee](cool-bee.md), [Bucko Bee](bucko-bee.md), [Buoyant Bee](buoyant-bee.md), [Carpenter Bee](carpenter-bee.md), and [Ninja Bee](ninja-bee.md). Bees that dislike the Bamboo Field are [Riley Bee](riley-bee.md), [Precise Bee](precise-bee.md), & [Windy Bee](windy-bee.md).

## Obtaining Boosts

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 75px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The Bamboo Field Boost icon.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

* Using the [Blue Field Booster](blue-field-booster.md) may give a Bamboo [Field Boost](field-boost.md). When it does, it will give +100% Bamboo Field pollen for 15 minutes.
* Using [Glitter](glitter.md) will add 100% Bamboo Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if the boost isn't already maxed out.
* Using [Field dice](field-dice.md), [Smooth Dice](smooth-dice.md), or [Loaded Dice](loaded-dice.md) may add +100% to 300% of Bamboo Field pollen for 15 minutes based on what type of dice is used, and also based on the current stack before the boost is added.
* Bamboo [Field Winds](field-wind.md) can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md) or [codes](codes.md). They have a base of +20% instant Bamboo Field conversion and give +5% instant Bamboo Field conversion and +15% Bamboo Field pollen for each stack. Bamboo Field Winds can stack up to a total of 13 times.
* It can be boosted by the [King Beetle Amulet](king-beetle-amulet.md). If it has a Bamboo Field Boost, it can boost the field by +1% to +100%.

### The methods below are no longer possible.

* Giving a present to certain NPCs (2018):
  * [Panda Bear](panda-bear.md) gave Bamboo Field Boost x4 and other [items](items.md).
  * [Gifted Bucko Bee](gifted-bucko-bee.md) gave Bamboo Field Boost x3 and other boosts and items.
  * [Onett](onett.md) gave Bamboo Field Boost x1 and other boosts and items.
  * Giving a present to Panda Bear during Beesmas 2020 gave Bamboo Boost x3 and other items.
  * Panda Bear gave Bamboo Field Boost x3 and other [items](items.md) during Beesmas 2021.
* Redeeming certain expired codes:
  * PandaPower (Gave Bamboo Field Boost x1 + other stuff).
  * Teespring (Gives Bamboo Field Boost x3 + Bamboo Field Winds x3 + other stuff).
  * 500mil (Gives Bamboo Field Boost x3 + other stuff).
  * 1MLikes (Gives Bamboo Field Boost x1 + other stuff).
  * WeekExtension (Gives Bamboo Field Boost x3 + Bamboo Field Winds x10 + other stuff).
  * Friday (Gave Bamboo Field Boost x5 + other stuff).
  * RobziRobot (Gave Bamboo Field Boost x4 + other stuff).
  * 1mFavorites (Gave Bamboo Field Boost x1 + other stuff).
  * ExoExtract (Gave Bamboo Field Boost x3 + other stuff).
  * August (Gave Bamboo Field Boost x3 + other stuff).
  * FestiveFrogs (Gave Bamboo Field Boost x3 and Bamboo Field Winds x3 + other stuff).
  * FestiveFinale (Gave Bamboo Field Boost x3 and Bamboo Field Winds x2 + other stuff).
  * BlackFriday (Activated Bamboo Field Code Buff + other stuff).
* Purchasing a Bamboo Field [Market Boost](buffs-debuffs.md#From_Areas) from the [Boost Market](boost-market.md) will give x1.5 Bamboo [Field Capacity](field-capacity.md), x1.25 Bamboo Field Pollen, x1.1 [Blue Pollen](system-page.md#Blue_Pollen), and x1.25 [Convert Rate At Hive](system-page.md#Hive_Convert_Rate).

## Trivia

* This is one of six fields that [fireflies](fireflies.md) can travel to, with the others being [Cactus Field](cactus-field.md), [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md), [Rose Field](rose-field.md), Spider Field, and Strawberry Field.
* The player can find 2 [royal jellies](royal-jelly.md) near the Bamboo Field, one in a nook under the ramp to the [Brave Bee Gate](brave-bee-gate.md), and the other at the end of an [obstacle course](obstacle-courses.md#Bamboo_Obby), starting in between this field and the Spider Field.
* The bamboo stems have claw marks on them, presumably from Panda Bear.
* If a player stands on the corner of the Bamboo Field closest to the Spider Field while a [Snowbear](snowbear.md) is summoned, the Snowbear can throw snowballs and damage the player.
* The three bees that dislike the Bamboo Field also have another disliked field (Riley Bee and Precise Bee; Pine Tree Forest, Windy Bee; Strawberry Field)
