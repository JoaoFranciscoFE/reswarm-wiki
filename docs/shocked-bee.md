**Shocked Bee** is a Colorless [Epic bee](bees-epic.md).

Shocked Bee's favorite treat is [Pineapples](pineapple.md).

Shocked Bee likes the [Spider Field](spider-field.md) and the [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md). It dislikes the [Mushroom Field](mushroom-field.md).

## Stats

* Collects 10 [Pollen](pollen.md) in 4 seconds.
* Makes 80 [Honey](honey.md) in 2 seconds.
* +40% [Movespeed](stats.md#Speed), +50% [Convert Speed](system-page.md#Convert_Rate), +1 [Attack](stats.md#Attack), -50% [Sleep Time](stats.md#Energy).
* 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](gifted-bee.md): x1.1 [White Pollen](system-page.md#White_Pollen).

### Abilities

* **[[Haste]](ability-tokens.md#Haste)** Grants +10% [Player Movespeed](system-page.md#Movespeed) for 20 seconds. Stacks up to 10 times.
* **[[Token Link]](ability-tokens.md#Token_Link)** Collects all other [Ability Tokens](ability-tokens.md), granting 25 [Honey](honey.md) (+10 per lvl) per token collected.

## Trivia

* Shocked Bee, [Hasty Bee](hasty-bee.md), [Shy Bee](shy-bee.md), and [Rascal Bee](rascal-bee.md) are the only [bees](bees.md) in the game that has a spelling or grammar mistake in their description.
* Shocked Bee requires less sleep than other bees, the only bee to have this trait.
* Shocked Bee and [Music Bee](music-bee.md) have the same skin pattern.
* Shocked Bee is the fastest bee to wake up from sleeping.
* Shocked Bee used to have the [Buzz Bomb ability](ability-tokens.md#Bomb), but it was replaced by Token Link.
* This is the only gifted bee to not have tinted wings.
