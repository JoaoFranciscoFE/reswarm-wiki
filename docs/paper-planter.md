The **Paper Planter** is a single-use [planter](planter.md) added in the 2021-12-26 update. Alone, it grows in 1 hour and stores 50,000 pollen. It can be bought in the [Pro Shop](pro-shop.md) for 25,000 [Honey](honey.md) and 1 [Sunflower Seed](sunflower-seed.md). A player can store up to 100 Paper Planters at a time, and up to 3 planters of any kind can be planted at once.

## Growth, pollen and nectar

* Grows in 1 hour of playtime.
* [Glitter](glitter.md) used in its field speeds it up: x1.5 for one, rising in halving steps up to x2 at 6 stacks. The stacks stay on the planter until it is harvested.
* Holds 50,000 [pollen](pollen.md) at full growth. A harvest gives capacity × growth × pollen bonus. It has no pollen bonus.
* [Nectar](nectar.md) multipliers: x0.75 Satisfying, x0.75 Comforting, x0.75 Invigorating, x0.75 Motivating, x0.75 Refreshing. On harvest it gives one Nectar type from its field, lasting grow time × growth × multiplier × 3 seconds.
* Up to 3 bees can sip from it at once.

## Puffshrooms

When it reaches 95% growth, it has a 10% chance to smoke and spawn 3 [Puffshrooms](puffshroom.md) (one at the planter and two nearby) at level 1.

They are always Common.

## Drops

A full harvest gives 10 item tokens. Harvesting early gives fewer, in proportion to growth, but always at least 1. Tokens are filled in this order: guaranteed items (full harvests only), then field rules, then random picks from the pool below.

### Field rules

* Full harvest in the [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md): +1 Enzymes. In the [Sunflower Field](sunflower-field.md): +1 Oil.
* In the [Coconut Field](coconut-field.md) the pool also gets Coconuts (1 / 1 / 3, weight 30).

## Trivia

* This is the cheapest and fastest-growing planter in the game.
* It is one of the many planters to not be sold in [Dapper Bear's Shop](dapper-bear-s-shop.md), the others being the [Red Clay Planter](red-clay-planter.md), [Blue Clay Planter](blue-clay-planter.md), [Festive Planter](festive-planter.md), [Ticket Planter](ticket-planter.md), [Sticker Planter](sticker-planter.md), [Petal Planter](petal-planter.md), [Hydroponic Planter](hydroponic-planter.md) and [Heat-Treated Planter](heat-treated-planter.md).
* This is the cheapest item in the [Pro Shop](pro-shop.md) in terms of honey, only costing 25,000 honey.
* This is the only item in the Pro Shop that is cheaper than an item in the [Noob Shop](noob-shop.md), being the [Helmet](helmet.md).
* This, the Ticket Planter and Sticker Planter the are the only one-time use planters that are renewable.
* This is the only planter in the game that stores more pollen than the honey it costs.
* It is one of the four planters that are one-time use. The remaining three are the [Sticker Planter](sticker-planter.md), [Ticket Planter](ticket-planter.md) and the [Festive Planter](festive-planter.md).
  * It is also the only one-time use planter that can be bought from a store.
* This is also the only planter that *reduces* nectar gained, multiplying all nectar by 0.75x. All other planters either boost nectar gain (although some of them only boost certain types) or do not change it at all.
