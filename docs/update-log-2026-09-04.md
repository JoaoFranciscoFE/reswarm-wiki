# Early rebirth nerfs

**Released:** September 4, 2026

Two nerfs to stop players almost instantly skipping early rebirths.

## Changes

* Nerfed Festive Gift honey.
* You must deal at least 5% of a [Bloom](bloom.md)'s damage to get honey from it.
