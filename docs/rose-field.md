# Rose Field

Rose Field

Red

BEE PREREQUISITES

15 bees

Motivating Nectar

FIELD DIMENSIONS

31 × 20 flowers

FLOWER COUNT

620

BEE PREFERENCES

LIKE

DISLIKE

The **Rose Field** is one of the four [fields](fields.md) located past the [Honey Bee Gate](honey-bee-gate.md). This field can be found near the [Red HQ](red-hq.md). It is protected by two [scorpions](scorpion.md), one level 5, and the other level 6.

This field can spawn [honey](honey.md), [strawberry](strawberry.md), [red boost](ability-tokens.md#Boost), [red extract](red-extract.md), [bitterberry](bitterberry.md), and [magic bean](magic-bean.md) tokens when collecting [pollen](pollen.md).

[Bees](bees.md) that like the Rose Field are [Rad Bee](rad-bee.md), [Rascal Bee](rascal-bee.md), [Rage Bee](rage-bee.md), [Riley Bee](riley-bee.md), [Precise Bee](precise-bee.md), [Crimson Bee](crimson-bee.md), and [Vicious Bee](vicious-bee.md). Bees that dislike the Rose Field are [Puppy Bee](puppy-bee.md), [Stubborn Bee](stubborn-bee.md), [Bucko Bee](bucko-bee.md), [Demo Bee](demo-bee.md), [Baby Bee](baby-bee.md), and [Diamond Bee](diamond-bee.md).

This field grants Motivating [Nectar](nectar.md) when a [planter](planter.md) is planted here.

A level 5-7 [Rogue Vicious Bee](rogue-vicious-bee.md) has a chance of spawning here.

This is a 20×31 field, containing 620 [flowers](flowers.md). The flowers are red and white, in sizes of single, double, and triple.

## Obtaining Boosts

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 75px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The Rose Field Boost icon.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

* Using the [Red Field Booster](red-field-booster.md) may give a Rose [Field Boost](field-boost.md). When it does, it will give +100% Rose Field pollen for 15 minutes.
* Using [glitter](glitter.md) will add 100% Rose Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if the boost isn't already maxed out.
* Using [field dice](field-dice.md), [smooth dice](smooth-dice.md), or [loaded dice](loaded-dice.md) may add +100% - 300% Rose Field pollen for 15 minutes based on the type used and based on its current stack before getting another one.
* Rose [Field Winds](field-wind.md) can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md) or [codes](codes.md). They have a base of +20% instant Rose Field conversion, and give +5% instant Rose Field conversion and +15% Rose Field pollen for each stack. Rose Field Winds can stack up to a total of 13 times.

### The methods below are no longer possible.

* Giving a [present](present.md) to [Onett](onett.md) gave Rose Field Boost x1 and other boosts and [items](items.md) and giving [Gifted Riley Bee](gifted-riley-bee.md) a present gave Rose Field Boost x3 and other boosts and items during Beesmas 2021.
* Redeeming certain expired codes.
  * LateNightGumdrops (Gave Rose Field Boost x2 + other stuff).\
  * Discord100k (Gives Rose Field Boost x3 + other stuff).
  * CarmenSanDiego (Activates Rose Field Code Buff + other stuff).
  * 1MLikes (Gives a x1 Field Boost for all Fields + other stuff).
  * PlushFriday (Gives Rose Field Code Buff + other stuff).
  * Afternoon (Gave Rose Field Boost x3 + other stuff).
  * GummyBoost (Gave Rose Field Boost x3 + other stuff).
  * Reboot (Gave Rose Field Boost x3 + other stuff).
  * TeraBriteFight (Gave Rose Field Boost x3 + other stuff).
  * ThatGuyGift (Gave Rose Field Boost x2 + other stuff).
  * TeraTreasure (Gave Rose Field Boost x2 + other stuff).
  * BeeMine (Gave Rose Field Boost x3 + other stuff).
  * 300mVisits (Gave Rose Field Boost x2 + other stuff).
  * August (Gave Rose Field Boost x3 + other stuff).
  * Valentine (Gave Rose Field Boost x3 and Rose Field Capacity x3 + other stuff).
  * RebootFriday (Gave Rose Field Code Boost + other stuff).
  * 5mMembers (Gave Rose Field Boost x3 and Rose Field Capacity x3 + other stuff).
* Purchasing a Rose Field [Market Boost](buffs-debuffs.md#From_Areas) from the [Boost Market](boost-market.md) gave 1.25x Rose Field Pollen along with x1.5 Rose [Field Capacity](field-capacity.md), +10% [Bee Attack](system-page.md#Bee_Attack), and +50 [Convert Amount](system-page.md#Convert_Amount).

## Trivia

* This is one of six fields that [fireflies](fireflies.md) travel to, with the others being [Bamboo Field](bamboo-field.md), [Cactus Field](cactus-field.md), [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md), [Spider Field](spider-field.md), and [Strawberry Field](strawberry-field.md).
* Vicious Bee likes this field most likely because roses commonly have thorns on their stems.
  * It is also the only blue bee to like a field of the opposite color. The only other bee to share this trait is [Festive Bee](festive-bee.md) and [Pine Tree Forest](pine-tree-forest.md).
* This field was one of the fields which has a small section outside of the map.
* There is a [royal jelly](royal-jelly.md) token next to this field in a small ledge, which can be accessed by walking a small path along the [Badge Bearer's Guild](badge-bearer-s-guild.md) wall.
* This is the only field past the Honey Bee Gate to not be defended by the [Werewolf](werewolf.md).
* After the [2019-09-28 update](updates.md#2019-09-28), treat tokens could naturally spawn in this field.
* This is the only field to be at a lower elevation than the gate leading to it.
* If a Rogue Vicious Bee spawns in this field, it can attack players inside the Red HQ with its spikes.
* Stepping in the row of flowers closest to [Robo Bear](robo-bear.md) does not spawn the Scorpions.
