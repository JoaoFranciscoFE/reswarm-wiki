# Painter Bee

**Painter Bee** is an [Event bee](bees-event.md) introduced in the [Painter Bee event](painter-bee-event.md). It has a [Gifted](gifted-bee.md) variant.

Painter Bee paints the flowers it works on. Both of its abilities and its passive also pollinate the flowers they touch.

## Abilities

### Color Splat

Paints and [pollinates](pollination.md) flowers in the field.

### Painter's Haze

A haze that paints and pollinates flowers in the field.

Only one Painter's Haze can be active in a field at a time. If another Painter Bee would start a second haze in the same field, it uses Color Splat instead for 1 minute afterwards.

## Passive: Paint Residue

Every flower Painter Bee gathers from gets painted and pollinated.

If the flower it targets is already the same colour and at maximum tier, the paint is not wasted: it repaints a random flower elsewhere in the field instead.

## How to obtain

* Hatching a Painter Bee egg. The Rebirth 35 reward is a Painter Bee egg.
* Using a Painter Bee Voucher or a 1st Edition Painter Bee Voucher.

Event bee eggs give the bee plus its event [Royal Jelly](royal-jelly.md).
