# Field Capacity

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The Pepper Patch Capacity Buff Icon.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

**Field Capacity** is a [mechanic](mechanics.md) that increases the player's [Capacity Multiplier](capacity.md) depending on what kind of [field](fields.md) they are in. There are 2 types of field capacity: colored field capacity (shown as a stat) and field capacity for a specific field (shown as a buff).

## Ways to Obtain

* Donating certain stickers to the sticker stack can grant field capacity.
* Red Field Capacity can be obtained from [Riley Bee's](riley-bee.md) gifted hive bonus, the [Demon Mask](demon-mask.md) and [Scorching Star](passive-abilities.md#Scorching_Star).
* Blue Field Capacity can be obtained from [Bucko Bee's](bucko-bee.md) gifted hive bonus and [Bubble Bloat](buffs-debuffs.md#From_Passives) (Bubble Bloat can only be obtained if the player has [Pop Star](passive-abilities.md) passive on [Supreme Star Amulet](star-amulet.md)).
* White Field Capacity can be obtained from [Exhausted Bee's](exhausted-bee.md) gifted hive bonus, the [Gummy Mask](gummy-mask.md) and [Gummy Star](passive-abilities.md#Gummy_Star).
* Coconut Field Capacity can be obtained from the Coconut Canister (x1.25).
* Redeeming certain valid [codes](codes.md):
  * ThreeBeeVee (gives [Mountain Top Field](mountain-top-field.md) Code Buff, granting +100% Mountain Top Field Capacity).
* Using certain [Beequips](beequip.md) that include a Field Capacity boost stat.
  * The [Warm Scarf](warm-scarf.md) grants +1% to +3% Red and White Field Capacity.
  * The [Pinecone](pinecone.md) grants +8% to +15% Pine Tree Forest Capacity.
  * The [Snow Tiara](snow-tiara.md) grants +2% to +5% Blue Field Capacity and +3% to +5% White Field Capacity.
  * The [Rose Headband](rose-headband.md) grants 14% to +40% Rose Field Capacity.

## Colored Field Capacity Formula

The following formula shows how much capacity multiplier a player gets in a field:

Let x equal the colored capacity multiplier.  
Let y equal the percentage of that [pollen](pollen.md) type in the field.

Capacity multiplier in the field = \((x-1)\times \frac{y}{100}+1\)

## Trivia

* The [Spider Field](spider-field.md) is the only field where the player is guaranteed to get 100% of a colored capacity bonus, specifically white field capacity.
  * This is achievable with Exhausted Bee's gifted hive bonus, by using the [Gummy Mask](gummy-mask.md), activating Gummy Star, the Warm Scarf's hive bonus, or the Snow Tiara's hive bonus.
  * The only opposite version of this is in the [Mountain Top Field](mountain-top-field.md); it generates no white flowers and therefore doesn't give white field capacity.
  * It is also possible to get 100% of a colored capacity bonus on any other field if they generate entirely with one color of flowers, but the odds of a field generating like that is mathematically impossible.
