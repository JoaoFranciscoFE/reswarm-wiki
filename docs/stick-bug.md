*This page is for the Stick Bug NPC. For attack behaviour and the actual challenge, please see [Stick Bug Challenge](stick-bug-challenge.md)*

**Stick Bug** is a NPC found on the tallest mushroom in the [Mushroom Field](mushroom-field.md), next to the Top Stick Bug Fighters Leaderboard. It is accessible by using the [Red Cannon](red-cannon.md)/[Yellow Cannon](yellow-cannon.md)/[Blue Cannon](blue-cannon.md) with the [Parachute](parachute.md)/[Glider](glider.md), or using the [Beekeeper's Boots](beekeeper-s-boots.md) and jumping from the second mushroom to the one with the [Royal Jelly](royal-jelly.md). Players will need a [Translator](translator.md) to talk to Stick Bug, where they can then ask it to start the [Stick Bug Challenge](stick-bug-challenge.md). Stick Bug then appears as a boss mob and always spawns in the [Sunflower Field](sunflower-field.md) first. Upon defeat, it flees to a random [Field](fields.md) from this list:

* [Sunflower Field](sunflower-field.md)
* [Clover Field](clover-field.md)
* [Strawberry Field](strawberry-field.md)
* [Bamboo Field](bamboo-field.md)
* [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md)
* [Cactus Field](cactus-field.md)
* [Pumpkin Patch](pumpkin-patch.md)
* [Mountain Top Field](mountain-top-field.md)

The player can start the [Stick Bug Challenge](stick-bug-challenge.md) for free every 1 and a half days (1d, 12hr), by initiating it anytime for 50 [Tickets](ticket.md), or by giving Stick Bug a [Present](present.md) during Beesmas.

When the challenge ends, the player will be awarded an [amulet](stick-bug-amulet.md) depending on their score (*More information in the [Stick Bug Amulet](stick-bug-amulet.md) page*).

## Trivia

* If the player gave Stick Bug a [Present](present.md), started its Egg Hunt quest, started its 2019 Beesmas Quest, or finished any of its Beesmas quests (except for 2019), the [Stick Bug Challenge](stick-bug-challenge.md) would automatically start.
* Stick Bug, [Shadow Bear](shadow-bear.md), the [Cub Buddies](cub-buddy.md), [Bee Bear](bee-bear.md) and [Spirit Bear](spirit-bear.md) are the only NPCs that dance.
* There is a [glitch](glitches.md#Stick_Bug_Falls_Down) where Stick Bug may fall over from its regular location on top of the tallest mushroom. If this occurs, it will either be in the [Dandelion Field](dandelion-field.md), the [Mushroom Field](mushroom-field.md), or somewhere in between.
  * Another glitch is on the [Brave Bee Gate](brave-bee-gate.md) ramp, where walking to the bottom of the ramp and on the corner of the [Bamboo Field](bamboo-field.md) will cause Stick Bug to briefly get stuck there when it tries to follow the player.
* In Beesmas 2019, Stick Bug gave the most [Honey](honey.md) when given a present (33,333,333 [Honey](honey.md)), the next being [Panda Bear](panda-bear.md) at only 5,000 [Honey](honey.md).
* If Stick Bug moves to the [Mountain Top Field](mountain-top-field.md), it has a chance of falling and getting stuck in the [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md) or [Pumpkin Patch](pumpkin-patch.md).
* Stick Bug and [Wild Windy Bee](wild-windy-bee.md) are the only mobs that are impossible to defeat, as depleting all of their HP will only level them up and regain their HP.
* Stick Bug waves when the [Stick Bug Challenge](stick-bug-challenge.md) ends.
* Stick Bug is one of the three NPCs that requires a [Translator](translator.md) to interact with, the others being [Gifted Riley Bee](gifted-riley-bee.md) and [Gifted Bucko Bee](gifted-bucko-bee.md).
* Stick Bug's boss fight theme became part of the popular bait-and-switch meme "[Get stickbugged lol](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fC7oUOUEEi4)", which spread far from the BSS Community.
  * Stick Bug is aware of this meme, indicated by his Beesmas 2020 dialogue for receiving a [Present](present.md).
* In [Spirit Bear's](spirit-bear.md) dialogue, she states that Stick Bug scared her as a cub, which indicates that Stick Bug has been around for a long time.
* Stick Bug's Beesmas 2022 Quest originally gave 1 [Caustic Wax](caustic-wax.md), no [Swirled Waxes](swirled-wax.md), no [Ticket Planters](ticket-planter.md), and 1 [Invigorating Vial](invigorating-vial.md). This was soon buffed to 3 [Caustic Waxes](caustic-wax.md), 1 [Swirled Wax](swirled-wax.md), 1 [Ticket Planter](ticket-planter.md), and 2 [Invigorating Vials](invigorating-vial.md).
* As stated by [Spirit Bear](spirit-bear.md), Stick Bug loves [pineapples](pineapple.md).
* Stick Bug is the only NPC that needs a [Translator](translator.md) that isn't also a permanent quest giver.
* During [Beesmas 2024](updates.md#2024-07-17), [Onett](onett-developer.md) would start a Festive [Stick Bug Challenge](stick-bug-challenge.md) at random every few days.
* During the [Egg Hunt 2019 Event Update](updates.md#2019-04-17), Stick Bug would plant an Epic, Legendary, and Supreme [Sprout](sprout.md) after finishing its Egg Hunt quest.
  * This makes Stick Bug the only NPC able to spawn [Sprouts](sprout.md).
