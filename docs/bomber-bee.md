**Bomber Bee** is a Colorless [Rare bee](bees-rare.md).

Bomber Bee's favorite treat is [Sunflower Seeds](sunflower-seed.md).

Bomber Bee likes the [Dandelion Field](dandelion-field.md) and the [Cactus Field](cactus-field.md). It dislikes the [Pumpkin Patch](pumpkin-patch.md).

## Stats

* Collects 10 [pollen](pollen.md) in 4 seconds.
* Makes 120 [honey](honey.md) in 4 seconds.
* +10% [Movespeed](stats.md#Speed), +40 [convert amount](system-page.md#Convert_Amount), +1 [attack](stats.md#Attack).
* 🌟[Gifted Hive Bonus](gifted-bee.md): x1.1 [Bomb Pollen](system-page.md#Bomb_Pollen).

### Abilities

* **[Buzz Bomb](ability-tokens.md#Bomb)** Collects 7 pollen from 13 surrounding [flowers](flowers.md) (+10% pollen per [level](bond.md)). Combo with other bombs to increase power and stacks up to 10 times.
