# Bubble

**Bubbles** are objects that can be produced by certain [tools](tools.md), [abilities](ability-tokens.md), or [passive abilities](passive-abilities.md). Bubbles have a percentage chance to be spawned by certain bees when gathering. Bubbles spawn on the flower that the bee gathered and last for 4 seconds. When popped, they collect 4 red, 6 white, and 8 blue pollen from 29 surrounding flowers (+15% per gifted blue bee type, +5% per bee level) and cause nearby flowers to grow back to full.

Bubbles can be generated by [Bubble Bee](bubble-bee.md), [Tadpole Bee](tadpole-bee.md), [frogs](frog.md), the [Bubble Wand](bubble-wand.md), [Bubble Mask](bubble-mask.md) and [Diamond Mask's](diamond-mask.md) Bubble Bombs passive, and the [Pop Star](passive-abilities.md) passive.

Bubbles can be popped by other players, petal shurikens, [coconuts](coconut.md), tornadoes, [Star Saw](passive-abilities.md), [Fuzz Bombs](ability-tokens.md#Fuzz_Bombs), the [Dark Scythe](dark-scythe.md)'s trail, [Puppy Bee](puppy-bee.md)'s Fetch and the [Tide Popper](tide-popper.md)'s waves.

Popping bubbles helps to grow Pop Stars and will increase [Bubble Bloat](buffs-debuffs.md#From_Passives). The bigger the Pop Star grows, the more bubbles it will spawn.

If a player pops a bubble belonging to another player, the pollen collected by the bubble will be granted to the owner of the bubble. This also applies to Pop Star growth.

## Gathering Bubbles

The **Gathering Bubbles** passive gives a chance for a bee to spawn a bubble while gathering pollen from a field. Gathering Bubbles+ gives a higher chance for a bee to spawn a bubble.

There is a 25% chance (50% if gifted) for Bubble Bees' Gathering Bubbles passive to spawn a bubble while gathering, and a 65% (85% if gifted) chance for Tadpole Bees' Gathering Bubbles+ passive to spawn a bubble while gathering.

A [Bead Lizard](bead-lizard.md) gives from +10% Gathering Bubbles chance to +12% Gathering Bubbles chance for an individual bee.

## Bubble Pollen

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The Bubble Pollen stat in the System Page.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

**Bubble Pollen** is the amount of pollen that bubbles can collect. The player can check how much bubble pollen they have in the system page.

Bubble Pollen can be increased by a few ways.

* The [Diamond Mask](diamond-mask.md) gives a x1.5 Bubble Pollen multiplier.
* The [Bubble Mask](bubble-mask.md) gives a 1.25x Bubble Pollen multiplier.
* [Gifted Bubble Bee](bubble-bee.md) gives a x1.25 Bubble Pollen multiplier.
* [Beequips](beequip.md):
  * A Bead Lizard can give +1% Bubble Pollen as a hive bonus from waxing.
  * A [Bubble Light](bubble-light.md) gives from +15% Bubble Pollen to +30% Bubble Pollen for an individual bee. Using [waxes](waxes.md) can increase the amount up to +60% Bubble Pollen.
  * A Bubble Light can give +1% Bubble Pollen as a hive bonus. Using waxes can increase the amount up to +11% Bubble Pollen.
  * A Camphor Lip Balm can give 15% Bubble pollen hive bonus. Using waxes can increase the amount up to 25% Bubble pollen.
* [Cog Amulets](cog-amulet.md):
  * The Bronze Cog Amulet can give from +3% Bubble Pollen up to +5% Bubble Pollen.
  * The Silver Cog Amulet can give from +5% Bubble Pollen up to +6% Bubble Pollen.
  * The Gold Cog Amulet can give from +8% Bubble Pollen up to +9% Bubble Pollen.
  * The Diamond Cog Amulet can give extra Bubble Pollen.
  * The Supreme Cog Amulet can give from +15% Bubble Pollen up to +20% Bubble Pollen.
* [Robo Bear Challenge](robo-bear-challenge.md):
  * Epic Upgrades:
    * Pop-Up: This upgrade gives x2 Bubble Pollen.
    * RGB: This upgrade gives x1.25 Bubble Pollen.

## Improving Bubbles

Aside from increasing the amount of pollen that bubbles can collect, the player can also improve bubbles a few other ways.

### Golden Bubbles

A **Golden Bubble** is a special variant of a bubble that can only occur when a [golden balloon](balloon.md#Golden_Balloon) floats over a normal bubble or it can be summoned by a gifted frog.

When a golden bubble is popped, it collects +100% more pollen than a normal bubble per field boost stack, contributes 50% more time to Pop Star's bubble bloat than a normal bubble, has 100% [Instant Conversion](instant-conversion.md) (unless going into a balloon), and has a 10% chance to spawn a honey token.

The only way to increase the Golden Bubble pollen besides [Field Boost](field-boost.md) is with Camphor Lip Balm.

### Instant Bubble Conversion

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The Instant Bubble Conversion stat in the System Page.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

**Instant Bubble Conversion** is how much pollen collected by the bubbles is instantly converted. There are currently no ways to increase Instant Bubble Conversion.

### Bubble Duration

**Bubble Duration** affects how long bubbles last before they disappear. The base duration is 4 seconds and can be increased to 5 seconds. There is currently only one way to increase Bubble Duration. Note that bubbles spawned from Bubbles Bombs last 8 seconds (10 seconds if one has a Gifted Tadpole bee).

* Gifted Tadpole bee grants +25% Bubble Duration

### Other Improvements

Certain upgrades from the Robo Bear Challenge can be used to improve bubbles.

* Epic Upgrades:
  * Invert: This upgrade makes bubbles collect x4 pollen from red flowers.
  * Pop-Up: This upgrade gives x1.5 Bubble Lifespan.

## Trivia

* The maximum Bubble Pollen a player can have at a time is 925%. This can be achieved by having the Diamond Mask (x2), a Bead Lizard (+1%), three Bubble Lights (+33%), a camphor lip balm (+25%), a Supreme Cog Amulet (+20%), Gifted Bubble Bee (x1.25), activating the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md) with certain [stickers](sticker.md) (+6%), Pop-Up upgrade (x2), RGB upgrade (x1.25).
* Popping a bubble has a 1/1,000,000 chance to spawn a Tadpole sticker.
* Bubbles are the counterpart of [flames](flame.md).
