**Sun Bear** is a traveling bear who appeared during special events. He is one of the four temporary [quest givers](quest-givers.md), the others being [Bee Bear](bee-bear.md), [Gummy Bear](gummy-bear.md), and [Bubble Bee Man](bubble-bee-man.md).

He made five appearances. His first visit was from April 27, 2018, to May 13, 2018. He gave 12 quests with rewards including the [Belt Bag](belt-bag.md) and a final reward of a [Mondo Belt Bag](mondo-belt-bag.md). His second visit was from September 10, 2018, to October 8, 2018. He gave 14 quests with rewards including the [Basic Boots](basic-boots.md), the [Hiking Boots](hiking-boots.md), and the [Beekeeper's Boots](beekeeper-s-boots.md). His third visit was from September 28, 2019, to October 28, 2019. He gave 10 quests themed around Bean Bugs, with a final reward of 250 [tickets](ticket.md). His fourth visit was from April 6, 2020, to May 21, 2020, for the Roblox Egg Hunt 2020. His fifth visit was on August 1, 2024, for the Roblox "The Games" event, later the bear stayed in the game for sometime and at October 29, 2024, he received a new quest line. He left from his fifth visit on April 30, 2025, the same day 2024 Winter Beesmas ended.

## Trivia

* He was the only traveling bear to not introduce his own [event bee](bees-event.md).
* In the quest '**Focus Feature'**, Sun Bear said to defeat a [Werewolf](werewolf.md) instead of 3 [Spiders](spider.md).
* The [Ability Badge](badges.md#Ability_Badge) is represented by him in the game's description.
* After his spring visit, the belt bags can be bought in [Pro Shop](pro-shop.md) ([Belt Bag](belt-bag.md)) and the [Mountain Top Shop](mountain-top-shop.md) ([Mondo Belt Bag](mondo-belt-bag.md)).
* During his spring visit, there was a [Ticket](ticket.md) behind Sun Bear's RV. When Sun Bear went away, the ticket stayed there for a little time, but now it's gone.
* Sun Bear uses the [Knight Animation Package](https://web.roblox.com/catalog/734338331/Knight-Animation-Package).
* Sun Bear says that only [Rage](rage-bee.md), [Exhausted](exhausted-bee.md), and [Music](music-bee.md) Bees spawn [Token Link](ability-tokens.md#Token_Link), but [Shocked Bee](shocked-bee.md) does as well. This was fixed a few days later.
* In his second visit (2018-09-10 Update) and his third visit (2019-09-28 Update), his RV had a [Flag of Phoenix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Phoenix) bumper sticker on the back.
* During his first visit, Sun Bear was shown to be wearing the [Mondo Belt Bag](mondo-belt-bag.md), which he gave at the very end of his quests. During his second visit, he was shown to be wearing both the Mondo Belt Bag and [Beekeeper's Boots](beekeeper-s-boots.md), which he gave at the end of his quests.
* Sun Bear gives 48 [Tickets](ticket.md) throughout all his quests with the ticket token that was behind the RV.
* During the quest '**Sun Bear's Scavenger Hunt'**, he says 3 more quests are needed to be completed to get the [Beekeeper's Boots](beekeeper-s-boots.md), when it is actually 4 quests. However, Onett could have done this on purpose, because Sun Bear was completely surprised by [Vicious Bee](rogue-vicious-bee.md) in his dialogue, and gives you a quest around this.
* He was the first traveling bear added to the game, with [Gummy Bear](gummy-bear.md) being the second and [Bee Bear](bee-bear.md) being the third.
* The item prices of his rewards after his visit are: [Basic Boots](basic-boots.md) are 4,400, [Hiking Boots](hiking-boots.md) are 2,200,000, Beekeeper's Boots are 15,000,000, [Belt Bag](belt-bag.md) is 440,000, and [Mondo Belt Bag](mondo-belt-bag.md) is 12,400,000 [Honey](honey.md).
* He was the only traveling bear who never gave an egg on his last quest.
* He and [Bubble Bee Man](bubble-bee-man.md) were the only NPCs to give an Accessory.
* It was hinted that Sun Bear is from Arizona, this is from the [Code](codes.md) "**Arizona**", and the City of Arizona flag that was on his RV the second and the third visit.
* During the quests in his third visit, it is revealed that the reason he had Bean Bugs was that he was taking them to Orlando for an unknown reason.
* In the quest '**Total Focus'**, Sun Bear said to collect 200 [Focus Tokens](ability-tokens.md#Focus) before you received the quest, but he didn't say to collect 200 Focus Tokens after you received the quest.
* For the '**Total Focus'** quest, after you finish the dialogue with Sun Bear, a [Memory Match](memory-match.md) game will start automatically for free.
* He stated only [Basic Bee](basic-bee.md) doesn't generate tokens, but [Brave Bee](brave-bee.md) doesn't as well, even though both of them can generate [Inspire tokens](ability-tokens.md#Inspire) when they are [gifted](gifted-bee.md).
* To complete every quest on his third visit, you must collect 3835 tokens from Bean Bugs.
* It was possible for his trapped Bean Bug to jump out of its cage.
  * It was also possible for the player to go in and out of the Bean Bug's cage.
  * This could possibly be the reason that they got out in the first place
* Onett has stated that Sun Bear isn't welcome on the mountain anymore for accidentally releasing Bean Bugs and the [chicks](chicks.md). It seems as if Sun Bear was unbanned in 2024 with his visit during The Games.
* In “Boot Lootin” Sun Bear mentions that he has been to a desert before.
* In “Light Up The Night” Sun Bear mentions that he prefers [Nighttime](day-night-cycle.md) to Daytime, as he is able to relax.
* Sun Bear likes [mantises](mantis.md). Referring to them as ‘cool’ in his quest “Show Me The Honey”.
* Sun Bear mentions in his quest “Vicious Bee Begone” that he has had an encounter with a [Rogue Vicious Bee](rogue-vicious-bee.md) while going to visit [Brown Bear](brown-bear.md).
* In his quest “Total Focus” Sun Bear mentions that he transports ‘exotic things’ around in his RV as a job.
