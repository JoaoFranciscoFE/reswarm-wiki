## Royal Jelly

**Royal Jelly** is an inventory item that can be found as a token around the map, received as certain [quest](quests.md) rewards, redeemed in valid [codes](codes.md), purchased from various [shops](shops.md) or dispensers, or various other different ways. When used on a [bee](bees.md), it turns it into either a [Rare](bees-rare.md), [Epic](bees-epic.md), [Legendary](bees-legendary.md), [Mythic](bees-mythic.md), a [gifted](gifted-bee.md) variant of any of those bees, or a [mutated](mutation.md) variant of those bees.

Royal Jelly does **NOT** necessarily turn a bee into a better rarity. For example, if a player used it on a Legendary bee, they have the same 70% chance of getting a Rare bee as if they had used it on a [Basic Bee](basic-bee.md). This rule also applies to gifted and mutated bees.

## Ways to Obtain

* Completing [Brown Bear's](brown-bear.md) quests rewards one or more Royal Jelly and other items. Other quest givers give Royal Jelly less frequently.
* Completing [Black Bear](black-bear.md)'s Diamond Egg, Star Jelly, and Mythic Egg quests rewards one or more royal jellies.
* Completing [Spirit Bear](spirit-bear.md)'s quests often rewards multiple Royal Jellies. The amount generally increases with each further quest taken.
* The [Royal Jelly Dispenser](royal-jelly-dispenser.md) near the [Slingshot](slingshot.md), [Clover Field](clover-field.md), and [Dandelion Field](dandelion-field.md) dispenses them for 6 [tickets](ticket.md) each while also giving 10x [Haste](ability-tokens.md#Haste).
* Royal Jelly can be purchased individually or in packs of 10 from the [Robux Shop](robux-shop.md). It costs 45R$ for one Royal Jelly, and 300R$ for 10 royal jellies.
* Royal Jelly can be found in different hidden and hard-to-reach locations. These jellies can be obtained only once for every player (In other words, they don't respawn).
* Royal Jelly can be claimed using the dispenser in the [Star Hall](star-hall.md) every 22 hours. The number of Royal Jelly the player obtain depends on the number of [Gifted bees](gifted-bee.md) in their [hive](hive.md).
* Finishing the [Ant Challenge](ant-challenge.md) gives at least 2 Royal Jelly with the quantity increasing as the score increases by 50 points.
* From the [sparkles](sparkles.md) made by the faces that can appear on certain objects or by [fireflies](fireflies.md).
* Finishing the [Stick Bug Challenge](stick-bug-challenge.md) or drops from [Stick Nymphs](stick-nymph.md) during the challenge.
* Royal Jelly can also naturally spawn in [fields](fields.md), but the probability is extremely low.
* Growing a [sprout](sprout.md) may drop Royal Jelly tokens depending on the type of sprout.
* The [Royal Jelly Shop](royal-jelly-shop.md) near the [Red HQ](red-hq.md), where players can buy a Royal Jelly for 250,000 [honey](honey.md) initially, with the price increasing by 10,000 honey with each purchase until capping at 1,000,000 honey.
* As a drop from specific [Planters](planter.md) ([Ticket](ticket-planter.md), [Plastic](plastic-planter.md), [Blue Clay](blue-clay-planter.md), [Tacky](tacky-planter.md), [Petal](petal-planter.md)).
* A sprout in Clover Field drops more Royal Jelly than any other field.
* As a reward from placing certain [Stickers](sticker.md) on the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md).
* Defeating [mobs](mobs.md) may drop Royal Jelly.
* As a reward from the [Memory Match](memory-match.md).
* As a drop from [Festive Gifts](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Gift).
* As a drop from [Festive Mark](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Mark).
* As a reward from the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md).
* As a gift from a [Cub Buddy](cub-buddy.md).

## Star Jelly

Star Jelly turns a bee into a random gifted bee, with the same rarity chances as normal Royal Jellies.

* Certain quests are guaranteed to give Star Jellies, while others are randomized.
  * There is a chance to get a Star Jelly for doing [Black Bear](black-bear.md)'s repeatable quests.
  * Obtained by finishing all of the quests in [Black Bear](black-bear.md)'s Star Jelly questline, by completing Bubble Trouble and Pollen Fetcher 6 on the [Mythic Egg](egg.md#Mythic_Egg) questline.
  * Obtained by finishing [Science Bear](science-bear.md)'s Beesperanto and Epistemological Endeavor quests in the [Translator](translator.md) questline and Corrupting The [Glitched Drive](drives.md#Glitched_Drive) in the Drives questline.
  * Obtained by finishing the 90th and 195th quest from [Brown Bear](brown-bear.md).
  * Obtained by finishing the 5th, 15th, and 21st - 30th quests from [Spirit Bear](spirit-bear.md).
  * Obtained by starting the 7th [Spirit Bear](spirit-bear.md) quest.
  * Obtained by finishing the 25th quest from [Gifted Bucko Bee](gifted-bucko-bee.md) or [Gifted Riley Bee](gifted-riley-bee.md).
  * Obtained by finishing the 5th quest and 10th quest from [Dapper Bear](dapper-bear.md).
  * Obtained by finishing the 2nd quest in the Drive questline.
  * Every 10 quests completed from [Honey Bee (NPC)](honey-bee-npc.md) rewards a Star Jelly until the 100th quest.

    

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">A Star Jelly token.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

* Can be crafted using the [Blender](blender.md) (100 Royal Jelly and 3 [Glitter](glitter.md)).
* Obtained from certain [Robux packs](robux-shop.md).
* A guaranteed drop from [Stump Snail](stump-snail.md), [Coconut Crab](coconut-crab.md) and when capturing [Commando Chick](commando-chick.md) for the 15th time.
* A chance of being a drop from a [Spider](spider.md), [Werewolf](werewolf.md), [Mondo Chick](mondo-chick.md), [Tunnel Bear](tunnel-bear.md), [Stick Bug](stick-bug.md), [Wild Windy Bee](wild-windy-bee.md) and [King Beetle](king-beetle.md).
* If a player's score is 10M+ in the [Stick Bug Challenge](stick-bug-challenge.md), they are guaranteed at least 1 Star Jelly. The higher their score, the more Star Jelly they can get, and also Star Jelly drops from stick bug when he is defeated.
* There is an exceptionally rare chance to get a Star Jelly from the [Clover Field](clover-field.md) or from [leaves](leaves.md) in the Clover Field.
* There is a chance to get a Star Jelly from a [Festive Gift](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Gift) (only if the [Festive Bee](festive-bee.md) is gifted).
* There is a chance to get a Star Jelly from nudging [fireflies](fireflies.md).
* Obtained from Rare, Epic, Legendary, Supreme, and Moon [Sprouts](sprout.md).
* A reward from Mega, Night, and Extreme [Memory Matches](memory-match.md).
* A slim chance of obtaining through a [Cub Buddy](cub-buddy.md) gift.
* Catching a [Meteor](mythic-meteor-shower.md) has a small chance to award a Star Jelly.
* A rare drop from any planter.
  * A common drop from the [Petal Planter](petal-planter.md) and [The Planter Of Plenty](the-planter-of-plenty.md).
* As a rare drop from a [Puffshroom](puffshroom.md).
* A rare drop from [Robo Bear Challenge](robo-bear-challenge.md).
* A reward from the [Retro Swarm Challenge](retro-swarm-challenge.md).

## Specific Bee Jelly Variants

Instead of transforming into a random bee of a particular rarity, **specific bee jellies** will always hatch into the jellies respective bee. All of these jellies share the same appearance of a royal jelly. These are primarily obtained from limited-time codes, Bee Bear's Catalog during Beesmas, and Bubble Bee Man.

* [Basic Bee](basic-bee.md) Jelly was obtained from the code *DontUseThisJelly* [Expired]; it turns any bee into a Basic Bee.
* [Bumble Bee](bumble-bee.md) Jelly is obtained from completing [B.B.M Mission](bubble-bee-man.md#Quest_.28Inside_30_Bee_Gate.29) quest from Bubble Bee Man in the [30 Bee Area](bear-gate.md#Inside_The_30_Bee_Area) and from the codes "BeeDay2019" [Expired] and "Cubly" [Expired]; it turns any bee into a Bumble Bee.
* [Hasty Bee](hasty-bee.md) Jelly is obtained from receiving Bee Bear's Beesmas Blitz quest and from the code *HasteHelper* [Expired]; it turns any bee into a Hasty Bee.
* [Looker Bee](looker-bee.md) Jelly was obtained from the code *ThoseEyes* [Expired]; it turns any bee into a Looker Bee.
* [Rad Bee](rad-bee.md) Jelly was obtained from the code *Nonchalant* and *Strawbeary* [Both expired]; it turns any bee into a Rad Bee.
* [Rascal Bee](rascal-bee.md) Jelly was obtained from the code *MischiefMaker* [Expired] and *Boo Swarm* [Expired]; it turns any bee into a Rascal Bee.
* [Stubborn Bee](stubborn-bee.md) Jelly is obtained from the expired code *BANNED*; it turns any bee into a Stubborn Bee.
* [Bubble Bee](bubble-bee.md) Jelly is obtained from completing B.B.M Mission quest from Bubble Bee Man in the 30 Bee Area; it turns any bee into a Bubble Bee.
* [Commander Bee](commander-bee.md) Jelly was obtained from the code *Keen4* [Expired]; it turns any bee into a Commander Bee.
* [Demo Bee](demo-bee.md) Jelly was obtained from the code *Poke* [Expired]; it turns any bee into a Demo Bee.
* [Exhausted Bee](exhausted-bee.md) Jelly was obtained from purchasing Night Bundle from [Bee Bear's Catalog](bee-bear-s-catalog.md), and from the codes *OnettJelly* and *CantSleep* [Both expired]; it turns any bee into an Exhausted Bee).
* [Fire Bee](fire-bee.md) Jelly was obtained from purchasing Cozy Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog; it turns any bee into a Fire Bee.
* [Frosty Bee](frosty-bee.md) Jelly was obtained from purchasing Snowglobe Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog; it turns any bee into a Frosty Bee.
* [Honey Bee](honey-bee.md) Jelly was obtained from buying the Honeysuckle Bundle in Bee Bear’s Catalog 2021 or by giving a present to Honey Bee during Beesmas 2020 and 2021; turns any bee into a Honey Bee.
* [Rage Bee](rage-bee.md) Jelly was obtained from giving a [present](present.md) to [Panda Bear](panda-bear.md), by receiving [Brown Bear's Egg Hunt 2020 quest](brown-bear.md#Egg_Hunt_2020_Quest), "Commando Chick's Hideout", or from purchasing the Anti-Ant Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog; it turns any bee into a Rage Bee.
* [Shocked Bee](shocked-bee.md) Jelly is obtained from the expired code *SecretProfileCode*, or from purchasing the Whirling bundle; it turns any bee into a Shocked Bee.
* [Carpenter Bee](carpenter-bee.md) Jelly was obtained from purchasing the Pinecone Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog; it turns any bee into a Carpenter Bee.
* [Diamond Bee](diamond-bee.md) Jelly was obtainable from the Royal Winter Bundle, and was also obtained from purchasing Snow Queen Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog; it turns any bee into a Diamond Bee.
* [Music Bee](music-bee.md) Jelly was obtained from purchasing the Percussive Bundle and the Beesmas Tree Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog, and through the now expired Funky Present; it turns any bee into a Music Bee.
* [Shy Bee](shy-bee.md) Jelly was obtained through the code *NewMic* [Expired]; it turns any bee into a Shy Bee.
* [Tadpole Bee](tadpole-bee.md) Jelly was obtained from giving a [Present](present.md) to Bubble Bee Man during Beesmas 2020; it turns any bee into a Tadpole Bee.
* [Buoyant Bee](buoyant-bee.md) Jelly was obtained from giving a [Present](present.md) to Bubble Bee Man during Beesmas 2021, 2022 and 2024; it turns any bee into a Buoyant Bee.
* [Fuzzy Bee](fuzzy-bee.md) Jelly was obtained by completing B.B.M's Naughty List during Beesmas 2022; it turns any bee into a Fuzzy Bee.
* [Precise Bee](precise-bee.md) Jelly was obtained by completing B.B.M's Naughty List during Beesmas 2021; it turns any bee into a Precise Bee.

Attempting to use a specific bee jelly on a bee that is already the one that's guaranteed by the egg will not work (Using a Honey Bee jelly on a Honey Bee won't work and the jelly won't be used).

## Trivia

* Event bee jellies have infinite uses; as long as the hive does not already have that bee, the player can use the jelly.
* Royal Jelly is based off the real-life counterpart. The eponymous substance is used as nutrition for honey bee larvae and queens.
* [Sun Bear](sun-bear.md) gave a hint about the Royal Jelly on top of the Ticket Tent in his dialogue.

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 220px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> </figcaption> </figure>

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 220px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> </figcaption> </figure>

* When the player is transforming any bee with any type of royal jelly, the number of exclamation marks after the bee's name is the same as that bee's tier.
  * Common/Rare = 1 exclamation mark (!), Epic = 2 exclamation marks (!!), Legendary/Event = 3 exclamation marks (!!!) and Mythic = 4 exclamation marks (!!!!).
* Using a specific jelly on said bee (e.g. using a Bubble Bee Jelly on a Bubble Bee) does not use the jelly, and no transformation pop-up appears.
* Event Bee jellies cannot make the bee gifted even if it was gifted before.
* The plural for royal jelly is incorrectly spelled as 'Royal Jellys', rather than 'Royal Jellies'. This is a mistake that has happened with other items with a plural form that exchanges a 'y' for 'ies'.
* Using a normal jelly on a gifted/mutated bee is almost **CERTAIN** to cause the bee to lose its gifted/mutated status. It is a 1/250 chance to get a gifted bee back and 1/5000 chance to get a mutated bee back. However, the bee will most likely have a different mutation.
* If you attempt to use a royal jelly on a bee in between [Robo Bear Challenge](robo-bear-challenge.md) rounds, it will prompt you with the message: "⚠ Hatching or transforming a bee will end your Robo Bear Challenge. Continue?⚠". Choosing "Yes" will end your Robo Bear Challenge, and "No" will cancel it.
* The rarest possible bee obtained from Royal Jelly is a specific mythic bee that is gifted and has the mutation bee ability rate with a chance of 1/104T.
* The description of Royal Jelly does not actually say that you can get a Mythic bee.
* Star Jelly token behind [Blue Maze](mazes.md#Blue_Maze) used to give 3 Star Jellies, instead of 1.
* An effective method of obtaining large amounts of Star Jelly is fighting [Stick Bug](stick-bug.md) with its Beesmas quests completed, as the player can obtain a few star jellies when the challenge ends and some from Festive Nymphs or [Stick Bug](stick-bug.md) himself.

* Star Jelly’s raw ingredients cost:
  * 1125 [Strawberries](strawberry.md)
  * 1125 [Blueberries](blueberry.md)
  * 1500 [Pineapples](pineapple.md)
  * 175 Royal Jellies or 175M [Honey](honey.md)
  * 3 [Magic Beans](magic-bean.md) or 60 [Tickets](ticket.md)
