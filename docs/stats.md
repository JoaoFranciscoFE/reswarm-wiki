# Stats

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">A bee's level up page.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

*This page is for stats that apply to bees. For stats that apply to the player, collecting pollen, and the [hive](hive.md), see [System Page](system-page.md).*

**Stats** (short for statistics) refer to the aspects of each bee that are the basis for how that bee performs. Certain stats increase with higher [levels](bond.md).

## Rarity

**Rarity** refers to how often bees of each category can be expected to be generated from eggs or [royal jelly](royal-jelly.md). Bees of greater rarity tend to be of higher stats and possessing their own unique ability, but rarity itself does not have any bearing on performance. An [Event](bees-event.md) tier of bees are acquired through their unique obtainment method.

## Color

**Color** affects a bee's ability to gather [pollen](pollen.md) from [flowers](flowers.md), improving their Gather Amount by 50% when collecting from flowers of a matching color. Blue and Red are the only colors of bees that have this bonus. Colorless bees do not experience any boost to Gather Amount, even from white flowers.

## Bond

Main article: [Bond](bond.md)

**[Bond](bond.md)** is a stat to determine if a bee has enough "experience" to level up. The following methods can affect experience:

* Treats
* Collecting Pollen
* Bond from battle
* Puppy Love
* Digging in to the [Beesmas Feast](beesmas-feast.md) during Beesmas.

## Energy

Main article: [Energy](energy.md)

**Energy** represents the number of times a bee will gather pollen from a flower and attack [mobs](mobs.md) before returning to the [hive](hive.md) to rest (30 seconds to fully recharge upon entering the hive)

* Spawning [Ability Tokens](ability-tokens.md) does not cost a bee any energy
* The energy of all bees in a swarm can be increased with stacks of *[Polar Power](buffs-debuffs.md#From_NPCs)* from [Polar Bear](polar-bear.md)'s quests, or from an energy [mutation](mutation.md)
* Energy is increased by 5% for each level above 1
* Resetting yourself will cause bees to go back to the hive, but they will not recharge their energy
* An *energy reset* can be achieved by reseting again when bees are inside of the hive after a prior reset

The formula for energy is: *Base Energy \* (1 + .05 \* (Level - 1)) \* Polar Power + Energy Mutation*

## Speed

**Speed**, short for **Bee Movespeed**, is how fast a bee moves and performs actions (contrary to its name). Speed indrectly affects all basic processes of a bee such as:

* Gathering
* Attacking
* Sipping nectar in a planter
* Converting at hive.

Bees move at approximately 1.5 times their move speed when not close to their owner and 0.5 of their move speed when returning to the hive to rest. The speed of all bees in a swarm can be increased by [Gifted](gifted-bee.md) [Ninja Bee's](ninja-bee.md) hive bonus, [Beesmas Cheer](ability-tokens.md#Beesmas_Cheer), [Playtime Badge](badges.md#Playtime_Badge), [oil](oil.md), [super smoothies](super-smoothie.md), and activating [Coconut Haste](passive-abilities.md#Coconut_Haste).

The formula for Bee Movespeed is: *Base Bee Movespeed \* (1 + .03 \* (Level - 1)) \* (1 + Ninja Bee Hive Bonus + Playtime Badge Bonus)*

## Attack

**Attack** is how much damage the bee will do when fighting mobs. Attack remains constant at all levels, but can be boosted through various accessories, [items](items.md), [amulets](amulet.md), certain [gifted hive bonuses](gifted-bee.md#List_of_Hive_Bonuses) and ability tokens. Gifted bees have 50% more attack.

The formula for a bee's Attack is: (Base Attack Damage + Attack Buffs) \* Attack multiplier (\* 1.5 If the bee is gifted)

## Gather Amount

**Gather Amount** describes how much pollen a bee will collect from a flower and how quickly it does so. The amount collected increases by 10% for each level above 1. Gather amount may be increased with; amulets, certain Gifted Bee bonuses, [Gamepasses,](robux-shop.md) or items.

The formula for Gather Amount is: *Base Gather Amount \* (1 + .10 \* (Level - 1)) \* Gifted Multiplier*

## Production Amount

**Production Amount** describes how much pollen a bee will convert into [honey](honey.md) at the hive and how quickly it does so. The amount converted increases by 10% for each level above 1.

The formula for Production Amount is: *(Base Production Amount + Production Amount) \* (1 + .10 \* (Level - 1)) \* Conversion Rate \* Gifted Multiplier*

## Mutations

Main article: [Mutation](mutation.md)

**Mutations** are a bonus granted through feeding bitterberries to a bee when they are under the **radioactive** effect. Mutations can grant bonus:

* Attack
* Convert Amount
* Gather Amount
* Energy
* Bee Ability Rate
* Bee Movespeed
* Critical Chance
* Instant Conversion

## Gifted

Main article: Gifted Bees

**Gifted** is a bonus granted through feeding a bee's favourite treat, [Star Treat](star-treat.md), [Gingerbread Bears](gingerbread-bear.md), or [Aged Gingerbread Bears](aged-gingerbread-bear.md). This stat grants:

* Multiplier to gather amount, convert amount, and bee attack all together by 1.5x
* Ability to spawn - [[Inspire](ability-tokens.md)] tokens
* A gifted hive bonus (only once for each bee)
* A gifted ability (if the bee has one)
* A new appearance
