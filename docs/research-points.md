# Research Points

![Research Points](img/Research_Points.png){ align=right width=150 }

*"Available points used to unlock upgrades in the Melittology Tree."*

**Research Points** are the currency of the [Melittology](melittology.md) tree. You spend them to unlock its upgrades, ending with the [Honey Hammer](honey-hammer.md), which costs 10 Research Points.

## Ways to obtain

* Completing the Melittology Research quests, which unlock at [Rebirth 10](rebirths.md). The 25 ranks give 76 Research Points in total. See [Melittology](melittology.md#melittology-research-quests).
