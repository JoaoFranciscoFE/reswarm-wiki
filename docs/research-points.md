# Research Points

![Research Points](img/Research_Points.png){ align=right width=150 }

*"Available points used to unlock upgrades in the Melittology Tree."*

**Research Points** are the currency of the Melittology tree. You spend them to unlock its upgrades, ending with the [Honey Hammer](honey-hammer.md), which costs 10 Research Points.

## Ways to obtain

* Earned through the Melittology system.
