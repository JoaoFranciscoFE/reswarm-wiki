# Treat

Treat

*"Increases your bond with a bee by 10."*

A **Treat** is a type of [treat](treats.md) that can be fed to a [bee](bees.md) to raise its bond by 10. They are the easiest **treat** to obtain but are not the favorite treat of any bee. They can also be used to craft the [Honey Mask](honey-mask.md) where 9,999 **treats** are needed.

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">A treat token.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

## Ways to Obtain

* Using the [Treat Dispenser](treat-dispenser.md) near the [Pro Shop](pro-shop.md)/[Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md) (Like the [Honey Dispenser](honey-dispenser.md), this requires being a member of the [BSS Club](https://www.roblox.com/groups/3982592/Bee-Swarm-Simulator-Club#!/about)).
* Buying from the [Treat Shop](treat-shop.md) next to under Mother Bear's awning for 100 [Honey](honey.md) per treat and capping out at 10,000 [Honey](honey.md) per treat.
* As a reward from [memory matches](memory-match.md).
* As a stack reward for adding certain [stickers](sticker.md) to the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md):
  * The Fork And Knife rewards 500 treats.

### Drops

* Farming in the [Dandelion](dandelion-field.md), [Mushroom](mushroom-field.md), [Clover](clover-field.md), [Spider](spider-field.md), [Cactus](cactus-field.md), [Pumpkin](pumpkin-patch.md), [Coconut](coconut-field.md), or [Stump](stump-field.md) Fields.
  * Drops from [leaves](leaves.md) in these fields may also yield treats.
* A relatively high chance of dropping from [rhino beetles](rhino-beetle.md), [ladybugs](ladybug.md), [spiders](spider.md), [mantises](mantis.md), [scorpions](scorpion.md), [werewolves](werewolf.md), [aphids](aphid.md), [Stick Bug](stick-bug.md), [Stick Nymphs](stick-nymph.md), [Tunnel Bear](tunnel-bear.md), [King Beetle](king-beetle.md), and [stump snail](stump-snail.md).
* A guaranteed drop from [Mondo Chicks](mondo-chick.md).
* 2nd and 3rd capture of [Commando Chick](commando-chick.md) rewards 2 and 5 treats respectively.
* "Playing" [Fetch](ability-tokens.md#Fetch) with [Puppy Bee](puppy-bee.md).
* As a drop from a [Festive Gift](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Gift) or [Festive Mark](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Mark).
* As a gift from a [cub buddy](cub-buddy.md).
* As a reward for completely growing any kind of [sprout](sprout.md) except for Gummy and Debug sprouts.
* Harvesting [sparkles](sparkles.md).
* As a drop from [Puffshrooms](puffshroom.md).
* As a drop from Planters, which include:
  * [Plastic Planters](plastic-planter.md)
  * [Candy Planters](candy-planter.md)
  * [Red Clay Planters](red-clay-planter.md)
  * [Tacky Planters](tacky-planter.md)
* Activating certain targets grants a small amount of treats.
* Using [Polar Bear's](polar-bear.md) [Beesmas Feast](beesmas-feast.md) during Beesmas will give treats and other items.

### Quests

* Black Bear gives treats on the following quests:
  * Get Red-y gives 100 treats.
  * One Stop On The Tip Top gives 200 treats.
  * Blue Mushrooms 2 gives 300 treats.
  * Pretty Pumpkins gives 400 treats.
  * Black Bear, Why? gives 500 treats.
  * Bee A Star gives 1,000 treats.
  * The [Mythic Egg](egg.md#Mythic_Egg) questline gives treats on all quests, at a sum of 195,500 treats.
  * Black Bear's repeatable quests also reward treats.
* [Science Bear](science-bear.md) gives treats on the following quests:
  * Mark Mechanics gives 3,000 treats.
  * Epistemological Endeavor gives 10,000 treats.
* All of Mother Bear's quests give treats, 4,020 treats in total.
* All of Polar Bear's quests give 10 treats.
* All of Onett's quests give treats, 25,000 treats in total.
* Every 5th completion of Honey Bee's quest may give treats.
