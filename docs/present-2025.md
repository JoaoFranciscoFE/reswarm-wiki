# Present/2025

Presents are available during the Beesmas event. Their main function is adding [Ornaments](ornaments.md) to the [Beesmas Tree](beesmas-tree.md) when given to NPCs. Players can't give a present to [Sun Bear](sun-bear.md), as the bear is not present during the event.

## Ways to obtain

* Multiple quests from [Bee Bear](bee-bear.md) award a total of 10 [Presents](present.md), along with other rewards.
* [Bee Bear's Catalog](bee-bear-s-catalog.md) sold 1 [Present](present.md) for 250 [Snowflakes](snowflake.md) and 10 [Gingerbread Bears](gingerbread-bear.md).
* [Onett](onett.md) gives 1 [Present](present.md) (along with other items) upon claiming a hive for the first time during the event's period.
* Donating [Strawberries](strawberry.md) to the [Galentine Shrine](wind-shrine.md#Galentine_Shrine) has a chance to give 1 [Present](present.md), along with other rewards.
* Completing Robo Party's rank 25 for the first time gives 1 [Present](present.md), along with other rewards.
