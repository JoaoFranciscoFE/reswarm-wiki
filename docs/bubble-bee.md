**Bubble Bee** is a Blue [Epic bee](bees-epic.md).

Bubble Bee's favorite treat is [Blueberries](blueberry.md).

Bubble Bee likes the [Blue Flower Field](blue-flower-field.md) and the [Pine Tree Forest](pine-tree-forest.md). It dislikes the [Strawberry Field](strawberry-field.md).

## Stats

* Collects 10 [Pollen](pollen.md) in 4 seconds.
* Makes 160 [honey](honey.md) in 4 seconds.
* +15% [Movespeed](stats.md#Speed), +80 [Convert Amount](system-page.md#Convert_Amount), +2 [Attack](stats.md#Attack).
* 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](gifted-bee.md): x1.25 Bubble Pollen.

### Abilities

* **[Blue Bomb](ability-tokens.md#Bomb)** Collects 10 pollen from 13 surrounding blue [Flowers](flowers.md) (+10% pollen per level). Combo with other Bombs to increase power.
* **[[Passive:](passive-abilities.md#Gathering_Bubbles) [Gathering Bubbles]](bubble.md#Gathering_Bubbles)** 35% chance (50% if Gifted) to spawn a [bubble](bubble.md) when gathering. If any Player touches it, it pops, collecting 4 red/6 white/8 blue pollen (+15% per gifted blue bee type, +5% per bee level) from 29 nearby Flowers and causing them to grow.

## Trivia

* Bubble Bee is the blue counterpart of [Fire Bee](fire-bee.md).
* Bubble Bee emits a bubble effect, a trait also shared with [Honey Bee](honey-bee.md) and its gifted variant.
* Bubble Bee and [Tadpole Bee](tadpole-bee.md) are the only [bees](bees.md) that have the Gathering Bubbles passive ability.
* The [Bubble Mask](bubble-mask.md), which can be purchased in the [Blue HQ](blue-hq.md), has a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Bubble Bee.
* While gathering in the [Clover Field](clover-field.md), it has an uncommon chance to find a Sitting Green Shirt Bear [Sticker](sticker.md).
* In spite of this bee's name, Bubble Bee has nothing to do with [Bubble Bee Man](bubble-bee-man.md).
  * However, Bubble Bee Man did give out a [Bubble Bee Jelly](egg.md#Bubble_Bee_Jelly) in Beesmas 2018 and a [Bubble Bee Egg](egg.md#Bubble_Bee_Egg) in Beesmas 2020 and Beesmas 2021.
