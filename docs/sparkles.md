# Sparkles

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">A patch of flowers that has sparkles.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

**Sparkles** are an effect applied to certain patches of [flowers](flowers.md) that yields a token containing [Honey](honey.md), random [treats](treats.md) corresponding to the [field](fields.md) it is in, [Moon Charms](moon-charm.md), or rarely [Tickets](ticket.md), [Royal Jelly](royal-jelly.md), [Neonberry](neonberry.md), [Star Jelly](royal-jelly.md#Star_Jelly), or [Glitter](glitter.md) when harvested. When enough [pollen](pollen.md) is collected from a sparkling flower, it will yield a token for any player to collect. If it isn't collected within 10 seconds, the token will disappear.

The largest plants in the [Sunflower Field](sunflower-field.md), [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md), and [Pumpkin Patch](pumpkin-patch.md) will occasionally generate a face and blow bubbles 3 to 5 times on their respective fields, creating 13 patches of pollen that sparkle. The [Blue HQ's](blue-hq.md) Bubble Wand blows bubbles on the [Dandelion Field](dandelion-field.md), [Mushroom Field](mushroom-field.md), [Blue Flower Field](blue-flower-field.md), and [Clover Field](clover-field.md) in a similar fashion. Each aforementioned location can generate sparkles around every 45 minutes, individually.[1] Individual [fireflies](fireflies.md) that spawn during the [night](day-night-cycle.md) also leave behind sparkles on flowers they were on as they are chased off.

## Trivia

* In the [2018-07-11 update](updates.md#2018-07-11), the change to the size of the Clover Field was not taken into account, so the [Bubble Wand](bubble-wand.md) model on the Blue HQ could sometimes produce sparkles in the area where there are no flowers.
* Prior to the [2019-09-28 update](updates.md#2019-09-28), sparkles covered 4 flowers instead of 13.
* In the [2018-09-10 update](updates.md#2018-09-10), the chances of sparkles being created were doubled.
* The Bubble Wand model on the Blue HQ can leave sparkles right under the largest mushroom in the Mushroom Field, making it difficult to collect the rewards.
* Sparkles used to be called "spit" by the community, which came from the various faces looking like they were spitting on the field. [Onett](onett-developer.md) later on named this effect "sparkles" like it is today.

  

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> </figcaption> </figure>

  * According to Onett, the verb form for sparkles is ‘sparkletificate’.
* When disappearing, the eyes on a face close before fading away.
* Spirit Bear mentions the sparkles in her dialogue before you receive her 8th quest, remarking how the Sparkles are "gross" in terms of how they were created by the faces.
