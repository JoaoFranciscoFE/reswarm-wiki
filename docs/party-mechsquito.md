The **Party Mechsquito** is one of the mobs exclusive to Robo Party. It functions similarly to a regular [Mechsquito](mechsquito.md), but roams around a field instead of following the player around.

## Strategies

Since they are essentially the same as their normal counterparts, Mechsquitos, the same methods used for them can be followed.

* Party Mechsquitos aim for where the player is, not where the player will be. Because of this, moving around in circles will often result in Party Mechsquitos missing their attack.
* Stand near the Party Mechsquito, and this will allow your bees to lock on to the Party Mechsquito and quickly deal damage.
* [Vicious Bee](vicious-bee.md) and [Windy Bee](windy-bee.md) provide excellent crowd control, especially since Party Mechsquitos often come in pairs or packs. [Digital Bee](digital-bee.md) can also stun many Party Mechsquitos at once and allow them to take extra damage via [Mind Hack](ability-tokens.md#Mind_Hack).
* Unlike their normal counterparts, they stay in fields which can be used for the players advantage. Stand right outside the field they are in and your bees will attack it while it doesn't aim for you. Note that this can be a bit risky since they can hover around the other parts of the field and don't come close enough for your bees to target them.

## Trivia

* This is one of the first mobs to appear during a Robo Party if the player is just starting at the first ranks.
* This, [Party Cogturrets](party-cogturret.md), and [Party Mega Mechsquitos](party-mega-mechsquito.md) have the same health per level as their normal counterparts.
