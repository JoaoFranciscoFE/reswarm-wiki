# Loaded Dice

Loaded Dice

*"Boosts 3 random fields with 300% pollen for 15 minutes. A bit biased towards the field you're in..."*

COOLDOWN

3 Seconds

CAP

25

**Loaded Dice** is an [inventory item](items.md) added in the [2021-12-26 update](updates.md#2021-12-26). It boosts three random [fields](fields.md) with 300% [pollen](pollen.md) for 15 minutes, and the field the player is in has a biased chance, meaning it has a higher chance to boost the field the player is standing in. If the player uses it when there is another dice boost active, it will replace the last dice boosts and duration. It has a cooldown of 3 seconds.

## Bias Mechanism

To activate the bias, the player must be standing on a field. The first loaded dice gives a 25% chance for the first field picked to be the field the player is standing in. As more loaded dice is used, the chance will increase by 25%, maxing out at 100% where it is guaranteed. If the player leaves the field, the chance will reset.

## Ways to obtain

* Crafting via [Blender](blender.md) for:
  * 3 [Smooth Dice](smooth-dice.md)
  * 3 [Hard Waxes](hard-wax.md)
  * 3 [Oils](oil.md)
  * 1 [Glue](glue.md)
* As a rare reward from [Robo Bear Challenge](robo-bear-challenge.md) (Up to 3 **Loaded Dice**).
* As a Stack Reward for adding certain [stickers](sticker.md) to the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md):
  * Bomber Bee Bear rewards 1 **Loaded Dice**.
  * Triple Exclamation rewards 1 **Loaded Dice**.

### Drops

* As a very rare drop from the [Werewolf](werewolf.md).
* As a very rare drop from [Tunnel Bear](tunnel-bear.md).
* As a rare drop from Epic, Legendary, and Mythic [Puffshrooms](puffshroom.md).
* As a drop from certain [planters](planter.md):
  * [Festive Planter](festive-planter.md)
  * [Tacky Planter](tacky-planter.md) and [Petal Planter](petal-planter.md) has a chance to drop 1 **Loaded Dice** as a bonus drop.
    * Planting the [Tacky Planter](tacky-planter.md) in the [Mountain Top Field](mountain-top-field.md) may yield 1 **Loaded Dice** (36-day cooldown).
  * Planting the [Heat-Treated Planter](heat-treated-planter.md) in the [Mushroom Field](mushroom-field.md) may yield 1 **Loaded Dice** (~1 day cooldown).
  * Planting the [Hydroponic Planter](hydroponic-planter.md) in the [Blue Flower Field](blue-flower-field.md) may yield 1 **Loaded Dice** (~1 day cooldown).

### Quests

* Completing [Dapper Bear's](dapper-bear.md) 10th quest rewards 1 **Loaded Dice** and other items.
* Completing Dapper Bear's 15th quest rewards 3 **Loaded Dice** and other items.

### Token Location

* A token near the [Red HQ](red-hq.md)'s roof grants 1 **Loaded Dice**.

## Trivia

* If you some how roll another dice while a dice is already being rolled, this notification would pop up:

You are already rolling a dice

* This is the only dice to currently have a cap (25).
  * [Field Dice](field-dice.md) used to have a cap of 25 until the [2021-12-26 update](updates.md#2021-12-26).
* This and the [Smooth Dice](smooth-dice.md) are the only [items](items.md) which can produce more than 1 [field boost](field-boost.md) at a time, and produce more than x1 stack of field boost at a time.
* The fields shown on the dice are the [Sunflower Field](sunflower-field.md), the [Clover Field](clover-field.md), the [Coconut Field](coconut-field.md), the [Pumpkin Patch](pumpkin-patch.md), the [Pine Tree Forest](pine-tree-forest.md) and the [Blue Flower Field](blue-flower-field.md).
  * There also is an X icon on the front dice. It is presumed it's the "biased" slot for the field that the player is standing on.
* In real life, loaded dice are rigged dice designed to make one side show up more often than the rest, which correlates to the dice's in-game bias of the field your standing in.
