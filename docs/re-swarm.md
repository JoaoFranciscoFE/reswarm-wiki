# Re://:Swarm

**Re://:Swarm** is a [Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/) game made by **[ReplicatedEvents](https://www.roblox.com/users/4940357204/profile)**. It is a rebirth-style reimagining of an older version of [Bee Swarm Simulator](https://www.roblox.com/games/1537690962/) (made by [Onett](onett-developer.md)), rebuilt with new assets. The purpose of the game is to hatch [bees](bees.md) to make a swarm, collect [pollen](pollen.md), and make it into [honey](honey.md).

Re://:Swarm is a separate game from Bee Swarm Simulator. Much of this wiki was adapted from the Bee Swarm Simulator Wiki, so some pages still describe Bee Swarm Simulator and may not match Re://:Swarm exactly.

## Gameplay

New players first claim an unclaimed [hive](hive.md). After claiming, [Onett](onett.md) greets them with a Honeyday welcome, and once that conversation closes the game asks whether they want to do the [Tutorial](tutorial.md). The player can use their tool to collect pollen from any [Fields](fields.md) available. Their bag will store the pollen collected, and their bee(s) can convert the pollen into honey while the player is at their hive. The honey can be used to purchase new [tools](tools.md), Accessories, [Eggs](egg.md), and various other [Items](items.md) to progress further into the game.

The player can also receive [quests](quests.md) from NPCs, which mainly focus on collecting pollen and defeating [Mobs](mobs.md), but can also have other, more advanced objectives, like collecting [Ability tokens](ability-tokens.md) and [Goo](goo.md).

As a rebirth variant, Re://:Swarm has rebirth levels with their own rewards (for example, rebirth 35 rewards a Painter Bee egg).
