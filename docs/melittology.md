# Melittology

![Melittology](img/Melittology.png){ align=right width=150 }

**Melittology** is Re://:Swarm's research tree. It unlocks at [Rebirth 10](rebirths.md) and lets you spend [Research Points](research-points.md) on permanent upgrades, ending with the [Honey Hammer](honey-hammer.md) and upgrades for [The Supreme Saturator](the-supreme-saturator.md).

## How it works

* **Unlock:** you need **[Rebirth 10](rebirths.md)**. Until then the game shows "Reach Rebirth 10 to unlock Melittology". The research quests need Rebirth 10 too.
* **Opening it:** use the **Melittology Tree** tile in your Boosts bar, or the [Melittology Machine](melittology-machine.md) on the mountain top.
* **Paths:** you pick **one Color path** (Red, Blue or White) and **one Discipline path** (Bees, Tools or Abilities). Each path has 10 nodes in a line, and each node needs the one before it.
* **Costs:** tiers 1 to 9 cost **1** Research Point each and the tier 10 finale costs **5**, so a full path costs **14**.
* **Capstone:** once both of your paths are finished, the capstone costs **10** Research Points and gives you the [Honey Hammer](honey-hammer.md). The whole base tree costs 38.
* **Supreme paths:** after the capstone, three Supreme paths upgrade [The Supreme Saturator](the-supreme-saturator.md).
* **Respec:** free. You can reset your Color path, your Discipline path, both, or only your Supreme paths. Resetting a base path also removes the capstone, everything after it and the Honey Hammer. A Supreme-only reset keeps the capstone.
* **Rebirths** don't reset the tree.

## Research Points

![Research Points](img/Research_Points.png){ align=right width=60 }

[Research Points](research-points.md) come only from the **Melittology Research** quests, given by the [Melittology Quests](melittology-quest-giver.md) pad on top of the mountain. They're a chain of 25 ranks, and each rank needs the one before it. See [Melittology Research](melittology-research.md) for every rank's tasks.

* Ranks 1 to 20 give **40** Research Points in total: enough for the whole base tree (38) with 2 left over.
* Ranks 21 to 25, the **Saturation Studies**, give **36** more. Together with the 2 left over, that's exactly enough for one full Supreme path and two nodes of a second (see below).
* Rank 21 also gives the **Eureka!** [sticker](sticker.md), once per account.

## Capstone

![Honey Hammer](img/Honey_Hammer.png){ align=right width=60 }

**A Beginning to a end.** costs **10** Research Points and needs both of your paths fully researched. *"Master both active paths to unlock the Honey Hammer, the ultimate collector."* It adds the [Honey Hammer](honey-hammer.md) to your collectors. If the capstone is reset, the Honey Hammer is taken away again.

## Supreme paths

These open after the capstone and only work through [The Supreme Saturator](the-supreme-saturator.md). Their nodes cost 2, 4, 6, 8 and 12 Research Points, so a full path costs 32.

* At most **two** of the three Supreme paths can be active.
* Once one path goes past 3 nodes, the other is capped at 2. So 5 and 2 or 3 and 3 are allowed, but 4 and 3 is not.

### Supreme Red

*Turns the Saturator's spray into flames.*

| Tier | Node | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Ignition Spray** | 2 | Each spray has a chance to start 3-10 flames inside its ring. |
| 2 | **Soot Mist** | 4 | Each spray has a chance to darken a flame inside its ring. |
| 3 | **Standing Heat** | 6 | Flames inside the ring stop burning down while the sprinkler runs. |
| 4 | **Backdraft** | 8 | When a dark flame finally burns out it lights 2-3 more around it. |
| 5 | **Firestorm** | 12 | Every spray starts flames, and dark flames convert part of what they gather on the spot. |

### Supreme Blue

*Turns the Saturator's spray into bubbles.*

| Tier | Node | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bubble Seeding** | 2 | Each spray has a chance to blow 3-10 Golden Bubbles inside its ring. |
| 2 | **Skimming** | 4 | Each spray has a chance to cash in bubbles inside its ring without popping them. |
| 3 | **Slow Swell** | 6 | Bubbles left sitting inside the ring keep growing and hold more. |
| 4 | **Gold Rinse** | 8 | Each spray has a chance to turn ordinary bubbles inside the ring golden. |
| 5 | **Tide Lock** | 12 | Every spray cashes in every bubble in the ring, and bubbles stop expiring while it runs. |

### Supreme White

*Turns the Saturator's spray into the flowers themselves.*

| Tier | Node | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Grafting** | 2 | Each spray has a small chance to raise the tier of 3-10 flowers inside its ring. |
| 2 | **Deep Soak** | 4 | Flowers hit by a spray regrow faster for a while afterwards. |
| 3 | **Runoff** | 6 | Some of the pollen from sprayed flowers comes back as goo instead. |
| 4 | **Held Charge** | 8 | Flowers hit by a spray hold a bonus that pays out on the next collection. |
| 5 | **Full Bloom** | 12 | The whole ring stays at max flower tier while the sprinkler runs. |
