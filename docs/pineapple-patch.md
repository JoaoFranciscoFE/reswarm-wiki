The **Pineapple Patch** is a [field](fields.md) located past the [Brave Bee Gate](brave-bee-gate.md) and to the right of the [Pro Shop](pro-shop.md). It is protected by a level 4 [mantis](mantis.md) and a level 5 [rhino beetle](rhino-beetle.md).

This field can spawn [honey](honey.md), [pineapple](pineapple.md), [bitterberry](bitterberry.md), [neonberry](neonberry.md), [enzyme](enzymes.md), [ticket](ticket.md), [magic bean](magic-bean.md), [glue](glue.md) and [glitter](glitter.md) tokens when gathering [pollen](pollen.md).

This field grants Satisfying [Nectar](nectar.md) when a [planter](planter.md) is planted here.

[Bees](bees.md) that like the Pineapple Patch are [Shocked Bee](shocked-bee.md), [Diamond Bee](diamond-bee.md), [Lion Bee](lion-bee.md), [Gummy Bee](gummy-bee.md), and [Photon Bee](photon-bee.md). Bees that dislike the Pineapple Patch are [Vector Bee](vector-bee.md), [Cobalt Bee](cobalt-bee.md), and [Crimson Bee](crimson-bee.md).

A level 1-20 [Wild Windy Bee](wild-windy-bee.md) has the chance of spawning here.

This is a 33x23 field, containing 759 [flowers](flowers.md). The flowers are white, red, and blue, in sizes of single, double, and triple.

## Obtaining Boosts

* Using the [Field Booster](field-booster.md) may give a [Pineapple Patch Boost](field-boost.md). When it does, it will give +200% Pineapple Patch pollen for 15 minutes.

  

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 75px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The Pineapple Patch Boost icon.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

* Using [glitter](glitter.md) will add 100% Pineapple Patch pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if it isn't already maxed out.
* Using [field dice](field-dice.md), [smooth dice](smooth-dice.md), or [loaded dice](loaded-dice.md) may add +100% - +300% Pineapple Patch pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out.
* Pineapple Patch [winds](field-wind.md) can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md) or [codes](codes.md). They have a base of +20% instant Pineapple Patch conversion and give +5% instant Pineapple Patch conversion and +20% Pineapple Patch pollen for each stack. Pineapple Patch Winds can stack up to a total of 13 times.
* The [Moon Amulet](moon-amulet.md) can give a pollen boost for the Pineapple Patch between +1% and +150%.
* It can be boosted by the [Shell Amulet](shell-amulet.md). If it has a Pineapple Patch Boost, it can boost the field by +7% to +30% depending on the amulet rarity.
* Donating either the Menacing Mantis or Pineapple Patch Stamp [Sticker](sticker.md) to the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md) boosts the Pineapple Patch by 5% and 10% respectively.

## Trivia

* This is the biggest field in the game.
* The mobs in this field ([Rhino Beetle](rhino-beetle.md) and [Mantis](mantis.md)) are associated with blue, despite the field being white.
* There is a [pineapple](pineapple.md) token behind the pineapple closest to the shop. Collecting it grants the player five pineapples.
* There is a [ticket](ticket.md) token worth three tickets on top of the giant pineapple. It can be obtain by using the [Red Cannon](red-cannon.md) and the [Parachute](parachute.md) or [Glider](glider.md), or by jumping up from the side of the pineapple.
* There is a [glue](glue.md) token behind the biggest pineapple between the [Stump Field](stump-field.md).
* This was the third biggest field in the game before the 2018-07-11 update.
* This field is one of the three fields to have the word "Patch" in their name instead of "Field," along with the [Pumpkin Patch](pumpkin-patch.md) and the [Pepper Patch](pepper-patch.md).
  * The [Pine Tree Forest](pine-tree-forest.md) is another field without the word "Field" in it, having "Forest" in its name instead.
* This is one of six fields that [fireflies](fireflies.md) can travel to, with the others being [Bamboo Field](bamboo-field.md), [Cactus Field](cactus-field.md), [Rose Field](rose-field.md), [Spider Field](spider-field.md), and [Strawberry Field](strawberry-field.md).
* This is one of the fields that the [Stick Bug](stick-bug.md) can move to during the [Stick Bug Challenge](stick-bug-challenge.md).
* This field, along with the [Sunflower Field](sunflower-field.md) and the [Pumpkin Patch](pumpkin-patch.md), is the only field where an object may come to life and shoot sparkles.
* The biggest pineapple will occasionally grow a face, where it will blow bubbles at patches of flowers, producing [sparkles](sparkles.md) (in diamond formation) upon contact. Collecting pollen on the affected flowers can produce tickets, pineapples, [moon charm](moon-charm.md), [royal jelly](royal-jelly.md), [enzymes](enzymes.md), or honey tokens.
* While doing one of [Spirit Bear](spirit-bear.md)'s quests, Spirit Bear states that the Pineapple Patch was originally supposed to be a "Rhubarb Field". Instead, she and Stick Bug planted pineapples.
* This is the only field to be guarded by two mobs of different tiers (Rhino Beetle and Mantis), if you don't include the [Werewolf](werewolf.md) guarding the Pine Tree Forest as it merely happens to be located in the Werewolf's attack territory.
