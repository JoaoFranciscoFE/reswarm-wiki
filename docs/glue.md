# Glue

Glue

*"Grants x1.25 Bee Gather Pollen and Tools for 10 minutes."*

COOLDOWN

1 second

**Glue** is a craftable inventory item added in the [2018-11-25 update](updates.md#2018-11-25). Its purpose is as a creating material for accessories, or as a 10-minute boost that grants x1.25 [Bee Gather Pollen](system-page.md#Bee_Gather_Pollen) and [tools](system-page.md#Tool_Pollen) when used directly. The [buff](buffs-debuffs.md) cannot stack and will only reset its timer when another glue is used when one is currently active. Using a [Purple Potion](purple-potion.md) or [Super Smoothie](super-smoothie.md) will override the buff.

## Ways to Obtain

* Crafting it in the [Blender](blender.md) for 50 [Gumdrops](gumdrops.md) and 10 [Royal Jellies](royal-jelly.md).
* [Black Bear](black-bear.md) gives glue on the following quests:
  * All-Whitey Then gives 1 glue and other items.
  * Black Bear's repeatable quests occasionally reward glue.
* [Brown Bear](brown-bear.md) will give glue on the completion of these quests:
  * Brown Bear's 140th quest rewards 1 glue.
  * Brown Bear's 235th quest rewards 1 glue.
  * Brown Bear's 310th quest rewards 3 glues.
  * Brown Bear's 395th quest rewards 5 glues.
* [Science Bear](science-bear.md) gives glue on the following quests:
  * Chemical Analysis rewards 1 glue and other items.
  * Epistemological Endeavor gives 4 glues and other items.
* As a rare drop from [mobs](mobs.md) such as the [Spider](spider.md), [Werewolf](werewolf.md), [Puffshroom](puffshroom.md), [King Beetle](king-beetle.md), and [Coconut Crab](coconut-crab.md), or guaranteed from the [Stump Snail](stump-snail.md).
* As a drop from [Robo Bear Challenge](robo-bear-challenge.md) (The chances are affected by the round the challenge ends at)
* A drop from [Armored aphids](aphid.md#Armored_Aphids).
* Small chance of dropping from the [Dandelion Field](dandelion-field.md), [Pumpkin Patch](pumpkin-patch.md), and the [Stump Field](stump-field.md) in place of a honey token.
* A rare drop from [leaves](leaves.md) that appear in the [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md), [Dandelion Field](dandelion-field.md), [Pumpkin Patch](pumpkin-patch.md), [Stump Field](stump-field.md), or [Spider Field](spider-field.md).
* A rare drop when collecting [pollen](pollen.md) in the [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md), [Spider Field](spider-field.md), or the [Pumpkin Patch](pumpkin-patch.md).
* Rarely, a glue token can appear from collecting [goo](goo.md) from [flowers](flowers.md).
* You can claim 2-5 Glues from the [Glue Dispenser](glue-dispenser.md) depending on your Goo [Badge](badges.md). Hotshot rewards 2, Ace rewards 3, Master rewards 4, and Grandmaster rewards 5 Glues. The Glue Dispenser is located inside [Gummy Bear's Lair](gummy-bear-s-lair.md).
* As a drop from [Gummy Sprouts](sprout.md).
* As a rare drop from [Festive Gifts](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Gift) (if [Festive Bee](festive-bee.md) is [gifted](gifted-bee.md)).
* As a rare drop from [Festive Mark](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Mark).
* As a badge reward by donating to the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md).
* Matching a pair of glue in [Memory Match](memory-match.md).
* Capturing [Commando Chick](commando-chick.md) the 5th time drops 1 Glue.
* As a rare drop from the [Candy Planter](candy-planter.md), [Blue Clay Planter](blue-clay-planter.md), [Pesticide Planter](pesticide-planter.md) and [Festive Planter](festive-planter.md).
  * Planting the Candy Planter in the [Stump Field](stump-field.md) 3 times in a row will guarantee at least 10 **Glues** (Can give 20 glues, 1 week cooldown)
* A glue token is hidden behind the big pineapple in between the Pineapple Patch and the Stump Field.
* As a Stack Reward for adding certain [stickers](sticker.md) to the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md):
  * Right Facing Stump Snail rewards 1 glue.
  * Yellow Sticky Hand rewards 1 glue.
  * Glowering Gummy Bear Rewards 1 glue.
