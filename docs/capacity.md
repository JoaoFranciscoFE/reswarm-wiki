# Capacity

**Capacity** is a [mechanic](mechanics.md) that shows how much [pollen](pollen.md) the player can store at a time. Capacity can be increased in a variety of ways including [buffs](buffs-debuffs.md), [items](items.md), [stickers](sticker.md), and [amulets](amulet.md). Capacity can be increased by static amounts or by multipliers. Static increases can be shown simply through addition symbols (+) while multipliers can be shown through multiplication symbols (x) or percentages (%). The player's Capacity is displayed in the top-middle section, labelled as "Pollen" and also on the player's bag. The player can see how much their capacity is multiplied by looking for the Capacity Multiplier stat in the [System Page](system-page.md).

### Amulets

There are several amulets that add a static amount of Capacity for the player. Star Amulets give a Capacity Multiplier.

  * The Silver Stick Bug Amulet gives from +100,000 Capacity up to +150,000 Capacity.
  * The Gold Stick Bug Amulet gives from +150,000 Capacity up to +200,000 Capacity.
  * The Diamond Stick Bug Amulet gives from +200,000 Capacity up to +300,000 Capacity.
* The [Cog Amulet](cog-amulet.md) can give up to +250,000 Capacity depending on the tier.
  * The Bronze Cog Amulet gives from +22,500 Capacity up to +26,500 Capacity.
  * The Silver Cog Amulet gives from +45,000 Capacity up to +51,500 Capacity.
  * The Gold Cog Amulet gives from +90,000 Capacity up to +108,000 Capacity.

### Passives

There are two [passives](passive-abilities.md) that give a Capacity Multiplier. They can be obtained via a Diamond Star Amulet or Supreme Star Amulet. The Guiding Star Aura could also be obtained from [Onett's Lid Art](onett-s-lid-art.md) during Beesmas.

* The Guiding Star passive gives a x1.25 Capacity Multiplier.
  * The Guiding Star Aura gives x2.5 Capacity to the player who summoned the star and x1.25 Capacity to other players.
* The Star Shower passive give a x1.25 Capacity Multiplier.

### Buffs

There are several ways to get a buff to capacity such as inventory items, [ability tokens](ability-tokens.md), defeating [mobs](mobs.md), and redeeming [codes](codes.md).

* Using a [Purple Potion](purple-potion.md) gives a x1.25 Capacity Multiplier. The buff can also be given from certain codes.
* Using a [Super Smoothie](super-smoothie.md) gives a x1.5 Capacity Multiplier. The buff can also be given from certain codes.
* Using a [Marshmallow Bee](marshmallow-bee.md) gives +50% Capacity. The buff can also be given from certain codes.
* Using a [Snowflake](snowflake.md) or collecting the [Snowglobe Shake](ability-tokens.md#Snowglobe_Shake) ability token from the [Snowglobe](snowglobe.md) Beequip gives a x1.25 Capacity Multiplier.
* Using a [Glitched Drive](drives.md#Glitched_Drive) during the [Robo Bear Challenge](robo-bear-challenge.md) gives a x1.25 Capacity Multiplier.
* Making combos with [Reindeer Fetch](ability-tokens.md#Fetch) from [Puppy Bee](puppy-bee.md) with the [Reindeer Antlers](reindeer-antlers.md) Beequip equipped can give from a x1.01 Capacity Multiplier up to a x1.1 Capacity Multiplier.
* Defeating the [Mondo Chick](mondo-chick.md) for the Mondo Chick Blessing can give from a x1.02 Capacity Multiplier up to a x1.2 Capacity Multiplier.
* Balloon Blessing can give from a x1.03 capacity multiplier up to a x4 Capacity Multiplier.
* Galentine's Blessing (Beesmas only) gives +50% Capacity.
* Beesmas Repentance (Beesmas only) gives a x1.05 Capacity Multiplier.
* Defeating [Festive Nymphs](festive-nymph.md) during Beesmas will guarantee a Festive Nymph Blessing token drop which can stack up to give from +1,000 Capacity up to +250,000 Capacity. The buff can also be given from certain codes.
* Defeating Party Robots or completing a Robo Party during Beesmas can give Robo Party Blessing which can stack up to give from +1,000 Capacity up to +100,000 Capacity. The buff can also be given from certain codes.
* The Guiding Star Aura gives x2.5 Capacity to the player who summoned the star and x1.25 Capacity to other players.
* The Capacity Code buff, which is given by certain codes, gives a x2 Capacity Multiplier.

## Downgrades

There is a debuff that reduces the Capacity of the player.

* The Robo Bear Challenge Debuff reduces the Capacity by a depending on what round the player is on in the Robo Bear Challenge. At Round 1, it has a x0.1 Capacity Multiplier and increases by 0.015 per round to a total of a x0.46 Capacity Multiplier.

There are certain upgrades that can be bought with cogs during the Robo Bear Challenge which can downgrade Capacity.

* Common Upgrades:
  * Overfit: Red: This upgrades starts at x0.9 Capacity and maxes at 10 upgrades with x0.5 Capacity.
  * Sharpen: This upgrades starts at x0.95 Capacity and maxes at 10 upgrades with x0.75 Capacity.
* Rare Upgrades:
  * Crypto: This upgrades starts at x0.85 Capacity and maxes at 3 upgrades with x0.55 Capacity.
* Epic Upgrades:
  * Malware: This upgrades starts at x0.75 Capacity and maxes at 3 upgrades with x0.25 Capacity.
* Legendary Upgrades:
  * Overclock: This upgrade gives x0.75 Capacity.
