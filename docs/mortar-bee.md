**Mortar Bee** is a Red [Mythic bee](bees-mythic.md). It never gathers pollen or makes honey. Instead it fires mortar shells at the field.

Mortar Bee's favorite treat is [Strawberries](strawberry.md).

The game has no real picture of Mortar Bee yet. Its in-game icon is a placeholder, so the infobox uses the Happy Mortar Bee sticker for both tabs.

## Stats

* Does not collect [pollen](pollen.md) or make [honey](honey.md).
* 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](gifted-bee.md): x1.75 Bomb Pollen.

### Abilities

* **Passive: Mortar Shell.** Fires a shell every 9 seconds that collects 30 pollen (+10% per level) from 29 flowers. Each flower hit has a 2% chance (+0.1% per level, +2% if Gifted) to catch fire and a 1% chance (+0.05% per level, +0.5% if Gifted) to degrow. Each bee hit instantly converts 5% of your pollen.
* **Passive: Scorching Shells.**
* **Passive: Steam Engine.** Movespeed and gather speed go up by as much as 100% with Flame Heat.
* **Passive: Sluggish.**
* **Passive: Inspiring Shell** (Gifted only).

The game data does not include descriptions for Scorching Shells, Sluggish or Inspiring Shell yet.
