# Star Hall

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 268px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The entrance of the Star Hall.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 271px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The interior of the Star Hall.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

The **Star Hall** is located on the roof of the [Noob Shop](noob-shop.md). Players can reach it using the [Parachute](parachute.md) or the [Glider](glider.md) after using a [cannon](red-cannon.md). They can also glide down from the [Lion Bee Gate](lion-bee-gate.md). If the player has enough [Jump Power](system-page.md#Jump_Power), they can glide from the rock that is above [Brown Bear](brown-bear.md) to the edge of the Star Hall or jump off the top of the [Royal Jelly Dispenser](royal-jelly-dispenser.md) that is next to the [Clover Field](clover-field.md), the player can also use the stockings decoration during beesmas and glide to the edge of the star hall.

Outside of the Star Hall, there is a [Royal Jelly](royal-jelly.md) token to the right of the gate and a Green Cog with a [Robo Pass](robo-pass.md) token to the left of the gate. Inside, it has five types of [Star Amulet](star-amulet.md) Generators, an [Ant Pass](ant-pass.md) token (located behind the Supreme Star Amulet Generator), and the Royal Jelly Dispenser.

* **Royal Jelly Dispenser**: Allows players to claim 1 [Royal Jelly](royal-jelly.md) per [Gifted Bee](gifted-bee.md) in their [hive](hive.md) and activates the [Haste+](ability-tokens.md#Haste) boost. This boost lasts for exactly 60 seconds after using the machine. Naturally, players need at least one Gifted Bee in order to use the machine. It does not cost anything to use the machine, but there is a 22-hour cooldown.
* **Bronze Star Amulet Generator**: Players must discover 5 Gifted Bee types (not gifted bees in hive) to use this machine. It costs 25 million [honey](honey.md) to generate a Bronze Star Amulet and has a 1 in 100,000 chance to give the player a [Black Star Sticker](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) upon use.
* **Silver Star Amulet Generator**: Players must discover 10 Gifted Bee types (not gifted bees in hive) to use this machine. It costs 50 million honey to generate a Silver Star Amulet.
* **Gold Star Amulet Generator**: Players must discover 20 Gifted Bee types (not gifted bees in hive) to use this machine. It costs 250 million honey to generate a Gold Star Amulet and has a 1 in 100,000 chance to give the player a [Shining Star Sticker](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) upon use.
* **Diamond Star Amulet Generator**: Players must discover 30 Gifted Bee types (not gifted bees in hive) to use this machine. It costs 1 billion honey to generate a Diamond Star Amulet and has a 1 in 10,000 chance to give the player a [Cyan Star Sticker](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) upon use. It is also has a 1 in 1 million chance (1,000,000) to grant a [Star Cub](cub-buddy.md#Skins) upon summoning.
* **Supreme Star Amulet Generator**: Players must discover 40 Gifted Bee types (not gifted bees in hive) to use this machine. It costs 10 billion honey to generate a Supreme Star Amulet and an option to spend 500 billion honey (if the player has 500 billion concurrent honey) to guaranteed two Supreme Star Amulet [Passive Abilities](passive-abilities.md).

## Trivia

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 220px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The ant pass at the back of the Star Hall. Now behind the supreme star amulet generator.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

* The room gets its name from the [music](music.md) playing inside it: "[starhall](https://www.roblox.com/library/2062483297/starhall)".
* The cog outside the Star Hall is a hint to the in-game [code](codes.md), "Cog".
* This room contains 5 of the 6 Amulet Generators in the game, the last one being the [Moon Amulet Generator](moon-amulet-generator.md).
* Before the [2019-09-28 update](updates.md#2019-09-28), the Royal Jelly Dispenser inside the Star Hall gave out 1 [Royal Jelly](royal-jelly.md) per Gifted Bee type, rather than the number of gifted bees you have.
* Before the [2020-04-06 update](updates.md#2020-04-06), the [Ant Pass](ant-pass.md) token used to be behind the Diamond Star Amulet Generator. This is because of how the Diamond Star Amulet Generator sat in the location of the Supreme Star Amulet generator before the [2020-06-06 update](updates.md#2020-06-06).
* The Supreme Star Amulet Generator is currently the only Amulet Generator in the Star Hall that has not been released with the Star Hall itself, as the generator was released in the 2020-06-06 update.
* In order to use the Supreme Star Amulet Generator, the player must discover at least 1 Gifted [Mythic](bees-mythic.md) bee types (if not counting [Bear Bee](bear-bee.md)), because there are only 39 non-mythic bee types (zero Mythic bee type if including Bear Bee).
* The sound pitch when creating a Star Amulet decreases when going up in tiers.
* An ant pass can be found at the back of the Star Hall before the [2020-06-06](updates.md#2020-06-06) update
* If the player jumps on top of the Supreme Star Amulet Generator, the player can partly glitch outside the roof.
