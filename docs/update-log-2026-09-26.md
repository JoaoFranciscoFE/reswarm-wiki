# Equipment models and amulets

**Released:** September 26, 2026

New models for all equipment, Rebirth 39, and amulet rerolls with stat locking.

## What's new

* **All equipment has new models.**
* You can now go up to Rebirth 39.
* Reroll the Cog, Stick and Stump amulets by clicking their icon, paid in tickets:
    * **Stump:** unlocks at Rebirth 15 for 50k tickets (no stat locking).
    * **Stick:** unlocks at Rebirth 20 for 100k tickets (stat locking).
    * **[Cog](cog-amulet.md):** unlocks at Rebirth 25 for 250k tickets (stat locking).
* Amulet prices do not go up with each locked stat.
* Rebirths now give +1.5% BAR every 4 rebirths.

## Changes

* [Crimson Bee](crimson-bee.md) and [Cobalt Bee](cobalt-bee.md) give 1.25x of their color's pollen.
* [Mortar Bee](mortar-bee.md) changed from 15% attack to 1.75x bomb pollen.
* Removed the First Edition pack.
* The [Shell Amulet](shell-amulet.md) still rolls its tier from your kills.

## Fixes

* Fixed about 48 stickers being unobtainable.
* Fixed some stickers being awarded from the wrong place.
