# Neonberry

![Neonberry](img/Neonberry.png){ align=right width=150 }

Neonberry

*"Increases bond with a bee by 500 and makes it Radioactive for a while."*

A **Neonberry** is a type of [treat](treats.md) that, when fed to a [bee](bees.md), raises its [bond](bond.md) by 500 and makes it [radioactive](radiation.md) for 11 minutes. This is the only treat to have guaranteed chance of making the bee radioactive. These are not the favorite treat of any bee.

## Ways to Obtain

* As a reward from the [Ant Challenge](ant-challenge.md).
* As a common reward from [Robo Bear Challenge](robo-bear-challenge.md).
* As a Stack Reward for adding certain stickers to the Sticker Stack:
  * [Wobbly Looker Bee Sticker](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) rewards 3 neonberries.
  * [Traffic Light Sticker](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) rewards 3 neonberries.
  * [Red Beesmas Light Stickerrewards](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) 3 neonberries.
  * [Green Beesmas Light Sticker](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) rewards 3 neonberries.
  * [Yellow Beesmas Light Sticker](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) rewards 3 neonberries.
  * [Blue Beesmas Light Sticker](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) rewards 3 neonberries.
  * [Atom Symbol Sticker](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) rewards 5 neonberries.

### Drops

* Has a low chance of dropping from [rhino beetles](rhino-beetle.md), [ladybugs](ladybug.md), [mantises](mantis.md), [King Beetle](king-beetle.md), [Stump Snail](stump-snail.md), [Mondo Chick](mondo-chick.md), and [Coconut Crab](coconut-crab.md).
* Has a low chance of appearing when harvesting [Sparkles](sparkles.md).
* Farming in the [Blue Flower Field](blue-flower-field.md), [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md), [Mountain Top Field](mountain-top-field.md), and the [Pine Tree Forest](pine-tree-forest.md) has a rare chance to drop neonberries.
  * Harvesting [Leaves](leaves.md) in these fields may also yield neonberries.
* As a rare drop from a [Festive Mark](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Mark).
* As a gift from a [cub buddy](cub-buddy.md).
* As an uncommon drop from all [sprouts](sprout.md).
  * Debug sprouts are guaranteed to drop neonberries.
* As a rare drop from [Mythic Meteors](mythic-meteor-shower.md).
* As an uncommon drop from [Puffshrooms](puffshroom.md).
* As a drop from [planters](planter.md).
  * The [Pesticide Planter](pesticide-planter.md) drops extra neonberries in the [Stump Field](stump-field.md), [Mountain Top Field](mountain-top-field.md), and the [Coconut Field](coconut-field.md).
  * Planting the [Pesticide Planter](pesticide-planter.md) in the [Stump Field](stump-field.md), then the [Mountain Top Field](mountain-top-field.md), then the [Coconut Field](coconut-field.md) results in at least 25 neonberries (1 week cooldown).

### Quests

* Black Bear's Triple Treck gives 1 neonberry.
