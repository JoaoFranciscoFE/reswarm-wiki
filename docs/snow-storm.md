# Snow Storm

![Snow Storm](img/Snow_Storm.png){ align=right width=150 }

The following content has been removed from the game. The contents below may be archival, but feel free to edit below.

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:Error mw:File/Frameless"><figcaption>An active Snow Storm.</figcaption></figure>

A **Snow Storm** is a server-wide event that can be summoned by using the [Snow Machine](snow-machine.md) or randomly in the game. It is similar to a [Honeystorm](honeystorm.md), but spawns [Snowflakes](snowflake.md) instead. A Snow Storm lasts for 20 seconds.

When a player uses the Snow Machine to summon a Snow Storm, a server-wide announcement states:
❄️ {Username} has activated the Snow Machine! ❄️

[Snowflakes](snowflake.md) will start to fall rapidly from the sky on every field. There is no audio at the beginning of a Snow Storm, unlike a Honeystorm or [Mythic Meteor Shower](mythic-meteor-shower.md).

When a Snow Storm is randomly summoned, the message states: 
❄️ A Snow Storm has begun... ❄️

## Gallery

Occasionally, Onett would spawn a Snow Storm, usually under an alias. This is similar to how he globally summons Mythic Meteor Showers, [nighttime](day-night-cycle.md#Night), special [sprouts](sprout.md), and [mobs](mobs.md).

## Trivia

* Like Honeystorms and Mythic Meteor Showers, Snow Storms can light up the [Werewolf's Cave](werewolf-s-cave.md) enough to see the [Cave Monsters](cave-monster.md) and the [Gold Egg](egg.md#Gold_Egg) at the end of the cave.
  * Unlike Honeystorms, Snow Storms don't spawn snowflake tokens in the [Ant Field](ant-challenge.md).
* During Beesmas 2020, 2021, 2022, 2024, and 2025, giving [Spirit Bear](spirit-bear.md) a [Present](present.md) would trigger a Snow Storm after her dialogue finishes and the [ornament](ornaments.md) was given.
  * Finishing Spirit Bear's Beesmas quest would also summon a Snow Storm.
* The [Snowflakes](snowflake.md) keep falling for a few seconds after a Snow Storm ends because it takes time for the [Snowflakes](snowflake.md) to reach the ground.
* If a Snow Storm is summoned during [nighttime](day-night-cycle.md#Night), the white fog will stay even after the Snow Storm has ended until nighttime ends.
