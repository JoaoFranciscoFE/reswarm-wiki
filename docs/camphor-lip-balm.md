The **Camphor Lip Balm** is a [level](bond.md) 12 [Beequip](beequip.md). It has an equip limit of one. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to [Cool](cool-bee.md), [Stubborn](stubborn-bee.md), [Commander](commander-bee.md), [Bubble](bubble-bee.md) and [Crimson](crimson-bee.md) Bees.
