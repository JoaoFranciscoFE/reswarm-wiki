# Rebirth 35 and Melittology paths

![Rebirth 35 and Melittology paths](img/Dapper_Bear.png){ .update-log-icon }

**Released:** September 11, 2026

Rebirth 35, three new Melittology paths and five new stickers.

## What's new

* **Rebirth 35** and its quest from [Dapper Bear](dapper-bear.md).
* **5 new Melittology quests**, needed for the 3 new paths after Honey Hammer: Supreme White, Supreme Red and Supreme Blue. You can go 5-2-0 (max one path and take 2 upgrades in another). The path also changes the color of your [sprinklers](sprinklers.md).
* The Melittology machine has a model now.
* Buy Max in the Melittology tree.
* You can add stickers in the middle of a [sticker stack](sticker-stack.md) to refresh the buff.
* **5 new stickers** (4 obtainable now, 1 in a future update): Joyous Gubbee, Menacing Crimbolt Bee, Flying Mortar Bee, Eureka! and Red Pointing Arrow.
* Craftable Festive Sprouts.

## Fixes

* Fixed stickers quickly closing when inspected.
* Fixed Robo Party respawning mobs.
