The **Basic Bee Gate**, also called the **5 Bee Gate**, is the first gate a player is tasked with unlocking. The gate requires you to own 5 [bees](bees.md) or more to pass through.

It gets its name from the large [Basic Bee](basic-bee.md) that's on top of it.

The audio known as "[bpatrol](music.md)" plays in the daytime while you are past this gate.

## Trivia

* This is the only gate to have more than one gate directly leading from it, with those being the [Brave Bee Gate](brave-bee-gate.md) and the [Honey Bee Gate](honey-bee-gate.md).
* This gate requires the least amount of bees to pass through.
* This and the [Ant Gate](ant-gate.md) are the only gates that lead to a zone where there is nothing that can be purchased with [Honey](honey.md).
* [Bubble Bee Man](bubble-bee-man.md) used to be behind the Global Top Battlers leaderboard. However, he was removed after Beesmas 2018.
* Before the 2020-06-06 update, [Honey Bee](honey-bee-npc.md) was located next to the ramp.
* Underneath the ramp, on the side facing the [Blue HQ](blue-hq.md), there is a [ticket](ticket.md) token, previously a Plastic Egg.
* Due to a glitch, the [Onett statue](easter-eggs.md#Onett_NPC), located near the hives could have been seen near this gate. This glitch is now patched.
* This gate is the only gate that cannot have a [Wild Windy Bee](wild-windy-bee.md) spawn in any of its fields (without the assistance of Onett, during Beesmas or flying there from another field) (not counting the [Ant Gate](ant-gate.md) as it has only a challenge field or [Bear Gate](bear-gate.md) as the Bear Gate does not have any fields).
* This gate is the only gate that doesn't teleport you back to the spawn point if you don't have the required bees.
* During Beesmas, the Basic Bee model is seen wearing a festive Santa hat.
  * This also makes the Basic Bee Gate the only gate that changes the bee's appearance during an event.
