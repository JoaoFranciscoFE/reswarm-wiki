# Festive Bean

Festive Bean

*"Plants a Festive Sprout in the field you're standing in."*

COOLDOWN

2 hours

A **Festive Bean** is a special variant of the [Magic Bean](magic-bean.md) that always plants a [Festive Sprout](sprout.md) in a [field](fields.md). When harvested, it grants [Tickets](ticket.md), crafting materials, and unique tokens that are commonly found in [festive gifts](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Gift) in abundant quantities. There is a 120-minute (2-hour) cooldown between planting Festive Sprouts. Only the player who planted can receive the [Festive Blessing](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Blessing) and [Beesmas Cheer](ability-tokens.md#Beesmas_Cheer) buffs to avoid overuse. When a player plants a Festive Sprout, the following message appears, reading:
"🌱 {Username} has planted a Festive Sprout 🌱

## Ways to Obtain

* An exceptionally rare chance to obtain a **Festive Bean** from a [Festive Gift](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Gift). The [Festive Bee](festive-bee.md) that made the gift does not need to be [gifted](gifted-bee.md).
* An extremely low chance to drop from a [Festive Mark](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Mark).
* A very rare drop from a [Festive Planter](festive-planter.md).
* Purchasing the Snow Queen Bundle in [Bee Bear's Catalog](bee-bear-s-catalog.md) grants 1 **Festive Bean** + other stuff.
* Purchasing the Tempting Ticket Pack in [Bee Bear's Catalog](bee-bear-s-catalog.md) grants 5 **Festive Beans** + other stuff.
* Purchasing the Feelin' Festive Pack in the [Robux Shop](robux-shop.md) for 800 Robux grants 5 **Festive Beans** + other stuff.
* A very rare reward from the [Winter Memory Match](memory-match.md).
* A very rare drop from the [Stockings](stockings.md).
* An extremely rare drop from [Onett's Lid Art](onett-s-lid-art.md).

## Trivia

* At the end of Beesmas 2019, tokens from Festive Sprouts became unshareable. However, they are now shareable again; however, the "Festive Blessing" and "Beesmas Cheer" buffs are only obtainable for the player who plants it..
  * If summoned by [Onett](onett-developer.md) (or one of his many aliases), a Festive Sprout's [Festive Blessing](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Blessing), and [Beesmas Cheer](ability-tokens.md#Beesmas_Cheer) can be shared between all the players. They cannot be grabbed by abilities that collect personal tokens (e.g. [Token Link](ability-tokens.md#Token_Link) or [Triangulate](ability-tokens.md#Triangulate)) as the tokens do not belong to any player.
* This bean is the only variant of [Magic Beans](magic-bean.md).
* This and the [Marshmallow Bee](marshmallow-bee.md) are the only event items that can be donated to the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md).
* The **Festive Bean** is the only way for a Festive Sprout to be planted, with the exception of Onett planting them.
* The **Festive Bean** is the item with the longest cooldown in the game, being 2 hours.
* This and the [Gingerbread Bear](gingerbread-bear.md) are the only event items that are infinitely renewable outside of an event.
* This item cannot be used during the [Robo Bear Challenge](robo-bear-challenge.md).
* Festive Beans are the only way to get [Snowflakes](snowflake.md) outside of Beesmas, though they won't appear in the player's inventory even if they pick up the tokens.
  * They will be visible if [Snowflakes](snowflake.md) are already in the player's hotbar.
* When summoned, the server-wide sound that plays is the same as [Festive Bee's](festive-bee.md) [Festive Gift](ability-tokens.md).
