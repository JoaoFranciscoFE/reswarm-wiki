**Stubborn Bee** is a Colorless [Rare bee](bees-rare.md).

Stubborn Bee's favorite treat is [Strawberries](strawberry.md) .

Stubborn Bee likes the [Dandelion Field](dandelion-field.md) and the [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md). It dislikes the [Rose Field](rose-field.md).

## Stats

* Collects 10 pollen in 4 seconds.
* Makes 80 [Honey](honey.md) in 3 seconds.
* -15% [movespeed](stats.md#Speed), +25% convert speed, +1 [attack](stats.md#Attack).
* 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](gifted-bee.md): +15% [Ability Token](ability-tokens.md) Lifespan.

## Abilities

* **[Pollen Mark](ability-tokens.md#Mark)** Marks a random area on the [field](fields.md) for 7 seconds (+0.25s per level) that increases all pollen up to 50% while you stand in it. Stacks up to 3 times.

## Trivia

* Stubborn Bee was one of the two bees added in the [2018-11-25 update](updates.md#2018-11-25), the other being [Carpenter Bee](carpenter-bee.md).
* Stubborn Bee and [Looker Bee](looker-bee.md) are the only Rare bees added after the game launch.
* The gifted hive bonus (+15% ability token lifespan) used to be broken. The player could have a Gifted Stubborn Bee in their hive, but the ability token lifespan would not apply. This was fixed on an unknown update in February 2021.
* Stubborn Bee is the only Colorless Rare Bee that does not have its own guard in the [Pro Shop](pro-shop.md). This is because it was added in the [2018-11-25](updates.md#2018-11-25) update, after the guards were added.
