**Looker Bee** is a Colorless [Rare bee](bees-rare.md).

Looker Bee's favorite treat is [Sunflower Seeds](sunflower-seed.md).

Looker Bee likes the [Clover Field](clover-field.md) and the [Mountain Top Field](mountain-top-field.md). It dislikes the [Sunflower Field](sunflower-field.md) and the [Pepper Patch](pepper-patch.md).

## Stats

* Collects 13 [pollen](pollen.md) in 4 seconds.
* Makes 160 [honey](honey.md) in 4 seconds.
* +80 [convert amount](system-page.md#Convert_Amount), +3 [gather amount](stats.md#Gather_Amount), +5% [Critical Chance](critical-hits.md).
* 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](gifted-bee.md): +25% [Critical Power](system-page.md#Critical_Power).

### Abilities

* **[Focus](ability-tokens.md#Focus)** Grants +3% [Critical Chance](system-page.md#Critical_Chance) for 20 seconds. Stacks up to 10 times. [Critical Hits](critical-hits.md) increase pollen and damage by 100%.

## Trivia

* Looker Bee was the "oldest designed character",[1] not necessarily in the form of a bee. It had the signature eyes, was green, and had two little legs.[2]
* Looker Bee was the first [bee](bees.md) to have a [royal jelly](royal-jelly.md#Royal_Jelly_Variant) only turning a bee into it. The jelly was obtainable by using the [code](codes.md), 'ThoseEyes' (expired).
* The [Looker Guard](looker-guard.md), which can be bought in the [Pro Shop](pro-shop.md), has a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Looker Bee.
* Looker Bee used to have the same gifted hive bonus as [Tabby Bee](tabby-bee.md) before the nerf on 2021-12-26 update
* Looker Bee was one of the first bees that were added to the game in an [update](updates.md), along with [Commander Bee](commander-bee.md), [Music Bee](music-bee.md), and [Photon Bee](photon-bee.md).
* Looker Bee and [Stubborn Bee](stubborn-bee.md) are the only Rare bees that were added after the game's launch.
* Looker Bee's skin is the same as Commander Bee's skin.
* Looker Bee is the only bee that dislikes Sunflower Field.
* Looker Bee's field preferences are based on liking good vantage points.[3]
  * The reason Looker Bee likes the Mountain Top Field might be because high places have very good views from above, and possibly for a similar reason as the Clover Field.
  * The reason Looker Bee dislikes the Sunflower Field is because the sunflowers obstruct its view.
