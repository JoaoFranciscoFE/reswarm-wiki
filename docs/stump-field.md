The **Stump Field** is a field that was added in the 2018-11-25 update and is located past the [Brave Bee Gate](brave-bee-gate.md) next to the [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md).

This field can spawn [honey](honey.md), red [boost](ability-tokens.md#Boost), blue boost, [treat](treat.md#Treat), [gumdrop](gumdrops.md), [glue](glue.md), [magic bean](magic-bean.md), [ticket](ticket.md) and [blue extract](blue-extract.md) tokens when collecting [pollen](pollen.md).

[Bees](bees.md) that like the Stump Field are [Bumble Bee](bumble-bee.md), [Exhausted Bee](exhausted-bee.md), [Tadpole Bee](tadpole-bee.md), and [Gummy Bee](gummy-bee.md). Bees that dislike the Stump Field are [Hasty Bee](hasty-bee.md), [Baby Bee](baby-bee.md), and [Spicy Bee](spicy-bee.md).

This field grants Motivating [Nectar](nectar.md) when a [planter](planter.md) is planted here.

This field contains 563 [flowers](flowers.md). The flowers are white, red, and blue, and the only natural size is triple.

## Possible Events

* [Stump Snail](stump-snail.md) protects the field by circling around the circumference of the tree stump but never attacks you directly. While the Stump Snail is still alive, your Bees will prioritise damaging it rather than collecting pollen/spawning tokens which collect pollen (bombs, boosts, etc.).
* When there is a [Stick Bug Challenge](stick-bug-challenge.md) active, if [Stick Bug](stick-bug.md) is in Mountain Top Field, Bamboo Field or Pineapple Patch, he may summon a defense totem.

## Obtaining Boosts



* Using the [Blue Field Booster](blue-field-booster.md) may give a Stump [Field Boost](field-boost.md), if the Stump Snail is on respawn cooldown. When it does, it will give +100% Stump Field pollen for 15 minutes.
* Using [glitter](glitter.md) will add 100% Stump Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if it isn't already maxed out.
* Using [field dice](field-dice.md), [smooth dice](smooth-dice.md), or [loaded dice](loaded-dice.md) may add +100% - +300% Stump Field pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out and is based on the type of dice used.
* Stump [Field Winds](field-wind.md) can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md) or [codes](codes.md). They have a base of +20% instant Stump Field conversion and give +5% instant Stump Field conversion and +10% Stump Field pollen for each stack for 30 minutes. Stump Field Winds can stack up to a total of 15 times.
* It can be boosted by the [Shell Amulet](shell-amulet.md), which boosts the field by +10% to +65% depending on the amulet's rarity. All Shell Amulets are guaranteed to give this buff.
* Activating the [Guiding Star](passive-abilities.md#Guiding_Star) passive can summon a guiding star to this field if it is one of the player's five least collected fields.
* Using [Onett's Lid Art](onett-s-lid-art.md) could summon a guiding star to this field.

### The methods below are no longer possible.

* Giving a [present](present.md) to [Onett](onett.md) gave Stump Field Boost x1 and other boosts and [items](items.md).
* Purchasing a Stump Field [Market Boost](buffs-debuffs.md#From_Areas) from the [Boost Market](boost-market.md) will give x1.5 Stump [Field Capacity](field-capacity.md), x1.25 Stump Field Pollen, x1.1 [Goo](system-page.md#Goo), and x1.25 [Convert Rate At Hive](system-page.md#Hive_Convert_Rate) for 30 minutes.
* Redeeming certain expired codes:
  * MoreTime (Gave Stump Field Boost x1 + other stuff).
  * 2MFavorites (Gave Stump Field Boost x2 + other stuff).
  * Reboooot (Gave Stump Field Boost x1 + other stuff).
  * Willgoold (Gave Stump Field Boost x1 + other stuff).
  * GravyGoodies (Gave Stump Field Boost x1 + other stuff).
  * RobziRampage (Gave Stump Field Boost x1 + other stuff).
  * FrozenBugReboot (Gives Stump Field Code Buff and Stump Field Boost x4 + other stuff).

## Trivia

* Before the 2019-09-28 update, the Stump Field was originally a more balanced colored field. It was updated to act as a blue-oriented counterpart to the [Pepper Patch](pepper-patch.md).
* This is the first field the player will encounter that only has triple flowers naturally, the second one being the Mountain Top Field. Third being the [Coconut Field](coconut-field.md) and fourth being Pepper Patch.
* This is the only field where gumdrops can be obtained from [leaves](leaves.md).
* This and the [Hub Field](hub-field.md) are the only non-quadrilateral fields in the game.
  * Before the 2024-01-12 Update, the Stump Field was the only non-quadrilateral field in the game.
* This is the only blue field where [blueberries](blueberry.md) cannot spawn naturally.
  * This field is also the only blue field that has red flowers.
* This, the Ant Field and the [Hub Field](hub-field.md) are the only fields where [Wild Windy Bee](wild-windy-bee.md) cannot spawn in nor fly to.
  * However, Onett can summon Wild Windy Bees in this field. If they are defeated, they will fly in place until their timer is up as there is no flight pattern programmed for Stump Field Windy Bees.
* This, the Coconut Field, the Pepper Patch, the [Ant Field](ant-challenge.md) and the [Hub Field](hub-field.md) are the only fields that were added after the game's first release.
* Stump Field, Coconut Field, and [Spider Field](spider-field.md)'s caves have the same appearance.
  * No special rewards can be found in the Stump Snail's Cave, unlike the [Werewolf's Cave](werewolf-s-cave.md) and the [Coconut Cave](coconut-cave.md), making it closer to the Spider Field's cave.
* There is a [glue](glue.md) token between the large pineapple and the base of the stump.
* According to [Black Bear](black-bear.md), there used to be a gigantic tree that blocked the sunlight and didn't let the plants and flowers grow. Then one day, a huge bear came and cut the tree down. A field then grew on the stump of the tree, which many assumed is what the Stump Field is. It is unknown which bear cut the tree down in the first place.
  * [Spirit Bear](spirit-bear.md) also climbed the tree before it was cut down and met [Windy Bee](windy-bee.md). This is said when getting the quest, "The Sky Over The Stump".
* This field is one of three fields with no decorations in it, indicating what field it is. The other two fields to do this are the Ant Field, the [Mountain Top Field](mountain-top-field.md) and the Hub Field.
  * This field gets its name from the stump it sits on top of instead.
* During Beesmas 2019 and 2020, there was an [obby](obstacle-courses.md#Mythic_Present_Obby) in a hole to the left of the Stump Field. Completing the obby would lead to the [Purple Gift Box](gift-boxes.md).
  * This has been replaced by [Dapper Bear's Shop](dapper-bear-s-shop.md).
* This is the only field that can be completely covered by a sprinkler, that being [The Supreme Saturator](the-supreme-saturator.md).
* This Field is currently the third smallest field in the game, only being behind the [Pumpkin Patch](pumpkin-patch.md) and the Ant Field.
* Because the field has a square hitbox, it is possible to place planters on the outer rim of the field.
  * Harvesting a planter utilizing this glitch will cause the loot tokens spawn on the closest flowers in the field.
* This is the only field in the game with the flowers being arranged into a circle, while others being rectangles or squares.
