# Enzymes

Enzymes

*"Grants +10% Instant Conversion and x1.25 Convert Rate for 10 minutes."*

COOLDOWN

1 second

**Enzymes** are an inventory item that was added in the [2018-11-25 update](updates.md#2018-11-25). When used, it grants +10% [Instant Conversion](system-page.md#Instant_Conversion) and x1.25 [Convert Rate](system-page.md#Convert_Rate) for 10 minutes when activated. Using another **Enzymes** will not stack them, but will reset the timer. **Enzymes** can also be used for crafting other accessories or items. Using a [Super Smoothie](super-smoothie.md) will override the buff.

## Ways to Obtain

* Crafting it via [Blender](blender.md) for:
  * 50 [Pineapples](pineapple.md)
  * 10 [Royal Jellies](royal-jelly.md)
* As a potential match from all [Memory Matches](memory-match.md).
* The [Stick Bug Challenge](stick-bug-challenge.md) includes other sources of **Enzymes** on top of rewarding **Enzymes** upon completion:
  * Stick Bug can drop **Enzymes**.
  * [Defense Totems](stick-bug-challenge.md#Defense_Totem) can drop **Enzymes**.
* Redeeming certain valid [Codes](codes.md).
* As a Stack Reward for adding certain [Stickers](sticker.md) to the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md):
  * Honey Bee Bear rewards 1 **Enzymes**.
  * Panicked Science Bear rewards 1 **Enzymes**.
  * Forward Facing Spider rewards 1 **Enzymes**.
  * Standing Caterpillar rewards 1 **Enzymes**.
  * Yellow Hi rewards 1 **Enzymes**.
  * Taunting Doodle Person rewards 1 **Enzymes**.
  * Orange Step Array rewards 1 **Enzymes**.
  * Orange Green Tri Deco rewards 1 **Enzymes**.

### Drops

* As a drop from the [Armored Aphid](aphid.md#Armored_Aphid), [King Beetle](king-beetle.md), [Tunnel Bear](tunnel-bear.md), and the [Coconut Crab](coconut-crab.md).
* As a rare drop from a [scorpion](scorpion.md) and the [spider](spider.md).
* Farming in the [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md) and the [Spider Field](spider-field.md) may rarely yield **Enzymes**.
  * Harvesting [leaves](leaves.md) in those fields may also yield **Enzymes**
* As a rare drop from [sprouts](sprout.md) (the odds increase depending on the rarity of the sprout and if the sprout is in the Pineapple Patch).
* As a drop from [Stick Bug](stick-bug.md), [stick nymphs](stick-nymph.md), or [defense totems](stick-bug-challenge.md#Defense_Totem).
* As a drop from a [Festive Gift](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Gift) (if [Festive Bee](festive-bee.md) is [gifted](gifted-bee.md)) or [Festive Mark](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Mark).
* As a rare gift from a [cub buddy](cub-buddy.md).
* As a drop from [planters](planter.md).
  * Planters in [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md) may drop **Enzymes**.
  * The [Heat-Treated Planter](heat-treated-planter.md) drops **Enzymes** as a bonus drop.
    * Planting it in [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md) and [Pepper Patch](pepper-patch.md) may make it drop 100 **Enzymes** (unknown cooldown).
      * Planting it in [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md) three times in a row guarantees 100 **Enzymes** (unknown cooldown).
* As a drop from [Stockings](stockings.md).

### Quests

* [Black Bear](black-bear.md) gives **Enzymes** on the following quests:
  * The 15 Bee Zone gives 1 **Enzymes** and other items.
  * Black Bear will occasionally give **Enzymes** following the completion of his repeatable quests.
* [Brown Bear](brown-bear.md)'s 45th, 115th, 130th, 165th, and 260th quests give 1 **Enzymes**, while his 230th quest gives 3 **Enzymes**.
* [Science Bear](science-bear.md) gives **Enzymes** on the following quests:
  * Breaking Down Badges gives 1 **Enzymes** and other items.
  * Meditating On Phenomenon gives 1 **Enzymes** and other items.
  * The Power Of Information gives 3 **Enzymes** and other items.
  * Epistemological Endeavor gives 4 **Enzymes** and other items.
* [Polar Bear](polar-bear.md) has a chance to give **Enzymes** after completing a quest.
* [Spirit Bear](spirit-bear.md) gives enzymes on the following quests:
  * Rhubarb That Could Have Been gives 5 **Enzymes** and other items.
  * Dancing With Stick Bug gives 25 **Enzymes** and other items.

### Token Locations

* There is an **Enzymes** token in the bottom left corner of [Gummy Bear's Lair](gummy-bear-s-lair.md).
* Right beside [Night Memory Match](memory-match.md#Night_Memory_Match) inside the [Bear Gate](bear-gate.md).
* There is an **Enzymes** token in the tunnel that can be accessed through the hole above the [Coconut Dispenser](coconut-dispenser.md).

## Trivia

* **Enzymes** used to look like pills. However, Roblox removed them because they appeared to have violated the Roblox Terms Of Service. They were later re-created, published under the name "Pineapple Candies" and with the appearance they have currently.
* If multiple **Enzymes** are used in a [Blender's](blender.md) crafting recipe, the chat log displaying the lost materials would display "Enzymess" adding an extra 's' instead of the proper plural. This small bug applies to [Gumdrops](gumdrops.md) and various other items.
