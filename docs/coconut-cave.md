# Coconut Cave

![Coconut Cave](img/places/Coconut_Cave.png){ .wiki-photo }



The **Coconut Cave** is a cave located next to the [Coconut Field](coconut-field.md). To access the cave, the player is required to defeat the [Coconut Crab](coconut-crab.md) when it respawns out of the cave, If the player has not defeated the Coconut Crab yet and attempts to enter the cave (without actually standing in the field), they will be teleported back to the spawn place. There is also an invisible barrier on top of the Cave to prevent players from entering the cave when the Coconut Crab is not defeated yet, this is used to avoid lagging players from entering the cave without Coconut Crab being defeated.

The interior contains the Fastest Crab Slayers Leaderboard, the [Coconut Dispenser](coconut-dispenser.md), and a [shop](shops.md) where the player can buy the [Coconut Clogs](coconut-clogs.md), the [Coconut Canister](coconut-canister.md) and the [Coconut Belt](coconut-belt.md).

## Trivia

* If the player is in the Coconut Cave when the Coconut Crab respawns, they will be teleported the same way if they tried to bypass a gate without meeting the requirements. The teleport area also encompasses the roof of the cave. This can be avoided by going into the hole above the Coconut Dispenser.
* There is a hole above the Coconut Dispenser that leads to a tunnel containing 4 tokens: one [enzyme](enzymes.md), 5 [tickets](ticket.md), 5 [jelly beans](jelly-beans.md), and a [Robo Pass](robo-pass.md).
* This is the only shop that is guarded by a mob.
* This shop, [Robo Bear’s Shop](robo-bear-s-shop.md), [Ticket Tent](ticket-tent.md), and Dapper Bear’s Shop are the only shops that do not play the [vendor](music.md#Vendor) soundtrack.
* If the player looks through the cave with the [Porcelain Dipper](porcelain-dipper.md) or through the [Hydroponic Planter](hydroponic-planter.md), they can see the interior of the cave without having to go in.
