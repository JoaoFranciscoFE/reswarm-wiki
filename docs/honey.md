# Honey

<figure class="mw-halign-center" style="text-align:center"><img alt="honey" src="img/Honey.png" width="60"/><figcaption><p class="caption">The honey icon.</p></figcaption></figure>

**Honey** is the main currency in [Re://:Swarm](re-swarm.md).

## Overview

### Usage

**Honey** can be used to purchase many [items](items.md) such as [tools](tools.md), [bags](bags.md), and in-game accessories such as guards and gliding tools, and to play [Memory Match](memory-match.md). Honey can also be used to purchase products such as [Tickets](ticket.md), [Basic Eggs](egg.md#Basic_Egg), [Royal Jellies](royal-jelly.md), [Treats](treat.md), and [hive slots](hive-slot.md). In addition, Honey used along with [Tickets](ticket.md), could have been used to purchase [Market Boosts](buffs-debuffs.md#From_Areas) from the [Boost Market](boost-market.md), which is now removed. The price of many consumables increases with each purchase, for example, [Basic Eggs](egg.md#Basic_Egg).

### Obtaining Honey

Honey is usually obtained by converting [Pollen](pollen.md) into it. It can be converted at the [Hive](hive.md) or (partly) instantly converted using an [Instant Converter](instant-converter.md) or with a [Micro-Converter](micro-converter.md), catching a falling [Coconut](coconut.md) or Science Bear's falling [Beesmas Light](beesmas-lights.md) (only during Beesmas), activating [Coin Scatter](passive-abilities.md#Coin_Scatter) and/or [Diamond Drain](passive-abilities.md#Diamond_Drain). Honey can also be obtained by completing [quests](quests.md), redeeming [codes](codes.md), defeating [mobs](mobs.md), and collecting [Sparkles](sparkles.md) and [Goo](goo.md) from [fields](fields.md). [Vicious Bee](vicious-bee.md)'s [Impale](ability-tokens.md#Impale) ability spawns a honey token if it hits a mob. [Festive Bee](festive-bee.md)'s [festive gift](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Gift) ability always spawns Honey tokens. Some other [bees](bees.md) have the ability to give a [Honey Gift](ability-tokens.md#Honey_Gift) or a [Token Link](ability-tokens.md#Token_Link). Certain hats, guards, and [amulets](amulet.md) have the ability to [instantly convert](instant-conversion.md) a percentage of pollen into honey. Popping [bubbles](bubble.md), spawning a [flame](flame.md), having field conversion such as [Field Winds](field-wind.md), collecting a [Bomb token](ability-tokens.md#Bomb) produced by a bee can result in instant conversion as well. The player can also get honey for participating in the [Ant Challenge](ant-challenge.md). Honey tokens can appear when the player collects pollen in a [field](fields.md) and can be obtained from Honeystorms. The [Mythic Meteor Shower](mythic-meteor-shower.md) awards Honey tokens after catching a meteor. The [Honey Dispenser](honey-dispenser.md) is also an option, however, it is only available to those who are in the [Bee Swarm Simulator Club](https://www.roblox.com/groups/3982592/Bee-Swarm-Simulator-Club#!/about).

## [Convert Rate](system-page.md#Convert_Rate)

Bees convert pollen into Honey at a set rate. For example, [Basic Bee](basic-bee.md) makes 80 honey in 4 seconds, translating into a conversion rate of 20 honey per second. [Badges](badges.md), accessories, [gamepasses](robux-shop.md#Gamepasses), [Enzymes](enzymes.md), and [Science Enhancement](buffs-debuffs.md#From_NPCs) can increase the convert rate. The production rate stacks and adds up. There is currently no convert rate cap in the game.
