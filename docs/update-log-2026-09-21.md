# Honeycomb Blueprint upgrades

**Released:** September 21, 2026

The Honeycomb Blueprint gets leveling, mutation rolling and hive slot buying.

## What's new

* **Honeycomb Blueprint upgrades:**
    * **Rebirth 10:** level up bees directly in the blueprint. Pick the bees and the level, and it shows the honey cost.
    * **Rebirth 15:** roll until a chosen [mutation](mutation.md) (pick the mutation, the stat or stat range, and the bees).
    * **Rebirth 20:** roll until a chosen bee with a chosen mutation.
* Buy hive slots in the blueprint, and use any item there that works on your hive.
* You can buy a lot more [Royal Jelly](royal-jelly.md).
* Auto [Cloud Vial](cloud-vial.md).
* Badges are claimed automatically.
* New music track for the 30 Bee Zone and the [Star Hall](star-hall.md).

## Fixes

* Fixed new quests going to the bottom of the list.
* Fixed some issues with Beequips and the Honeycomb Blueprint.
