**Pro Bear** is a bear who runs the [Pro Shop](pro-shop.md). He is the second shop bear encountered by most players. His presence is not required to purchase [items](items.md) in the shop.

Pro Bear has a similar appearance to [Noob Bear](noob-bear.md) and [Top Bear](top-bear.md): yellow skin, red shirt, green pants, and a smiley face. The only difference between them is the colors of their clothing.

## Trivia

* Pro Bear is one of the four bears in the [10 Bee Area](brave-bee-gate.md), the others being [Science Bear](science-bear.md), [Dapper Bear](dapper-bear.md), and [Tunnel Bear](tunnel-bear.md).
* This bear, Noob Bear, Top Bear, and [Shadow Bear](shadow-bear.md) are the only bears without a function whatsoever.
* Behind Pro Bear, there is a [maze](mazes.md#Pro_Shop_Maze) that contains a [royal jelly](royal-jelly.md) token, sunflower seed token, and has the ability to host [hidden stickers](sticker.md#Hidden_Stickers).
