# Melittology Quests

![Melittology Quests](img/places/Melittology_Quests.png){ .wiki-photo }

The **Melittology Quests** pad gives out the [Melittology Research](melittology-research.md) quests, which earn [Research Points](research-points.md) for the [Melittology](melittology.md) tree. It's a blue and white pad on the yellow ledge on the [mountain top](mountain-top-field.md), right beside the [Melittology Machine](melittology-machine.md), near the [Boost Market](boost-market.md) and [Onett's Lid Art](onett-s-lid-art.md).

There's no NPC: the pad itself is the quest giver. A floating sign next to it reads *"was too lazy to put together a build for this"*.

## Usage

It needs [Rebirth 10](rebirths.md). The quests come one rank at a time, and the prompt changes with your progress:

| When | Prompt |
|---|---|
| No quest yet | "Begin Melittology Research (Rank N of 25)" |
| Quest in progress | "Research Rank N is in progress..." |
| Quest finished | "Turn in Research Rank N (+X Research)" |
| All 25 ranks done | "All Melittology Research studies are complete." |

There's no dialogue. Turning in a rank shows "+X Research". Rank 21 also gives the **Eureka!** [sticker](sticker.md) with the message *"Eureka! Your research turned up something else."*
