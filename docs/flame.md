# Flame

> *Not to be confused with the flame trail produced by [Fire Ants](fire-ant.md).*

**Flames** are objects that can be produced by certain [tools](tools.md), [ability tokens](ability-tokens.md), or [passive abilities](passive-abilities.md). Flames have a percentage chance to be spawned by certain bees when gathering, lasting for 4 seconds. Each second, the flames collect 5 red, 3 white, or 2 blue [pollen](pollen.md) from 9 surrounding flowers (+2% per red bee and +4% per gifted red bee if spawned by the player, +10% per bee level if spawned by a bee), deals 10 + (25% of [base Bee Attack](bee-attack.md)) damage per second to any mob (15 if spawned by the player), and grants the player [flame heat](buffs-debuffs.md#From_Passives).

Flames can be generated by the [Scythe](scythe.md), [Fire Bee](fire-bee.md), [Demon Bee](demon-bee.md), [Spicy Bee's](spicy-bee.md) [Inferno](ability-tokens.md#Inferno), bees with the [Bead Lizard](bead-lizard.md) or [Electric Candle](electric-candle.md) beequips, [Fire Mask](fire-mask.md)'s Ignite passive, and [Demon Mask](demon-mask.md)'s X-Flame passive.

Flames are used to help grow Scorching Stars.

## Flame Heat

**Flame Heat** grants extra Red Pollen and Bee Attack starting at x1.2 [Red Pollen](system-page.md#Red_Pollen) and +5% [Bee Attack](bee-attack.md) Multiplier. The more flames around the player, the faster the Flame Heat grows up to a maximum of 20 seconds at x2 Red Pollen and +25% Bee Attack Multiplier. Flame Heat boosts [Spicy Bee's](spicy-bee.md) Movespeed and gather speed and can also be consumed by [Precise Bee](precise-bee.md) to convert Pollen into Honey.

## Gathering Flames

The **Gathering Flames** passive gives a chance for a bee to spawn a flame while gathering pollen from a field. Gathering Flames+ gives a higher chance for a bee to spawn a flame.

There is a 35% chance (50% if gifted) for Fire Bees' Gathering Flames passive to spawn a flame while gathering, and a 65% (85% if gifted) chance for Demon Bees' Gathering Flames+ to spawn a flame while gathering.

The Gathering Flames passive chance can be increased with the [Bead Lizard](bead-lizard.md) or [Electric Candle](electric-candle.md) beequip for individual bees.

* A [Bead Lizard](bead-lizard.md) gives from +9% Gathering Flames chance to +14% Gathering Flames chance.
* An [Electric Candle](electric-candle.md) gives from +1% Gathering Flames chance to +5% Gathering Flames chance. Using waxes can increase the amount up to +25% Gathering Flames chance.

## Dark Flames

Flames that are hit with [Dark Scythe](dark-scythe.md) will be converted into **dark flames**, a dark violet version of normal flames which collects pollen from 13 surrounding flowers (instead of 9), deals 50% more damage, and grants dark heat and also normal flame heat. Dark flames also grant 33% more growth to Scorching Stars.

## Flame Stats

There are multiple aspects of flames that can be improved by stats and modifiers.

### Flame Pollen

**Flame Pollen** is the amount of pollen that flames can collect. The player can check how much flame pollen they have in the system page.



Flame Pollen can be increased in a few ways:

* The Demon Mask gives a x1.25 Flame Pollen multiplier.
* Gifted Fire Bee gives a x1.25 Flame Pollen multiplier.
* [Beequips](beequip.md):
  * An [Electric Candle](electric-candle.md) gives from +5% Flame Pollen to +10% Flame Pollen for an individual bee. Using [waxes](waxes.md) can increase the amount up to 50% Flame Pollen.
  * An [Electric Candle](electric-candle.md) can give +1% Flame Pollen as a hive bonus. Using waxes can increase the amount up to 11% Flame Pollen.
* [Cog Amulets](cog-amulet.md):
  * The Silver Cog Amulet can give up to +5% Flame Pollen.
  * The Gold Cog Amulet can give up to +8% Flame Pollen.
  * The Diamond Cog Amulet can give from +12% Flame Pollen up to +14% Flame Pollen.
  * The Supreme Cog Amulet can give from +15% Flame Pollen up to +20% Flame Pollen.
* [Stickers](sticker.md):
  * The Small Flame Bee Sticker gives +3% Flame Pollen.
  * The Dark Flame Sticker gives +3% Flame Pollen.
  * The Orange Swirled Marble Sticker gives +3% Flame Pollen.
* [Robo Bear Challenge](robo-bear-challenge.md):
  * Epic Upgrades:
    * Furnace: This upgrade gives x1.5 Flame Pollen.

### Flame Duration

**Flame Duration** is how long flames will last. Flame Duration can be increased by Spicy Bee's gifted bonus (25% more time) and [Gifted](gifted-bee.md) Spicy Bee's [Flame Fuel](ability-tokens.md#Flame_Fuel) ability (50% more time). In addition, Flame Fuel causes the flames to instantly convert 50,000 pollen plus 10% (+5% per Field Boost) of the hive's total convert amount.

### Instant Flame Conversion



**Instant Flame Conversion** is how much pollen collected by flames are instantly converted. It can be increased by a few ways.

* The Fire Mask gives +50% Instant Flame Conversion.
* The Demon Mask gives +50% Instant Flame Conversion.
* A Scorching Star gives +50% Instant Flame Conversion.

### Other Improvements

Certain upgrades from the Robo Bear Challenge can be used to improve flames.

* Epic Upgrades:
  * Furnace: This upgrade gives x1.5 Flame Duration and x1.5 Flame Damage.
  * Invert: This upgrade makes flames collect x4 pollen from blue flowers.

## Trivia

* The maximum Flame Pollen a player can have is 380%. This can be achieved by having the Demon Mask (x1.25), Gifted Fire Bee (x1.25), a Supreme Cog Amulet (+20%), three [Electric Candles](electric-candle.md) (+33%), activating the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md) with certain stickers (+9%), and the Furnace upgrade (x1.5).
  * The exact amount is 379.6875% but it rounds up to 380%.
* The maximum Instant Flame Conversion a player can have is 100%. This can be achieved by having the Fire Mask (+50%) or the Demon Mask (+50%) while having a Scorching Star (+50%).
* The texture for flames were changed in the 2021-12-26 update. This was probably to help with the texture for dark flames.
* Flames generated by another player's bees will appear dark red instead of yellow-orange.
* Flames are the counterpart of [bubbles](bubble.md).
