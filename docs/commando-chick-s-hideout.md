# Commando Chick's Hideout

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The entrance to the Commando Chick's hideout.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

The **Commando Chick's Hideout** is a location in the [Starter Zone](starter-zone.md) that can be entered by completing the [obby](obstacle-courses.md#Commando_Chick's_Hideout_Obby) between the [Blue HQ](blue-hq.md) and the [Wealth Clock](wealth-clock.md). To enter the parkour, the player first needs to cut the vines blocking the entrance with either the [Clippers](clippers.md), [Scissors](scissors.md), [Scythe](scythe.md) or the [Dark Scythe](dark-scythe.md). It is home to the [Commando Chick](commando-chick.md).

## Strategy

Standing on the last stair, nearest to Commando Chick's hideout normally makes the chick flee. However, if the player stands on the edge of that stair (as close as possible to the hideout where one of your body parts is inside of the hideout), the Commando Chick does not flee; therefore, the player's bees can defeat the Commando Chick whilst they take no damage. This strategy does not 100% work as there could be a slight chance of the Commando Chick fleeing. The player could just move into the hideout and then back onto the stair again to fix this.

## Trivia

* This location used to house the [Most Commando Captures leaderboard](most-commando-captures.md) before it was removed from the game.
* In Beesmas 2019, this location contained the [Golden Present](ornament-presents.md).
  * The obstacle course remained after the presents were removed. When the [2020-04-06 update](updates.md#2020-04-06) came out, it was repurposed into the hideout.
