# Present

![Present](img/Present.png){ align=right width=150 }

*This article is about the gifts given to NPCs. Not to be confused with [Ornament Presents](ornament-presents.md) and [Gift Boxes](gift-boxes.md).*

Present

*"A festively wrapped gift. Exchange it with an NPC for prizes!"*

COOLDOWN

(None)

A **Present** is a Beesmas-exclusive inventory item from the Beesmas event. Presents can be given to most NPCs in the game in exchange for various prizes from the NPC. Each NPC can only receive one gift.

To give a present, the player needs to talk to an NPC of their choice. When interacted with, special dialogue will appear, allowing the player to give the present to the chosen NPC. If the player does not have a present in their inventory or have already recently given said NPC a present, normal dialogue will display. Some NPCs may have other requirements for the player before it allows itself to receive a gift.

## Beesmas

Presents are available during the Beesmas event. Their main function is adding [Ornaments](ornaments.md) to the [Beesmas Tree](beesmas-tree.md) when given to NPCs. Players can't give a present to [Sun Bear](sun-bear.md), as the bear is not present during the event.

## Ways to obtain

* Multiple quests from [Bee Bear](bee-bear.md) award a total of 10 **Presents**, along with other rewards.
* [Bee Bear's Catalog](bee-bear-s-catalog.md) sold 1 **Present** for 250 [Snowflakes](snowflake.md) and 10 [Gingerbread Bears](gingerbread-bear.md).
* [Onett](onett.md) gives 1 **Present** (along with other items) upon claiming a hive for the first time during the event's period.
* Donating [Strawberries](strawberry.md) to the [Galentine Shrine](wind-shrine.md#Galentine_Shrine) has a chance to give 1 **Present**, along with other rewards.
* Completing Robo Party's rank 25 for the first time gives 1 **Present**, along with other rewards.
