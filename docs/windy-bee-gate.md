The **Windy Bee Gate**, or the **35 Bee Gate**, is a gate that requires 35 [bees](bees.md) to unlock. It is located on the right of the [Ticket Tent](ticket-tent.md) and [Red Cannon](red-cannon.md). It gets its name from the large [Windy Bee](windy-bee.md) figure on top of it.

Inside this Gate is the [Coconut Field](coconut-field.md), the [Pepper Patch](pepper-patch.md), [Spirit Bear](spirit-bear.md), the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md), the Extreme [Memory Match](memory-match.md), and two [Shops](shops.md): the [Petal Shop](petal-shop.md) and [Coconut Cave](coconut-cave.md). The [Petal Shop](petal-shop.md) sells the [Petal Wand](petal-wand.md), [Petal Belt](petal-belt.md) and the [Petal Planter](petal-planter.md). while the Coconut Cave sells the [Coconut Canister](coconut-canister.md), [Coconut Clogs](coconut-clogs.md) and [Coconut Belt](coconut-belt.md).

## Trivia

* This gate is the first and currently the only gate in the game that has an [Event bee](bees-event.md) representing it.
* This gate, along with the [Bear Gate](bear-gate.md), are currently the only gates that require purchasing [hive slots](hive-slot.md) to pass.
  * The gate also plays the same music as the Bear Gate.
* This gate gives access to the blocked off area in the [Blue Maze](mazes.md#Blue_Maze) through the [Petal Shop](petal-shop.md).
* This gate is the only place where sprouts can't appear naturally or be planted with the [Special Sprout Summoner](special-sprout-summoner.md).
* The sprouts planted by a player require more pollen to grow than in other locations for better loot and guaranteed more drops.
