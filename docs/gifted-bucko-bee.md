## Rewards

The reward will vary depending on the number of quests the player has completed. Rewards will always contain one [Blue Extract](blue-extract.md) and [Honey](honey.md).

Bucko Bee's "Petals" quest rewards a [Bloom Shaker](bloom-shaker.md).

The amount of honey the player receives when completing a quest is based on this table, where *Q* is the number of quests the player has completed plus 1. Only the highest value in the table is taken.

## Trivia

* Since the quests are all chosen at random, including the first, the formula for the first requirements of any quest is "x divided by (number of Gifted Bucko Bee quests completed +1)".
* Gifted Bucko Bee is one of the three NPCs to be based on [bees](bees.md), the other two being [Gifted Riley Bee](gifted-riley-bee.md) and [Honey Bee (NPC)](honey-bee-npc.md).
  * This Bee is the only quest bee to reside indoors, though Gifted Riley Bee resided indoors too before an update.
* In the Bucko Bee's [Snow Machine](snow-machine.md) for Beesmas 2020 dialogue, Gifted Bucko Bee says to defeat ten spiders. However, the [quest](quests.md) requires the player to defeat five spiders.
* Gifted Bucko Bee enjoys playing with marbles, as evidenced by their [Present](present.md) dialogue in which they remark “Can't wait to crack this open and roll those bad boys around.”
