# Commando Chick/Drops

Stub

*This article is a stub. You can help Re://:Swarm Wiki by [expanding it](commando-chick-drops.md).*

Milestone rewards include:

* [Glue](glue.md) upon the 5th capture.
* [Gold Egg](egg.md#Gold_Egg) upon the 10th capture.
* [Star Jelly](royal-jelly.md#Star_Jelly) upon the 15th capture.
* 3 [Blue Extracts](blue-extract.md) upon the 20th capture.
* 100 [Tickets](ticket.md) upon the 25th capture.
* 10 [Stingers](stinger.md) upon the 30th capture.
* 10 [Ant Passes](ant-pass.md) and 10 [Stingers](stinger.md) upon the 40th capture.
* [Mythic Egg](egg.md#Mythic_Egg) upon the 50th capture.
* [Star Treat](star-treat.md) upon the 100th capture.
* [Commander Bee Egg](egg.md#Commander_Bee_Egg) upon the 200th capture.
