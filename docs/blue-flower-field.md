The **Blue Flower Field** is one of the five [starting](starter-zone.md) [fields](fields.md) any player can visit upon starting the game. It is the first blue field accessible in the game. It is protected by a level 1 [rhino beetle](rhino-beetle.md).

On the north-east side of the field, a glass-like panel can be seen in the wall, with a blue flower leading into it. Making contact with it will teleport the player inside [King Beetle's Lair](king-beetle-s-lair.md).

You can find the [Blue HQ](blue-hq.md) on the other side of the field.

This field can spawn [honey](honey.md), [blueberry](blueberry.md), [neonberry](neonberry.md), [ticket](ticket.md), [blue boost](ability-tokens.md#Boost), [blue extract](blue-extract.md), and [magic bean](magic-bean.md) tokens when collecting [pollen](pollen.md).

[Bees](bees.md) that like the Blue Flower Field include [Bumble Bee](bumble-bee.md), [Bubble Bee](bubble-bee.md), [Bucko Bee](bucko-bee.md), [Frosty Bee](frosty-bee.md), [Baby Bee](baby-bee.md), [Diamond Bee](diamond-bee.md), [Ninja Bee](ninja-bee.md), and [Buoyant Bee](buoyant-bee.md). Bees that dislike the Blue Flower Field include [Rage Bee](rage-bee.md), [Bear Bee](bear-bee.md), and [Festive Bee](festive-bee.md).

This field grants Refreshing [Nectar](nectar.md) when a [planter](planter.md) is planted here.

This is a 17×43 field made up of 731 [flowers](flowers.md). The flowers are blue and white, in sizes of single and double.

## Obtaining Boosts

* Using the [Blue Field Booster](blue-field-booster.md) may give a Blue Flower [Field Boost](field-boost.md). When it does, it will give +300% Blue Flower Field pollen for 15 minutes.
* Using [glitter](glitter.md) will add 100% Blue Flower Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if it isn't already maxed out.
* Using [field dice](field-dice.md), [smooth dice](smooth-dice.md), and [loaded dice](loaded-dice.md) may add +100% - 300% Blue Flower Field pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out.
* Blue Flower [Field Winds](field-wind.md) can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md) or [codes](codes.md). They have a base of +20% instant Blue Flower Field conversion and gives +5% instant Blue Flower Field conversion and +25% Blue Flower Field pollen for each stack. Blue Flower Field Winds can stack up to a total of 13 times.
* It can be boosted by the [King Beetle Amulet](king-beetle-amulet.md). If it has a Blue Flower Field Boost, it can boost the field by +1% to +100%.

### The methods below are no longer possible.

* Giving a [present](present.md) to certain NPCs:

  * [Onett](onett.md) gave Blue Flower Field Boost x1 and other boosts and items.
* Completing [Gifted Bucko Bee's Egg Hunt 2019 Quest](gifted-bucko-bee.md#Egg_Hunt_Quest_2019) gave Blue Flower Field Boost x7 (although the boost only went up to x5) and other items.

  * ClubParty (Gave Blue Flower Field Boost x6 + other stuff).
  * AceUnhackedLoot (Gave Blue Flower Field Boost x2 + other stuff).
  * PingBoost (Gave Blue Flower Field Boost x4 + other stuff).
  * Discord50k (Gave Blue Flower Field Boost x4 + other stuff).
  * Buzzy (Gave Blue Flower Field Boost x3 and Blue Flower Field Winds x3 + other stuff).

* Purchasing a Blue Flower Field [Market Boost](buffs-debuffs.md#From_Areas) from the [Boost Market](boost-market.md) and codes will give x1.5 Blue Flower [Field Capacity](field-capacity.md), x1.25 Blue Flower Field Pollen, x1.5 [Bubble Pollen](system-page.md#Pollen_From_Bubbles), and x1.25 [Convert Rate At Hive](system-page.md#Hive_Convert_Rate).

## Trivia

* This field is adjacent to the [Blue HQ](blue-hq.md) and [King Beetle's Lair](king-beetle-s-lair.md).
* Sometimes, the giant [Bubble Wand](bubble-wand.md) model on the Blue HQ gets a face and will blow bubbles towards this field, along with the [Mushroom Field](mushroom-field.md), [Clover Field](clover-field.md), and [Dandelion Field](dandelion-field.md), to produce [sparkles](sparkles.md).
* In the early days of the game, the decorative flowers in this field used to face upwards. This was changed since rhino beetles would get stuck behind the flowers, unable to attack.
  * However, you can still see the decorative flowers facing upwards in the tutorial.
* According to [Spirit Bear](spirit-bear.md), this field used to be the "Blueberry Field."
  * According to Onett, the reason why it isn't Blueberry Field is because the blueberry 3D texture he finds/makes are too close to being a "Blue ball".[1]
