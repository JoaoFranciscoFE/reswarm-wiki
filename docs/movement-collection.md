# Movement Collection

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">A moon amulet with the movement collection buff.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

**Movement Collection** is a type of boost that allows the player to collect [pollen](pollen.md) when walking on [flowers](flowers.md). The higher the movement collection, the greater amount of pollen the player collects from walking on flowers. This boost also collects [goo](goo.md) if the player walks on any goo patches.

## Ways to Increase

The player can get this boost from wearing boots or from a [Moon Amulet](moon-amulet.md).

* The Moon Amulet can give from +1 Movement Collection to +3 Movement Collection.
* Boots:
  * The [Basic Boots](basic-boots.md) give +2 Movement Collection.
  * The [Hiking Boots](hiking-boots.md) give +6 Movement Collection.
  * The [Beekeeper's Boots](beekeeper-s-boots.md) give +10 Movement Collection.
  * The [Coconut Clogs](coconut-clogs.md) give +14 Movement Collection.
  * The [Gummy Boots](gummy-boots.md) give +16 Movement Collection.

## Trivia

* Movement Collection isn't shown in the [System Page](system-page.md), unlike most other boosts, such as [Loot Luck](loot-luck.md) and [Instant Conversion](instant-conversion.md).
* If the player has movement collection on their Moon Amulet, they can still collect pollen even without boots equipped.
* The maximum Movement Collection a player can have is 19. This can be achieved by having a Gummy Boots (+16) and a Moon Amulet (+3).
