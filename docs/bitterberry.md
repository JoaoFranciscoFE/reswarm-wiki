# Bitterberry

![Bitterberry](img/Bitterberry.png){ align=right width=150 }

Bitterberry

*"Increases bond with a bee by 100. Small chance to cause Mutations."*

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The text that appears in the chat when a bee gains a mutation, fed with bitterberry.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

A **Bitterberry** is a type of [treat](treats.md) that, when fed to a [bee](bees.md), raises its [bond](bond.md) by 100 and has a 1/1000 chance to make it [mutated](mutation.md). The chance is boosted to 1/100 if the bee is [radioactive](radiation.md). These are not the favorite treat of any bee.

## Ways to Obtain

* Redeeming certain valid [codes](codes.md):
  * BeesBuzz123 (rewards 5 **Bitterberries** + other stuff).
  * 15MMembers (rewards 15 **Bitterberries** + other stuff).
* As a stack reward for adding certain [stickers](sticker.md) to the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md):
  * [Squashed Head Bear Stickers](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) rewards 25 **Bitterberries**.
* Finding the 10 **Bitterberries** under the [Beesmas Tree](beesmas-tree.md).

### Drops

* Farming in the [Mushroom Field](mushroom-field.md), [Spider Field](spider-field.md), [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md), [Cactus Field](cactus-field.md), [Rose Field](rose-field.md), [Mountain Top Field](mountain-top-field.md), and [Pepper Patch](pepper-patch.md).
  * Harvesting [leaves](leaves.md) in these fields may also yield bitterberries.
* As a guaranteed drop from [Commando Chick](commando-chick.md), [Mondo Chick](mondo-chick.md), and the [Coconut Crab](coconut-crab.md).
* As a drop from [ladybugs](ladybug.md), [spiders](spider.md), [scorpions](scorpion.md), [Rage Aphids](aphid.md#Rage_Aphid), [Tunnel Bear](tunnel-bear.md), and [King Beetle](king-beetle.md).
* As a gift from a [cub buddy](cub-buddy.md).
* As a common drop from [Mythic Meteors](mythic-meteor-shower.md).
* As a drop from a Debug [Sprout](sprout.md).
* As an uncommon drop from [Puffshrooms](puffshroom.md).
* As a drop from the [Pesticide Planter](pesticide-planter.md) and [The Planter Of Plenty](the-planter-of-plenty.md).
  * The [Pesticide Planter](pesticide-planter.md) has a chance of dropping a bonus 100 **Bitterberries** when harvested at full growth in the Spider Field, Mountain Top Field, or Pepper Patch.

### Quests

* Black Bear rewards bitterberries on the following quest:
  * Sweet and Sour gives 25 bitterberries.
  * Weed Wacker 3 gives 25 bitterberries.
* Brown Bear rewards bitterberies on the completion of these quests:
  * Brown Bear's 20th quest rewards 25 bitterberries.
  * Brown Bear's 40th quest rewards 50 bitterberries.
  * Brown Bear's 135th quest rewards 50 bitterberries.
  * Brown Bear's 180th quest rewards 50 bitterberries.
  * Brown Bear's 210th quest rewards 50 bitterberries.
* Every 20th Brown Bear quest rewards a varying amount of bitterberries.
