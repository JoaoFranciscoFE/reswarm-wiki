# Instant Conversion

**Instant Conversion** is a [mechanic](mechanics.md) that instantly converts some of the player's collected [pollen](pollen.md) into [honey](honey.md). The amount of converted pollen depends on how much instant conversion (%) the player has, with support from the respective type of instant conversion (white, red, and blue instant conversion, etc.). This effectively makes the player's [bag](bags.md) bigger, in the sense that the player can collect more pollen than the bag would normally hold. The player's [Honey Per Pollen](honey-per-pollen.md) stat boosts the amount of honey received from instant conversion. There are also color variants for each [flower](flowers.md) color (blue, red, white) that only instantly convert pollen of their respective colors.

## Instant Conversion Variants

Instant Conversion has **8 variants**:

* Flame Conversion
* Field Conversion (e.g. [field winds](field-wind.md))
* Colored Conversion
* Goo Conversion
* Bomb Conversion
* Unique Instant Conversion
* Instant Tool Conversion
* Bee Gather Conversion

### Goo Conversion

*Goo Conversion* converts a **flat amount of pollen** equal to a percentage of the [Goo](goo.md) collected from a flower.

### Unique Instant Conversion

*Unique Instant Conversion* is applied **independently** of all other Instant Conversion types.

For example, if you have **two different sources of 50% Unique Instant Conversion**, each source is applied separately. This results in an effective **75% Instant Conversion**, not 100%.

## Conversion Order

Instant Conversion is applied in the following order:[1]

1. Colored Conversion
2. Field Conversion
3. Regular Instant Conversion
4. Goo Conversion

It is unknown where Unique Instant Conversion and Ability-specific Instant Conversion is placed in this order.

For example, if a player has **50%** of **four** different conversion variants and collects **10,000** red pollen, **50%** of the pollen is converted, then **50%** of the remaining amount, and so on for each variant.

## Increasing Instant Conversion

* Instant Conversion is increased by:
  * [Enzymes](enzymes.md) (+10%)
  * [Tropical Drinks](tropical-drink.md) (+10%)
  * [Super Smoothies](super-smoothie.md) (+20%, overwrites [Enzymes](enzymes.md) and [Tropical Drinks](tropical-drink.md))
  * Cactus Badge (+5%)
  * [Gifted](gifted-bee.md) [Photon Bee](photon-bee.md) (+5%)
  * [Port-O-Hive](port-o-hive.md) (5%)
  * [Coconut Canister](coconut-canister.md) or [Porcelain Port-O-Hive](porcelain-port-o-hive.md) (10%)
  * [Star Amulet](star-amulet.md) (up to 12%)
  * [Moon Amulet](moon-amulet.md) (up to 5%)
  * [Festive Blessing Buff](buffs-debuffs.md#From_Ability_Tokens) (100%)
* Maximum Instant Conversion is 157% not accounting for codes.
* Instant White Conversion is increased by amulets, the [Pineapple Badge](badges.md#Pineapple_Badge), Gifted [Windy Bee](windy-bee.md) or with the [Coconut Canister](coconut-canister.md).
* Instant Red Conversion is increased by the [Mushroom Badge](badges.md#Mushroom_Badge) (up to 15%), Gifted [Crimson Bee](crimson-bee.md) (15%), or the [Demon Mask](demon-mask.md) (20%).
* Instant Blue Conversion is increased by the [Blue Flower Badge](badges.md#Blue_Flower_Badge) (up to 15%), Gifted [Cobalt Bee](cobalt-bee.md) (15%) or the [Diamond Mask](diamond-mask.md)(15%).
* Instant Field Conversion (field winds) are granted by donating to the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md) or from certain [codes](codes.md).
* Goo Conversion is increased by the [Shell Amulet](shell-amulet.md), [Gummy Mask](gummy-mask.md), [Gummy Morph](passive-abilities.md#Gummy_Morph), or [Gummy Star](passive-abilities.md#Gummy_Star).
* Instant Flame Conversion is granted by the [Fire Mask](fire-mask.md), [Demon Mask](demon-mask.md) and [Scorching Star](passive-abilities.md#Scorching_Star).
* Instant Bomb Conversion is granted by [Festive Mark](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Mark) and Gifted [Demon Bee](demon-bee.md).
* Unique Instant Conversion is granted by [Coconut Combo](coconut-belt.md), Clouds, [Puffshroom Blessing](puffshroom.md), [Beequips](beequip.md), [Refreshing Nectar](nectar.md) and [Blooms](bloom.md)
* Instant Bee Gather Conversion is granted by the [Pink Jelly Bean](jelly-beans.md#Types_and_Effects) buff.
* Instant Tool Conversion is granted by the [Teal Jelly Bean](jelly-beans.md#Types_and_Effects) buff.

## Effects

* When instant conversion is in effect, and pollen text is turned on, the player will see yellow numbers in addition to the blue/red/white pollen numbers while they're working in the [fields](fields.md). Those yellow numbers are the amount of pollen that instantly became honey; Collected honey amounts also show up in yellow, but those are also listed in the bottom right corner of the player's screen.

* The formula for the effective increase in capacity given by instant conversion is: (x/(100-x)), where x is the player's total instant conversion.

## Trivia

* The [Festive Blessing](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Blessing) [buff](buffs-debuffs.md) from [Festive Bee's](festive-bee.md) Festive Gift token or [Festive Sprouts](sprout.md) grants 100% Instant Conversion temporarily. If the player has a full container with 100% Instant Conversion, collecting pollen will not transfer into honey.
  * The [Gummy Morph](passive-abilities.md#Gummy_Morph) also grants 200% Goo Conversion while covering the whole field in goo.
  * The [Ant Challenge Buff](buffs-debuffs.md#From_Ability_Tokens) grants 100% instant [Ant Field](ant-field.md) Conversion.
  * Pollen collected from the [Hub Field](hub-field.md) is instantly converted.
* [Super-Crits](critical-hits.md#Super-Crit) and [Golden Bubbles](bubble.md#Golden_Bubbles) instantly convert all pollen they collect.
* Tadpole Bee used to give 25% Instant Bubble Conversion before the 2024-05-23 update.
