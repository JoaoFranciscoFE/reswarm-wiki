**Carpenter Bee** is a Colorless [Legendary bee](bees-legendary.md).

Carpenter Bee's favorite treat is [Sunflower Seeds](sunflower-seed.md).

Carpenter Bee likes the [Bamboo Field](bamboo-field.md), and [Pine Tree Forest](pine-tree-forest.md). It dislikes the [Mountain Top Field](mountain-top-field.md).

## Stats

* Collects 10 [Pollen](pollen.md) in 3 seconds.
* Makes 120 [honey](honey.md) in 4 seconds.
* -20% [Movespeed](stats.md#Speed), +25% [Energy](energy.md), +25% gather speed, +40 [Convert Amount](system-page.md#Convert_Amount), +4 [Attack](stats.md#Attack).
* 🌟[Gifted Hive Bonus](gifted-bee.md): +25% [Tool Pollen](system-page.md#Tool_Pollen).

## Abilities

* [**[Honey Mark+]**](ability-tokens.md#Mark) Marks a large random area on the [Field](fields.md) for 8 seconds (+0.25s per level) that grants 2 Conversion Links and x1.25 [Convert Rate](system-page.md#Convert_Rate) while you stand on it. Stacks up to 3 times.
* **[Pollen Mark](ability-tokens.md#Mark)** Marks a random area on the field for 7 seconds (+0.2s per level) that increases all pollen up to 50% while you stand in it. Stacks up to 3 times.

## Trivia

* Carpenter Bee and Music Bee are also the only Legendary bees added in an update.
* This bee, [Spicy Bee](spicy-bee.md), and [Vector Bee](vector-bee.md) are the only non-[Event bees](bees-event.md) that have special body parts (hammer mesh).
* This is one of two bees added in the 2018-11-25 Update, along with [Stubborn Bee](stubborn-bee.md).
* One reason Carpenter Bee likes the [Pine Tree Forest](pine-tree-forest.md) might be because pine trees are made of wood.
  * One reason Carpenter Bee dislikes the [Mountain Top Field](mountain-top-field.md) might be because on the tops of tall mountains, there are no trees.
* Carpenter Bee, along with [Honey Bee](honey-bee.md) and [Bumble Bee](bumble-bee.md), are the only bees named after their real-life counterparts.
* Carpenter Bee is the only bee that can generate the Honey Mark+ ability token.
* This bee was one of the bees to have a specific jelly in the [Bee Bear's Catalog](bee-bear-s-catalog.md). The jelly was given as part of the Pinecone Bundle.
* This bee is the only Legendary bee who can't find a [sticker](sticker.md) while gathering.
