The **Mushroom Field** is one of the five [starting](starter-zone.md) [fields](fields.md) any player is able to visit upon starting the game. It is the first red field accessible to players in the game. The field is protected by a level 1 [ladybug](ladybug.md).

This field can spawn [treat](treat.md), [strawberry](strawberry.md), [bitterberry](bitterberry.md), [ticket](ticket.md), [red boost](ability-tokens.md#Boost), [honey](honey.md), [royal jelly](royal-jelly.md), [magic bean](magic-bean.md), and [red extract](red-extract.md) tokens when collecting [pollen](pollen.md).

Bees that like the Mushroom Field include [Rad Bee](rad-bee.md), [Rascal Bee](rascal-bee.md), [Fire Bee](fire-bee.md), [Riley Bee](riley-bee.md), [Baby Bee](baby-bee.md), [Demon Bee](demon-bee.md), and [Festive Bee](festive-bee.md). Bees that dislike the Mushroom Field include [Bumble Bee](bumble-bee.md), [Frosty Bee](frosty-bee.md), [Shocked Bee](shocked-bee.md), and [Ninja Bee](ninja-bee.md).

This field gives Motivating [Nectar](nectar.md) when a [planter](planter.md) is planted here.

This is a 32x23 field made up of 736 [flowers](flowers.md). The flowers are red and white, in sizes of single and double.

## Obtaining Boosts

* Using the [Red Field Booster](red-field-booster.md) may give a Mushroom [Field Boost](field-boost.md). When it does, it will give +300% Mushroom Field pollen for 15 minutes.
* Using [glitter](glitter.md) in the field will add 100% Mushroom Field pollen onto the current boost if the boost isn't already maxed out.
* Using [field dice](field-dice.md), [smooth dice](smooth-dice.md), or [loaded dice](loaded-dice.md) may add +100% - +300% Mushroom Field pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out and based on the type used.
* Mushroom [Field Winds](field-wind.md) can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md) or [codes](codes.md). They have a base of +20% instant Mushroom Field conversion and give +5% instant Mushroom Field conversion and +25% Mushroom Field pollen for each stack. Mushroom Field Winds can stack up to a total of 15 times.
* It can be boosted by the [King Beetle Amulet](king-beetle-amulet.md). If it has a Mushroom Field Boost, it can boost the field by +1% to +100%.

## Trivia

* There is a small [parkour course](obstacle-courses.md#Mushroom_Field_Obby) in the Mushroom Field that can be done to obtain a royal jelly.
* The [Bubble Wand](bubble-wand.md) model on top of the [Blue HQ](blue-hq.md) can occasionally grow a face and blow [sparkles](sparkles.md) onto this field, along with the [Blue Flower](blue-flower-field.md), [Clover](clover-field.md), and [Dandelion Field](dandelion-field.md).
* [Stick Bug](stick-bug.md) is found on the tallest mushroom, along with the Top Stick Bug Fighters leaderboard. This is where the player can start the [Stick Bug Challenge](stick-bug-challenge.md) if they have given Stick Bug a [translator](translator.md).
* After the [2018-11-25 update](updates.md#2018-11-25) was released, Stick Bug would sometimes fall off the tallest mushroom and land in or near the [Dandelion Field](dandelion-field.md). This was a visual [glitch](glitches.md#Onett/Stick_Bug/Mondo_Chick_Falling) and didn't affect gameplay. This glitch was patched a few days after the update.
* This is one of the two fields in the Starter Zone with a Ladybug in it, the other being the Clover Field.
* The [Yellow Walking Wiggly Person sticker](sticker.md) cannot spawn in this field.
