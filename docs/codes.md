# Codes

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 288px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The codes menu.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

Promo **codes** are a feature added in the May 18, 2018 update. It is located at the top of the [System Page](system-page.md). Redeeming them gives prizes such as [honey](honey.md), [tickets](ticket.md), [gumdrops](gumdrops.md), [royal jelly](royal-jelly.md), crafting materials, [wealth clock](wealth-clock.md), [magic beans](magic-bean.md), boosts from [ability tokens](ability-tokens.md), or [field boosts](field-boost.md).

[Onett](onett-developer.md) posts codes (or hints for codes) in the game itself, on the game's [Roblox page](https://www.roblox.com/games/1537690962), on the [Bee Swarm Simulator Club](https://www.roblox.com/communities/3982592) page, on [his Twitter account](https://twitter.com/OnettDev), and on the game's [official Discord server](https://discord.gg/bee). He also sometimes gives out codes to YouTubers, and there have been reports of codes posted on his [Roblox profile page](https://www.roblox.com/users/1912490) and on Roblox advertisements.

## Notes

* Do ***NOT*** join the Discord server just to ask for codes! Instead, use the search function to look for posts by Onett (onett1, Onett#0001, 428649652871495691).
* Codes can only be used **once**.
* Some older codes may tell you once you redeem them that you have obtained a >x3 boost, though they will still only give you x3.
* They are *not* case-sensitive: "Bopmaster", "BopMaster", "BOPMASTER", and "bopmaster" will all work equally.
  * Codes allow spaces in between: "Bees Buzz 123" and "BeesBuzz123" will work equally.
* Expired codes do not always say "expired"; sometimes, they say "invalid."
* When you try to redeem a valid code that you have already used, you will be told: "You have already redeemed [code]."
