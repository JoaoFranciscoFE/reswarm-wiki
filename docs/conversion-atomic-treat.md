# Conversion Atomic Treat

![Conversion Atomic Treat](img/Conversion_Atomic_Treat.png){ align=right width=150 }

*"Always gives a bee a Conversion Mutation."*

The **Conversion Atomic Treat** is a [treat](treats.md) that always gives a [bee](bees.md) a **Convert Amount** [mutation](mutation.md). It works like an [Atomic Treat](atomic-treat.md), but the mutation is always Convert Amount instead of a random one.

## Effect

* +1,000 [bond](bond.md).
* Always mutates the bee. The new mutation replaces any mutation it already had.

| Mutation | Chance | Value |
|---|---|---|
| % Convert Amount | 2 in 3 | +10% to +30% |
| + Convert Amount | 1 in 3 | +20 to +80 |

The value is random and leans towards the low end, but 7% of the time it rolls the maximum. The bee's level doesn't change the range.

## Ways to obtain

* **Rebirth rewards** (rebirth number, with the amount in brackets): 17 (5), 19 (10), 20 (25), 30 (100), 34 (750), 35 (1,000), 36 (1,200), 37 (1,400), 38 (1,600), 39 (1,800).
