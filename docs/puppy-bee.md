**Puppy Bee** is a Colorless [Event bee](bees-event.md). It hatches out of a [Puppy Bee Egg](egg.md#Puppy_Bee_Egg), which is available in the [Ticket Tent](ticket-tent.md) for 500 [Tickets](ticket.md).

Like all other Event bees, this bee does not have a favorite treat, and the only way to make it gifted is by feeding it a [Star Treat](star-treat.md), [Gingerbread Bears](gingerbread-bear.md) or [Aged Gingerbread Bears](aged-gingerbread-bear.md).

Puppy Bee likes the [Clover Field](clover-field.md) and the [Pumpkin Patch](pumpkin-patch.md). It dislikes the [Rose Field](rose-field.md).

## Stats

* Collects 25 [Pollen](pollen.md) in 4 seconds.
* Makes 280 [Honey](honey.md) in 4 seconds.
* +100% [Energy](energy.md), +15% [Movespeed](stats.md#Speed), +15 Gather Amount, +200 [Convert Amount](system-page.md#Convert_Amount), +1 [Attack](stats.md#Attack).
* 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](gifted-bee.md): +20% [Bond from Treats](system-page.md#Bond_From_Treats).

### Abilities

* **[Fetch](ability-tokens.md#Fetch)** Creates a ball that collects tokens and increases pollen by 100% (+15% per [Level](bond.md)) as you and Puppy Bee kick it to each other. Collection increases per kick. Combo hits to win [Treats](treat.md).
* **[Puppy Love](ability-tokens.md#Puppy_Love)** Increases your bond with up to 10 nearby bees by 60 (+20 per lvl). Grants +10% Bee Movespeed and +50% Bee Gather Amount for 10 seconds (+1s per lvl).

## Trivia

* When Puppy Bee came out on the 2018-09-10 Update, it was on sale in the Ticket Tent for 250 tickets until September 22, 2018.
* It takes 3 hits of the ball by the owner and 3 hits by the Puppy Bee (3 volleys) to drop a [treat token](treat.md). The more times the ball is hit in a row, the more treats are earned from the treat token (Doubled every time a combo is done, starting at 1, capping at 256).
  * Once the treat token reaches the capped amount of treats, the ball will permanently stay light green and will not be able to produce any more treat tokens.
  * It takes a total of 54 hits to reach the max amount of treats, and 60 to get 10x Reindeer Guidance.
  * When a treat token of this kind is collected, even if more than 1 treat is earned from the treat token, it will always count as 1 treat token collected in quests.
* Puppy Bee was the first bee to be able to create treat tokens, the second being [Festive Bee](festive-bee.md) with its [festive gift](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Gift) ability.
* Puppy Bee was likely made to be [Tabby Bee's](tabby-bee.md) canine counterpart.
* Sometimes, the ball spawned by the fetch token can get stuck, allowing the player and Puppy Bee to easily pass it back and forth.
* Puppy Bee was the first [bee](bees.md) that has a 3D model on its face (its tongue).
* Puppy Bee, [Bear Bee](bear-bee.md), and Tabby Bee are currently the only bees that were sold at a discount for a limited amount of time.
* This bee, Bear Bee, [Gummy Bee](gummy-bee.md), Tabby Bee, Festive Bee, [Digital Bee](digital-bee.md), and [Windy Bee](windy-bee.md) are the only Event bees that can be a [First Edition Bee](first-edition-bee.md).
  * First Edition Puppy Bee could have been obtained by buying it before September 22, 2018.
* Treat tokens produced by Puppy Bee's fetch can be collected with a Token Link, similar to [Honey Gifts](ability-tokens.md#Honey_Gift).
* Puppy Bee is the third bee to produce a token that has its face on it, the second being Tabby Bee, and the first being [Rage Bee](rage-bee.md).
* This is the only bee that is able to raise the bond of other bees, through its puppy love token.
* This bee, [Vicious Bee](vicious-bee.md), [Crimson Bee](crimson-bee.md), [Cobalt Bee](cobalt-bee.md), and Windy Bee are the only Event bees that don't have an 'enhanced' ability if gifted.
* The puppy love token glows when collected.
* Puppy Bee's wings clip through its ears.
* Bee Bear's Puppy Bee is male.
* This and Festive Bee are the only bees that can produce gingerbread bears.
* This bee, Festive Bee, and [Demon Bee](demon-bee.md) are the only bees that have an exclusive beequip for themselves.
* During the Beesmas 2020 Event, players could purchase a special bundle for 800 Robux in the [Robux Shop](robux-shop.md) that would grant them a [Puppy Bee Egg](egg.md#Puppy_Bee_Egg), the special [Reindeer Antlers](reindeer-antlers.md) Beequip specifically made for the Puppy Bee, and 50 [Gingerbread Bears](gingerbread-bear.md) as a bonus.
  * If the player already had a Puppy Bee when they bought the bundle, they would get 500 tickets instead.
  * This makes Puppy Bee the only bee available to purchase in the Robux Shop twice.
* This bee, along with [Tadpole Bee](tadpole-bee.md), Tabby Bee, [Lion Bee](lion-bee.md), and Bear Bee are the only bees based on real animals.
