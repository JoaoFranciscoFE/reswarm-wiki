# Bloom

*For a similar mob that only appears in Retro Swarm Challenge, see [Brick Bloom](brick-bloom.md).*

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">A Bloom.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

**Blooms** are passive field-based entities that can spawn naturally on fields or be summoned using a [Bloom Shaker](bloom-shaker.md). It first appeared in the [2025-12-25 update](updates.md#2025-12-25). They manifest as large flowers adorned with colored petals. The maximum amount of petals a bloom can contain scales with its level.

Collecting petals from blooms gives the respective petal's buff, and honey equal to half of its original HP. Petals will give at least guaranteed 50 honey when collected, even if the bloom's health is under 100 pollen.

When a bloom is first created, it has a 20-second timer and a set "base" petal colour, which will generally make up the majority of the bloom. Once the pollen requirements are met, the bloom will pop, scattering each petal on the bloom in a circular pattern. Once popped, another "child" bloom of higher level and identical base colour will spawn. After a minute after the original "parent" bloom had spawned, child blooms of that parent will no longer be spawned. When a bloom is created, it has a small chance to be "mutated", where one or more petals are a different colour to the base colour of the bloom. As level increases, the chance of a bloom being mutated increases. A parent bloom being mutated does not affect the chance of a child bloom also having a mutation.

Hitting blooms with a Petal Shuriken (either from [Petal Wand](petal-wand.md) or [Petal Belt](petal-belt.md)'s ability) makes them spawn bonus petals. It also deals 20% of the remaining HP and 5% of the Bloom's max HP.

## Levels

Blooms have multiple levels. Each time a bloom is defeated, it respawns at a higher level, similarly to how [puffshrooms](puffshroom.md) work.

### Petals per Level

A bloom has different amounts of petals depending on its level. The number of petals a bloom can have is capped at 11, at level 20 or above.

The formula below can be used to find the number of petals a bloom of any level below 20 will have. \(\lfloor (level\times 2)\div 5\rfloor +3\)

## Petals

Once the required amount of pollen is collected within the bloom's radius, it explodes, releasing multiple petals that scatter around in arranged in a ring around its position. Collecting these petals grants a stack of petal buff and honey, with the amount scaling based on the bloom's level and field it's on. Its petals are randomized but have altered chances depending on the field. However, the [Hub Field](hub-field.md)'s petals have nearly equal chances as each other.

Additionally, each petal provides a temporary boost corresponding to its color, working similarly to [Jelly Bean](jelly-beans.md) boosts. These boosts can stack up to 100 times, with each individual stack lasting 10 seconds. Every stack grants at least +40% [Unique Instant Conversion](instant-conversion.md#Unique_Instant_Conversion).
