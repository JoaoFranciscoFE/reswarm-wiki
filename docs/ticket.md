# Ticket

![Ticket](img/Ticket.png){ align=right width=150 }

Ticket

*"Used to activate and purchase special things!"*

**Tickets** are a form of currency added in the [2018-04-27 update](updates.md#2018-4-27). They can be used to purchase certain [items](items.md).

## Ways to Obtain

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The <a href="ticket-shop.html">Ticket Shop</a>.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

* The player can obtain **Tickets** as a reward for acquiring [badges](badges.md). Collecting badges gives:
  * 5 **Tickets** for earning Cadet badges.
  * 10 **Tickets** for earning Hotshot badges.
  * 15 **Tickets** for earning Ace badges.
  * 25 **Tickets** for earning Master badges.
  * 40 **Tickets** for earning Grandmaster badges.
  * In total, 2,375 **Tickets** can be obtained from Badges. (2,335 **Tickets** if not counting the currently impossible Sticker Stack Grandmaster Badge.)
* The player can buy **Tickets** from the [Ticket Shop](ticket-shop.md) with [Honey](honey.md), located outside the [Mountain Top Shop](mountain-top-shop.md). The price increases with every **Ticket** bought.
* Some bears give **Tickets** as rewards for completing certain [quests](quests.md).
* Redeeming certain valid [codes](codes.md):
  * 38217 (Gives 5 **Tickets**).
  * Bopmaster (Gives 5 **Tickets**).
  * Crawlers (Gives 5 **Tickets**).
  * Roof (Gives 5 **Tickets**).
  * Connoisseur (Gives 5 **Tickets**).
  * Wax (Gives 5 **Tickets** + other items).
* Buying them from the [Robux Shop](robux-shop.md) for Robux:
  * 1 Ticket for 10 Robux ( 10 Robux per **Ticket**).
  * 100 Tickets for 400 Robux ( 4 Robux per **Ticket**).
  * 510 Tickets for 1,700 Robux ( 3.33 Robux per **Ticket**).
  * 1,800 Tickets for 4,500 Robux ( 2.5 Robux per **Ticket**).
* The [Wealth Clock](wealth-clock.md) gives **Tickets** every hour after interacting with it, and increases incrementally every hour, stopping at the fifth (1 ticket first time, 2 second time, 3 third time, 4 fourth time, and 5 fifth time and onwards).
* They can spawn as a potential match from Normal, Mega, Night and Extreme [Memory Matches](memory-match.md).
* Clicking on any Hidden [Sticker](sticker.md) grants 1 **Ticket** each (excluding seeker-stickers).
* Adding certain [Stickers](sticker.md) to the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md) grants **Tickets**.
* As a Reward for being in the top 100 of any daily leaderboard, ranging from 25, 50 to 100 **Tickets** depending on the leaderboard.
* Redeeming a [Ticket Voucher](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) grants 100 **Tickets**.
* 1,000 **Tickets** will be given if you already have a [Digital Bee](digital-bee.md) after purchasing the [Robo Bear's Shop's](robo-bear-s-shop.md) [Digital Bee](digital-bee.md) deal.

### Drops

* As a drop from [planters](planter.md):
  * The number of **Tickets** it can potentially drop depends on the type of planter.
  * [Ticket Planters](ticket-planter.md) always drop 10+ **Tickets** when harvested at full growth.
* As a common drop from [Mythic Meteors](mythic-meteor-shower.md).
* As a drop from [Stockings](stockings.md).
* There is a chance of getting a **Ticket** as a reward for defeating [mobs](mobs.md). The probability depends on the type of mob as well as the player's Loot Luck.
* [Tunnel Bear](tunnel-bear.md), [Stump Snail](stump-snail.md), and [King Beetle](king-beetle.md) are guaranteed to grant **Tickets** upon defeat.
* [Wild Windy Bee](wild-windy-bee.md) will yield a limited amount of **Tickets** per day.
* The [Commando Chick](commando-chick.md) will always drop 1 **Ticket** upon being captured.
  * Capturing it for the 25th time rewards 100 **Tickets**.

* Defeating [Snowbear](snowbear.md) will drop an amount of **Tickets** equal its level upon defeat.
* Completing a [Robo Party](robo-party-cake.md#Robo_Party) will reward an amount of **Tickets** equal to the party's rank.
* Through a [Cub Buddy](cub-buddy.md#Skins) gift.
* [Puffshrooms](puffshroom.md) drop **Tickets** (the rarer the Puffshroom, the more it gives).
* [Honeystorms](honeystorm.md) are guaranteed to spawn **Ticket** tokens.
* As a [Retro Swarm Challenge](retro-swarm-challenge.md) Reward.
* As a rare drop from [Festive Gifts](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Gift).
* As a [Sprout](sprout.md) reward, besides Moon and Debug variants (the rarer the sprout, the more **Tickets** it will yield; rare sprouts and above guarantee **Tickets**).
* [Sparkles](sparkles.md) may yield **Tickets**.
* Very rarely, [flowers](flowers.md) may generate a **Ticket** token. The base probability is around 1/1000 (0.1%), however it does increase with [Loot Luck](loot-luck.md) and [Ticket Chance](system-page.md#Ticket_Chance).

### Token Locations

* On top of the biggest pineapple in the [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md). Grants 3 **Tickets**.
* At the back of the cave that the spider comes from. Grants 1 **Ticket**.
* In the back of [Red HQ](red-hq.md), near the ceiling (the best way to get it seems to be to get x5 [Haste](ability-tokens.md#Haste), climb on top of the [teleporter](red-teleporter.md), jump up and walk on the edge of the leaderboard, and then glide to the **Ticket**. The enhanced jump ability granted by certain hats, boots, and/or the [bear morph](ability-tokens.md#Bear_Morph) will also help).
* Next to the Glider inside the [Mountain Top Shop](mountain-top-shop.md).
* On a platform in the [Cloud Obby](obstacle-courses.md#Cloud_Obby), fairly high above the [Pine Tree Forest](pine-tree-forest.md) and [Pumpkin Patch](pumpkin-patch.md). Grants 5 **Tickets**. (Jump off the tip of the red shoulder guard of the 30 Bee Gate, then glide or parachute down to it).
* On a small ledge between the [Lion Bee Gate](lion-bee-gate.md) and the [Brave Bee Gate](brave-bee-gate.md). Grants 5 **Tickets**. The player must use the slingshot and move to the left. Parachute at the right moment to land on the rock. Alternative: Jump down from in front of the Lion Bee Gate. A Parachute/Glider can be helpful, but not absolutely necessary.
* Behind the red shoulder guard of the [Bear Gate](bear-gate.md) (i.e. inside the 30 Bee Area). Grants 10 **Tickets**.
* On the awning over [Mother Bear's](mother-bear.md) area. (Easily reached from the ramp that leads to the [Honey Bee Gate](honey-bee-gate.md)). Grants 1 **Ticket**.
* On top of the [Ant Gate](ant-gate.md). Grants 5 **Tickets**. (Reachable by jumping on top of the [Stinger Shop](stinger-shop.md), then crossing to the ledge.
* Behind the [Honey Dispenser](honey-dispenser.md) in the [Lava Obby](obstacle-courses.md). Upon entering, immediately turn right, and jump up onto the ledge. Grants 3 **Tickets**. This replaced one of the Plastic Egg locations.
* Underneath the [Basic Bee Gate](basic-bee-gate.md) ramp, where [Honey Bee](honey-bee-npc.md) used to be. Grants 3 **Tickets**. This replaced one of the Plastic Egg locations, when they were removed.
* In the [Blue Maze](mazes.md#Blue_Maze), turn left and then take the first right. Grants 5 **Tickets**. This replaced one of the Plastic Egg locations.
* Inside the tunnel behind the [Coconut Dispenser](coconut-dispenser.md) in the [Coconut Cave](coconut-cave.md). Grants 5 **Tickets**.

## Uses

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The Ticket Tent.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

* Buying a [Royal Jelly](royal-jelly.md) From the [Royal Jelly Dispenser](royal-jelly-dispenser.md) for 6 **Tickets** with a cooldown of 3 seconds.
* Buying [Gumdrops](gumdrops.md) from the [Gumdrop Shop](gumdrop-shop.md) at the cost of 1 **Ticket** per 3 [Gumdrops](gumdrops.md).
* Buying [Stingers](stinger.md) from the [Stinger Shop](stinger-shop.md) at the cost of 10 **Tickets** per [Stinger](stinger.md).
* Buying [Magic Beans](magic-bean.md) from the [Magic Bean Shop](magic-bean-shop.md) at the cost of 20 **Tickets** per [Magic Bean](magic-bean.md).
* 1 **Ticket** is required to use an [Instant Converter](instant-converter.md).
* 10 **Tickets** are required to use the [Ant Pass Dispenser](ant-pass-dispenser.md).
* The [Ticket Tent](ticket-tent.md) has the following items available:
  * [Silver Egg](egg.md#Silver_Egg) (50 **Tickets**).
  * [Gold Egg](egg.md#Gold_Egg) (100 **Tickets**).
  * [Diamond Egg](egg.md#Diamond_Egg) (400 **Tickets**).
  * [Mythic Egg](egg.md#Mythic_Egg) (2,500 **Tickets**).
  * [Cobalt Bee Egg](egg.md#Cobalt_Bee_Egg) and [Crimson Bee Egg](egg.md#Crimson_Bee_Egg) (250 **Tickets** each, one time purchase).
  * Miscellaneous [Event Bees](bees-event.md) (500 **Tickets** each, one time purchase).
  * [Star Treat](star-treat.md) (1,000 **Tickets**).
* 50 **Tickets** are required to start another [Stick Bug Challenge](stick-bug-challenge.md) when the player's free challenge is already used.
* **Tickets** can be used to speed up materials that are currently being made in the [Blender](blender.md).
* 5 **Tickets** are required to store [nectar](nectar.md) in the [Nectar Pot](nectar-pot.md).
* 50 **Tickets** are required to use the [Robo Pass Dispenser](robo-pass-dispenser.md).
* Varying amounts of **Tickets** can be used to increase the maximum amount of [Beequips](beequip.md) one can hold in their Beequip Storage, requiring 123,500 **Tickets** in total to max it out.
* Varying amounts of **Tickets** (Starting at 20 and capping at 1,000 per upgrade) can be used to increase the maximum amount of [Stickers](sticker.md) one can hold in their Sticker Storage, requiring 42,420 **Tickets** in total to max it out.
* **Tickets**, along with [Honey](honey.md), are used to purchase Beequips in [Dapper Bear's Shop](dapper-bear-s-shop.md).

## Trivia

* It bears the face of a [Basic Bee](basic-bee.md).
* Currently, the most expensive purchase that can be made using **Tickets** is the final slot upgrade for the [Beequip Storage](beequip.md), costing 20,000 **Tickets** to increase storage from a maximum of 95 stored Beequips to 100 stored Beequips.
* **Tickets**, along with [Honey](honey.md), were used to purchase Market Boosts from the [Boost Market](boost-market.md).
* You need a total of 179,420 **Tickets** in total to Max out Sticker and Beequip storages, buy all event bees from the [Ticket Tent](ticket-tent.md) and gift all of the event bees with [Star Treat](star-treat.md).
