# Battle Points

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The Global Top Battlers leaderboard.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

**Battle Points** are points used for Global Top Battlers Leaderboard rankings and the [Battle Badge](badges.md#Battle_Badge). They are obtained by defeating certain [mobs](mobs.md).

The player can see how many battle points they have by stepping on the pad in front of the Global Top Battlers Leaderboard or by looking in your [badges](badges.md) and scrolling down to the Battle Badge.

## Battle Points per Mob

* [Ladybugs](ladybug.md), [rhino beetles](rhino-beetle.md), and [cave monsters](cave-monster.md): 1 point.
* [Mantises](mantis.md) and [scorpions](scorpion.md): 5 points.
* [Spiders](spider.md): 10 points.
* [Aphids](aphid.md): 10 points if normal. If not, 20 points.
* [Werewolves](werewolf.md): 25 points.
* [King Beetle](king-beetle.md): 150 points.
* [Tunnel Bear](tunnel-bear.md): 200 points.
* [Coconut Crab](coconut-crab.md): 400 points.
* [Stump Snail](stump-snail.md): 500 points.

**Mobs not mentioned give zero battle points.**

## Trivia

* The first player to get the Battle Master Badge was DB4444.
* The first player to get the Battle Grandmaster Badge was MunkNull.
* No mob has been added since the [2019-09-28](updates.md#2019-09-28) update that gives Battle Points.
