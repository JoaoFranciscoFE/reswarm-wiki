The **Pumpkin Patch** is a [field](fields.md) located past the [Honey Bee Gate](honey-bee-gate.md) and it lies right beside the [Cactus Field](cactus-field.md). It is protected by the [werewolf](werewolf.md).

This field can spawn [honey](honey.md), [treat](treat.md), [ticket](ticket.md), [magic bean](magic-bean.md), and [glue](glue.md) tokens when collecting [pollen](pollen.md).

[Bees](bees.md) that like the Pumpkin Patch are [Honey Bee](honey-bee.md), [Shy Bee](shy-bee.md), [Bear Bee](bear-bee.md), and [Photon Bee](photon-bee.md). Bees that dislike the Pumpkin Patch are [Bomber Bee](bomber-bee.md), [Hasty Bee](hasty-bee.md), and [Gummy Bee](gummy-bee.md).

This field grants Satisfying [Nectar](nectar.md) and an increased chance on dropping waxes when a [planter](planter.md) is planted here.

A level 1-20 [Wild Windy Bee](wild-windy-bee.md) has a chance of spawning here.

Additionally, the largest pumpkin in the corner of the field seldomly makes a face before generating [sparkles](sparkles.md) in the field.

This is a 17x33 field, containing 561 [flowers](flowers.md). The flowers are white, red, and blue, in sizes of double and triple.

## Obtaining Boosts

* Using the [Field Booster](field-booster.md) may give a Pumpkin [Patch Boost](field-boost.md). When it does, it will give +200% Pumpkin Patch pollen for 15 minutes.

  

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 75px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The Pumpkin Patch boost icon.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

* Using [glitter](glitter.md) in the field will add 100% Pumpkin Patch pollen onto the current boost if it isn't already maxed out.
* Using [Field Dice](field-dice.md), [Smooth Dice](smooth-dice.md), or [Loaded Dice](loaded-dice.md) may add +100% - 300% Pumpkin Patch pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out.
* Pumpkin [Patch Winds](field-wind.md) can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md) or [codes](codes.md). They have a base of +20% instant Pumpkin Patch conversion and give +5% instant Pumpkin Patch conversion and +20% Pumpkin Patch pollen for each stack. Pumpkin Patch Winds can stack up to a total of 13 times normally and can be boosted by up to 15x by using codes.
* It can be boosted by the [Shell Amulet](shell-amulet.md). If it has a Pumpkin Patch Boost, it can boost the field by +5% to +30% depending on the amulet rarity.
* Giving Polar Bear a [Present](present.md) during Beesmas gives the player Pumpkin Patch Boost x3.

### The methods below are no longer possible.

* Giving a [present](present.md) to certain NPCs:
  * [Polar Bear](polar-bear.md) gave Pumpkin Patch Boost x4 and other boosts and [items](items.md).
  * [Onett](onett.md) gave Pumpkin Patch Boost x1 and other boosts and items.
  * During Beesmas 2021, Polar Bear gave Pumpkin Patch Boost x3 and other boosts and items.
* Redeeming certain expired codes:
  * Tabby (Gave Pumpkin Patch Boost x1 + other stuff).
  * ThnxCyasToyBox (Gave Pumpkin Patch Boost x3 and +100% Pumpkin Patch Capacity + other stuff).
  * Xanthan (Gave Pumpkin Patch Boost x4 + other stuff).
  * PumpkinOfJustice (Gave Pumpkin Patch Boost x7 + other stuff).
  * PuppyPerk (Gave Pumpkin Patch Boost x2 + other stuff).
  * TrickOrTreat (Gave Pumpkin Patch Boost x6 + other stuff).
  * GravyCatFan (Gave Pumpkin Patch Boost x4 + other stuff).
  * DarzethPack (Gave Pumpkin Patch Boost x3 + other stuff).
  * Trying (Gave Pumpkin Patch Boost x2 + other stuff).
  * MoreTime (Gave Pumpkin Patch Boost x3 + other stuff).
  * BeeDay2019 (Gave Pumpkin Patch Boost x4 + other stuff).
  * Leftovers (Gave Pumpkin Patch Boost x3 and Pumpkin Patch Winds x3 + other stuff).
  * Boo swarm (Gives 4x Pumpkin Patch Boost + 15x Pumpkin Patch Winds + other stuff)
* Purchasing a Pumpkin Patch [Market Boost](buffs-debuffs.md#From_Areas) from the [Boost Market](boost-market.md) will give x1.5 [Pumpkin Patch Capacity](field-capacity.md), x1.25 Pumpkin Patch Pollen, x1.25 [Capacity](system-page.md#Capacity_Multiplier), and +50 [Convert Amount](system-page.md#Convert_Amount).

## Trivia

* This [field's badge](badges.md#Pumpkin_Badge) and the [Sunflower Badge](badges.md#Sunflower_Badge) are used 3 times for chances in [Memory Match](memory-match.md), the most out of any field badges.
* There is a [Royal Jelly](royal-jelly.md) token behind the two pumpkins in the corner.
* This is one of the 3 fields to have objects come to life. The others are [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md) and [Sunflower Field](sunflower-field.md).
* Before the 2018-04-27 update, the Pumpkin Patch had the same mix of [flowers](flowers.md) as the Cactus Field.
* This field used to be the smallest field until the [Stump Field](stump-field.md) was added.
  * Due to the expansion of the Stump Field, this field has now become the smallest again (if one weren't to count the [Ant Field](ant-field.md)).
* This field is one of three fields to have the word "Patch" in their name instead of "Field," with the [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md) and [Pepper Patch](pepper-patch.md) being the other two.
* Prior to the 2022-12-26 update, the pumpkins nearest to the [Werewolf's Cave](werewolf-s-cave.md) had collision. Their collision has since been removed.
