# Pineapple

Pineapple

*"Increases your bond with a bee by 25."*

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 168px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">A <span typeof="mw:Error mw:File"></span><strong class="mw-selflink selflink"><span class="color-template color-template-pineapple color-template-background-clip">Pineapple</span></strong> token.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

A **Pineapple** is a type of [treat](treats.md) that can be fed to a [bee](bees.md) to raise its bond by 25. However, when fed to a [Hasty](hasty-bee.md), [Brave](brave-bee.md), [Honey](honey-bee.md), [Exhausted](exhausted-bee.md), [Shocked](shocked-bee.md), [Fire](fire-bee.md), [Demon](demon-bee.md), [Lion](lion-bee.md), or [Fuzzy](fuzzy-bee.md) Bee, the amount of [bond](bond.md) they gain is doubled. These bees also have a small chance of becoming gifted when fed **Pineapples**. [Mythic bees](bees-mythic.md) have a 1/24,000 chance to be gifted; [Legendary Bees](bees-legendary.md), a 1/12,000 chance; [Epic bees](bees-epic.md), a 1/10,000 chance; and [Rare bees](bees-rare.md), a 1/8,000 chance. Feeding a radioactive bee a pineapple has a 0.004% (1 in 25,000) to [mutate](mutation.md) a bee.

## Ways to Obtain

* 5 **Pineapples** can be found behind the pineapple near the [Treat Dispenser](treat-dispenser.md).
* 1 **Pineapple** is given to the player every time they use the Treat Dispenser.
* As a reward from the [Ant Challenge](ant-challenge.md).
* As a reward from [Memory Matches](memory-match.md) (Every type except [Night Memory Match](memory-match.md#Night_Memory_Match) and the [Winter Memory Match](memory-match.md#Winter_Memory_Match)).
* As a stack reward for adding certain [Stickers](sticker.md) to the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md):
  * [Shocked Hive Slot Sticker](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) rewards 50 **Pineapples**.
  * [Rhubarb Sticker](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) rewards 50 **Pineapples**.

### Drops

* Farming in the [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md) and in the [Hub Field](hub-field.md).
  * Harvesting [leaves](leaves.md) in [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md) may also yield **Pineapples**.
* As a drop from [mantises](mantis.md), [scorpions](scorpion.md), [spiders](spider.md), [werewolves](werewolf.md), [Commando Chick](commando-chick.md), [Stick Nymphs](stick-nymph.md), [Stick Bug](stick-bug.md), [King Beetle](king-beetle.md), and [Tunnel Bear](tunnel-bear.md).
* As a drop from a [Festive Gift](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Gift) or [Festive Mark](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Mark).
* As a gift from a [cub buddy](cub-buddy.md).
* As a reward for completely growing any kind of [sprout](sprout.md) except for Moon, Gummy, and Debug sprouts.
  * Planting a sprout in the Pineapple Patch yields 7x more **Pineapples** than other fields.
* Harvesting [sparkles](sparkles.md) (Higher chances in the [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md)).
* As a drop from [Puffshrooms](puffshroom.md).
* As a drop from [planters](planter.md), which include:
  * [Ticket Planters](ticket-planter.md)
  * [Festive Planters](festive-planter.md)
  * [Plastic Planters](plastic-planter.md)
  * [Candy Planters](candy-planter.md)
  * [Blue Clay Planters](blue-clay-planter.md)
  * [Red Clay Planters](red-clay-planter.md)
  * [Pesticide Planters](pesticide-planter.md)
  * [Planters Of Plenty](the-planter-of-plenty.md)
* Digging into the [Beesmas Feast](beesmas-feast.md) sometimes grants **Pineapples**.

### Quests

* [Onett](onett.md) gives pineapples on the following quest:
  * Star Journey 1 gives 25 **Pineapples**.
* [Spirit Bear](spirit-bear.md) gives **Pineapples** on the following quests:
  * Witness Grandeur gives 100 **Pineapples**.
  * Nature's Lessons gives 250 **Pineapples**.
  * Rhubarb That Could Have Been gives 250 **Pineapples**.
  * Do You Bee-Lleve in Magic? gives 300 **Pineapples**.
  * Beans Becoming gives 500 **Pineapples**.
  * Echoing Call gives 500 **Pineapples** upon receiving the quest.
