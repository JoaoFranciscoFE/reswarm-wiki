# Artisan Bear

**Artisan Bear** is a [quest giver](quest-givers.md) added with the [Painter Bee event](painter-bee-event.md). Artisan Bear is a painter who finds the fields "lovely... but so predictable", and paints patches of them in the opposite colour for you to harvest.

The questline has **15 quests**. The last one rewards a **Painter Bee Voucher**, which redeems for a [Painter Bee](painter-bee.md) Egg.

## Location

Artisan Bear stands at an easel next to the [Royal Jelly Dispenser](royal-jelly-dispenser.md), close to the [Clover Field](clover-field.md).

## Questline

* The first quest has no requirements. Each later quest unlocks when you finish the one before it.
* Every quest is one-time only. None of them can be repeated.
* In the quest log, quests show as **🎨 Artisan Bear (N/15): Quest name**.
* After quest 15, Artisan Bear only says: *"The fields have never looked better. Come visit my gallery any time."*

## Painted flowers

Every quest asks you to **harvest Painted Flowers** in that quest's field.

* While anyone in the server has an Artisan Bear quest, Artisan Bear paints that quest's field. Each paint job covers 3 patches, each with a radius of 7 studs.
* The paint lasts **75 seconds**. The next paint job on that field comes 45 seconds after the paint dries.
* The paint is the opposite of the field's main colour. Red fields get blue paint and blue fields get red. White or mixed fields get red or blue at random.
* Players on the quest get an alert: *"🎨 Artisan Bear painted patches of the (field) (colour)!"*
* Each painted flower counts once per paint job. Only flowers harvested while the quest is active count.

## Rewards

* **Painter Bee Voucher** (quest 15): redeems for a [Painter Bee](painter-bee.md) Egg, once per account. Extra copies can go on the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md); see [Painter Stickers](painter-stickers.md).
* **[Toy Drum](toy-drum.md)** (quests 5 and 10): an Epic [Beequip](beequip.md).
* Across all 15 quests: 1,918 Tickets, 323 Royal Jelly, 36 Star Jelly, 38 Cloud Vials, 20 Glitter, 20 Glue, 13 Magic Beans, 2 Enzymes, 25 Blueberries and 10 Treats.
