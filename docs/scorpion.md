The **Scorpion** is a [mob](mobs.md) that spawns in the [Rose Field](rose-field.md).

It takes 20 minutes for a scorpion to respawn after being defeated (17 minutes with [Gifted](gifted-bee.md) [Vicious Bee](vicious-bee.md)). Defeating it guarantees 5 [Battle Points](battle-points.md) and 1000 [Honey](honey.md). Various other rewards are possible with their probability increasing with [Loot Luck](loot-luck.md). It deals 25 damage to the player (before accounting [Defense](system-page.md#Defense)).

## Drops

* 5 Battle Points.
* ~1000 [Honey](honey.md).
