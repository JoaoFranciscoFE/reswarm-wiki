A **Mechsquito** is one of the five [Mobs](mobs.md) that spawns during [Robo Bear's Challenge](robo-bear-challenge.md).

This is the first Robo Bear Challenge mob that the player will encounter, appearing as early as round one. Mechsquitos often spawn a few seconds into the round.

Mechquitos spawn far away from the player and will fly towards the players' vicinity. Mechquitos hover away from the player when they are near them when not in an attacking state. When starting to attack, they will become immobile and only aim at the player for a few seconds before shooting a small exploding projectile aimed at the player. The projectile explodes upon contact or after a short while. When in the vicinity of its explosion radius, the player will get damaged and receive [Mechsquito Toxin](buffs-debuffs.md#Debuffs), lowering the Pollen and Movespeed of the player and can stack up to 10 times. Note: The toxin projectile cannot damage and/or affect other players, only the player playing the challenge.

A Mechsquito's level can increase depending on what round of Robo Bear's Challenge the player is on.

## Strategies

* Mechsquitos aim for where the player is, not where the player will be. Because of this, moving around in circles will often result in Mechsquitos missing their attack.
* Stand near the Mechsquito, this will allow your bees to lock on to the Mechsquito and quickly deal damage.
* [Vicious Bee](vicious-bee.md) and [Windy Bee](windy-bee.md) provide excellent crowd control, especially since Mechsquitos often come in pairs or packs. [Digital Bee](digital-bee.md) can also stun many Mechsquitos at once and allow them to take extra damage via [Mind Hack](ability-tokens.md#Mind_Hack).

## Trivia

* Mechsquito is a portmanteau of "Mechanical" and "Mosquito".
* Mechsquitos have a party variant called Party Mechsquito.
* Mechsquitos have a stronger variant called [Mega Mechsquito](mega-mechsquito.md).
* Mechsquitos are the only mob that can appear in every round of Robo Bear's Challenge.
  * It's also the mob with the most levels in Robo Bear's Challenge.
