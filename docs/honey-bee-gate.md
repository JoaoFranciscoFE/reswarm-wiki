The **Honey Bee Gate**, also called the **15 Bee Gate**, is a gate that requires 15 [bees](bees.md) to be able to pass through. It is the third gate that the player should be able to access, ahead of the [Brave Bee Gate](brave-bee-gate.md) and the [Basic Bee Gate](basic-bee-gate.md).

There are 4 different fields behind the Honey Bee Gate, being the [Cactus Field](cactus-field.md), the [Pumpkin Patch](pumpkin-patch.md), the [Pine Tree Forest](pine-tree-forest.md), and the [Rose Field](rose-field.md).

It is given its name from the large [Honey Bee](honey-bee.md) located on top of it.

## Trivia

* This gate has the highest number of [fields](fields.md) behind it, which is four.
* There were numerous ways to bypass the gate without 15 bees, but as of the 2019-09-28 update, it will always teleport the player back to spawn.
* It has the second-most quest givers, at 4, with the Starter Zone beating it with 5.
* This gate holds the ramp that leads to the [Lion Bee Gate](lion-bee-gate.md), also known as the 25 Bee Gate.
* This gate has the most assets in it, with 19 of them.
