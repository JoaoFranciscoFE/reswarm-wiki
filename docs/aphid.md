# Aphid

**Aphids** are hostile [mobs](mobs.md) that have a 0.932% chance (1 in 107) of spawning from collecting [leaves](leaves.md), with the chances increasing when a Field is experiencing a period of low activity (i.e. players are not visiting said field very often). Their level and health correlate to which field the aphid spawns in, like [Rogue Vicious Bee](rogue-vicious-bee.md). Sharing a similar trait with [Tunnel Bear](tunnel-bear.md), [Coconut Crab](coconut-crab.md), [Chicks](chicks.md#Chick), [Hostage Chicks](chicks.md#Hostage_Chick), [Wild Windy Bee](wild-windy-bee.md), [Mondo Chick](chicks.md#Mondo_Chick), and [Puffshrooms](puffshroom.md), players have a limited time (30 seconds) to defeat an aphid. After the time is up, the aphid despawns. The timer is shown above its health. Aphids attack in a similar matter to [Stick Nymphs](stick-nymph.md) and [Giant Ants](ants.md), following the nearest player around slowly. Aphids are public mobs, meaning that anyone can collect the drops regardless of whether or not they attacked it.

Defeating an aphid awards 10 [Battle Points](battle-points.md) (20 if defeating one of the 3 rare types), [Bond](bond.md), and various other rewards. Some rewards have a cooldown, meaning that they will not be obtainable for a while after receiving them once, regardless of the level or type. However, you can still get them through other players spawning an aphid with said rewards at the expense of applying the same cooldown to them also.

## Types

Aphids always appear as 1 of 4 different variants: Normal, Rage, Armored, and Diamond. Different types of aphids may spawn at different encounter rates. Rewards vary between these types. [Honey](honey.md) amounts depend on the aphid's level and player's honey per token boost.

### Rage Aphid

They are colored similar to a [Gifted](gifted-bee.md) [Rage Bee](rage-bee.md) or Gifted [Spicy Bee](spicy-bee.md), with red bodies and green eyes, legs, and antennae.

## Tips

* Jumping and/or running around aphids will prevent them from attacking effectively since they slowly follow players.
* If players cannot defeat an aphid due to higher level and health, leveling up bees and having bees specializing in attack, such as [Vicious Bee](vicious-bee.md), will help kill the aphid faster.
* Using various consumables and [passives](passive-abilities.md) such as [Stingers](stinger.md), [Coconuts](coconut.md), [Tropical Drinks](tropical-drink.md), [Oils](oil.md), or [Star Saw](passive-abilities.md#Star_Saw) can help defeat aphids faster.
