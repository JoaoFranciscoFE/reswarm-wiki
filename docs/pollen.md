# Pollen

*Are you looking for [Flowers](flowers.md) or [Honey](honey.md)?*

**Pollen** is one of the main features in [Re://:Swarm](re-swarm.md). Pollen is collected in order to make [honey](honey.md). It can be obtained by using [tools](tools.md) (via tapping or clicking) on [flowers](flowers.md), from [bees](bees.md) collecting pollen and/or activating their [abilities](ability-tokens.md), from some [passive abilities](passive-abilities.md), from [meteors](mythic-meteor-shower.md), and from [coconuts](coconut.md). Flowers can have different sizes and different colors. Some [quests](quests.md) require pollen to be collected from a specific flower color or field. There are many sources of pollen multipliers, including [amulets](amulet.md), [bags](bags.md), masks, [badges](badges.md), [hive bonuses](gifted-bee.md), abilities, [field boosts](field-boost.md), [field winds](field-wind.md), and certain passive abilities.

Pollen is mainly used to convert it into [honey](honey.md) at the player's [hive](hive.md), through [Instant Converters](instant-converter.md), by using a [Micro-Converter](micro-converter.md), [Honeysuckle](honeysuckle.md), etc. This is one of the most common ways to get honey. Pollen is also used as a counter to track how much a player has farmed from a field in [badges](badges.md).

## Pollen Amount Per Flower

In order to get more pollen per collection, collect the biggest flowers on the field. The bigger the flower, the larger the pollen multiplier. There are 5 different flower sizes: single, double, triple, large, and star.

Collection amount and efficiency can be increased by using better tools (either collecting in a large area or multiplying pollen collection. For example, the [Scythe](scythe.md) and the [Bubble Wand](bubble-wand.md) each collect double red and blue pollen, respectively). Collecting [Bear Morph](ability-tokens.md#Bear_Morph), [Inspire](ability-tokens.md#Inspire), [Beesmas Cheer](ability-tokens.md#Beesmas_Cheer), [Baby Love](ability-tokens.md#Baby_Love) ability tokens, [Bear Bee](bear-bee.md) and [Basic Bee](basic-bee.md) [gifted](gifted-bee.md) bonuses, badges, using game [mechanics](mechanics.md), expanding the hive, different [amulets](amulet.md), using [items](items.md), and different [passive abilities](passive-abilities.md) can all lead to better pollen collection.

## Pollination

Main article: [Pollination](pollination.md)

When a flower is pollinated, it upgrades any kind of flower, with star flowers being the exception. Flowers can be pollinated by [Fuzzy Bee](fuzzy-bee.md), The [Diamond Drain](passive-abilities.md#Diamond_Drain) Passive, bees with the [Poinsettia](poinsettia.md) or [Lei](lei.md) beequip, and [Guiding Star](passive-abilities.md#Guiding_Star).

## Trivia

* Single, double, and triple flowers are the only types of flowers that can naturally spawn in fields. Large and star flowers can only be spawned through pollination.
* Before the 2020-04-06 update, the only stages of flowers were single, double, and large, with large flowers being the third level. This was changed to the current system, where the third tier was replaced with triple flowers, moving large to stage four.
* In real life, nectar is used by bees to be turned into honey and not pollen.
