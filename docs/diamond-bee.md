**Diamond Bee** is a Blue [Legendary bee](bees-legendary.md).

Diamond Bee's favorite treat are [Blueberries](blueberry.md).

Diamond Bee likes the [Blue Flower Field](blue-flower-field.md), and [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md). It dislikes the [Rose Field](rose-field.md).

## Stats

* Collects 10 [Pollen](pollen.md) in 4 seconds.
* Makes 1000 [Honey](honey.md) in 4 seconds.
* +920 [Convert Amount](system-page.md#Convert_Amount).
* 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](gifted-bee.md): x1.2 [Convert Rate](system-page.md#Convert_Rate).

### Abilities

* <img alt="Blue Boost" src="img/Blue_Boost.png" style="height:1.6em;width:auto;vertical-align:middle"/> **[Blue Boost](ability-tokens.md#Boost)** Grants x1.2 pollen from Blue flowers for 15 seconds. Stacks up to 10 times.
* <img alt="Honey Gift+" src="img/Honey_Gift+.png" style="height:1.6em;width:auto;vertical-align:middle"/> **[Honey Gift+](ability-tokens.md#Honey_Gift)** Sometimes spawns a Honey Token. The amount of honey is equal to 250 \* bee level \* bee level. (250 times the bee's level squared)
* **[Passive: Shimmering Honey](passive-abilities.md#Shimmering_Honey)** When converting at the hive, this bee grants 25% bonus honey (+2.5% per level). This bonus is doubled if Gifted. This is noticeable by the light blue conversion link when converting.

## Trivia

* The light colors around Diamond Bee make it nearly translucent.
* Diamond Bee has the highest base convert rate out of all bees, only beaten by [Tabby Bee](tabby-bee.md) when its Tabby Love stack is at 626 or higher.
* Diamond Bee and [Honey Bee](honey-bee.md) are the only [bees](bees.md) in the game which can generate Honey Gift or Honey Gift+ tokens.
* This, [Bubble Bee](bubble-bee.md), [Fire Bee](fire-bee.md), [Honey Bee](honey-bee.md), [Demon Bee](demon-bee.md), and [Gummy Bee](gummy-bee.md) are the only bees to have masks based on them, specifically the [Diamond Mask](diamond-mask.md) for Diamond Bee.
* Diamond Bee is the blue counterpart of [Demon Bee](demon-bee.md).
* Diamond Bee and Gummy Bee are the only bees to have a sparkle effect.
* Diamond Bee has a unique conversion link color (cyan/light blue). It is the only bee to have a conversion link that is not yellow.
* Diamond Bee is one of only two bees to have the same name as an [egg](egg.md) they can hatch from; the other being [Basic Bee](basic-bee.md), and those eggs being the [Diamond Egg](egg.md#Diamond_Egg) and [Basic Egg](egg.md#Basic_Egg) respectively.
* Diamond bee may like the [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md) because pineapples have historically been symbols of wealth.
