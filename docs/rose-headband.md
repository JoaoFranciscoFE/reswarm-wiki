The **Rose Headband** is a [level](bond.md) 13 [Beequip](beequip.md). It has an equip limit of one. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to [Basic](basic-bee.md), [Rascal](rascal-bee.md), [Riley](riley-bee.md), [Diamond](diamond-bee.md), [Windy](windy-bee.md) and [Vicious](vicious-bee.md) [bees](bees.md).
