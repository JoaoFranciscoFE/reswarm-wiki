# System Page

*This page is for stats that apply to the player, collecting [pollen](pollen.md), and the hive. For stats that apply to [bees](bees.md), see [Stats](stats.md).*

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The icon for the System Page.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">A small part of the System Page.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

The **System Page** is shown by clicking the cog in the menu bar. It is in between the [Badges](badges.md) page and the [Robux Shop](robux-shop.md) page. Here, the player can redeem [codes](codes.md), send [Trade Requests](trading.md), view the [Global Leaderboard](leaderboards.md), use the Cub Console, view the players' stats, turn on and off in-game [music](music.md), toggle Pollen Text, Abbreviate Pollen, allow Trade Requests, display and hide other players' [bees](bees.md), toggle Reducing Strobing VFX, and toggle Auto-Jelly. Turning off all in-game music only silences the music, not the sound effects (such as the sounds of [Ability Tokens](ability-tokens.md) and obtaining [Legendary](bees-legendary.md), [Mythic](bees-mythic.md), [Gifted](gifted-bee.md), and [Event](bees-event.md)). You can mute all sound by turning off the in-game sound on the Roblox menu.

## Character Stats

The tab also lists many of the player's active stat values, though not all of them. The following values are listed and explained:

### Convert Amount

Boosts the base conversion of your bees before multipliers by a certain amount.  
Example: A [Basic Bee](basic-bee.md) with 20 Convert Amount will convert 100 [Honey](honey.md) in 4 seconds instead of 80 [Honey](honey.md) in 4 seconds.  
It can be boosted with [amulets](amulet.md), the [Honey Mask](honey-mask.md) and certain belts. This stat was formerly named 'Conversion Amount' until it was changed in the Beesmas 2019 update.

### Convert Rate

Boosts your bees conversion by the following percentage.  
Example: A Basic Bee with 200% Convert Rate will convert 160 [Honey](honey.md) in 4 seconds instead of 80 [Honey](honey.md) in 4 seconds.  
It can be boosted with amulets, [Enzymes](enzymes.md), [Marshmallow Bees](marshmallow-bee.md), [Super Smoothies](super-smoothie.md), [Science Enhancement](buffs-debuffs.md#From_NPCs), the Conversion Buff, the [Honeyday Event buff](buffs-debuffs.md#From_Other), the [Honey Badge](badges.md#Honey_Badge), [Gifted](gifted-bee.md) [Diamond Bee](diamond-bee.md), the Yellow [Jellybean](jelly-beans.md), Jellybean Sharing Bonus, certain [Stickers](sticker.md) in the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md), some [beequips](beequip.md) and most [Bags](bags.md). This stat was formerly named 'Conversion Rate' until it was changed in the Beesmas 2019 update.

### Convert Rate At Hive

Boosts your bees’ conversion by the following percentage at the [hive](hive.md). This stat can stack with Convert Rate.  
Example: A level 1 Basic Bee with 200% Convert Rate and 200% Hive Convert Rate will convert 320 [Honey](honey.md) in 4 seconds while converting at the hive, but it will only convert 160 [Honey](honey.md) outside of the hive.  
It can be increased with the [Pumpkin Badge](badges.md#Pumpkin_Badge), Bubble Bloat and certain items. This buff was used to be "Hive Convert Rate" until the 2021-12-26 update.

### Bonus Convert Amount

Increases the base conversion of the player's bees. It can be increased by certain amulets and items.

### [Loot Luck](loot-luck.md)

Loot Luck is a passive attribute that increases the chances of receiving rewards from defeated [mobs](mobs.md).   
It can be increased with [Baby Love](ability-tokens.md#Baby_Love), the [Clover Badge](badges.md#Clover_Badge), [Baby Bee's](baby-bee.md) Gifted Hive Bonus and certain items.  
This stat was formerly named 'Luck' until it was changed in the 2019-04-05 update.

### [Critical Chance](critical-hits.md#Critical_Chance)

The chance of your bees hitting stronger or collecting more pollen. More accurately - by how big your Critical Power is.  
Example: A Basic Bee with 50% Critical Chance will hit with increased damage half of the time.  
It can be boosted with amulets, [Tropical Drinks](tropical-drink.md), [Focus](ability-tokens.md#Focus), the [Green Jellybean](jelly-beans.md), Gifted [Commander Bee](commander-bee.md) and certain items. Currently, the highest critical chance a player can have at a time is 88%.

### [Critical Power](critical-hits.md#Critical_Power)

This stat multiplies how strong a Critical Hit will be.
It applies to fighting and pollen collecting.  
Example: Basic Bee with 300% Critical Power (on critical hit) will hit with the power of 3 Basic Bees (3 damage).  
It can be boosted with the Ant Amulet, [Melody](ability-tokens.md#Melody), the [Battle Badge](badges.md#Battle_Badge), Gifted [Tabby Bee](tabby-bee.md), Gifted [Looker Bee](looker-bee.md) and with certain items. The calculated max Critical Power is 695%.

### [Super-Crit Chance](critical-hits.md#Super-Crit_Chance)

The possibility for a critical hit to Super-Crit. Super-Crits deals 33% more damage and collect twice as much pollen as a regular critical hit, and are instantly converted. When a hit is a Super-Crit, there will be a purple color indicating the damage and the **!** sign next to it. It can be boosted by having a Gifted Precise Bee, Tabby Blessing, Precision, a precise mark, the Bruteforce upgrade in the [Robo Bear Challenge](robo-bear-challenge.md) or using a [Super Smoothie](super-smoothie.md). The max possible Super-Crit Chance you can have at a time is 53% (assuming you are in the Robo Bear Challenge).

### [Super-Crit Power](critical-hits.md#Super-Crit_Power)

The amount pollen is multiplied by when performing Super-Crits (after applying Critical Power). Super-crit attacks are only increased by 33% as much as Pollen Super-crits. It can only be boosted by the [Whistle](whistle.md) and Kazoo Beequips, a [Cog Amulet](cog-amulet.md) or temporarily boosted by the [Dark Scythe](dark-scythe.md).

### Bee Ability Rate

Increases the speed at which the player's bees create [Ability Tokens](ability-tokens.md).  
Example: If it takes a bee about 20 seconds to produce a token, with 20% Bee Ability Rate, the bee is likely to take ~16 seconds to produce the token.  
Bee ability rate can be boosted by Star Amulets, Motivating Nectar, certain masks, items, bee mutations, and Beequips.

### [Pollen](pollen.md)

Increases pollen from all sources (both from [bees](bees.md) and [tools](tools.md)).  
Example: With 100% pollen, the player can collect 1,000 pollen, and with 110% pollen, it will able to collect 1,100 pollen from the same amount of time and [flowers](flowers.md).  
It can be increased with amulets, [Bear Morph](ability-tokens.md#Bear_Morph), [Inspire](ability-tokens.md#Inspire), Baby Love, [Pollen Marks](ability-tokens.md#Mark), the [Honeyday Event buff](buffs-debuffs.md#From_Other), from Clouds, Gifted Basic Bee, Gifted Bear Bee, the [Periwinkle Jellybean](jelly-beans.md) or with certain items.

### White Pollen

Boosts your white pollen collection. It can be increased with amulets, [Tropical Drinks](tropical-drink.md), [Marshmallow Bees](marshmallow-bee.md), [White Boost](ability-tokens.md#Boost), the [Spider Badge](badges.md#Spider_Badge), the [Coconut Badge](badges.md#Coconut_Badge), Gifted [Shocked Bee](shocked-bee.md), the [White Jellybean](jelly-beans.md), Wind Boosts, or with certain items.

### Red Pollen

Boosts your red pollen collection. It can be increased with amulets, [Red Extracts](red-extract.md), [Red Boost](ability-tokens.md#Boost), [Flame Heat](buffs-debuffs.md#From_Passives), the [Strawberry Badge](badges.md#Strawberry_Badge), Gifted Rad Bee or with certain items.

### Blue Pollen

Boosts your blue pollen collection. It can be increased with Amulets, [Blue Extracts](blue-extract.md), [Blue Boost](ability-tokens.md#Boost), with the [Bamboo Badge](badges.md#Bamboo_Badge), Gifted Cool Bee or with certain items.

### Bomb Pollen

Boosts your pollen collection from all bombs.  
Example: If the player collects 5,000 pollen with a Buzz Bomb token, with 200% Bomb Pollen they will collect 10,000 pollen from the same flowers.  
It can be increased with Amulets, [Bomb Combos](buffs-debuffs.md#From_Ability_Tokens), Gifted Bomber Bee, Festive Mark, Black Jelly Bean or with certain items.

### Buzz Bomb Pollen

Boosts your pollen collection from Buzz Bombs and [Fuzz Bombs](ability-tokens.md#Fuzz_Bombs). It can be increased with amulets, Gifted Demo Bee, or with certain items.

### Red Bomb Pollen

Boosts your pollen collection from Red Bombs. It can be increased with Amulets, Gifted Rascal Bee, Festive Mark or with certain items.

### Blue Bomb Pollen

Boosts your pollen collection from Blue Bombs. It can be increased with Amulets, Gifted Frosty Bee, or with certain items.

### [Bubble Pollen](bubble.md#Bubble_Pollen)

Boosts your pollen collection from [bubbles](bubble.md).  
Example: If the player collects 5,000 pollen with a bubble, with 200% Pollen from Bubbles they will collect 10,000 pollen from the same flowers.  
It can be increased with Gifted [Bubble Bee](bubble-bee.md), [Cog Amulet](cog-amulet.md), [Bubble Mask](bubble-mask.md) or the [Diamond Mask](diamond-mask.md).

### [Flame Pollen](flame.md#Flame_Pollen)

Boosts your pollen collection from [flames](flame.md) created by bees, [ability tokens](ability-tokens.md), and [passive abilities](passive-abilities.md).  
Example: If the flames collect 1,000 over time, with 200% Pollen from Flames they will collect 2,000 pollen from the same flowers.  
It can be increased with Gifted [Fire Bee](fire-bee.md), [Cog Amulet](cog-amulet.md), or the [Demon Mask](demon-mask.md).

### Bee Gather Pollen

Boosts pollen collection for all bees.  
Example: With 200% Bee Gather Pollen, Basic Bee will collect 20 pollen in 4 seconds.  
It can be increased with amulets, [Glues](glue.md), Gifted [Music Bee](music-bee.md), the [x2 Pollen from Bees Gamepass](robux-shop.md) or with certain items.

### Bee Ability Pollen

Boosts pollen collection from all bees' abilities and passives.  
It can be increased with Gifted [Shy Bee](shy-bee.md) Hive Bonus, certain Beequips, or [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md).

### Tool Pollen

Boosts your pollen collection from your tool.  
Example: With 100% (base) Tool Pollen, the player will collect 1,000 pollen with their tool at once. With 125% Tool Pollen player will collect 1,250 pollen with their tool at once at the same place.  
It can be increased with amulets, [Glues](glue.md), Gifted [Carpenter Bee](carpenter-bee.md), with the [Stump Badge](badges.md#Stump_Badge) or with certain items.

### [Instant Conversion](instant-conversion.md)

Instantly converts your pollen when you are collecting pollen on a field.  
Example: a Basic Bee with 50% Instant Conversion that collects 10 pollen will make half of it instantly convert into [Honey](honey.md). You will have 5 [Honey](honey.md) and 5 pollen, so you can collect more pollen.  
It can be boosted with amulets, [Enzymes](enzymes.md), the [Dandelion Badge](badges.md#Dandelion_Badge), the [Cactus Badge](badges.md#Cactus_Badge), Gifted [Photon Bee](photon-bee.md), [Festive Blessing](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Blessing) buff, or with certain items.

### Instant White Conversion

Instantly converts white pollen you collect, in addition to Instant Conversion. It can be boosted with amulets, the [Pineapple Badge](badges.md#Pineapple_Badge), Gifted [Windy Bee](windy-bee.md), or with the [Coconut Canister](coconut-canister.md).

### Instant Red Conversion

Instantly converts red pollen you collect, in addition to Instant Conversion. It can be boosted with amulets, the [Mushroom Badge](badges.md#Mushroom_Badge), Gifted [Crimson Bee](crimson-bee.md), or with certain items.

### Instant Blue Conversion

Instantly converts blue pollen you collect, in addition to Instant Conversion. It can be boosted with amulets, the [Blue Flower Badge](badges.md#Blue_Flower_Badge), Gifted [Cobalt Bee](cobalt-bee.md), [Diamond Mask](diamond-mask.md), or with certain items.

### Instant Bomb Conversion

Instantly converts pollen collected by the player's bomb. It can be temporarily boosted by standing in a [festive mark](ability-tokens.md#Festive_Mark), or can be permanently increased by owning a Gifted [Demon Bee](demon-bee.md).

### [Instant Flame Conversion](flame.md#Instant_Flame_Conversion)

Instantly converts pollen collected by the player's summoned flames. It can be boosted by having the Demon Mask equipped, or you can temporarily get 50% Instant Flame Conversion by triggering the [Scorching Star Passive](passive-abilities.md#Scorching_Star).

### [Instant Bubble Conversion](bubble.md#Instant_Bubble_Conversion)

Instantly converts pollen collected by the player's popped bubbles. There are currently no ways to obtain it.

### [Honey Per Pollen](honey-per-pollen.md)

Allows you to make more [Honey](honey.md) from the same amount of pollen, which works on conversion, [Instant Converters](instant-converter.md), [Micro-Converters](micro-converter.md), and Instant Conversion.  
Example: With 200% [Honey](honey.md) Per Pollen, a player will make 2,000 [Honey](honey.md) from 1,000 Pollen.  
It can be increased with the [Moon Amulet](moon-amulet.md), the [Wealth Clock](wealth-clock.md), the [Goo Badge](badges.md#Goo_Badge), Gifted [Gummy Bee](gummy-bee.md) or Nectar.

### Honey At Hive

Increases the [Honey](honey.md) you convert when converting at hive. You can boost this stat with Satisfying Nectar and Balloon Blessing. [Diamond Bee](diamond-bee.md)'s Shimmering [Honey](honey.md) directly multiplies this stat.

### Movespeed

Movespeed of your character in studs. It can be increased with amulets, [Oil](oil.md), [Super Smoothie](super-smoothie.md), Haste or Haste+, Bear Morph, [Coconut Haste](passive-abilities.md#Coconut_Haste), Gifted [Hasty Bee](hasty-bee.md), or with boots.

### Movement Collection

Allows the player to collect [pollen](pollen.md) when walking on [flowers](flowers.md). The player can get this boost from wearing boots or from a [Moon Amulet](moon-amulet.md). The higher the movement collection, the greater amount of pollen the player collects from walking on flowers. This boost also collects [goo](goo.md) if the player walks on any goo patches.

### Jump Power

Jump power of your character in studs. It can be increased with Bear Morph, all boots (except for the [Basic Boots](basic-boots.md)), or the [Propeller Hat](propeller-hat.md). The minimum jump power is 55 and the maximum jump power is 97.

### Defense

Reduces the damage that you take.  
Example: If a mob deals 50 damage, with 50% Defense it will deal 25 damage instead.  
It can be increased with the [Shell Amulet](shell-amulet.md), hats, or with guards. Having 100% defense means that you take no damage and can be achieved by activating the [emergency coconut shield](passive-abilities.md#Emergency_Coconut_Shield) passive. The maximum defense without the emergency coconut shield is 60%. This is achieved by having the [Demon Mask](demon-mask.md) or [Diamond Mask](diamond-mask.md) (+35%), both the [Crimson](crimson-guard.md) and [Cobalt Guards](cobalt-guard.md) (+10% each), and +5% on a Shell Amulet. Defense was originally Damage Reduction.

### Bond From Treats

Gives additional [bond](bond.md) for your bees when you give them [treats](treats.md).  
Example: If you feed 100 normal treats (1,000 total bond) to your bee, with 125% Bond From Treats it will grant 1,250 bond instead.  
It can be increased with the [Moon Amulet](moon-amulet.md), Gifted [Puppy Bee](puppy-bee.md) or as a Hive Bonus from the [Reindeer Antlers](reindeer-antlers.md) beequip.

### Bond From Battle

Gives additional bond for your bees when you are defeating a mob.  
Example: Normally, [scorpions](scorpion.md) give 15 bond for each of your bees when you defeat it. With 150% Bond From Battle, your bees will receive 22.5 Bond instead.  
It can be increased with amulets.

### Honey From Tokens

Boosts [Honey](honey.md) that you receive from all [Honey](honey.md) giving tokens (Honey Gift, Honey Gift+, [Impale](ability-tokens.md#Impale), mob loot, [Coin Scatter](passive-abilities.md#Coin_Scatter), [Honeystorm](honeystorm.md), and others).  
Example: A level 2 [Diamond Bee](diamond-bee.md) will give 2,000 [Honey](honey.md) with its Honey Gift+ token. With 200% [Honey](honey.md) From Tokens, it will give 4,000 [Honey](honey.md) instead.  
It can be increased with amulets, Gifted [Honey Bee](honey-bee.md), or with certain items.

### [Bee Attack](bee-attack.md#Regular_Bee_Attack)

Makes all your bees, regardless of their colors, deal more damage per hit. It will add one bee attack (Damage) per bee attack stat that you have.   
Example: With +4 Bee Attack, Basic Bee will attack with the power of 5 Basic Bees (Base Attack + Bee Attack multiplier).  
It can be increased with amulets, with Rage, or Gifted [Brave Bee](brave-bee.md), or with certain items buffs and beequips.

### [Red Attack](bee-attack.md#Red_Bee_Attack)

Same as Bee Attack, but applies only for Red bees. It can be boosted with amulets or certain items.

### [Blue Attack](bee-attack.md#Blue_Bee_Attack)

Same as Bee Attack, but applies only for Blue bees. It can be boosted with amulets or certain items.

### [Colorless Attack](bee-attack.md#Colorless_Bee_Attack)

Same as Bee Attack, but applies only for Colorless bees. It can be boosted with amulets, or certain items.

### [Attack Multiplier](bee-attack.md#Bee_Attack_Multiplier)

Multiplies attack of all bees, but with a percentage. It can be increased with Amulets, [Stingers](stinger.md), Flame Heat, [Emergency Coconut Shield](passive-abilities.md#Emergency_Coconut_Field) , Gifted Rage Bee or the [Demon Mask](demon-mask.md).

### [Goo](goo.md)

Multiplies all collected goo. It can be increased with the Shell Amulet, [Gummy Morph](passive-abilities.md#Gummy_Morph), [Gummy Mask](gummy-mask.md), [Gummy Boots](gummy-boots.md), [Gummy Star](passive-abilities.md#Gummy_Star), or [Gummyballer](gummyballer.md).

### Goo Conversion

Instantly converts pollen equal to the goo gathered, which can convert all the pollen gathered (effectively serving as 100% Instant Conversion) with a high enough Goo Conversion % and Goo multiplyer. It can be increased by amulets, Gummy Mask, Gummy Morph, or Gummy Star.

### [Capacity Multiplier](capacity.md)

Gives additional pollen storage by the amount of the percentages.  
Example: If your backpack can hold up to 200,000 pollen, with 200% Capacity Multiplier, your backpack can hold up to 400,000 instead.  
It can be increased with amulets, [Sunflower Badge](badges.md#Sunflower_Badge), [Quest Badge](badges.md#Quest_Badge), [Bumble Bee](bumble-bee.md)'s Gifted Hive Bonus, [Buoyant Bee](buoyant-bee.md)'s Gifted Hive Bonus and with certain items. If your capacity multiplier is a decimal percentage, it doesn't show the decimal and rounds the number, but the rounding doesn't affect your capacity.

### [White Field Capacity](field-capacity.md)

Gives additional pollen storage by the number of percentages based on the percentage of white flowers in a field.

Example: If you have 15,000,000 Capacity (With Capacity Multiplier) and your White Field Capacity is 180%, your total capacity if in the Spider Field is 27,000,000. It can be increased with things like the [Gummy Mask](gummy-mask.md), Exhausted Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus, [Polar Bear](polar-bear.md)'s Ornament and other inventory items. If your capacity multiplier is a decimal percentage, it doesn't show the decimal and rounds the number, but the rounding doesn't affect your capacity.

### [Red Field Capacity](field-capacity.md)

Gives additional pollen storage by the number of percentages based on the percentage of red flowers in a field.

Example: If you have 15,000,000 Capacity (With Capacity Multiplier) and your Red Field Capacity is 150%, your total capacity if on a field with all flowers being red is 22,500,000. It can be increased with things like [Demon Mask](demon-mask.md), Riley Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus, [Gifted Riley Bee (NPC)](gifted-riley-bee.md) Ornament, the Rose Badge and other inventory items. If your capacity multiplier is a decimal percentage, it doesn't show the decimal and rounds the number, but the rounding doesn't affect your capacity.

### [Blue Field Capacity](field-capacity.md)

Gives additional pollen storage by the amount of percentage based on the percentage of blue flowers in a field.

Example: If you have 15,000,000 Capacity (With Capacity Multiplier) and your Blue Field Capacity is 200%, your total capacity if on a field with only blue flowers is 30,000,000. It can be increased with buffs like Bucko Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus, [Gifted Bucko Bee (NPC)](gifted-bucko-bee.md) Ornament, the Pine Tree Badge, and other inventory items. If your capacity multiplier is a decimal percentage, it doesn't show the decimal and rounds the number, but the rounding doesn't affect your capacity.

### Convert Total

The total convert amounts of all of your bees combined (after conversion modifiers).

### Gather Total

The total gather amounts of all of your bees combined (before pollen modifiers).

### Ticket Chance

A stat boost which increases chances of obtaining [Tickets](ticket.md), can be obtained through the [Moon Amulet](moon-amulet.md), Festive Wreath, stacking the [Ticket Vouchers](sticker.md#Sticker_Index) and the now unobtainable Ticket Chance gamepass.

### Attack Total

The total attack of all of your bees combined (after attack modifiers).

## Auto-Jelly Settings

<figure class="thumb mw-halign-right" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The Auto-Jelly settings.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The message when an auto-jelly setting is on.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

The **Auto-Jelly Settings** can be found at the bottom of the System Page. There are options for [Royal Jellies](royal-jelly.md) or [Star Jellies](royal-jelly.md#Star_Jelly) to auto roll until the requirements are fulfilled. It can be used to help players obtain a bee they desire quickly. There are options for the requirement the jelly will roll into:

* Until Legendary: Jellies will auto roll until you get a [Legendary](bees-legendary.md) or [Mythic](bees-mythic.md) Bee.
* Until Mythic: Jellies will auto roll until you get a Mythic Bee.
* Require Gifted: Jellies will auto roll until you get a [Gifted](gifted-bee.md) or Mythic Bee.
* Require Mutation: Jellies will auto roll until you get a [Mutated](mutation.md) or Mythic Bee.

Multiple options can be turned on at a time, whilst also making the auto jelly stop on the combined requirement.  
Example: When the Legendary and Gifted options are turned on, the jellies will auto roll until you get a Gifted Legendary Bee. When the Legendary, Gifted, and Mutation options are turned on, the jellies will auto roll until you get a Gifted Legendary Bee with a Mutation.

If the Until Legendary option or both the Until Legendary and Until Mythic options are turned on, it will auto roll until you get a Legendary Bee or Mythic Bee. If only the Require Gifted option is turned on, it will auto roll until you get a Gifted Bee or a Mythic Bee. If only the Require Mutation option is turned on, it will auto roll until you get a Gifted Bee or a Mutated Bee. If both the Require Gifted and Require Mutation options are turned on, it will auto roll until you get a Gifted Mutated Bee or Mythic Bee.

If the Mutation option is turned on, but the bee you selected isn't radioactive, a pop-up after using a [Royal Jelly](royal-jelly.md) will say that you can't auto-roll [Royal Jellies](royal-jelly.md) for mutation. Unless the bee is radioactive, you will have to use the [Royal Jellies](royal-jelly.md) one at a time.

When using a [Royal Jelly](royal-jelly.md) on a bee with an auto-jelly option enabled, a reminder appears after the message that the auto-jelly option is on.

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The extra warning pop-up when selecting extremely rare options.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

If you turn on the following options, an extra pop-up will warn the player that the options they selected are very rare:

* Mythic and Gifted.
* Mythic and Mutation.
* Mythic, Gifted, and Mutation.
* Legendary, Gifted, and Mutation.

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">An extra warning pop-up that appears when you try to auto-jelly with star jellies.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

When using a [Star Jelly](royal-jelly.md#Star_Jelly) on a bee, another pop-up will appear, reminding the player that an auto-jelly setting is on and will use as many star jellies as it needs in order to fulfill its requirement. This pop-up will appear even if only the Until Gifted option is turned on. If you are using the Require Gifted option with [Star Jellies](royal-jelly.md#Star_Jelly), you will still receive the warning, but nothing will happen.
