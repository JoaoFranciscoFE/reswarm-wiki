# Ability Tokens

This piece of content contains information obtained through datamining.

Due to the nature of the information, details may be inaccurate or outdated.

Datamined information:

* How many tokens, majorly Impale, Rain Cloud and Tornado, works internally
* The use cooldown, trial cooldown and trial success rate of every ability

Some of the information were taken from the [Ability Token Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1J_-839-bUuO9hUhTiBzkCQmBhMWAaBb9pqei3RUjTMg/edit?gid{{{=}}}0#gid{{{=}}}0). — December 19th, 2024

This content contains assumptions.

Though the precise details may be inaccurate, most information comes from great testing and supported assumptions.

Assumptions made: Much of the technical information provided is based on Onett's Discord messages, most of which are dated back to 2018. The assumption is that the information is still accurate to this day.

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 300px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">An array of ability tokens.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

**Ability tokens** are tokens generated by [bees](bees.md), which can be collected to activate special abilities and buffs. Almost all bees spawn ability tokens, with the exceptions being ungifted [Basic Bee](basic-bee.md) and [Brave Bee](brave-bee.md) without [Beequips](beequip.md). If the player doesn't collect a token within a certain amount of time, it fades away. Players can't collect the tokens generated by other players' bees and vice versa, as the tokens are client sided. Prior to the [May 7th, 2018 update](updates.md#2018-05-07), other players' tokens were visible, but still couldn't be collected by others.

Some tokens aren't generated by the player's bees (such as honey tokens from flowers). The player can tell apart the two types of tokens by the fact that tokens dropped by bees give off gold sparkles, while tokens generated through other means do not sparkle. Also, tokens dropped by someone else's bees are not visible to the player, which means every visible token is collectible ([though there is a bug where some loot tokens cannot be collected, usually at the end of a Honeystorm or Snowstorm](glitches.md)).

## Details

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">A <a href="looker-bee.html">Looker Bee</a> generating a focus token.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

### Spawning categories

Ability tokens fall into 3 categories, based on when a bee can spawn that token:

* **Gather:** Ability tokens that can be generated when the bees are collecting [pollen](pollen.md). Most ability tokens that can be generated under this situation can collect pollen and/or boost pollen collection.
* **Battle:** Ability tokens that can be generated when the bees are targeting/attacking [mobs](mobs.md). Most ability tokens that can be generated under this situation can inflict damage or [debuffs](buffs-debuffs.md) to enemies and boost bees' combat process.
* **Idle:** Ability tokens that can be generated when the bees are not doing any other activities (gathering, attacking, converting,...)

Some ability tokens fall into multiple or all of the categories above.

### Spawning attempts

When a bee pauses after making movement (either to gather a flower, to attack, or to simply turn), it will attempt to spawn its ability tokens.

Each ability token has 3 variables that dictate how often the ability token can spawn:

* **Use cooldown:** The amount of time it takes after the ability token has been spawned for the bee to attempt to summon that ability token again.
* **Attempt cooldown:** The amount of time a bee has to wait in-between attempts before it can attempt to spawn the ability token again.
* **Attempt success rate:** The probability that the attempt succeeds. If the attempt succeeds, the ability token is added to a pool that the bee uses to spawn its ability tokens.

When a bee attempts to spawn its ability tokens, it goes through every token ability it can spawn and check if its use cooldown and attempt cooldown has expired. If it has, the bee attempts to spawn the token. If this attempt succeeds, the token is added to a pool. Once every ability has been checked, the bee looks in its pool and picks a random token to spawn, removing that token while keeping every other token in the pool for the next spawn attempt. The spawned token then starts its use cooldown again.

## Stats

These are stats which affect the characteristics of ability tokens.

### [Bee Ability Rate](system-page.md#Bee_Ability_Rate)

Bee Ability Rate increases or decreases how often bees spawn their ability tokens, by dividing the use cooldown and attempt cooldown, as well as multiplying the attempt success rate. Note that because of this, Bee Ability Rate increases or decreases ability tokens' spawn rate by much more than its stated amount.

For example: a 200% bee ability rate will reduce an ability's use and attempt cooldown to 50% of its original value (100% ÷ 200%), and increase its success rate by 100% (or double it).

### Ability Token Lifespan

Ability Token Lifespan extends the duration of uncollected ability tokens generated by bees before they despawn. It can be boosted by a [Gifted](gifted-bee.md) [Stubborn Bee](stubborn-bee.md) and [Cog Amulet](cog-amulet.md) and by the [Paper Angel](paper-angel.md) and [Paperclip](paperclip.md) beequips, and also temporarily by adding the [Drooping Stubborn Bee Sticker](sticker.md) to the [Sticker Stack](sticker-stack.md). The maximum amount currently possible is 145%.

### Ability Duplication Chance

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">An ability token being duplicated and that token being collected</p> </figcaption> </figure>

When there is a chance, ability tokens will spawn its duplicated/duped variants randomly on a field. Duped tokens hover over the field and last for double the tokens' lifespan, and that lifespan is further increased by 5% per [Digital Bee's](digital-bee.md) level. Duped tokens can be collected/activated by standing beneath it for 1 second.

Ability duplication chance can be increased by [field corruption](buffs-debuffs.md) from Digital Bee's Glitch and Map Corruption ability tokens. It also can be increased from [Gifted](gifted-bee.md) Digital Bee. Other than from Digital Bees, ability duplication chance can also be increased by the 'Corrupt' upgrade purchasable from the [Robo Bear Challenge](robo-bear-challenge.md) and only applies during that challenge. It can also be provided by [Science Bear](science-bear.md) during his "Corrupting The Glitched Drive" quest, where he corrupts the [Clover](clover-field.md), [Spider](spider.md), [Pineapple](pineapple-patch.md), [Rose](rose-field.md), [Pine Tree](pine-tree-forest.md) and [Mountain Top](mountain-top-field.md) fields at random.

### [Bee Movespeed](stats.md#Speed)

Bee Movespeed affects how fast bees move and perform actions.

While not directly related to ability tokens, having more Bee Movespeed allows bees to make more attempts at creating Ability Tokens, and vice versa. This overall increases or decreases the average spawn rate of an ability token.

## Bee Ability Tokens

### Bomb

<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left" style="width: 203px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The <b>Red Bomb</b> (left), <b>Blue Bomb</b> (middle), and <b>Buzz Bomb </b>(right) tokens.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

**Bomb** collects pollen from flowers in an area.

Split by the types of flowers the ability collects, there are 3 categories:

* **Buzz Bombs** collect 7 pollen from all types of flowers.
* **Red Bombs** collect 10 pollen from only Red flowers.
* **Blue Bombs** collect 10 pollen from only Blue flowers.

The amount of pollen collected increases by 10% per level (e.g., 200% on a level 20 bee).

Split by the range of the ability, there are 2 categories:

* **Bomb** tokens collect from 13 surrounding flowers.
* **Bomb+** tokens collect from 29 surrounding flowers.

Collecting Bomb tokens also grants the [Bomb Combo](buffs-debuffs.md#Bomb_Combo) buff, which grants a buff to Bomb Power.

Many bees have at least one of the bomb variants:

* Red Bomb is granted by [Rascal](rascal-bee.md), [Demon](demon-bee.md), and [Shy](shy-bee.md) Bees.
* Red Bomb+ is granted by [Fire](fire-bee.md), [Demon](demon-bee.md), and [Festive Bees](festive-bee.md).
* Blue Bomb is granted by [Bumble](bumble-bee.md), [Bubble](bubble-bee.md), and [Buoyant](buoyant-bee.md) Bees.
* Blue Bomb+ is granted by [Frosty](frosty-bee.md), [Ninja](ninja-bee.md), and [Vicious](vicious-bee.md) Bees.
* Buzz Bomb is granted by [Bomber](bomber-bee.md), [Commander](commander-bee.md), and [Exhausted](exhausted-bee.md) Bees.
* Buzz Bomb+ is granted by [Demo](demo-bee.md), [Lion](lion-bee.md), and [Fuzzy](fuzzy-bee.md) Bees.

There are also other sources of Bomb tokens:

* Balloons during a Surprise Party have a chance to generate Blue and a Buzz Bomb tokens.
* Many Beequips can give the bee that equipped it Bomb abilities, if the beequip has them.
  * [Bang Snaps](bang-snap.md) can give Bomb and Bomb+ of any color.
  * [Smiley Stickers](smiley-sticker.md) can give Blue Bomb.
  * [Bottle Caps](bottle-cap.md) can give Buzz Bomb.

If Red Bomb Sync is on, all Red Bombs collect white pollen. If Blue Bomb Sync is on, all Blue Bombs collect white pollen. If both Syncs are active, all Bombs collect any color of pollen.

When Pollen Haze is active, all Bomb ability tokens are automatically collected, and they may also [pollinate](pollination.md) flower patches that they collect from.

Bomb falls into the Gather category, meaning it spawns while gathering from flowers.

Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Bomb has a use cooldown of 8 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 4 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 25%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 20 seconds.

The token lasts for 4 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.

### Boost

<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left" style="width: 185px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The <b>Red Boost</b> (left) and <b>Blue Boost</b> (right) tokens.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 90px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The <b>White Boost</b> token.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

**Boost** grants the player the [Boost](buffs-debuffs.md#Boost) buff of the respective color, which gives buffs to the matching-color pollen. When collected, red/blue (depending on the type) and white sparkles appear on the player's bees and their character.

Many bees have at least one of the Boost variants:

* Red Boost is generated by [Rad](rad-bee.md), [Riley](riley-bee.md), and [Shy](shy-bee.md) Bees.
* Blue Boost is generated by [Frosty](frosty-bee.md), [Cool](cool-bee.md), [Bucko](bucko-bee.md), [Diamond](diamond-bee.md), and [Tadpole](tadpole-bee.md) Bees.
* White Boost is generated by only [Windy Bee](windy-bee.md).

There are also other sources of Boost tokens:

* Balloons during a Surprise Party have a chance to generate White and Blue Boost tokens.
* Unactivated targets from Target Practice spawn Red Boost tokens.
* Festive Gifts and Festive Mark commonly spawn Red Boost tokens as one of its rewards.
* Many Beequips can give the bee that equipped it Boost abilities, if the beequip has them.
  * [Berets](beret.md) can give Blue Boost.
  * [Leis](lei.md) can give Red or Blue Boost.

Sometimes flowers generate Boost tokens instead of Honey tokens. These tokens look almost identical to Boost tokens generated by bees, except the latter have gold sparkles while the former does not. Boost tokens from flowers take more time to fade away. These Boost tokens generated by flowers do not count for Ability Token quests and badges. Token Links collect Boost tokens generated by the player's bees, but not tokens spawned by the field.

The buff granted by the Boost token can be found in other sources. For more information, [see the relevant article](buffs-debuffs.md).

Boost falls into the Gather category, meaning it spawns while gathering from flowers.

Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Boost has a use cooldown of 4.5 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 4.5 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 33.33%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 13.5 seconds.

The token lasts for 4 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.

### Honey Gift

<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left" style="width: 90px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The <b>Honey Gift</b> token.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

There are 2 types of Honey Gift tokens in the game, visually identical to each other. They serve the same purpose of giving the player honey.

* **Honey Gift** tokens are worth 100 [honey](honey.md) multiplied by bee [level](bond.md) squared.
* **Honey Gift+** tokens are worth 250 honey multiplied by bee level squared.

These amounts can be increased by having a higher [Honey from Tokens](system-page.md#Honey_From_Tokens) percentage.

[Honey Bee](honey-bee.md) is the only bee to have the Honey Gift ability, and [Diamond Bee](diamond-bee.md) is the only bee to have the Honey Gift+ ability. There are no other sources of Honey Gift nor Honey Gift+ in the game.

Honey Gift falls into the Gather, Battle, and Idle categories, meaning it spawns while gathering from flowers, while attacking mobs, and while idling.

Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Honey Gift has a use cooldown of 120 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 60 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 25%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 300 seconds.

The token lasts for 10 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.

### Token Link

<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left" style="width: 90px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The <b>Token Link</b> token.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

A **Token Link** collects all other ability tokens (except for other Token Links) and grants 25 honey and an additional 10 honey per level for each other token collected this way (e.g. 175 honey per token collected on level 15 bee).

For stacking purposes, note that the ability tokens are collected based on how early they were spawned. If two or more link tokens are collected at the same time, they may link the same tokens. The other tokens activate once, but the player gets two sets of honey from link tokens.

A Token Link can also collect Honey Gift tokens generated by [Honey Bee](honey-bee.md), [Diamond Bee](diamond-bee.md), honey tokens from [Vicious Bee's](vicious-bee.md) Impale, [treat](treat.md) tokens from [Puppy Bee's](puppy-bee.md) Fetch ability, tokens from [Festive Bee's](festive-bee.md) Festive Gift ability, tokens from Cub Buddy gifts, and tokens from mobs. However, it does not collect honey tokens generated by [Coin Scatter](passive-abilities.md#Coin_Scatter), [Gummy Star](passive-abilities.md) or Target Practice.

[Exhausted](exhausted-bee.md), [Rage](rage-bee.md), [Shocked](shocked-bee.md), and [Music](music-bee.md) bees have the ability. Other sources of Token Link tokens include:

* Balloons during a Surprise Party have a chance to generate Token Link tokens.
* [Paper Angels](paper-angel.md), [Paperclips](paperclip.md) and [Bead Lizards](bead-lizard.md) can give the bee that equipped it the ability, if the beequip has it.

Token Link falls into the Gather and Battle categories, meaning it spawns while gathering from flowers and while attacking mobs.

Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Token Link has a use cooldown of 5 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 5 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 33.33%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 15 seconds.

The token lasts for 4 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.

### Mark

<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left" style="width: 185px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The <b>Pollen Mark</b> (left) and <b>Honey Mark</b> (right) tokens.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 200px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">A Pollen Mark on a field.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

A **Mark** token deploys a Mark in a random area on a field that grants a specific buff when the player stands in its range. When a mark is activated, its radius and the green light on top of the mark lights up, and the player is given a buff based on what mark they activated.

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 200px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">Two Pollen Marks stacked above each other.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

Each Mark can be stacked to increase its effect, done by linking with each other (i.e. overlapping each other). If the player stands in a Mark that is linked to another one, all linked Marks are activated.

There are currently 4 types of marks in the game:

* **Pollen Mark** gives the [Pollen Mark](buffs-debuffs.md#Pollen_Mark) buff, which increases all pollen.
* **Honey Mark** gives the [Honey Mark](buffs-debuffs.md#Honey_Mark) buff, which grants conversion links and increases Convert Rate.
  * When the player walks off the Honey Mark's range, conversion links remain, and bees still convert honey normally until they finish converting. The player will lose the Convert Rate buff, however.
* **Precise Mark** gives the [Precise Mark](buffs-debuffs.md#Precise_Mark) buff, which increases Critical Chance and Super-Crit Chance. See the dedicated section for more details.
* **Festive Mark** gives the [Festive Mark](buffs-debuffs.md#Festive_Mark), which grants several buffs. See the dedicated section for more details.

The rest of this section will only concern Pollen Marks and Honey Marks.

The two Mark abilities are split into the Mark and Mark+ variant.

* The Mark variant spawns Marks that lasts for 7 seconds (+0.2s per bee level) with a range of 14 studs.
* The Mark+ variant spawns Marks that lasts for 8 seconds (+0.25s per bee level) with a range of 18 studs.

Many bees have at least one of the Mark variants:

* Pollen Mark is granted by [Stubborn](stubborn-bee.md) and [Carpenter](carpenter-bee.md) Bees.
* Pollen Mark+ is granted by [Vector Bee](vector-bee.md).
* Honey Mark is granted by [Honey](honey-bee.md) and [Festive](festive-bee.md) Bees.
* Honey Mark+ is granted by [Carpenter Bee](carpenter-bee.md).

There are also other sources of Mark tokens:

* Balloons during a Surprise Party have a chance to generate Pollen Mark and Honey Mark tokens.
* [Smiley Stickers](smiley-sticker.md) can give the bee that equipped it the Honey Mark ability, if the beequip has it.

Mark falls into the Gather category, meaning it spawns while gathering from flowers.

Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Mark has a use cooldown of 20 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 5 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 25%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 35 seconds.

The token lasts for 8 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.

The player can see other people's Marks, but they can't see those Marks' range, the Marks don't grant buffs to them, they can't be linked with their Marks, and vice versa (with the exception of Festive Marks).

## Mythic Bee-Exclusive Ability Tokens

### Inflate Balloons

<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left" style="width: 90px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The <b>Inflate Token</b> token.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

**Inflate Balloons** is an ability token exclusive to [Buoyant Bee](buoyant-bee.md).

When collected, Buoyant Bee increases the pollen stored inside the player's [balloons](balloon.md) on the field the player is in by +5%, which increases by +0.01% per level. ### Bear Morph

<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left" style="width: 254px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The <b>Bear Morph</b> tokens (first and third row); players with a Bear Morph active (<b>Black Bear</b>, <b>Brown Bear</b>, <b>Panda Bear</b>, <b>Polar Bear</b>, <b>Science Bear</b>, and <b>Mother Bear</b>).</p> </figcaption> </figure>

**Bear Morph** is an ability exclusive to [Bear Bee](bear-bee.md).

When collected, the player is granted the [Bear Morph](buffs-debuffs.md#Bear_Morph) buff, which transforms the player into the bear on the token (either [Black Bear](black-bear.md), [Brown Bear](brown-bear.md), [Panda Bear](panda-bear.md) or [Polar Bear](polar-bear.md)). This gives the player a buff in pollen gain, and sets the player's base [movement speed](system-page.md#Movespeed) and [base jump power](system-page.md#Jump_Power) to a higher value than normal.

If Bear Bee is Gifted, it has a 20% chance to produce a morph for [Mother Bear](mother-bear.md) or [Science Bear](science-bear.md), which enhances on the base buff:

* [Mother Bear Morph](buffs-debuffs.md#Mother_Bear_Morph) grants more pollen than the base buff, as well as extra bee gather pollen.
* [Science Bear Morph](buffs-debuffs.md#Science_Bear_Morph) grants an extra conversion link.

Note that while every morph comes from the same token, they are seen by the game as different buffs, meaning the player can have multiple Bear Morphs at the same time. This has a multiplicative effect, but does not increase the player's base movement speed and base jump power further, as each morph sets the two values to a given amount instead of increasing them. It is not possible to stack the same type of morph multiple times - it will only reset the buff's uptime.

Bear Morph falls into the Gather, Battle, and Idle categories, meaning it spawns while gathering from flowers, while attacking mobs, and while idling.

Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Bear Morph has a use cooldown of 45 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 15 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 50%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 60 seconds.

The token lasts for 15 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.

Collecting the Bear Morph plays the same sound as collecting a Boost token.

If the player dies while Bear Morph is active, the effect is lost.

### Fetch

<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left" style="width: 90px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The <b>Fetch</b> token.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

**Fetch** is an ability exclusive to [Puppy Bee](puppy-bee.md).

When collected, a ball spawns in front of the player. The player must kick the ball by touching it or it despawns in 5 seconds. Then the Puppy Bee chases the ball to try and pass it back to the player, despawning in the same amount of time. The player and the Puppy Bee can keep passing the ball back to each other until it despawns.

As the ball is passed over, it can collect pollen, tokens (other than treat tokens from Fetch itself), pop fuzz bombs, and bubbles. The amount of pollen collected is increased by 100% (+15% per level, e.g. 400% pollen increased on level 20 bee).

Every 3rd kick made by Puppy Bee creates a combo that spawns a treat token at Puppy Bee's current location, which increases in value. Unlike other treat tokens, these can be collected by Token Link. As the combo continues, the ball moves faster when kicked and it collects more pollen until it reaches the max combo that gives 256 treats (9 combos). Any further combo won't spawn treat tokens. The player can get a maximum of 511 treats per fetch ability, under normal conditions.

Fetch falls into the Gather category, meaning it spawns while gathering from flowers.

Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Fetch has a use cooldown of 140 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 5 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 50%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 145 seconds.

The token lasts for 8 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.

The ball can sometimes fall outside of the field, out of range for the player or Puppy Bee to continue passing the ball to each other. It also sometimes gets stuck and allows the player to 'pass' it easily.

While the Puppy Bee plays Fetch, it does not do anything else but play Fetch. Playing Fetch can be dangerous if the player's bees are attacking enemies, as they can run into an enemy as they chase the ball.

If Puppy Bee is wearing the [Reindeer Antlers](reindeer-antlers.md) beequip, the ability is enhanced to Reindeer Fetch.

### Glitch

<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left" style="width: 90px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The <b>Glitch</b> token.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

**Glitch** is an ability exclusive to [Digital Bee](digital-bee.md).

When collected, the field the player is currently in is [corrupted](buffs-debuffs.md#Field_Corruption) for 20 seconds (+1s per level). A glitch effect will occasionally appear on the field (which can be disabled in the [System Page](system-page.md)), every ability token collected in that field has a chance to be duplicated or "Duped", and Digital Bee will be able to spawn ☺︎ tokens.

The corruption on the field can be increased to raise the ability duplication chance and the pollen collection and instant conversion of duped abilities. The corruption can be increased by enhancing Digital Bee with [Drives](drives.md). Colored Drives increase the corruption of field with matching flowers while Glitched Drives increase corruption of all fields.

Glitch falls into the Gather and Battle categories, meaning it spawns while gathering from flowers and while attacking mobs.

Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Glitch has a use cooldown of 60 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 10 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 33.33%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 80 seconds.

The token lasts for 12 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.

### Map Corruption

<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left" style="width: 90px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The <b>Map Corruption</b> token.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

**Map Corruption** is an ability exclusive to [Gifted Digital Bee](digital-bee.md).

When collected, a random field is [corrupted](buffs-debuffs.md#Field_Corruption) by a small amount for 3 minutes (+15s per level) for all players in the server.

Once the token is collected, a server-wide message announces: 
☺︎ {Username}'s Digital Bee corrupted the [Field Name] ☺︎

The amount of corruption from this can be increased by enhancing Digital Bee with [Drives](drives.md). There are also special effects based on the most recent drive used to enhance Digital Bee:

* If the most recent drive is a colored drive, Map Corruption is more likely to corrupt the field with more flowers matching the color of that drive
* If the most recent drive is Glitched Drive, the field it chooses will be completely random but gains 25% more corruption.

Map Corruption falls into the Gather category, meaning it spawns while gathering from flowers.

Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Map Corruption has a use cooldown of 360 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 15 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 12.5%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 465 seconds.

The token lasts for 24 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.

### Mind Hack

<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left" style="width: 90px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The <b>Mind Hack</b> token.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

**Mind Hack** is an ability exclusive to [Digital Bee](digital-bee.md).

When collected, the minds of up to 3 nearby [enemies](mobs.md) (+1 per 3 lvls) are "hacked", stunning them for 3 seconds (+0.1s per lvl). While hacked, the enemies take 25% more damage (+0.1% per [Glitched Drive](drives.md#Glitched_Drive) Digital Bee has been enhanced with). Hacking is only half as effective against bosses.

This token also spawns a ☺︎ token if collected inside the field.

Mind Hack falls into the Battle category, meaning it spawns while attacking mobs.

Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Mind Hack has a use cooldown of 90 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 2 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 33.33%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 94 seconds.

The token lasts for 16 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.

### ☺

<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left" style="width: 90px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The <b>☺</b> token.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

**☺** (otherwise known as Smile Tokens) is an ability exclusive to [Digital Bee](digital-bee.md).

The token can only be generated as a duped token, while inside a corrupted field. When collected, they temporarily:

* increase [field corruption](buffs-debuffs.md#Field_Corruption) and activate all duped tokens,
* hack a nearby [non-boss enemy](mobs.md) (with a similar effect as Mind Hack),
* and collect and double pollen equal to 50% of Digital Bee's gather amount (+50% pollen multiplier per duped token activated) in a "☺", "&", "\*", "~" or "?" arrangement of flowers, with 50% of the pollen being instantly converted.
  * The flowers collected by this are also stamped for 10 seconds, causing them to yield x2 pollen.

The frequency for this token to be generated increases with the number of [Glitched Drives](drives.md#Glitched_Drive) Digital Bee has been enhanced with. While exact stats for the token are unknown, some information can be inferred from [update patch notes](updates.md#2024-05-03):

* The average base delay between Smile Tokens is 22 seconds.
* At maxed Glitched Drives, this average is 14 seconds.
* The first Smile Token will always be created after half the average of the base delay.

☺ tokens take roughly 4 seconds to fade away.

<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left" style="width: 90px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The <b>Snowglobe Shake</b> token.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

**Snowglobe Shake** is an ability exclusive to bees that have the [Snowglobe](snowglobe.md) beequip equipped.

When collected, they give all players in the server the [Cool Breeze](buffs-debuffs.md#From_Ability_Tokens) for 20 seconds, granting a plethora of stats.

The same buff can also be obtained from using [Snowflakes](snowflake.md) during the Beesmas event, which add 10 seconds of Cool Breeze per use.

Snowglobe Shake falls into the Gather, Battle, and Idle categories, meaning it spawns while gathering from flowers, while attacking mobs, and while idling.

Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Snowglobe Shake has a use cooldown of 300 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 30 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 16.67%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 450 seconds.

The token lasts for 8 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.

### Festive Mark

<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left" style="width: 90px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">The <b>Festive Mark</b> token.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 180px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">A Festive Mark on a field.</p> </figcaption> </figure>

**Festive Mark** is an ability exclusive to [Festive Bees](festive-bee.md) that have the [Festive Wreath](festive-wreath.md) beequip equipped.

When collected, a Festive Mark is deployed in a random area in the field that the player is standing in for 12 seconds (+0.5s per level), with a ~22-tile radius. Standing within its range grants the [Festive Mark](buffs-debuffs.md#Festive_Mark) buff, which gives extra Honey Per Pollen, Bomb Pollen, Red Bomb Pollen, Instant Bomb Conversion, and Conversion Links.

Unlike other marks, Festive Mark also grants boosts to other players and its range can be seen by other players. It also can link with other players' marks. However, the Festive Mark buff cannot be stacked even if two festive marks overlap each other.

When Festive Mark is active, it summons random gifts within its range that can be collected by any player and flashes colors cycling through red, blue, green and yellow at the top of the pole. Rarer gifts appear more likely with bee level and certain gifts are only obtainable when Festive Bee is gifted. Every 4th token generated by the festive mark is always a Red Boost token.

### Reindeer Fetch

The [Reindeer Antlers](reindeer-antlers.md) beequip, when equipped to Puppy Bee, enhances its Fetch ability into Reindeer Fetch.

Puppy's ball glows bright red and emits white sparkles, and every combo grants the player the [Reindeer Guidance](buffs-debuffs.md#Reindeer_Guidance) buff, which grants extra capacity.

<figure class="thumb" style="width: 225px" typeof="mw:File/Thumb">  <figcaption class="thumbcaption"> <p class="caption">A Gingerbread Bear and 512 Treats combo.</p> </figcaption> </figure>
