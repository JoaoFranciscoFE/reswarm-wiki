# Mortar Bee update

![Mortar Bee update](img/Mortar_Bee.png){ .update-log-icon }

**Released:** September 18, 2026

A new Mythic bee, new bag models, the Honeycomb Blueprint and many fixes.

## What's new

* **New Mythic bee: [Mortar Bee](mortar-bee.md)**, "A lazy artillery bee who would rather shell the field than work." More info is in the in-game Index.
* **New pack: Mortar Bee Pack**, with two Mortar Bee Eggs, a Flying Mortar Bee sticker, a Happy Mortar Bee sticker and an Explosion sticker.
* New sticker: Flying Mortar Bee, a rare drop from Mortar Bee's shells.
* **New item: Honeycomb Blueprint**, a free permanent item unlocked at Rebirth 10. Open it from your inventory to rearrange your hive: tap a bee, then another slot, to swap them. Bees keep their gift, mutations, levels and Beequips when moved. Zoom in and out to see more of your hive.
* **New models for every bag** (see them in the shops).
* [Bucko Bee](bucko-bee.md) and [Riley Bee](riley-bee.md) quests now give a permanent stacking buff: +0.01x blue pollen (Bucko Stack) or +0.01x red pollen (Riley Stack) per quest completed.
* Added a skip option to the [Tutorial](tutorial.md).
* Buying a Basic Egg auto-hatches it if you have an empty hive slot.
* New settings: Festive Gifts toggle (sound and popup) and Loot Popups toggle (all loot popups).

## Changes

* Bucko and Riley quests scale much harder and go further into the endgame.
* Refreshed styles for all tool stickers, and made them about 10x easier to get.
* Shop restock every 7 days > every 2 days.
* Changes to endgame Melittology requirements.
* Rebirth quests are given automatically.
* Removed the [Ant Challenge](ant-challenge.md) from Rebirth 5.
* All [Memory Match](memory-match.md) cooldowns halved.
* Mortar Bee now deals area damage at 50% of its attack, and is more likely to shoot at other bees.
* Mortar Bee converts 5% of current pollen per bee, instead of 10k + bee conversion x (6% + 6% per boost stack).

## Fixes

* Fixed collector tools breaking apart or having gaps when your avatar is scaled down.
* Fixed floating tool parts ([Gummyballer](gummyballer.md) ball, [Tide Popper](tide-popper.md) orbs) being pulled around when another player walked into them.
* Fixed the Honey Hammer taking your head.
* Fixed some tools having collision.
* Fixed some hive issues.
* Fixed click-to-turn-in on Melittology quests.
* Fixed not being able to peel vouchers off your hive.
* Fixed Memory Match.
