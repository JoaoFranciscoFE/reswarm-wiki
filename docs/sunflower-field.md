The **Sunflower Field** is one of the five [starting](starter-zone.md) [fields](fields.md) that any player can visit upon starting the game. There are no [mobs](mobs.md) that protect this [field](fields.md).

This field can spawn [sunflower seed](sunflower-seed.md), [ticket](ticket.md), [red boost](ability-tokens.md#Boost), [blue boost](ability-tokens.md#Boost), [oil](oil.md), and [magic bean](magic-bean.md) tokens when gathering [pollen](pollen.md).

This [field](fields.md) grants Satisfying [Nectar](nectar.md) when a [planter](planter.md) is planted here.

[Bees](bees.md) that like the Sunflower Field are [Basic Bee](basic-bee.md), [Baby Bee](baby-bee.md), [Hasty Bee](hasty-bee.md), and [Exhausted Bee](exhausted-bee.md). The only bee that dislikes the Sunflower Field is [Looker Bee](looker-bee.md).

This is a 20×33 field, containing 660 [flowers](flowers.md). The [flowers](flowers.md) are white, red, and blue, in sizes of single and double.

## Events

* Randomly, the large sunflower behind the [Daily Top Honeymakers](daily-top-honeymakers.md) make have a face and spit [sparkles](sparkles.md) around the [field](fields.md).
* When starting a [Stick Bug Challenge](stick-bug-challenge.md), [Stick Bug](stick-bug.md) first spawns in this [field](fields.md) and may also jump into this [field](fields.md) when defeating him in a round if he is not on this [field](fields.md).

## Obtaining Boosts

* Using the [Field Booster](field-booster.md) may give a Sunflower [Field Boost](field-boost.md). When it does, it will give +300% Sunflower Field pollen for 15 minutes.
* Using [glitter](glitter.md) will add 100% Sunflower Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes, if the current boost isn't already maxed out.
* Using [field dice](field-dice.md), [smooth dice](smooth-dice.md), or [loaded dice](loaded-dice.md) may add +100% Sunflower Field pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out.
* Sunflower [Field Winds](field-wind.md) can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](wind-shrine.md) or [codes](codes.md). They have a base of 20% instant Sunflower Field conversion and give +5% instant Sunflower Field conversion and +25% Sunflower Field pollen for each stack. Sunflower Field Winds can stack up to a total of 15 times.
* It can be boosted by the [Shell Amulet](shell-amulet.md). If it has a Sunflower Field Boost, it can boost the field by +1% to +27% depending on the amulet rarity.
* Giving a Present to Black Bear grants 3x Sunflower Field Boost + other stuff.
* Having an active [Autumn Sunhat](autumn-sunhat.md) [Beequip](beequip.md) with a Sunflower Field Pollen Hive Bonus (+18% to +24% base, up to +44% with Waxing)
* Having an active [Lei](lei.md) [Beequip](beequip.md) with a Sunflower Field Pollen Hive Bonus (+3% to +5% base, not guaranteed, up to +25% with Waxing)

## Trivia

* This is the only [field](fields.md) in the game where the player can find [sunflower seeds](sunflower-seed.md) that spawn naturally, without the need of [sprouts](sprout.md).
* This is the first field [Black Bear](black-bear.md) assigns a [quest](quests.md) to.
* The [Sunflower badges](badges.md#Sunflower_Badge) and the [Pumpkin badges](badges.md#Pumpkin_Badge) are used 3 times for chances in [Memory Match](memory-match.md), the most for any field badges.
* This was the second-biggest field in the game, the biggest one being the [Clover Field](clover-field.md). After the [2018-07-11 update](updates.md#2018-07-11), this isn't the case anymore.
  * Similar to why the [Clover Field](clover-field.md) was shrunk, this field was decreased in size to make room for [Mother Bear](mother-bear.md) and the [Treat Shop](treat-shop.md), which can be found at the end of the field.
* This field along with the [Pineapple Patch](pineapple-patch.md) and the [Pumpkin Patch](pumpkin-patch.md) are the only fields where an object may come to life and shoot [sparkles](sparkles.md).
* The fence bordering the Sunflower Field and the [hives](hive.md) was temporarily removed after Beesmas Winter 2024. It was soon added back in [the following update](updates.md#2025-06-30).
