**Fire Bee** is a Red [Epic bee](bees-epic.md).

Fire Bee's favorite treat is [Pineapples](pineapple.md).

Fire Bee likes the [Mushroom Field](mushroom-field.md), and [Strawberry Field](strawberry-field.md). It dislikes the [Pine Tree Forest](pine-tree-forest.md).

## Stats

* Collects 10 [Pollen](pollen.md) in 4 seconds.
* Makes 80 [Honey](honey.md) in 4 seconds.
* -20% [Movespeed](stats.md#Speed), +25% [Energy](energy.md), +3 [Attack](stats.md#Attack).
* 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](gifted-bee.md): x1.25 [Flame Pollen](flame.md#Flame_Pollen).

### Abilities

* **[[Red Bomb+]](ability-tokens.md#Bomb)** Collects 10 pollen from 29 surrounding red [Flowers](flowers.md) (+10% pollen per Level). Combo with other bombs to increase power.
* **[[Passive:](passive-abilities.md#Gathering_Flames) [Gathering Flames]](flame.md#Gathering_Flames)** 35% chance (50% if Gifted) to spawn a [Flame](flame.md) when gathering. Flames last 4 seconds. Each second they deal 10 damage (+25% of the bee's [base attack](bee-attack.md)) to nearby enemies and collect [5 Red / 3 White / 2 Blue] pollen (+10% per bee level) from 9 flowers. Standing near a flame grants Flame Heat, which increases [Red Pollen](system-page.md#Red_Pollen) (between x1.2 and x2) and [Bee Attack](bee-attack.md) (between +5% to +25%).

## Trivia

* The [Fire Mask](fire-mask.md), which can be purchased in the [Red HQ](red-hq.md), has a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Fire Bee.
* Fire Bee can be summoned by [Spicy Bee](spicy-bee.md) with its [Inferno](ability-tokens.md#Inferno) ability, making it the only bee summonable by [ability tokens](ability-tokens.md).
  * This bee can also be summoned by using the [Honeyday Candles](honeyday-candles.md).
* Fire Bee is the red counterpart of [Bubble Bee](bubble-bee.md).
* This bee was one of the bees to have a specific jelly in the [Bee Bear's Catalog](bee-bear-s-catalog.md). The jelly was given as part of the Cozy Bundle.
