**Brave Bee** is a Colorless [Rare Bee](bees-rare.md).

Brave Bee's favorite treat is [Pineapples](pineapple.md).

Brave Bee likes the [Clover Field](clover-field.md) and the [Spider Field](spider-field.md). It dislikes the [Dandelion Field](dandelion-field.md).

## Stats

* Collects 10 [pollen](pollen.md) in 4 seconds.
* Makes 200 [honey](honey.md) in 4 seconds.
* +20% [movespeed](stats.md#Speed), +50% [energy](energy.md), +120 [convert amount](system-page.md#Convert_Amount), +5 [attack](stats.md#Attack).
* 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](gifted-bee.md): +1 [Bee Attack](system-page.md#Bee_Attack).

### Abilities

* None.

## Trivia

* Brave Bee and [Basic Bee](basic-bee.md) are the only [bees](bees.md) in the game that don't produce any [ability tokens](ability-tokens.md) unless they are in their gifted form or have a [beequip](beequip.md) that grants an ability token.
* The [Brave Bee Gate](brave-bee-gate.md) has a large model of a Brave Bee on it.
* Brave Bee is the strongest Rare bee in terms of attack, with a base attack power of 6.
* The [Brave Guard](brave-guard.md), which is purchased in the [Pro Shop](pro-shop.md), has a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Brave Bee.
* Brave Bee's body color scheme is a darker version of the [Vector Bee's](vector-bee.md) colour scheme.
* Brave Bee is one of the 16 bees to have a sticker based on it.
* During [Bee Bear's](bee-bear.md) "Ability Token Tidings" quest [Bee Bear](bee-bear.md) refers to Brave Bee and Basic Bee as "Poor little merely mortals bees".
  * Bee Bear also says they aren't magic because they can't create ability tokens.
* Although it is non-functional, Brave Bee is the only bee in the game to have a coded disliked treat, being blueberries.
