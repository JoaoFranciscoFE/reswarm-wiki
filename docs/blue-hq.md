# Blue HQ

<aside class="portable-infobox pi-background pi-border-color pi-theme-wikia pi-layout-default" role="region">
<h2 class="pi-item pi-item-spacing pi-title pi-secondary-background" data-source="title1">Blue HQ</h2>
<div class="pi-item pi-data pi-item-spacing pi-border-color" data-source="row1">
<h3 class="pi-data-label pi-secondary-font">Location</h3>
<div class="pi-data-value pi-font">Behind the Blue Flower Field</div>

</aside>

The **Blue HQ** is a two-story base with many features. The entrance can be found at the far end of the [Blue Flower Field](blue-flower-field.md), located in the [Starter Zone](starter-zone.md). It has a [Bucko Bee](bucko-bee.md) model above the entrance. To enter, the player must have discovered 4 different blue bee types. The Blue HQ is the blue counterpart to the [Red HQ](red-hq.md).

On the roof, there is an enlarged [Bubble Wand](bubble-wand.md). This wand occasionally grows a face and blows [sparkles](sparkles.md) to nearby fields. This includes the [Clover Field](clover-field.md), [Mushroom Field](mushroom-field.md), Blue Flower Field, and the [Dandelion Field](dandelion-field.md).

## Interior

On the first floor, there are four different objects:

* A [Blue Teleporter](blue-teleporter.md) that will teleport the player near the [Ticket Tent](ticket-tent.md) and [Red Cannon](red-cannon.md),
* A set of blue [items](items.md) for sale, (See the 'Store' section below.)
* The [Blueberry Dispenser](blueberry-dispenser.md), which gives a varying amount [Blueberries](blueberry.md) depending on the number of \* blue bees the player has in their [hive](hive.md) every 4 hours,
* The All-Time Top Blue Collectors [leaderboard](leaderboards.md), which shows the top 100 players who have collected the most blue pollen.

On the second floor, there are more features:

* A [Royal Jelly](royal-jelly.md) token located near the front window.
* A [Blue Field Booster](blue-field-booster.md) that gives a 15-minute duration [field boost](field-boost.md) for either the [Stump Field](stump-field.md) (1 stack, only when [Stump Snail](stump-snail.md) is on cooldown), [Pine Tree Forest](pine-tree-forest.md) (1 stack), [Bamboo Field](bamboo-field.md) (1 stack), or the Blue Flower Field (3 stacks), and has a 45-minute cooldown.
* [Gifted Bucko Bee](gifted-bucko-bee.md), who will require a [Translator](translator.md) for it to hand out [quests](quests.md),
* The Top Bucko Bee Helpers leaderboard, which is located to the left of Gifted Bucko Bee and shows the top 100 players who have completed the most Bucko Bee quests,
* The Daily Top Blue Collectors leaderboard, which is located to the right of Gifted Bucko Bee and shows the top 100 players who have collected the most blue pollen in a day.

## Store

There's a store in the Blue HQ that sells nine items. To open the store, press E when near the store's items and *not* on the Blue Teleporter, Blueberry Dispenser, the Leaderboards, or the second floor.

## Trivia

* The Tide Popper is currently the most expensive item able to be bought in the Blue HQ.
* If the player goes to the corner of the ramp inside the Blue HQ closest to the [Clover Field](clover-field.md), they will be able to hear the Crawlers music.
* Unlike the [Riley Bee](riley-bee.md) model on the [Red HQ](red-hq.md), the wings of the Bucko Bee model don't have collision.
* On 2022-01-01, there was a glitch where if the player attempted to view the Tide Popper, the game would freeze and they won't be able to exit the shop, except for rejoining the game.
* The Blue HQ is also known as the "Blue Clubhouse." If the player does not have 4 blue bee types discovered, the top of their screen will say, "Discover 4 Blue Bee types to enter the **Blue Clubhouse**."
