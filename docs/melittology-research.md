# Melittology Research

![Research Points](img/Research_Points.png){ align=right width=100 }

**Melittology Research** is the questline that earns you [Research Points](research-points.md) for the [Melittology](melittology.md) tree. It has 25 ranks, from the [Melittology Quests](melittology-quest-giver.md) pad on the mountain top.

## How it works

* Every rank needs [Rebirth 10](rebirths.md) and the rank before it. Each rank can be done once.
* Each quest is called **"Melittology Research: Rank N"**, with the description *"Complete this study to earn X Research."*
* The quests give no items. Turning a rank in shows "+X Research" and gives that many Research Points.
* Ranks 1 to 20 give **40** Research Points, enough for the whole base tree (38) with 2 left over.
* Ranks 21 to 25, the **Saturation Studies**, give **36** more, for the [Supreme paths](melittology.md#supreme-paths).
* Rank 21 also gives the **Eureka!** [sticker](sticker.md), once per account: *"Eureka! Your research turned up something else."*

Goo amounts aren't fixed in the quest list, so they aren't shown here. Numbers use the game's abbreviations, as on the [Rebirths](rebirths.md#requirements-and-buffs) page.
