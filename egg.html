    Inventory Items

    Egg¶

    Eggs are a type of inventory item where the player drags into their hive to hatch a bee, can be donated to the Wind Shrine or are used to roll the Sticker Printer. They are also the items that help players start playing in the game. Eggs come in many different types, some offering chances at different rarities of bees, and some hatching a specific bee. Currently, a total of 6 (or 11 if counting the gifted variants) different types of eggs exist in the game, not including Plastic Eggs, specific bee eggs, the Flight of the Bumble Egg, or the Swarming Egg of the Hive.

    Ever since the 2019-09-28 update, players do not need a vacant hive slot to hatch an egg. Now, players can hatch an egg on top of an existing bee to replace it. The level of the bee remains the same, acting like a Royal Jelly.

    Please also note that due to regulations in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands, Belgium, and Australia, any eggs in the Robux Shop won't be available to purchase for British, Dutch, Belgian, and Australian consumers. This restriction applies to most of the eggs in the shop.

    General Egg Types¶

    Basic Egg¶

    Basic Egg¶

    The Basic Egg is the most common type of egg found in the game. It will almost always hatch into a Basic Bee (87% chance), but in rare cases, it can hatch into a Rare Bee (10% chance), an Epic Bee (2.5% chance), a Legendary Bee (0.5% chance), or into a Gifted Bee (0.4% chance), with a 0.348% (1 out of 287.35632184) chance of getting a Gifted Basic Bee from the egg. Mythic bees and Event bees cannot be hatched from this egg. This egg cannot be used on a Basic Bee.

    Ways to obtain a Basic Egg:

    • First-time players receive a free Basic Egg in their inventory.
    • Purchasing from the Basic Egg Shop located near the Sunflower Field for a base price of 1,000 Honey and capping at 10,000,000 Honey. Every time the player purchases a Basic Egg, the price of the next Basic Egg scales up.
    • Adding the Round Basic Bee Sticker to the Sticker Stack rewards 1 Basic Egg.

    Silver Egg¶

    Silver Egg¶

    The Silver Egg is a type of egg that will most likely hatch into a Rare bee (64.9% chance), but it has a small chance to hatch into an Epic bee (30% chance), a Legendary bee (5% chance), or a Mythic bee (0.1% chance). A Basic Bee or an Event bee cannot be hatched from this egg.

    Ways to obtain a Silver Egg:

    • Purchasing from Ticket Tent for 50 Tickets each.
    • Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 100 Robux.
    • Finishing Black Bear's and Panda Bear's Silver Egg questline.
    • As a reward from Brown Bear's 25th quest.
    • Completing the 25th Sticker-Seeker quest rewards you 1 Silver Egg.
    • An extremely low chance of dropping from a Mantis (in increments of 1 or 5).
    • A low chance of dropping from the King Beetle and Tunnel Bear.
    • A low chance to drop from Rare Sprouts.
    • An extremely rare chance from leaves when collecting pollen in the Clover Field.
    • As a drop from a Festive Planter.
    • A rare chance to drop from stockings.
    • A rare reward from the Winter Memory Match.

    Gifted Silver Egg¶

    A Gifted Silver Egg shares the same probabilities as a Silver Egg, but the egg will always hatch the bee into its gifted variant.

    Ways to obtain a Gifted Silver Egg:

    • Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg including a Gifted Silver Egg, but with lower chances of obtaining upon subsequent defeats.
    • As a reward for getting Top 25 in the Daily Top Legendary Bee Pollen Collectors by the end of a day.

    Gold Egg¶

    Gold Egg¶

    The Gold Egg is a type of egg most likely to hatch into an Epic bee (79% chance), but it has a chance to hatch into a Legendary bee (20% chance) or a Mythic bee (1% chance). A Basic Bee, Rare bee, or an Event bee cannot hatch from this egg.

    5 Gold Eggs are required in order to craft the Honey Mask.

    Ways to obtain a Gold Egg:

    • Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 100 Tickets each.
    • Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 200 Robux each.
    • A rare reward from the Robo Bear Challenge.
    • A rare reward from the Extreme Memory Match.
    • In token form in the Werewolf's Cave, which is guarded by two cave monsters.
    • Completing Black Bear's and Panda Bear's Gold Egg questline.
    • As a reward from Brown Bear's 50th, 340th and 390th quest.
    • Completing the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine's 50th quest rewards 1 Gold Egg.
    • Capturing Commando Chick 10 times.
    • A low chance of dropping upon defeating the King Beetle.
    • An extremely low chance of dropping upon defeating a Scorpion.
    • A rare chance to drop from Epic sprouts.
    • An extremely rare chance of dropping from leaves when collecting pollen in the Clover Field.
    • A rare drop from a Ticket Planter, Candy Planter, Red Clay Planter, Festive Planter, Pesticide Planter and Petal Planter.
    • A rare drop from the Stockings.
    • A low chance to drop from a Festive Nymph.
    • As a rare reward from the Winter Memory Match.

    Gifted Gold Egg¶

    A Gifted Gold Egg shares the same probabilities as a Gold Egg, but the egg will always hatch the bee into its gifted variant. This type of egg cannot be applied to craft the Honey Mask.

    Ways to obtain a Gifted Gold Egg:

    • Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg, including a Gifted Gold Egg, but with lower chances of obtaining upon subsequent defeats.
    • As a reward for getting Top 25 in the Daily Top Epic Bee Pollen Collectors by the end of a day.
    • As a Playtime Grandmaster badge reward from donating tickets to the Wind Shrine.
    • Completing 50 Gifted Bucko Bee quests or 50 Gifted Riley Bee quests awards 1 Gifted Gold Egg.
    • Completing 275 Brown Bear quests awards 1 Gifted Gold Egg.
    • Completing the 150th quest of the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine rewards 1 Gifted Gold Egg.
    • A very rare chance of dropping upon defeating the Stump Snail and the Coconut Crab.
    • An exceptionally rare chance to drop from mantis.

    Diamond Egg¶

    Diamond Egg¶

    The Diamond Egg is a type of egg that will most likely hatch into a Legendary bee (95% chance), but it has a rare chance to hatch into a Mythic bee (5% chance). A Basic Bee, Rare bee, Epic bee, or an Event bee cannot hatch from this egg.

    5 Diamond Eggs are required in order to craft the Diamond Mask.

    Ways to obtain a Diamond Egg:

    • A Diamond Egg token is located past the Bear Gate, under the platform where Onett stands on. The player can use a glider or a parachute and glide towards the token in front of Shadow Bear.
    • Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 400 Robux each.
    • Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 400 Tickets each.
    • A rare reward from Robo Bear Challenge.
    • A rare reward from the Extreme Memory Match.
    • Completing Black Bear's Diamond Egg questline.
    • Completing Panda Bear's fifth star treat/ant quest, "Colossal Combat".
    • Completing Dapper Bear's tenth quest, "The Long Haul".
    • Completing Brown Bear's 75th and 525th quest.
    • Completing Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine 75th quest.
    • A rare chance of dropping upon defeating the Stump Snail, Diamond Aphids, King Beetle, Tunnel Bear, and Coconut Crab.
    • A very rare chance of dropping upon defeating Spiders, Mantises, and Werewolves.
    • A rare chance to drop from Legendary sprouts.
    • A guaranteed drop from Supreme sprouts.
    • An extremely rare chance of dropping from leaves while collecting pollen from the Clover Field.
    • A very rare reward from catching a meteor.
    • A rare drop from a Festive Planter.
    • A rare reward from Winter Memory Match.

    Gifted Diamond Egg¶

    Ways to obtain a Gifted Diamond Egg:

    Mythic Egg¶

    Mythic Egg¶

    The Mythic Egg is a type of egg that will always hatch into a Mythic bee. The player cannot obtain any other type of bee from this egg besides a Mythic bee. (The player can obtain a Gifted Mythic bee from this egg, although it is very rare.)

    Ways to obtain a Mythic Egg:

    • As a reward for getting Top 25 in the Daily Top Memory Matchers by the end of a day.
    • Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 1,200 Robux each (limited to 3 eggs per player).
    • Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 2,500 Tickets each.
    • As a very rare reward from Robo Bear Challenge.
    • An extremely rare chance of getting from Extreme Memory Match.
    • Completing Black Bear's 75th quest, "Myth in The Making".
    • Completing Dapper Bear's 15th quest, "More Than Myth".
    • As a reward from Brown Bear's 100th, 200th, and 400th quests.
    • Completing the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine 300th quest awards one Mythic Egg.
    • Capturing Commando Chick for the 50th time guarantees a Mythic Egg.
    • An extremely rare chance of dropping upon defeating the Stump Snail, Mondo Chick, and Coconut Crab. Mondo Chick has a higher chance to drop when defeated faster.
    • An extremely rare chance of dropping when catching a meteor.
    • A very rare drop from a Festive Planter.
    • Purchasing it for 500 Snowflakes and 100 Gingerbread Bears in Bee Bear's Catalog.
    • Completing Gummy Bear’s Goo Year’s Beacon quest during Beesmas 2025 rewards you 1 Mythic Egg and other items.

    Gifted Mythic Egg¶

    Ways to obtain a Gifted Mythic Egg:

    Star Egg¶

    Star Egg¶

    The Star Egg is a type of egg that hatches into any type of gifted bee that is not already in the player's hive, aside from gifted Event bees. If the player already has all the different types of gifted bees in their hive besides gifted event bees, the egg will hatch into a random type of gifted bee instead. The egg will never hatch a gifted mythic bee until all other gifted non-event bees are in the hive.

    Ways to obtain a Star Egg:

    • Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg, including a star egg, but with lower chances of obtaining upon subsequent defeats.
    • Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 800 Robux each (limited to 5 eggs per player).
    • An exceptionally rare chance of dropping from a Mythic Meteor Shower.
    • Completing Mother Bear's first ten quests ("Seven To Seven" quest).
    • Completing Panda Bear's first ten star treat/ant quests ("Royal Rumble" quest).
    • An exceptionally rare chance to drop upon defeating the King Beetle.
    • An exceptionally rare chance of dropping from a Festive Planter.
    • As an exceptionally rare drop from the Party Mechsquito.
    • As an exceptionally rare drop from the Sticker Sprout.

    Trivia¶

    • The Digital Bee, Vicious Bee, Windy Bee, Bear Bee and Gummy Bee Eggs are the only types of Event bee eggs that could or can be obtained without spending Robux or Tickets. (A Bear Bee Egg can be obtained by redeeming a Bear Bee Voucher.)
    • Star Eggs and Basic Eggs are the only two eggs that can hatch into a Basic Bee(Gifted variant if Star Egg)
    • Plastic Eggs, the Flight of the Bumble Egg (Egg Hunt 2019), and the Swarming Egg of the Hive (Egg Hunt 2020) are the only types of eggs that do not hatch into a bee.
    • Any kind of specific bee egg and event bee egg will have the same icon as a Basic Egg in the inventory.
    • The Bear Bee, Festive Bee, Puppy Bee, Windy Bee, and Digital Bee Eggs are the only type of Event bee eggs that can or could be obtained directly by spending robux. However, as of now, Bear Bee is the only event bee that can be obtained directly by Robux
    • Basic Eggs are the only type of eggs that can be obtained directly with honey.
    • The star that appears on the Star Egg is slightly larger than the stars on the other types of gifted eggs.
    • The Mythic Egg is the only type of egg with the name of the bee rarity that it hatches into.
    • The Mythic Egg is also the only egg that has a 100% of hatching into a certain bee rarity (except for Event bee eggs and specific bee eggs). However, before mythic bees were added, Diamond Eggs used to have a 100% chance of hatching into a Legendary bee.
    • The Diamond and Basic Eggs are the only non-specific or event eggs that can hatch into a bee of their name (the Diamond and Basic Bees, respectively), though you can get other bees from these eggs.
    • The Mythic Egg is also the only egg with a letter on it.
    • The Choose-a-Mythic Egg is the only egg that allows the player to choose which bee hatches.
    • The Fuzzy Bee, Buoyant Bee, and the Precise Bee Eggs are the only (non-event) specific bee eggs that could have been bought from the Robux Shop.
    • The Gifted Silver Egg and the Star Egg are the only Gifted eggs that can be obtained by completing the main questlines from NPCs (Black Bear's "Getting Stumped", Mother Bear's "Seven To Seven", and Panda Bear's "Royal Rumble").
    • The player cannot hatch a specific bee egg on that specific bee. For example, a Bumble Bee Egg cannot be hatched on a Bumble Bee.
    • Even though Basic Eggs don't guarantee a Basic Bee, they still can't be used on Basic Bees.
    • The Basic Egg and Choose-A-Mythic Egg are the only eggs without gifted variants of themselves, excluding specific bee eggs.
    • Similarly, the Star Egg is the only gifted egg without a regular variant.
    • The Bubble Bee Egg is the only non-Event specific bee egg that can be obtained from a quest (B.B.M's Naughty List).
    • The Tadpole Bee Egg is the only egg that can be obtained by giving an NPC present (obtained specifically from Bubble Bee Man).
