    Mechanics

    Mutation¶

    This piece of content contains information obtained through datamining.

    Due to the nature of the information, details may be inaccurate or outdated.

    Datamined information: The probabilty of getting a certain mutation, and the probability of getting a certain strength of a mutation based on the bee's level. — December 19th, 2024

    Mutations are a mechanic that buffs the stats of certain bees in the player's hive. Mutated bees have a different colored level number in their hive slot and on their wings than the standard white, respective to what stat it's mutated with. A mutated bee can become temporarily radioactive, and the radiation can spread to neighboring bees in the hive.

    Every time the player feeds a bee a bitterberry, it has a 0.1% chance to get a random mutation. Royal Jellies and Star Jellies has a 0.02% chance of turning a bee into a mutated bee. Atomic treats guarantee a mutation when fed to a bee, regardless of whether it is radioactive or not.

    If the bee is radioactive:

    • The chance of a mutation when using a bitterberry is increased to 1%,
    • The chance of a mutation when using a royal jelly or star jelly is increased to 1%,
    • Using many special treats (sunflower seeds, strawberries, blueberries, pineapples, moon charms) have a 0.004% chance to mutate the bee. This is regardless of whether the bee likes the treat or not.

    Note that using a royal jelly or an egg on a mutated bee will almost certainly make the bee lose its mutation, just like gifted bees losing their gifted status.

    A mutated bee's GUI on discovery shows a pop-up to the left of the bee, which shows the player the mutation the bee has gained, with a text box reading, ☢️ {Bee type} gained a mutation! (+x% [Mutation Buff]) ☢️

    Possible Bee Bonuses¶

    Mutations are separate from every other modifiers in the game, meaning that:

    • If a mutation is given as a percentage, it is multiplied directly to the bee's base stats after every other modifiers are applied.
    • If a mutation is given as a whole number, it is added to the bee's base stats before multiplicative modifiers are applied.

    Mutation strength¶

    Some of the information in this section has been modified for better reading. For the raw data, visit Module:Mutation Stats and the data it uses.

    The upper bound of a mutation increases with the level of the mutated bee, allowing it to receive better mutations. More specifically, let lvl be the level of the mutated bee:

    • \(lvlPercentage=(lvl-1)/(25-1)\)
    • The upper bound of a mutation is scaled between the minimum and maximum upper bound of the mutation, based on lvlPercentage: \(upperBound=minUpperBound+(maxUpperBound-minUpperBound)\times lvlPercentage\).
    • The lower bound of a mutation also go through this process. However, the minimum and maximum lower bound of any mutation is the same, so there are no changes.

    After that, the randomBias function is invoked with the below parameters, and the result is rounded to the nearest interval to give the final result:
    randomBias(lowerBound, upperBound, lowerBound, 0.5)
    This gives a random value between lowerBound and upperBound, with a bias to values near lowerBound. This value is then rounded to the nearest interval to get the bee's mutation strength.
    For more information on how this function works, see the designated module page.

    The lower bound, minimum upper bound and maximum upper bound, and the interval of a mutation can be found below.

    Trivia¶

    • It is possible to obtain mutations that are almost entirely useless to certain bees. These include:
    • An Attack Mutation on Baby Bee, since it is unable to attack.
      • While Attack mutations do not allow Baby Bee to attack, it will still add to the player's total attack, which can buff Star Saw.
    • A Bee Ability Rate mutation on an ungifted Basic Bee or Brave Bee without a Beequip adding an ability, both of which lack abilities.
    • An Energy mutation on a Photon Bee or Exhausted Bee, as both bees have infinite energy.
      • However, this still increases the amount of nectar the two bees can sip from a Planter.
    • The Beesmas Tree Hat Beequip requires a bee to have a mutation to be equipped, making any mutation potentially useful for any bee.
    • The Bubble Light Beequip requires a bee to have an Energy mutation to be equipped.
    • When mutating a bee, any equipped Beequip is automatically unequipped and must be re-equipped by the player if they wish to continue using it.
    • Depending on the game's language, the radiation emoji might be changed.
