    Night Bell¶

    Night Bell

    "Summons nighttime. A Moon Sprout and a Vicious Bee are guaranteed to appear."

    COOLDOWN

    1 second

    A Night Bell is an inventory item that was added in the 2018-12-25 update. When used, it summons nighttime, along with a Moon Sprout and a Rogue Vicious Bee.

    Whenever a player activates a night bell, the sound of a bell chiming will play and a server-wide message will appear, saying:

    🌙 {Username} has summoned nighttime... 🌙

    Upon using a night bell, the server will receive the public announcement above, along with a sound.

    Ways to Obtain¶

    • As a rare drop from certain mobs:
    • Werewolf
    • King Beetle
    • Wild Windy Bee
    • Fireflies
    • Coconut Crab
    • Tunnel Bear
    • As a reward from Memory Matches.
    • A rare reward from Night Memory Match.
    • As a rare reward from Winter Memory Match.
    • Buying them from the Robux Shop for 80 Robux each.
    • As a Playtime Master Badge reward from donating Enzymes to the Wind Shrine.
    • Completing Spirit Bear's "Do You Bee-lieve In Magic?" quest rewards 1 night bell.
    • As a rare drop from a Festive Planter.
    • As a rare drop from Stockings.
    • As a rare drop from Festive Nymphs.

    Trivia¶

    • Using a Night Bell when it is already nighttime results in a message that says “You can only use Night Bells during the day.”
    • If you summon nighttime right after nighttime ends a new set of fireflies will be spawned when you can see the other fireflies fly away.
    • The sky will also flash if you summon nighttime even quicker (when the sky is changing from night to day).
    • Night Bells and Boxes-O-Frogs are the only two consumable items that summon mobs.
    • The Night Bell summons two types of mobs, Fireflies and Rogue Vicious Bee.
