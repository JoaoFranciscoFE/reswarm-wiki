    Gameplay Mechanics System

    System Page¶

    This page is for stats that apply to the player, collecting pollen, and the hive. For stats that apply to bees, see Stats.

    The System Page is shown by clicking the cog in the menu bar. It is in between the Badges page and the Robux Shop page. Here, the player can redeem codes, send Trade Requests, view the Global Leaderboard, use the Cub Console, view the players' stats, turn on and off in-game music, toggle Pollen Text, Abbreviate Pollen, allow Trade Requests, display and hide other players' bees, toggle Reducing Strobing VFX, and toggle Auto-Jelly. Turning off all in-game music only silences the music, not the sound effects (such as the sounds of Ability Tokens and obtaining Legendary, Mythic, Gifted, and Event). You can mute all sound by turning off the in-game sound on the Roblox menu.

    Character Stats¶

    The tab also lists many of the player's active stat values, though not all of them. The following values are listed and explained:

    Convert Amount¶

    Boosts the base conversion of your bees before multipliers by a certain amount.
    Example: A Basic Bee with 20 Convert Amount will convert 100 Honey in 4 seconds instead of 80 Honey in 4 seconds.
    It can be boosted with amulets, the Honey Mask and certain belts. This stat was formerly named 'Conversion Amount' until it was changed in the Beesmas 2019 update.

    Convert Rate¶

    Boosts your bees conversion by the following percentage.
    Example: A Basic Bee with 200% Convert Rate will convert 160 Honey in 4 seconds instead of 80 Honey in 4 seconds.
    It can be boosted with amulets, Enzymes, Marshmallow Bees, Super Smoothies, Science Enhancement, the Conversion Buff, the Honeyday Event buff, the Honey Badge, Gifted Diamond Bee, the Yellow Jellybean, Jellybean Sharing Bonus, certain Stickers in the Sticker Stack, some beequips and most Bags. This stat was formerly named 'Conversion Rate' until it was changed in the Beesmas 2019 update.

    Convert Rate At Hive¶

    Boosts your bees’ conversion by the following percentage at the hive. This stat can stack with Convert Rate.
    Example: A level 1 Basic Bee with 200% Convert Rate and 200% Hive Convert Rate will convert 320 Honey in 4 seconds while converting at the hive, but it will only convert 160 Honey outside of the hive.
    It can be increased with the Pumpkin Badge, Bubble Bloat and certain items. This buff was used to be "Hive Convert Rate" until the 2021-12-26 update.

    Bonus Convert Amount¶

    Increases the base conversion of the player's bees. It can be increased by certain amulets and items.

    Loot Luck¶

    Loot Luck is a passive attribute that increases the chances of receiving rewards from defeated mobs.
    It can be increased with Baby Love, the Clover Badge, Baby Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus and certain items.
    This stat was formerly named 'Luck' until it was changed in the 2019-04-05 update.

    Critical Chance¶

    The chance of your bees hitting stronger or collecting more pollen. More accurately - by how big your Critical Power is.
    Example: A Basic Bee with 50% Critical Chance will hit with increased damage half of the time.
    It can be boosted with amulets, Tropical Drinks, Focus, the Green Jellybean, Gifted Commander Bee and certain items. Currently, the highest critical chance a player can have at a time is 88%.

    Critical Power¶

    This stat multiplies how strong a Critical Hit will be. It applies to fighting and pollen collecting.
    Example: Basic Bee with 300% Critical Power (on critical hit) will hit with the power of 3 Basic Bees (3 damage).
    It can be boosted with the Ant Amulet, Melody, the Battle Badge, Gifted Tabby Bee, Gifted Looker Bee and with certain items. The calculated max Critical Power is 695%.

    Super-Crit Chance¶

    The possibility for a critical hit to Super-Crit. Super-Crits deals 33% more damage and collect twice as much pollen as a regular critical hit, and are instantly converted. When a hit is a Super-Crit, there will be a purple color indicating the damage and the ! sign next to it. It can be boosted by having a Gifted Precise Bee, Tabby Blessing, Precision, a precise mark, the Bruteforce upgrade in the Robo Bear Challenge or using a Super Smoothie. The max possible Super-Crit Chance you can have at a time is 53% (assuming you are in the Robo Bear Challenge).

    Super-Crit Power¶

    The amount pollen is multiplied by when performing Super-Crits (after applying Critical Power). Super-crit attacks are only increased by 33% as much as Pollen Super-crits. It can only be boosted by the Whistle and Kazoo Beequips, a Cog Amulet or temporarily boosted by the Dark Scythe.

    Bee Ability Rate¶

    Increases the speed at which the player's bees create Ability Tokens.
    Example: If it takes a bee about 20 seconds to produce a token, with 20% Bee Ability Rate, the bee is likely to take ~16 seconds to produce the token.
    Bee ability rate can be boosted by Star Amulets, Motivating Nectar, certain masks, items, bee mutations, and Beequips.

    Pollen¶

    Increases pollen from all sources (both from bees and tools).
    Example: With 100% pollen, the player can collect 1,000 pollen, and with 110% pollen, it will able to collect 1,100 pollen from the same amount of time and flowers.
    It can be increased with amulets, Bear Morph, Inspire, Baby Love, Pollen Marks, the Honeyday Event buff, from Clouds, Gifted Basic Bee, Gifted Bear Bee, the Periwinkle Jellybean or with certain items.

    White Pollen¶

    Boosts your white pollen collection. It can be increased with amulets, Tropical Drinks, Marshmallow Bees, White Boost, the Spider Badge, the Coconut Badge, Gifted Shocked Bee, the White Jellybean, Wind Boosts, or with certain items.

    Red Pollen¶

    Boosts your red pollen collection. It can be increased with amulets, Red Extracts, Red Boost, Flame Heat, the Strawberry Badge, Gifted Rad Bee or with certain items.

    Blue Pollen¶

    Boosts your blue pollen collection. It can be increased with Amulets, Blue Extracts, Blue Boost, with the Bamboo Badge, Gifted Cool Bee or with certain items.

    Bomb Pollen¶

    Boosts your pollen collection from all bombs.
    Example: If the player collects 5,000 pollen with a Buzz Bomb token, with 200% Bomb Pollen they will collect 10,000 pollen from the same flowers.
    It can be increased with Amulets, Bomb Combos, Gifted Bomber Bee, Festive Mark, Black Jelly Bean or with certain items.

    Buzz Bomb Pollen¶

    Boosts your pollen collection from Buzz Bombs and Fuzz Bombs. It can be increased with amulets, Gifted Demo Bee, or with certain items.

    Red Bomb Pollen¶

    Boosts your pollen collection from Red Bombs. It can be increased with Amulets, Gifted Rascal Bee, Festive Mark or with certain items.

    Blue Bomb Pollen¶

    Boosts your pollen collection from Blue Bombs. It can be increased with Amulets, Gifted Frosty Bee, or with certain items.

    Bubble Pollen¶

    Boosts your pollen collection from bubbles.
    Example: If the player collects 5,000 pollen with a bubble, with 200% Pollen from Bubbles they will collect 10,000 pollen from the same flowers.
    It can be increased with Gifted Bubble Bee, Cog Amulet, Bubble Mask or the Diamond Mask.

    Flame Pollen¶

    Boosts your pollen collection from flames created by bees, ability tokens, and passive abilities.
    Example: If the flames collect 1,000 over time, with 200% Pollen from Flames they will collect 2,000 pollen from the same flowers.
    It can be increased with Gifted Fire Bee, Cog Amulet, or the Demon Mask.

    Bee Gather Pollen¶

    Boosts pollen collection for all bees.
    Example: With 200% Bee Gather Pollen, Basic Bee will collect 20 pollen in 4 seconds.
    It can be increased with amulets, Glues, Gifted Music Bee, the x2 Pollen from Bees Gamepass or with certain items.

    Bee Ability Pollen¶

    Boosts pollen collection from all bees' abilities and passives.
    It can be increased with Gifted Shy Bee Hive Bonus, certain Beequips, or Sticker Stack.

    Tool Pollen¶

    Boosts your pollen collection from your tool.
    Example: With 100% (base) Tool Pollen, the player will collect 1,000 pollen with their tool at once. With 125% Tool Pollen player will collect 1,250 pollen with their tool at once at the same place.
    It can be increased with amulets, Glues, Gifted Carpenter Bee, with the Stump Badge or with certain items.

    Instant Conversion¶

    Instantly converts your pollen when you are collecting pollen on a field.
    Example: a Basic Bee with 50% Instant Conversion that collects 10 pollen will make half of it instantly convert into Honey. You will have 5 Honey and 5 pollen, so you can collect more pollen.
    It can be boosted with amulets, Enzymes, the Dandelion Badge, the Cactus Badge, Gifted Photon Bee, Festive Blessing buff, or with certain items.

    Instant White Conversion¶

    Instantly converts white pollen you collect, in addition to Instant Conversion. It can be boosted with amulets, the Pineapple Badge, Gifted Windy Bee, or with the Coconut Canister.

    Instant Red Conversion¶

    Instantly converts red pollen you collect, in addition to Instant Conversion. It can be boosted with amulets, the Mushroom Badge, Gifted Crimson Bee, or with certain items.

    Instant Blue Conversion¶

    Instantly converts blue pollen you collect, in addition to Instant Conversion. It can be boosted with amulets, the Blue Flower Badge, Gifted Cobalt Bee, Diamond Mask, or with certain items.

    Instant Bomb Conversion¶

    Instantly converts pollen collected by the player's bomb. It can be temporarily boosted by standing in a festive mark, or can be permanently increased by owning a Gifted Demon Bee.

    Instant Flame Conversion¶

    Instantly converts pollen collected by the player's summoned flames. It can be boosted by having the Demon Mask equipped, or you can temporarily get 50% Instant Flame Conversion by triggering the Scorching Star Passive.

    Instant Bubble Conversion¶

    Instantly converts pollen collected by the player's popped bubbles. There are currently no ways to obtain it.

    Honey Per Pollen¶

    Allows you to make more Honey from the same amount of pollen, which works on conversion, Instant Converters, Micro-Converters, and Instant Conversion.
    Example: With 200% Honey Per Pollen, a player will make 2,000 Honey from 1,000 Pollen.
    It can be increased with the Moon Amulet, the Wealth Clock, the Goo Badge, Gifted Gummy Bee or Nectar.

    Honey At Hive¶

    Increases the Honey you convert when converting at hive. You can boost this stat with Satisfying Nectar and Balloon Blessing. Diamond Bee's Shimmering Honey directly multiplies this stat.

    Movespeed¶

    Movespeed of your character in studs. It can be increased with amulets, Oil, Super Smoothie, Haste or Haste+, Bear Morph, Coconut Haste, Gifted Hasty Bee, or with boots.

    Movement Collection¶

    Allows the player to collect pollen when walking on flowers. The player can get this boost from wearing boots or from a Moon Amulet. The higher the movement collection, the greater amount of pollen the player collects from walking on flowers. This boost also collects goo if the player walks on any goo patches.

    Jump Power¶

    Jump power of your character in studs. It can be increased with Bear Morph, all boots (except for the Basic Boots), or the Propeller Hat. The minimum jump power is 55 and the maximum jump power is 97.

    Defense¶

    Reduces the damage that you take.
    Example: If a mob deals 50 damage, with 50% Defense it will deal 25 damage instead.
    It can be increased with the Shell Amulet, hats, or with guards. Having 100% defense means that you take no damage and can be achieved by activating the emergency coconut shield passive. The maximum defense without the emergency coconut shield is 60%. This is achieved by having the Demon Mask or Diamond Mask (+35%), both the Crimson and Cobalt Guards (+10% each), and +5% on a Shell Amulet. Defense was originally Damage Reduction.

    Bond From Treats¶

    Gives additional bond for your bees when you give them treats.
    Example: If you feed 100 normal treats (1,000 total bond) to your bee, with 125% Bond From Treats it will grant 1,250 bond instead.
    It can be increased with the Moon Amulet, Gifted Puppy Bee or as a Hive Bonus from the Reindeer Antlers beequip.

    Bond From Battle¶

    Gives additional bond for your bees when you are defeating a mob.
    Example: Normally, scorpions give 15 bond for each of your bees when you defeat it. With 150% Bond From Battle, your bees will receive 22.5 Bond instead.
    It can be increased with amulets.

    Honey From Tokens¶

    Boosts Honey that you receive from all Honey giving tokens (Honey Gift, Honey Gift+, Impale, mob loot, Coin Scatter, Honeystorm, and others).
    Example: A level 2 Diamond Bee will give 2,000 Honey with its Honey Gift+ token. With 200% Honey From Tokens, it will give 4,000 Honey instead.
    It can be increased with amulets, Gifted Honey Bee, or with certain items.

    Bee Attack¶

    Makes all your bees, regardless of their colors, deal more damage per hit. It will add one bee attack (Damage) per bee attack stat that you have.
    Example: With +4 Bee Attack, Basic Bee will attack with the power of 5 Basic Bees (Base Attack + Bee Attack multiplier).
    It can be increased with amulets, with Rage, or Gifted Brave Bee, or with certain items buffs and beequips.

    Red Attack¶

    Same as Bee Attack, but applies only for Red bees. It can be boosted with amulets or certain items.

    Blue Attack¶

    Same as Bee Attack, but applies only for Blue bees. It can be boosted with amulets or certain items.

    Colorless Attack¶

    Same as Bee Attack, but applies only for Colorless bees. It can be boosted with amulets, or certain items.

    Attack Multiplier¶

    Multiplies attack of all bees, but with a percentage. It can be increased with Amulets, Stingers, Flame Heat, Emergency Coconut Shield , Gifted Rage Bee or the Demon Mask.

    Goo¶

    Multiplies all collected goo. It can be increased with the Shell Amulet, Gummy Morph, Gummy Mask, Gummy Boots, Gummy Star, or Gummyballer.

    Goo Conversion¶

    Instantly converts pollen equal to the goo gathered, which can convert all the pollen gathered (effectively serving as 100% Instant Conversion) with a high enough Goo Conversion % and Goo multiplyer. It can be increased by amulets, Gummy Mask, Gummy Morph, or Gummy Star.

    Capacity Multiplier¶

    Gives additional pollen storage by the amount of the percentages.
    Example: If your backpack can hold up to 200,000 pollen, with 200% Capacity Multiplier, your backpack can hold up to 400,000 instead.
    It can be increased with amulets, Sunflower Badge, Quest Badge, Bumble Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus, Buoyant Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus and with certain items. If your capacity multiplier is a decimal percentage, it doesn't show the decimal and rounds the number, but the rounding doesn't affect your capacity.

    White Field Capacity¶

    Gives additional pollen storage by the number of percentages based on the percentage of white flowers in a field.

    Example: If you have 15,000,000 Capacity (With Capacity Multiplier) and your White Field Capacity is 180%, your total capacity if in the Spider Field is 27,000,000. It can be increased with things like the Gummy Mask, Exhausted Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus, Polar Bear's Ornament and other inventory items. If your capacity multiplier is a decimal percentage, it doesn't show the decimal and rounds the number, but the rounding doesn't affect your capacity.

    Red Field Capacity¶

    Gives additional pollen storage by the number of percentages based on the percentage of red flowers in a field.

    Example: If you have 15,000,000 Capacity (With Capacity Multiplier) and your Red Field Capacity is 150%, your total capacity if on a field with all flowers being red is 22,500,000. It can be increased with things like Demon Mask, Riley Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus, Gifted Riley Bee (NPC) Ornament, the Rose Badge and other inventory items. If your capacity multiplier is a decimal percentage, it doesn't show the decimal and rounds the number, but the rounding doesn't affect your capacity.

    Blue Field Capacity¶

    Gives additional pollen storage by the amount of percentage based on the percentage of blue flowers in a field.

    Example: If you have 15,000,000 Capacity (With Capacity Multiplier) and your Blue Field Capacity is 200%, your total capacity if on a field with only blue flowers is 30,000,000. It can be increased with buffs like Bucko Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus, Gifted Bucko Bee (NPC) Ornament, the Pine Tree Badge, and other inventory items. If your capacity multiplier is a decimal percentage, it doesn't show the decimal and rounds the number, but the rounding doesn't affect your capacity.

    Auto-Jelly Settings¶

    The Auto-Jelly Settings can be found at the bottom of the System Page. There are options for Royal Jellies or Star Jellies to auto roll until the requirements are fulfilled. It can be used to help players obtain a bee they desire quickly. There are options for the requirement the jelly will roll into:

    • Until Legendary: Jellies will auto roll until you get a Legendary or Mythic Bee.
    • Until Mythic: Jellies will auto roll until you get a Mythic Bee.
    • Require Gifted: Jellies will auto roll until you get a Gifted or Mythic Bee.
    • Require Mutation: Jellies will auto roll until you get a Mutated or Mythic Bee.

    Multiple options can be turned on at a time, whilst also making the auto jelly stop on the combined requirement.
    Example: When the Legendary and Gifted options are turned on, the jellies will auto roll until you get a Gifted Legendary Bee. When the Legendary, Gifted, and Mutation options are turned on, the jellies will auto roll until you get a Gifted Legendary Bee with a Mutation.

    If the Until Legendary option or both the Until Legendary and Until Mythic options are turned on, it will auto roll until you get a Legendary Bee or Mythic Bee. If only the Require Gifted option is turned on, it will auto roll until you get a Gifted Bee or a Mythic Bee. If only the Require Mutation option is turned on, it will auto roll until you get a Gifted Bee or a Mutated Bee. If both the Require Gifted and Require Mutation options are turned on, it will auto roll until you get a Gifted Mutated Bee or Mythic Bee.

    If the Mutation option is turned on, but the bee you selected isn't radioactive, a pop-up after using a Royal Jelly will say that you can't auto-roll Royal Jellies for mutation. Unless the bee is radioactive, you will have to use the Royal Jellies one at a time.

    When using a Royal Jelly on a bee with an auto-jelly option enabled, a reminder appears after the message that the auto-jelly option is on.

    If you turn on the following options, an extra pop-up will warn the player that the options they selected are very rare:

    • Mythic and Gifted.
    • Mythic and Mutation.
    • Mythic, Gifted, and Mutation.
    • Legendary, Gifted, and Mutation.

    When using a Star Jelly on a bee, another pop-up will appear, reminding the player that an auto-jelly setting is on and will use as many star jellies as it needs in order to fulfill its requirement. This pop-up will appear even if only the Until Gifted option is turned on. If you are using the Require Gifted option with Star Jellies, you will still receive the warning, but nothing will happen.
