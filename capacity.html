    Mechanics

    Capacity¶

    Capacity is a mechanic that shows how much pollen the player can store at a time. Capacity can be increased in a variety of ways including buffs, items, stickers, and amulets. Capacity can be increased by static amounts or by multipliers. Static increases can be shown simply through addition symbols (+) while multipliers can be shown through multiplication symbols (x) or percentages (%). The player's Capacity is displayed in the top-middle section, labelled as "Pollen" and also on the player's bag. The player can see how much their capacity is multiplied by looking for the Capacity Multiplier stat in the System Page.

    Ways to Obtain¶

    Bags¶

    Each bag adds a static amount of Capacity for the player. These are typically the bulk of the static amount of Capacity that the player can hold at once.

    • The Pouch gives +200 Capacity.
    • The Jar gives +750 Capacity.
    • The Backpack gives +3,500 Capacity.
    • The Canister gives +10,000 Capacity.
    • The Mega-Jug gives +25,000 Capacity.
    • The Compressor gives +50,000 Capacity.
    • The Elite Barrel gives +125,000 Capacity.
    • The Port-O-Hive gives +250,000 Capacity.
    • The Red Port-O-Hive gives +400,000 Capacity.
    • The Blue Port-O-Hive gives +400,000 Capacity.
    • The Porcelain Port-O-Hive gives +600,000 Capacity.
    • The Coconut Canister gives +1,000,000 Capacity.

    Belts¶

    Each belt adds a static amount of Capacity for the player. Three of the belts also give a Capacity Multiplier.

    • The Belt Pocket gives +5,000 Capacity.
    • The Belt Bag gives +25,000 Capacity.
    • The Mondo Belt Bag gives +100,000 Capacity.
    • The Honeycomb Belt gives +150,000 capacity. It also gives +25% Capacity.
    • The Petal Belt gives +250,000 capacity. It also gives +50% Capacity.
    • The Coconut Belt gives +350,000 capacity. It also gives +75% Capacity.

    Guards¶

    Each guard adds a static amount of Capacity for the player.

    • The Brave Guard gives +5,000 Capacity.
    • The Hasty Guard gives +5,000 Capacity.
    • The Bomber Guard gives +5,000 Capacity.
    • The Looker Guard gives +5,000 Capacity.
    • The Red Guard gives +25,000 Capacity.
    • The Elite Red Guard gives +75,000 Capacity.
    • The Riley Guard gives +150,000 Capacity.
    • The Blue Guard gives +25,000 Capacity.
    • The Elite Blue Guard gives +75,000 Capacity.
    • The Bucko Guard gives +150,000 Capacity.
    • The Crimson Guard gives +250,000 Capacity.
    • The Cobalt Guard gives +250,000 Capacity.

    Hats¶

    There are several hats/masks that add a static amount of Capacity or give a Capacity Multiplier for the player.

    • The Strange Goggles give +77 Capacity.
    • The Honey Mask gives a x1.25 Capacity Multiplier.
    • The Fire Mask gives a x1.25 Capacity Multiplier.
    • The Bubble Mask gives a x1.5 Capacity Multiplier.
    • The Gummy Mask gives a x1.5 Capacity Multiplier.
    • The Demon Mask gives a x1.5 Capacity Multiplier.
    • The Diamond Mask gives a x3 Capacity Multiplier.

    Amulets¶

    There are several amulets that add a static amount of Capacity for the player. Star Amulets give a Capacity Multiplier.

    • The Star Amulet gives a Capacity Multiplier depending on the tier.
    • The Bronze Star Amulet gives a x1.25 Capacity Multiplier.
    • The Silver Star Amulet gives a x1.5 Capacity Multiplier.
    • The Gold Star Amulet gives a x1.75 Capacity Multiplier.
    • The Diamond Star Amulet gives a x2 Capacity Multiplier.
    • The Supreme Star Amulet gives a x2.5 Capacity Multiplier.
    • The Moon Amulet gives +25,000 Capacity which can be upgraded to +250,000 Capacity.
    • The Stick Bug Amulet can give up to +300,000 Capacity depending on the tier.
    • The Bronze Stick Bug Amulet gives from +25,000 Capacity up to +100,000 Capacity.
    • The Silver Stick Bug Amulet gives from +100,000 Capacity up to +150,000 Capacity.
    • The Gold Stick Bug Amulet gives from +150,000 Capacity up to +200,000 Capacity.
    • The Diamond Stick Bug Amulet gives from +200,000 Capacity up to +300,000 Capacity.
    • The Cog Amulet can give up to +250,000 Capacity depending on the tier.
    • The Bronze Cog Amulet gives from +22,500 Capacity up to +26,500 Capacity.
    • The Silver Cog Amulet gives from +45,000 Capacity up to +51,500 Capacity.
    • The Gold Cog Amulet gives from +90,000 Capacity up to +108,000 Capacity.
    • The Diamond Cog Amulet gives from +127,500 Capacity up to +162,500 Capacity.
    • The Supreme Cog Amulet gives from +200,000 Capacity up to +250,000 Capacity.

    Gifted Hive Bonuses¶

    There are two bees that give a Capacity Multiplier if gifted.

    • Gifted Bumble Bee gives a x1.1 Capacity Multiplier.
    • Gifted Buoyant Bee gives a x1.2 Capacity Multiplier.

    Beequips¶

    There are a handful of beequips that add a static amount of Capacity or give a Capacity Multiplier.

    • A Beret gives from +6,000 Capacity up to +13,500 Capacity.
    • A Single Mitten gives from +7,500 Capacity up to +10,000 Capacity. Using waxes can increase the amount up to +15,000 Capacity.
    • A Warm Scarf can give from +1% Capacity to +10% Capacity from waxing.
    • A Beesmas Tree Hat gives from +10,000 Capacity up to +25,000 Capacity. Using waxes can increase the amount up to +35,000 Capacity.
    • The Reindeer Antlers can give from +1% Capacity to +20% Capacity from waxing.
    • The Festive Wreath gives from +5,000 Capacity up to +10,000 Capacity. Using waxes can increase the amount up to +110,000 Capacity.

    Passives¶

    There are two passives that give a Capacity Multiplier. They can be obtained via a Diamond Star Amulet or Supreme Star Amulet. The Guiding Star Aura could also be obtained from Onett's Lid Art during Beesmas.

    • The Guiding Star passive gives a x1.25 Capacity Multiplier.
    • The Guiding Star Aura gives x2.5 Capacity to the player who summoned the star and x1.25 Capacity to other players.
    • The Star Shower passive give a x1.25 Capacity Multiplier.

    Buffs¶

    There are several ways to get a buff to capacity such as inventory items, ability tokens, defeating mobs, and redeeming codes.

    • Using a Purple Potion gives a x1.25 Capacity Multiplier. The buff can also be given from certain codes.
    • Using a Super Smoothie gives a x1.5 Capacity Multiplier. The buff can also be given from certain codes.
    • Using a Marshmallow Bee gives +50% Capacity. The buff can also be given from certain codes.
    • Using a Snowflake or collecting the Snowglobe Shake ability token from the Snowglobe Beequip gives a x1.25 Capacity Multiplier.

    Downgrades¶

    There is a debuff that reduces the Capacity of the player.

    • The Robo Bear Challenge Debuff reduces the Capacity by a depending on what round the player is on in the Robo Bear Challenge. At Round 1, it has a x0.1 Capacity Multiplier and increases by 0.015 per round to a total of a x0.46 Capacity Multiplier.

    There are certain upgrades that can be bought with cogs during the Robo Bear Challenge which can downgrade Capacity.

    • Common Upgrades:
    • Overfit: Red: This upgrades starts at x0.9 Capacity and maxes at 10 upgrades with x0.5 Capacity.
    • Sharpen: This upgrades starts at x0.95 Capacity and maxes at 10 upgrades with x0.75 Capacity.
    • Rare Upgrades:
    • Crypto: This upgrades starts at x0.85 Capacity and maxes at 3 upgrades with x0.55 Capacity.
    • Epic Upgrades:
    • Malware: This upgrades starts at x0.75 Capacity and maxes at 3 upgrades with x0.25 Capacity.
    • Legendary Upgrades:
    • Overclock: This upgrade gives x0.75 Capacity.
