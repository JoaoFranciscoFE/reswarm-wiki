    Not to be confused with Bear Bee, an Event bee with a similar name.

    Bee Bear is a traveling bear who appears during Beesmas. He is one of the four temporary quest givers, the others being Sun Bear, Gummy Bear and Bubble Bee Man.

    In 2018, his quests were focused on creating Presents, which could be later given to NPCs. At the end of his quests, he gave players a special bee, Festive Bee. Alternatively, if the player already had one by buying the Festive Bee Pack or from the Ticket Tent, he gave 500 Tickets instead. He was located in front of the Ant Gate between December 19, 2018, to January 31, 2019.

    His second visit was from December 22, 2019, to February 10, 2020. Bee Bear rewarded the player a Cub Buddy after the 10 quests that a player completed, and the Bee Cub after another 5 quests. His quests consisted of a variety of different requirements. Some included collecting Pollen, Tokens, Goo, defeating mobs, putting Ornaments on the Beesmas Tree, and more. He stood in front of the Dandelion Field, next to the Festive Present, the first time that a traveling Bear was not located in front of the Ant Gate.

    His third visit was from December 25, 2020, to March 1, 2021. His quests were used to help introduce Beequips. His quests also required completing and using Beesmas Decorations from multiple NPCs, collecting Snowflakes, purchasing items from his Catalog, and delivering Presents. He rewarded players with a Festive Bee ( or 500 Tickets if Festive Bee was already owned by the player) when his first 15 quests were completed, and rewarded a Festive Wreath Beequip for the Festive Bee when all his quests were completed.

    His fourth visit was from December 26, 2021, to April 1, 2022. His quests were used to help introduce the new planters, rewarded a special type of his own (Festive Planters), alongside giving players the opportunity to obtain a Cub Buddy (or a Star Treat if already owned) by completing his first 15 quests once again. In the second part of Beesmas, he rewards players a Snow Cub after all of his quests (new ones included) were completed.

    His fifth visit was from December 26, 2022, to May 7, 2023. His quests were used to help introduce the Robo Bear Challenge and drives, another new addition in the update. He rewarded players with a Cub Buddy (or a Star Treat if Cub Buddy was already owned by the player) when his first 15 quests were completed. In the second part of Beesmas, he rewarded players a Peppermint Robo Cub after all of his quests (new ones included) were completed.

    His sixth visit was from July 17, 2024, to October 29, 2024 (confirmed with an in-game timer). His quests were used to help introduce stickers. After 5 of his quests were completed, he rewarded players a Sticker Planter and a Star Jelly. After 10 of his quests were completed, he rewarded players a Wavy Festive Hive Skin. Upon completing a further 5 quests, he rewarded players a Cub Buddy (Cub Buddy Voucher if the player already owns a Cub Buddy). When completing the final 5 quests, he rewarded players with a Doodle Cub.

    His seventh visit was originally for a Roblox event, the Winter Spotlight; however, he remained in the game for a few more months even though the event had ended. His visit lasted from December 17, 2024, at 9-10 pm 2024 to May 1, 2025. The focus was on almost everything in the game, not just one specific concept. The rewards included a Festive Planter, a Cub Buddy, and a Gloomy Cub.

    His eighth visit started on December 25, 2025; however, none of his quests were introduced until December 27, 2025, where he gave out ten quests for rewards including a Festive Planter. On January 16, 2026, 5 more quests were introduced for a Cub Buddy (Cub Buddy Voucher if the player already owns a Cub Buddy). On April 23, 2026, the last 5 quests were released, rewarding the player with the Petal Cub skin on completion. The main focus on his quests were Blooms. His visit ended on June 8, 2026, at midnight EST (GMT-5).

    Tips¶

    • Use field boosters whenever possible. They help with collecting pollen, these can also be stacked using present rewards or codes. If necessary, using crafting material boosts can also help.
    • Collect honey tokens from sprouts, honeystorms, and the Stick Bug Challenge. Players can summon a honey storm using the Honey Storm Summoner near Brown Bear every 4 hours or use the Special Sprout Summoner every 16 hours near the Red HQ. Stick Bug, Stick Nymphs, and Stick Bug's Defense Totems also drop honey tokens so collect those too. This applies to A Pinch Of Magic and Honeyday Harvest. Many of Bee Bear's quests (All I want for Beesmas is Goo, Unsilent Night, and A Beesmas Miracle) also require special tokens (Gumdrops/Moon Charms) that require active firefly hunting or sprout planting.
    • Use the Glue Dispenser. If the player doesn't have a Gummy Bee but has a Goo Hotshot badge, use a Field Booster and then the Glue Dispenser in Gummy Bear's Lair. Using it gives the player the Unlimited Gumdrops buff that, when coupled with a field booster, would help the player with the goo requirements in Festive Bee Workshops 1 and 3, as well as in All I Want for Beesmas is Goo.
    • Give presents to NPCs. If an NPC gives a Field Boost that the player needs for a quest, they should give them a present if they haven't yet. In 2018, players could only give a present to Onett after completing all of his Star Journey quests. Gifted Bucko Bee, Gifted Riley Bee, and Stick Bug require a Translator before a present can be given to them.
    • Use Magic Beans to summon Sprouts. In some of Bee Bear's quests, it is required to collect special treats such as strawberries. Sprouts are the best way to collect special treats. Sprout drops are varied towards the field it is in. For example, a sprout found in the Strawberry Field will drop more strawberry tokens than any other treat. Sprouts are especially useful for Festive Bee Workshop 3. Some of Bee Bear's 2019 quests also required tokens from sprouts.
    • Avoid defeating any mobs that take a long time to respawn if one of Bee Bear's upcoming quests requires completing Polar Bear quests. This includes mantises, scorpions, spiders, and werewolves. Otherwise, the player may have to wait longer to complete these quests due to their cooldowns.
    • Server-hop for Rogue Vicious Bees, Wild Windy Bees, and Bean Bugs. This is as many of Bee Bear's Cub Buddy quests require tokens from Wild Windy Bee or the defeat of Wild Windy Bee.
    • Place planters in specific fields ahead of time if you know you will need to harvest hours' worth of nectar in an upcoming quest.
    • During Beesmas Summer 2024, trade in the Hive Hub as much as possible to get all the stickers required for Bee Bear's quests.
