    Panda Bear is a quest giver, and one of the eight permanent quest bears that can be accessed in the game, the others being Black Bear, Brown Bear, Mother Bear, Science Bear, Dapper Bear, Polar Bear, and Spirit Bear.

    He is the fourth quest bear that the player can reach. His quests focus on defeating mobs. He is located on a small hill between the Blue Flower Field and the Bamboo Field, right next to the Gumdrop Shop. The Global Top Battlers Leaderboard is also next to him. Panda Bear was a quest giver for Egg Hunt 2019 and gave the Boxing Glove ornament during Beesmas (2019 and beyond).

    Panda Bear can be reached through many methods. One option is to climb the ladder on the side of the platform, closest to the Blue HQ. If the player has enough Jump Power, they can jump onto the platform without climbing the ladder. Another method is to jump on the Gumdrop Shop and then up to Panda Bear.

    Trivia¶

    • The Battle Badge set is represented by Panda Bear.
    • Panda Bear is the only bear whose quests consist solely of defeating mobs. This may be a reference to the Kung Fu Panda. However, his 2019, 2020 and 2021 Beesmas quest does include the collection of pollen.
    • Panda Bear is the second quest giver that requires the player to defeat Tunnel Bear in his quests; the first one being Onett, the third one being Science Bear, and the fourth being Bubble Bee Man.
    • Excluding limited-time quests, he and Mother Bear are the only bears who don't require collecting pollen from fields for their quests.
    • Like many of the other quest bears, Panda Bear is aware he is in a Roblox game because, in the conclusion, he says "maybe after the next update..."
    • Panda Bear gives a total of 120 tickets when completing all of his quests.
    • In the quest “Spider Slayer 2”, he says the player needs to defeat 10 spiders, but the quest only requires 5 to complete it.
    • If the player gave a Present to Panda Bear during Beesmas 2018, he gave 5,000 honey, 1 Rage Bee jelly, 15 stingers, 10 tickets, and a x4 Bamboo Field Boost.
    • The quest “The REAL Final Showdown” used to require 10 werewolves defeated, but this was nerfed.
    • During his conclusion dialogue, he says the word "BOPMASTER", which is the name of a code of the same name (valid). He also had a code named after him called "PandaPower" (expired).
