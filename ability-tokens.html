    Mechanics

    Ability Tokens¶

    Ability tokens are tokens generated by bees, which can be collected to activate special abilities and buffs. Almost all bees spawn ability tokens, with the exceptions being ungifted Basic Bee and Brave Bee without Beequips. If the player doesn't collect a token within a certain amount of time, it fades away. Ability tokens are only sent to the bee's owner, so players can't see or collect tokens made by other players' bees. Collection is checked by the server: you collect a token when you are within 4 studs of it horizontally and 6 studs vertically. Prior to the May 7th, 2018 update, other players' tokens were visible, but still couldn't be collected by others.

    Some tokens aren't generated by the player's bees (such as honey tokens from flowers). The player can tell apart the two types of tokens by the fact that tokens dropped by bees give off gold sparkles, while tokens generated through other means do not sparkle. Also, tokens dropped by someone else's bees are not visible to the player, which means every visible token is collectible (though there is a bug where some loot tokens cannot be collected, usually at the end of a Honeystorm or Snowstorm).

    Details¶

    Spawning categories¶

    Ability tokens fall into 3 categories, based on when a bee can spawn that token:

    • Gather: Ability tokens that can be generated when the bees are collecting pollen. Most ability tokens that can be generated under this situation can collect pollen and/or boost pollen collection.
    • Battle: Ability tokens that can be generated when the bees are targeting/attacking mobs. Most ability tokens that can be generated under this situation can inflict damage or debuffs to enemies and boost bees' combat process.
    • Idle: Ability tokens that can be generated when the bees are not doing any other activities (gathering, attacking, converting,...)

    Some ability tokens fall into multiple or all of the categories above.

    Spawning attempts¶

    When a bee pauses after making movement (either to gather a flower, to attack, or to simply turn), it will attempt to spawn its ability tokens.

    Each ability token has 3 variables that dictate how often the ability token can spawn:

    • Use cooldown: The amount of time it takes after the ability token has been spawned for the bee to attempt to summon that ability token again.
    • Attempt cooldown: The amount of time a bee has to wait in-between attempts before it can attempt to spawn the ability token again.
    • Attempt success rate: The probability that the attempt succeeds. If the attempt succeeds, the ability token is added to a pool that the bee uses to spawn its ability tokens.

    When a bee attempts to spawn its ability tokens, it goes through every token ability it can spawn and check if its use cooldown and attempt cooldown has expired. If it has, the bee attempts to spawn the token. If this attempt succeeds, the token is added to a pool. Once every ability has been checked, the bee looks in its pool and picks a random token to spawn, removing that token while keeping every other token in the pool for the next spawn attempt. The spawned token then starts its use cooldown again.

    A bee can spawn at most one ability token per action (gathering, attacking or idling), and none while it is converting at the hive or fetching. Gifted bees also roll Inspire when they gather.

    The average time between two tokens of the same ability can be found using the formula: \(time=useCooldown+attemptCooldown\times ({\frac {1}{attemptSuccessRate}}-1)\). The first attempt happens as soon as the use cooldown ends, and each failed attempt adds one attempt cooldown. If the attempt cooldown is longer than the use cooldown, use the attempt cooldown in place of the use cooldown.

    Stats¶

    These are stats which affect the characteristics of ability tokens.

    Bee Ability Rate¶

    Bee Ability Rate increases or decreases how often bees spawn their ability tokens, by dividing the use cooldown and attempt cooldown, as well as multiplying the attempt success rate. Note that because of this, Bee Ability Rate increases or decreases ability tokens' spawn rate by much more than its stated amount. Ability rate can't go below 0.1, and the success rate is capped at 100%.

    For example: a 200% bee ability rate will reduce an ability's use and attempt cooldown to 50% of its original value (100% ÷ 200%), and increase its success rate by 100% (or double it).

    Ability Token Lifespan¶

    Ability Token Lifespan extends the duration of uncollected ability tokens generated by bees before they despawn. It can be boosted by a Gifted Stubborn Bee and Cog Amulet and by the Paper Angel and Paperclip beequips, and also temporarily by adding the Drooping Stubborn Bee Sticker to the Sticker Stack. The maximum amount currently possible is 145%.

    A token's lifetime is its base lifetime × the Ability Token Lifespan multiplier. Most buff tokens last 4 seconds, and tokens with no set lifetime last 20 seconds. Lifespan sources in the game's code include the Melittology "Lingering Tokens" perk (ability tokens last 3 times longer), the Gifted bonus "+15% Ability Token Lifespan" and the Steering Wheel Ornament (+5%).

    Ability Duplication Chance¶

    When there is a chance, ability tokens will spawn its duplicated/duped variants randomly on a field. Duped tokens hover over the field and last for double the tokens' lifespan, and that lifespan is further increased by 5% per Digital Bee's level. Duped tokens can be collected/activated by standing beneath it for 1 second.

    Bee Movespeed¶

    Bee Movespeed affects how fast bees move and perform actions.

    While not directly related to ability tokens, having more Bee Movespeed allows bees to make more attempts at creating Ability Tokens, and vice versa. This overall increases or decreases the average spawn rate of an ability token.

    Bee Ability Tokens¶

    Bomb¶

    Bomb collects pollen from flowers in an area. All Bomb tokens have a black disc. Red, Blue and Buzz Bombs share one icon, tinted red, blue or white, and the Bomb+ tokens use the same icon with a plus sign.

    Split by the types of flowers the ability collects, there are 3 categories:

    • Buzz Bombs collect 7 pollen from all types of flowers.
    • Red Bombs collect 10 pollen from only Red flowers.
    • Blue Bombs collect 10 pollen from only Blue flowers.

    The amount of pollen collected increases by 10% per level (e.g., 200% on a level 20 bee).

    Split by the range of the ability, there are 2 categories:

    • Bomb tokens collect from 13 surrounding flowers.
    • Bomb+ tokens collect from 29 surrounding flowers.

    Collecting Bomb tokens also grants the Bomb Combo buff, which grants a buff to Bomb Power.

    Many bees have at least one of the bomb variants:

    • Red Bomb is granted by Rascal, Demon, and Shy Bees.
    • Red Bomb+ is granted by Fire, Demon, and Festive Bees.
    • Blue Bomb is granted by Bumble, Bubble, and Buoyant Bees.
    • Blue Bomb+ is granted by Frosty, Ninja, and Vicious Bees.
    • Buzz Bomb is granted by Bomber, Commander, and Exhausted Bees.
    • Buzz Bomb+ is granted by Demo, Lion, and Fuzzy Bees.

    There are also other sources of Bomb tokens:

    • Balloons during a Surprise Party have a chance to generate Blue and a Buzz Bomb tokens.
    • Many Beequips can give the bee that equipped it Bomb abilities, if the beequip has them.
    • Bang Snaps can give Bomb and Bomb+ of any color.
    • Smiley Stickers can give Blue Bomb.
    • Bottle Caps can give Buzz Bomb.

    If Red Bomb Sync is on, all Red Bombs collect white pollen. If Blue Bomb Sync is on, all Blue Bombs collect white pollen. If both Syncs are active, all Bombs collect any color of pollen.

    When Pollen Haze is active, all Bomb ability tokens are automatically collected, and they may also pollinate flower patches that they collect from.

    Bomb falls into the Gather category, meaning it spawns while gathering from flowers.

    Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Bomb has a use cooldown of 8 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 4 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 25%. This gives an average spawn rate of 1 token every 20 seconds.

    The token lasts for 4 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.

    Mark¶

    A Mark token deploys a Mark in a random area on a field that grants a specific buff when the player stands in its range. When a mark is activated, its radius and the green light on top of the mark lights up, and the player is given a buff based on what mark they activated.

    Each Mark can be stacked to increase its effect, done by linking with each other (i.e. overlapping each other). If the player stands in a Mark that is linked to another one, all linked Marks are activated.

    There are currently 4 types of marks in the game:

    • Pollen Mark gives the Pollen Mark buff, which increases all pollen.
    • Honey Mark gives the Honey Mark buff, which grants conversion links and increases Convert Rate.
    • When the player walks off the Honey Mark's range, conversion links remain, and bees still convert honey normally until they finish converting. The player will lose the Convert Rate buff, however.
    • Precise Mark gives the Precise Mark buff, which increases Critical Chance and Super-Crit Chance. See the dedicated section for more details.
    • Festive Mark gives the Festive Mark, which grants several buffs. See the dedicated section for more details.

    The rest of this section will only concern Pollen Marks and Honey Marks.

    The two Mark abilities are split into the Mark and Mark+ variant.

    • The Mark variant spawns Marks that lasts for 7 seconds (+0.2s per bee level) with a range of 14 studs.
    • The Mark+ variant spawns Marks that lasts for 8 seconds (+0.25s per bee level) with a range of 18 studs.

    Many bees have at least one of the Mark variants:

    • Pollen Mark is granted by Stubborn and Carpenter Bees.
    • Pollen Mark+ is granted by Vector Bee.
    • Honey Mark is granted by Honey and Festive Bees.
    • Honey Mark+ is granted by Carpenter Bee.

    There are also other sources of Mark tokens:

    • Balloons during a Surprise Party have a chance to generate Pollen Mark and Honey Mark tokens.
    • Smiley Stickers can give the bee that equipped it the Honey Mark ability, if the beequip has it.

    Mark falls into the Gather category, meaning it spawns while gathering from flowers.

    Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Mark has a use cooldown of 20 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 5 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 25%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 35 seconds.

    The token lasts for 8 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.

    The player can see other people's Marks, but they can't see those Marks' range, the Marks don't grant buffs to them, they can't be linked with their Marks, and vice versa (with the exception of Festive Marks).

    Target Practice¶

    Target Practice is an ability exclusive to Precise Bee.

    When collected on a field, it causes the bee to fly above the field the player is in and face the part of the field it is opposite from. Then it projects 3 targets, from the closest to farthest from the bee. Walking on and passing the target(s) activates it.

    After a few seconds:

    • All of the activated targets will be shot by the bee, collecting pollen from 29 surrounding flowers and spawning a Focus token.
    • The amount of pollen collected is equal to x2 Precise Bee's attack and increased by +10% per level.
    • With Flame Heat, the shot gains up to x4 pollen and up to +50% instant conversion.
    • All of the inactive targets spawn Red Boost tokens instead.

    If the player is shot while standing on a target, all ability tokens are collected, and all of the player's current Flame Heat buff is used to instantly convert pollen from the player's container, with half of that turning into 5 honey tokens around the shot target. The amount of pollen converted is equal to 50% total conversion + x10 Precise Bee's convert amount, and increased by up to x10 with consumed Flame Heat and up to x3 with field boost stacks.

    When all 3 targets are activated, all ability tokens are collected and the player is granted a stack of Precision, which grants extra super-crit chance.

    If the player collects a Target Practice token that is outside of a field, or when the bee that summoned the token is outside the field, they will immediately be granted either the Precision or Focus buff. The chances of getting the Precision buff are 20%, and 80% for Focus buff.

    Target Practice falls into the Battle and Gather categories, meaning it spawns while attacking mobs and while gathering from flowers.

    Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Target Practice has a use cooldown of 24 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 6 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 25%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 42 seconds.

    The token lasts for 8 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.

    Impale¶

    Impale is an ability exclusive to Vicious Bee.

    Vicious Bee generates an Impale token that summons a number of spikes equal to the bee's level, that damages enemies for 5% of their current health and increases with Vicious Bee's attack, but the damage is reduced past 1,000. Mobs that have been hit by a spike take less damage from other consecutive spikes.

    Each enemy hit by a spike generates a honey token worth 50 (+50 per level) honey. These honey tokens can be collected by Token Link and take 10 seconds to fade.

    If Vicious Bee is Gifted, gold spikes spawn instead of normal ones. Impale will have +1 levels of accuracy added (meaning Vicious Bee's spikes can hit mobs that are one level higher than it without missing).

    A more detailed explanation of the ability is provided below:

    • Impale first targets every living enemy in a 75-stud radius of the player.
    • If it finds a group of targets, it summons a spike at the nearest target's position, then waits up to 0.2 seconds before summoning another at the next target. This is repeated until every target in the group has been damaged once.
    • It then prepares a number of spikes equal to the level of the Vicious Bee that activated the ability.
    • If Impale has not run out of spikes yet, it will target every living enemy in a 75-stud radius of the player again. This is repeated until it runs out of spikes, or there are no more living targets in the radius.
    • The number of times an enemy has been targeted is stored, henceforth referred to in formulas as cnt.
    • The summoned spikes' accuracy work the same way as bee attacks, just with 1 extra level if Vicious Bee is gifted.
    • The damage dealt is calculated in this order:
    • Let atk be Vicious Bee's attack at the time of activation. Then \(baseDamage=2\times atk\).
    • If the target has the Humanoid tag (as of the current update, every enemy has this tag), adds 5% of target's HP to baseDamage.
    • \(baseDamage>1000\), excess damage is square rooted: \(baseDamage=1000+{\sqrt {baseDamage-1000}}\)
    • The damage then is nerfed by the number of times the enemy has been targeted: \(baseDamage=baseDamage\times 0.05+{\frac {baseDamage}{cnt}}\times 0.95\).
    • Critical hits and Super-Crits are then applied, along with any Impale damage multiplier, resulting in the variable finalDamage.
    • If a critical hit or Super-Crit occurred, the damage is nerfed to 75%: \(finalDamage=\left\lfloor {finalDamage\times 0.75+0.5}\right\rfloor\)
    • The enemy is dealt finalDamage damage.

    Impale falls into the Battle category, meaning it spawns while attacking mobs.

    Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Impale has a use cooldown of 30 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 1 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 100%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 30 seconds.

    The token lasts for 24 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.

    Tornado¶

    Tornado is an ability exclusive to Windy Bee.

    When collected, all of the player's Haste stacks are taken to summon a tornado at the position of the token, assuming the token is on a field. The tornado moves around the field while dealing damage to mobs, collecting pollen and tokens, and popping bubbles and fuzz bombs, around a 5-tile-radius range (approximately 49 surrounding flower tiles) every second. It will also collect pollen out of balloons it passes through.

    The speed, damage, and lifespan of a summoned tornado depends on the number of Haste stacks spent and the level of the bee that summoned it. Let x be the number of Haste stacks spent and y be the level of the bee that summoned it, the following formulas can be used:

    • \(multiplier=1+0.1\times {({\frac {y-1}{19}})}^{2}\)
    • \(duration=10+x\times 0.4+{\frac {x^{1.4}}{3.2}}\), add 2 if at least 1 stack of Haste was spent
    • \(damage=10+y^{1.5}\)
    • \(\left\lfloor 3.5+{\frac {y}{3}}\right\rfloor\) pollen from each flower around its radius per second. Pollen collected is multiplied by multiplier.
    • \(\left\lfloor 0.5+damage+(20+y^{1.5}\times 1.75-damage)\times {({\frac {x}{3}})}^{2}\right\rfloor\) damage to each enemy in its radius per second.
    • \(7+18\times {\frac {x}{10}}\) studs a second.
    • Lasts for \(\left\lfloor duration\times multiplier+0.5\right\rfloor\) seconds.

    Note that if the Tornado token is collected outside or above the field, it does not summon a tornado but still spends Haste stacks.

    Tornado falls into the Gather and Battle categories, meaning it spawns while gathering from flowers and while attacking mobs.

    Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Tornado has a use cooldown of 80 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 10 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 33.33%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 100 seconds.

    The token lasts for 24 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.
